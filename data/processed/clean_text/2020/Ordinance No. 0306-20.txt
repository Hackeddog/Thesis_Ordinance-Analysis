Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN
BEHALF OF THE CrTY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF USUFRUCT

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POUCY - It is hereby declared the policy of the
City of Davao to encourage advancement of education as it remains the most important
key in enhancing the knowledge, abilities, attitude, and values of society in a challenging
advanced economic and technological environment. It is the key to safeguard and secure
sustai nable development.
SECTION 3. DEED OF USUFRUCT - The Deed of Usufruct refers to the
instrument to be executed by and between the City Government of Davao and DepEdDavao City National High School.
SECTION 4. AUTHORIW - The City Mayor is hereby granted authority to sign,
for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao the Deed of Usufruct between DepEdDavao City National High School and the City Government of Davao, relative to the
temporary use of a poftion of the facilities of Davao City National High School for the
operation of the City College of Davao.
SECTION 5. REPEALING CAUSE - Any Ordinance, local issuances or rules
inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified
accordingly.
SECTION 6. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any SECTION or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and
effect.
SECTION 7. EFFECTMW - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.
ENACTED, July 7,2020, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
