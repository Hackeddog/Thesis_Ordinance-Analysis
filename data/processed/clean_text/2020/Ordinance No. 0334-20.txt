Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
APPROVING THE REQUEST OF DR. NOEL BACASNOT, PRESTDENT OF
MAGALLANES RESTDENCES CONDOMTNIUM CORPORATION (MRCC), BOLTON
EXTENSION, THIS CITY, FOR THE WAIVER OF PAYMENT OF THE REAL
pRopERTy TAX FOR THE YEAR 2020, BECAUSE THEY WERE NOT ABLE TO
UTILIZE THEIR PROPERTY DUE TO STRUCTURAL DAMAGE AND THE SAME
WAS DECLARED *UNSAFE" BY THE LOCAL AUTHORITIES DUE TO SERIES OF
EARTHQUAKES, CONSIDERED AS A NATURAL CALAMITY, THAT STRUCK
DAVAO CITY AND THE REST OF DAVAO REGION IN 2019".
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY SECTION 192 of the Local
Government Code states that "Local Government Units may, through ordinances duly
approved, grant tax exemptions, incentives or reliefs under such terms and conditions as
they may deem necessary".
SECTION 3. EXEMPTION - SECTION 276 of the Local Government Code states
that:
SECTION 276: Condonation or Reduction of Real Property Tax and
fnterest -In case of general failure of crops or substantial decrease in the price
of agricultural or agri-based products, or calamity in any province, city or
municipality, the sanggunian concerned, by ordinance passed prior to the first
day of January of any year and upon the recommendation of the Local Disaster
Coordinating Council, may Condone or Reduce, wholly or partialiy, the taxes anci
interest thereon for the succeeding year or years in any city or municipality
affected by the calamity.
SECTION 4. PERIOD - This waiver of payment of the real property tax will be
for the year 2020 only, and the Tax Condonation shall only be applied proportionately to
be computed by the Office of the CiW Building Official (OCBO), City Assessor's Offlce,
City Treasurer's Office, all of this City, and shall not apply to tax penalties and
surcharges previously incurred.
SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any SECTION or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and
effect.
SECTION 6. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval and valid only for Calendar Year 2020 Real Property Tax.
ENACTED, August 25, 2020, by a majority vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
