This Agreement, made and entered, by and between:
RIZAL MEMORIAL COILEGES, INC., educational

WITNESSETH: That

Emergenry by viftue of Proclamation No. 922 and calls for immediate decisive
action of the government to contain the transmission of COVID-19, through
re, medical tests, treatment and quarantine, among others;
WHEREAS, COVID 19 remains a threat to public health and national
security which can undermine the social, economic and well-being of the society.
WHEREAS, among the measures undeftaken by the LGU to contain the
spread of COVID 19 is the isolation of the patient and those under monitoring. For
this purpose, the LGU is in need of available buildings or spaces that will serve as
quarantine/isolation area for those affected with the present pandemic, to include
the frontliners.
WHEREAS, recognizing the threats brought about by the COVID 19
pandemic to the well-being of the Filipinos, the OWNER desires to assist and
support the LGU to contain the spread of the said COVID-l9 virus by offering its
Covered Gymnasium as isolation or quarantine area free of charge to the LGU.
NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the
Pafties hereto agree the following:

1.1 The Pafties hereby designate THE RMC PETRO GMZ ARENA, four
(4) Classrooms, one (1) Office, and monitoring space at the Lobby Entrance, all
located along Lopez Jaena Street, Davao City, as Quarantine Area (hereinafter
refened to as the "AREA"), primari[ for the residents of Davao City who are
_affected with the pandemic, to be managed and operated by the LGU.,

2.1 This Agreement shall commence on 20 April 2020 coinciding with
the actual entry and use of the AREA for a term of three (3) months.
2.2 This Agreement may be renewed or extended for a similar period of
three (3) months, under mutually acceptable terms and conditions, subject to the

the OWNER at least fifteen (15) days before the expiration of this Agreement of
its intention to renew and or extend.
3. THE OWNER
3.1 Make available and allows the RMC GYM, four (4) Classrooms, and
one (1) Office including the comfoft rooms as Quarantine Area for COVID 19
patients (suspect and probable cases) as determined beforehand by the LGU.
3.2 Allows the City Government of Davao to introduce temporary
improvements in the AREA for the purpose of converting it into an isolation facility
and to bring beds, beddings and other fixtures inside the AREA as needed.
3.3 Allow the personnel of the LGU to have access to the AREA anytime
of the day and night including the use of water and electricity.
3.4 Subject to prior notice, may enter the AREA during reasonable hours
of the day for the purpose of inspection, maintenance and repair of any work
necessary for the preseruation, conseruation, improvement of the AREA.
4. THE LGU
4.1 Undeftake to prepare the AREA for quarantine purpose following
the guidelines issued by the Depaftment of Health and other appropriate body.
4.2 Provide food, laundry, medical and other necessary seruices to the
patients.
4.3 Keep and maintain the AREA in a clean, safe and sanitary condition.
4.4 Provide medical teams and support staff and ensure that all front
liners in the AREA shall use personal protective equipment (PPE) to prevent
exposure to hazards that cause serious injury or illness, including but not limited
to masks, gloves, etc.
4.5 Assume all actual usage costs of consumption of all utilities and any
and all seruices i.e. electric, telephone, water, security, etc. required in the AREA
and pay for all charges for these utilities and services as they fall due.
4.6 Monitor and supervise the AREA to ensure the security, well-being,
health of the frontliners and the patients.
4.7 Within fifteen (15) days after the expiration or termination of this
Agreement, it shall cause the cleaning, sanitizing and disinfecting the AREA. It
surrender and return the premises in as good condition as the premises wereT
