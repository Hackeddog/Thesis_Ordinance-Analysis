for your mental health, stress management, understanding depression, parenting
seminar internet addiction among others. This aims to capacitate every member of
the community, and help them attain sound mental health and promote prevention
against mental disorders. The lecture / seminar/ forum shall be given to different
Barangay in the City of Davao, including government offices/ private and public
schools/ centers and any government agencies in need of mental health awareness;
3. Provide necessary services to the public/ private schools in implementing mental
health awareness through Education lnformation Services (ElS);
4. Render services to the people of Davao City in any means of services such as
consultatio nl referral/ coordination with different mental health
practitioners(Psychiatrist, Developmental Pediatricians, Psychologist, Guidance
Counselors) and other leading agency in mental health to address the needs of the
people of Davao City to help them in combating their day to day struggles; and
5. Submit the Liquidation Report reflecting the usage of funds in implementing the
programs and services of Philippine Mental Health Association Davao Chapter.
2. Provide Psychological assessment following the standards and implementing
procedures laid down by appropriate agencies to the wards under City Socials Services
and Development Office and clients from City Bovernment of Davao with free of
charge as direct beneficiaries;
