Ord. No. 0255-20
Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in
session assembled, that:
SECTIOil 1. TITLE- This Ordinance shall be known as "THE DAVAO CITY
FRONTLINERS, PATIENTS AND CLOSE CONTACT PROTECTION ORDINANCE.'
SECTION 2. DEFINITION OF TERMS - The following terms used in this
Ordinance shall mean:
1. Frontline Workers or Frontliners - are defined as persons employed in, whether
regular, casual, contractual, project based, job order, part-time, or other forms
of employment, or exercising essential frontline functions, such as:
a. Health workers, such as doctors, nurses, hospital or clinic aids,
administrative staff, laboratory technicians, pharmacists and drug store
employees;
b. Employees of the national, local and barangay units tasked to work on the
fronUines;
c. Members of the AFP, PNP, PCG, BFP, and other law enforcement
agencies;
d. Employees of non-government organizations assisting in health, safety,
disaster or emergency response such the Philippine Red Cross, World
Health OrganizaUon, and the like;
e. Employees of banks, bayad centers, money transfer services, cooperative
and savings establishments, and the like;
f . Employees of groceries, convenience stores, wet markets and the like;
g. Employees of food preparation, manufacturing and delivery services;
h. Those operating publk utility vehicles such as taxis, jeepneys, and
trirycles;
i. Those working in essential private service sectors such as security guards,
janitors, caretakers, funeral parlors, drivers of cargo vehicles and vehicles
hired to transport other frontline workers, business process outsourcing or
call centers, LPG and gas stations, water refilling stations, and the like;
and
j. Mass media personnel.
2. Patients - are deflned as:
a. Confirmed Case - any individual, irrespective of the preserrce or
absence of clinical signs and symptoms, who was laboratory
confirmed for COVID-19 in a test conducted at a DOH certified
laboratory testing facility.
b. Suspected Case - are those identified by DOH guidelines as falling
under such classification;
c. Probable Case- are those identified by DOH guidelines as falling
under such classiflcation.
Provided, that any change in classification of the DOH of cases shall fall under
the cabeEory of "patients" under this Ordinance.

Ord. No. 0255-20
3. Close Contact - is a person without proper personal protective equipment who is
providing direct care for a confirmed COVID-19 case, or a person which has
direct physical contact, or lived, worked, transacted or travelled in close
proximity (less than 1 rneter) for more than 15 minutes with a confirmed COVID
19 case, provided that any change by DOH of this definition shall be adopted
automatically under this Ordinance.
SE TION 3. PROHIBITED ACTS
A. No person, natural or juridical shall be allowed to:
1. Discriminate against, or hiring, engaging, influencing others to discriminate
against frontliners, patients or close conbacts or analogous circumstances:
a. Dishonoring existing contractual obligations such as lease contracts,
including the refusal to allow entry into such leased premises, solely
by reason of being a frontliner, patient or a close contact;
b. Performing any form of coercion or harassment to force or influence
a person to leave a leased or shared premises, solely by reason of
being a frontliner, patient or close contact;
c. Removing or firing a person from employment, solely by reason of
being a frontliner, patient or close contacfi
d. Refusal to follow set guidelines by the appropriate government
agencies on hazard pay, special risk allowance, or other mandatory
benefits, allowances or compensation;
e. Refusal of entry into or expelling frontliners from groceries,
convenience stores, markets, palengkes, banks, bayad centers or
other financial services, and the like;
f. Refusing to allow frontliners access to basic services such as food
preparation and delivery, transportation, laundry, healthcare,
medicine, and the like,
2. Commit any act intended to prejudice against, cause injury to, or harass or
those which shall tend to cause shame or humiliation to frontliners, patients,
or close contacts, or hiring, engaging, influencing others to do the same,
Such as:
a. Any form of physical, mental or emotional violence or abuse;
b. Any form of coercion or threat of physical, mental or emotional
violence;
c. Any form of financial or economic abuse such as, but not limited to,
refusing loans or forfeiting those already granted, rejecting valid
payments, failing to properly compensate, denying any support legally
due, solely by reason of being a frontliner, patient or close contact;

Ord. No. 0255-20
d. Disclosing the information of a person, including, but not limited to,
his/her name, known address, place of work or employment, known
alias, medical details, or implying the identity of, or claiming that a
person - whether verifled or not by DOH - is a frontliner, patient or
close contact, unless done so with the permission of such individual or
DOH;
e. Any form of cyber-bullying or the sharing of classified or private
communication without the consent of all involved in the same, or any
malicious acts done online, whether the information shared be true or
made-up, against the name, dignity or honor of a frontliner, patient or
close contact;
3. Any other act of discrimination or prejudice against which demeans, reduces
or nullifies the enjoyment or exercise of a frontliner, patient, or close
contact's human and legal rights, or hiring, engaging, influencing others to
do the same.
B. In addition to the above acts, it shall be unlawful for any public official,
employee, health or medical seruice provider or law enforcement officer to
refuse assistance, deny seruice to or block aid, or hiring, engaging,
influencing others to do the same, to a frontliner, patient or close contact
who wished to enforce his/her rights, unless such person is in violation of the
guidelines of the Department of Health and other existing laws.
SECTION 4. PENALW CLAUSE - Any violation of the provisions of this
Ordinance shall be punished by a fine of Five Thousand Pesos (P5,000) and/or
imorisonment of not more than 1 vear for each offense committed, without prejudice to
the prosecution of other administrative, criminal, or civil actions
Provided, that if the offender is a juridical person, the employees, owners,
paftners, managers or board of directors who participated, contributed or tolerated the
act shall be held individually liable.
SECTION 5. REPEALIN
UT
- Any Ordinance, local issuances or rules
inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified,
accordingly.
SECTION 6. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If , for any reason, any SECTION of this
Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof
which are not affected thereby, shall continue to be in full force and effect,
SECTION 7. EFFECTIVITI - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days
upon approval, posting and publication in a newspaper of local circulation in Davao City.
