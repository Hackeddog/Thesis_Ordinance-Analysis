On March 8,2020, the Office of the President issued Presidential Proclamation No. 922 and
declared a State of Public Health Emergency due to COVID-l9, throughout the entire
Philippines. Subsequently, on March 16, 2A20, President Rodrigo R. Duterte issued
Presidential Proclamation No. 929 and declared the country under a State of Calamity. Both
Presidential Proclamations enjoined all government agencies and Local Govemment Units to
render full assistance and cooperation with each other and mobilize necessary resources to
undertake critical, urgent and appropriate measwes to curtail and eliminate the threat of
COVID-19.
In response to the said Presidential Proclamations and pursuant to its mandate under Republic
Act No. 7160 to ensure and promote health and safety within its jurisdictio4 the City
Government of Davao immediately took action to stop COVID-19 transmissions within the
city and the entire Davao Region. One of the measures adopted by the city was to identify and
convert public spaces into temporary isolation and quarantine faciiities.
In connection thereto, the City Government of Davao requests that all facilities of Bolton
Elementary School be used as temporary isolation and quarantine areas, primarily for Davao
City residents who are affected with the COVID-l9 pandemic for tkee (3) montts from the
city's actual entry and use as such.
Moreover, the City Government of Davao undertakes to ensure the following:
1. safekeeping of all property and valuables in the school premises during the operation of the
facility;
2, payment of utilities for the period the school is used as a temporary isolation and quarantine
facility;
3. conduct of the general cleaning and fumigation, and repair and/or replacement of damaged
school facilities as a result of the use of the school; and
4. payment of expenses related to the setting-up, operation and clearing of the areas used.
The Offrce of the undersigned anticipates for your usual consideration.
