Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
GRANTING THE APPLICATION OF GLOBE TELECOM, rNC. THROUGH MOHAVE
MANAGEMENT CONSULTANCY, rNC. FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE USE FOR
THE CONSTRUCTION OF A CELL SITE TOWER IN BARANGAY TALOMO,
TALOMO DISTRICT, THIS CrrY;
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POUCY - Article XII, SECTION 1 of Ordinance
No. 0546-13, SERIES of 2013, otherwise known as the "Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance
of Davao City" provides for requests for Additional Allowable Use which reads:
"The uses enumerated in the preceding articles on general and all sub-zones are not
exhaustive nor all-inclusive. The SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod, upon application of the project
proponent and, upon favorable recommendation by the Local Zoning Board of Adjustment
and Appeals (LZBAA), ffidy allow other uses not enumerated therein as it may deem fit and
proper including, but not limited to, the following projects which are socio-economic and
environmental significance and/or national interest by a 3/+ majority vote of all Members of
the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod through resolution and ordinance".
ARTICLE XII, SECTION 1.3 of the Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City
provides the requirements that must be submitted in a request for Additional Allowable
Use, to wit:
a. Barangay Council Resolution of No Objection
b, Barangay Development Council Resolution favorably indorsing the project
c. City Engineert Office for Drainage Clearance
d. City Environment and Natural Resources for Solid Waste Management Plan
e. City Health Office for Sanitation Clearance
f. City Assessor's Office for New Tax Declaration
g. City Treasurert Office for Realty Tax Clearance
h, Davao City Water District for certification of water supply availability
i. Davao Light and Power Company for certification of power supply availability
j. Mines and Geosciences Bureau for certification for possible geohazard and
recommended mitigating measures
k. DENR-Environmental Management Bureau for waste treatment facilities and permit to
discharge effluents
l. Water Resource Management CouncifTask Force for projects within Water Resource
Zone
m. Barangay Watershed Management Council and City Watershed Management Council
for areas in the Conseruation Zone
n. Golf Construction and Development Committee (for golf course)
o. Civil Aviation Authority of the Philippines and Philippine Air Force Command Davao
City (for private landing strips, airpofts, heliports and helipads)
p. Philippine Pofts Authority (for pofts and harbors);
q. National Power Corporation Davao City (for power generation plant/station)
SECTION 3. APPROVAL OF REOUEST - The request for Additional Allowable
Use Of Globe Telecom, Inc. through Mohave Management Consultancy, Inc. for the
construction of a cell site tower in Barangay Talomo, Talomo District, this City, is hereby
granted.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
