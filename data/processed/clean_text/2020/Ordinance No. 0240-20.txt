Ord. No. 0240-20
Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE- This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE ON
THE COLLECTION OF CORRESPONDING BARANGAY CLEARANCE FEE IN THE
APPLICATION FOR ANY BUSINESS-RELATED TRANSACTION".
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - It is the policy of the City of Davao
to establish effective practices aimed at the efficient turnaround in the delivery of
government services, and expedite business and activity related transactions by
integrating and institutionalizing simplified requirements and procedures in the
application, issuance, and collection of barangay clearance and permit related to doing
business in the City Government of Davao.
SECTION 3. AUTHORIW - SECTION 11(Q of Republic Act No. 1L032, otherwise
known as the "Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of
2018", requires that barangay clearances and permits related to doing business shall be
applied, issued, and collected by the city, provided that, the share in the collections shall
be remitted to the respective barangays.
SECTION 4. ASSFSSM
AND ISSTIANCF rlE RAPANGAY t^I FAPAN'F
ENT
AND PERMITS-RELATED TO DOING BUSINESS - The corresponding Barangay
Clearance and Permit related to doing business shall be assessed, collected, and issued
by the City through the City Treasurer's Office and Business Bureau.
SECTION 5. COST OF BARANGAY CLEARANCE AND PERMIT RELATED TO
DOING BUSINESS - The amount assessed and collected by the City shall be uniform
for all Barangays in the amount of One Hundred Peso\P100.00).
SECTION 6. REPORTING AND REMITTANCE - The City of Davao shall remit
the Barangay Clearance Fee collected on behalf of the subject Barangay not later than
seven (7) working days of the ensuing month with the list of the issued barangay
clearances or permits.
SECTION 7. INFORMATION DISSEMINATION - Aside from the publication
of the Ordinance in a newspaper of general circulation, all Barangays within the
City of Davao shall contribute in disseminating public information through posters,
tarpaulins or streamers to be placed in at least three (3) conspicuous places within their
respective areas of jurisdiction.
SECTION 8. SEPARABILIW CLAUSE - If for any reason, any SECTION or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and
effect.
SECTION 9. EFFECTMTY CLAUSE This Ordinance shall take effect
immediately upon approval.
ENACTED, on the 10th day of March 2020, by a unanimous vote of all the
Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
