WHEREAS, SECTION 1.5, Article ll of the 1987 Philippine Constitution declares that the
State shall protect and promote the right to health of the people and instill health
consciousness among them.
WHEREAS, SECTION 16 of R.A. 7L60 otherwise known as the Local Government Code of
1991 mandates, among others, that every local government unit shall exercise its powers to
promote health and safety of its inhabitants;
WHEREAS, Proclamation No. 922, s.2020 was issued declaring a State of Public Health
Emergency throughout the Philippines due to COVID-19, enjoining all government agencies
and local government unity (LGUs) to render full assistance and cooperation and mobilize the
necessary resources to undertake critical, urgent, and appropriate response and measures in a
timely manner, to curtail and eliminate the threat of COVID-19;
WHEREAS,Republic Act No. 1t469, otherwise known as the Bayanihan to Heal as One
Act, further declared the existence of a state of national emergency over the Philippines due to
the COVID-19 pandemic, and authorized the President to adopt emergency measures in
response to the same;
WHEREAS,R.A. No. 11469 supports measures to accelerate, expand, and enhance the
capability of our health care system through the construction of temporary medical isolation
and quarantine facilities, field hospitals, dormitories for frontliners, and for the expansion of
government hospitals' capacity all over the country;
WHEREAS,Department of lnterior and Local Government (DILG) Circular No. 2020-064,
otherwise known as the Provincial/City/Municipal Special Care Facilities and lsolation Units
Amid COVID-19 Pandemic, directs all local chief executives to identify government facilities
that may be temporarily converted into units for isolation and quarantine facilities, and to
immediately identify, designate and establish their respective COVID-19 special care facilities;
WHEREAS,Ihe appropriation is provided in the Quick Response Fund under the General
Appropriations Act (CAA) of 2019 and 2020 for the Department of Health, which shall be used
for the construction of Temporary Treatment and Monitoring Facilities (TTMF) intended to
respond to the COVID-19 health emergency;
WHEREAS,Ihe DOH, through the Quick Response Fund, has appropriated the amount of
THIRTY MILLION PESOS (PhP 30,000,000.00) for the construction of three (3) TTMFs in the City,
broken down into TEN MILLION PESOS (PhP 10,000,000.00) per facility;

Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORIW TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO
AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM

(TTMF)',.

provides, to wit:
