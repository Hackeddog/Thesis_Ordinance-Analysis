Although [,ocal Government Units have the unenviable task of pursuing in all fronts

resources, they are not however alone in turning this task into reality.
The Kapit-Bisig Laban sa Kahirapan Comprehensive and Integrated Delivery of Social
ServicesKapangyarihan at Kaunlaransa Barangay (KALAHI-CIDSS KKB), is one of the
anti-poverty programs of the DSWD that works through a localized and participatory
process in marginalized and vulnerable communities. Through its Disaster Response
Operations Modality/Procedure (DROM/P), a platform that speeds-up post-disaster
recovery and rehabilitatio& the DSWD will be able to realize the actualization of the
recovery stage of the SAP and at the same time help the LGUs in the delivery of basic
services towards a holistic development of communities in Program-covered areas.
NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the parties
agree as follows;
SECTION 1: Purpose
The purpose of this Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) is to establish a partnership
between the DSWD and the LGU which is aimed at providing the constituents with the
most appropriate recovery measures to cushion the impact of COWD-19 in its locality
and to implement a social protection program that will address the needs of the poor
and marginalized and promote inclusive growth in the LGU.
SECTION 2: Obiectives
The DSWD and the LGU agree to engage in a partnership with the objectives of: (i)
assisting in the economic recovery among the poor and marginalized; (ii) capacitating
communities to protect themselves from the effects of plausible similar disasters in the
future; and, (iii) institutionalizing the DROM/P that was committed by the National
Government to be adopted as a strategy under the Policy Loan for Disaster Risk and
Response Management (DPL-DRRM).
