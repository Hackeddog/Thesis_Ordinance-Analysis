SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POUCY - SECTION 16 of Republic Act No. 7160
or the Local Government Code of 1991 states that:
"SEC. 16. General Welfare. - Every local government unit shall exercise
the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as
well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and
effective governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of
the general welfare. Within their respective territorial jurisdictions, local
government units shall ensure and support, among other things, the
preseruation and enrichment of culture, promote health and safety,
enhance the right of the people to a balanced ecology, encourage and
suppoft the development of appropriate and self-reliant scientific and
technological capabilities, improve public morals, enhance economic
prosperity and social justice, promote full employment among their
residents, maintain peace and order, and preserue the comfort and
convenience of their inhabitants."
SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority
to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Memorandum of
Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and between Davao City Water District (DCWD)
and the CiW of Davao, relative to the septage aspect of City Ordinance No. 0363-10,
SERIES of 2010, or the Septage and Sewerage Management Ordinance of Davao City", in
accordance with existing laws, rules and regulations of the Republic of the Philippines.
SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any SECTION or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and
effect.

WITNESSETH:
WHEREAS, the CITY is committed to protect and promote the right to health
of the people and instill health consciousness among them. It is an environmental
concern of the City to have a sustainable management of waterways to meet and
safeguard the recreational and life supporting capacity of water. It is committed to
improving the water quality of the water resource within its territory and acknowledges
that large amounts of contaminants or discharges come from untreated domestic
sewage discharges and trade wastes, or industrial liquid wastes, thereby causing
significant health risks to people near the river, and those in residential areas affected
by floods;
WHEREAS, under SECTION 20 of RA 9275 or otherwise known as the
Philippines Clean Water Act imposes upon local government units to share the
responsibility in the management and improvement of water utility within their
territorial j urisd ictions;
WHEREAS, under SECTION 17, RA 7160 mandates local government units
(LGUs) to effectively provide basic services and facilities to their constituents,
6including drainage and sewerage;

WHEREAS, as provided for in SECTION 2, Article I of Ordinance No. 036310, SERIES of 2013 otherwise known as the Septage and Sewerage Management
Ordinance, it is a declared policy of Davao City to adopt and implement a
comprehensive and integrated septage and sewerage management program, which
shall among others, reduce, if not eliminate, the prevalence of waterborne and
excreta-related diseases and pollution in bodies of water and encourage greater
publ ic-private partnership;
WHEREAS, majority of the homes in Davao City use septic tanks as a way
of providing partialtreatment of septage and sludge.
WHEREAS, the sludge is being disposed without proper treatment due to
the non-existence of an appropriate treatment facility that caters to this need. Thus,
the waste is being discharged directly to the drainage canals, creeks and other
bodies of water that become polluted;
WHEREAS, the DCWD aims to establish a proper Septage Management
Program which will include the construction of Septage Treatment Plant (SpTP);
WHEREAS, a Feasibility Study for the proposed Septage Management
Program was conducted by DCWD;
WHEREAS, the Feasibility Study through the establishment of a Septage
Management Program aims to reduce the pollution attributed to untreated septage
discharge to protect our water resource, to improve water quality, sanitation and
public health conditions, and provide efficient and affordable septage desludging
service initially to existing DCWD customers and to alltargeted areas of DCWD;
WHEREAS, the CITY enacted Ordinance No.0363-10 and Implementing
Rules and Regulations prescribing the procedures in the establishment of Septage
and Sewerage Management Program; and
WHEREAS, the CITY has manifested its intent to support in the
establishment of SpTP.

BINDING AND TERMINATION
The Parties are entering into this MOA while wishing to maintain their own
separate and unique missions and mandates, and their own accountabilities. Unless
specifically provided otherwise, the cooperation between the Parties as outlined in
this MOA is not to be considered or construed as a partnership or other type of
legal entity or personality. Nothing in this MOA shall be construed as superseding
or interfering in any way with other agreements or contracts entered into between
the Parties, either prior to or subsequent to the signing of MOA.
This MOA shall be for a period of fifty (50) years commencing from the
date of notarization, renewable Ibr another twenty-five (25) years by giving written
notice to renew at least ninety (90) days prior to the expiration of the original fiftyyear term, upon terms and conditions mutually agreed by the parties.
Any party may terminate this MOA only for valid and just cause. Within
thirty (30) days following the termination of this MOA, DCWD shall remit all the
monies due to the CITY under this MOA.
