Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in
session assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE- This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN
BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
BETWEEN THE OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT AND THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF
DAVAO RELATIVE TO THE RENEWAL OF FUNDING ALLOCATION OF FIFTY
MILLION PESOS (PHP50,000,000.00) MONTHLY FOR THE MALASAKIT
PROGRAM FOR 2021'.
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - SECTION 455 (b) (vi) of Republic Act
No. 7160 or the Local Government Code of 1991 provides:
"SECTION 455. Chief fuecutive; Powers, Duties and Compensatbn. ta<

WTNESSETH, that:
WHEREAS, the City Government of Davao (the "City Government") constantly
promotes programs that help alleviate the economic and health conditions of its
constituents through making services accessible to less fortunate and indigent members
of the communities;
WHEREAS, among the programs implemented by the City Govemment in order
to address poverty focuses on the provision of medical assistance and fundamental
needs of recognized and qualified beneficiaries;
WHEREAS, the continuing increase of the public beneficiaries shows the
effectiveness of these programs thereby giving the City Government more opportunity
to advance its implementation and strategy in this aspect for the general welfare of its
people;
WHEREAS, one of these programs is the Lingap Para sa Mahirap Program (the
"Lingap Program') that has, through the years, provided medical assistance and
benefits to indigents;
WHEREAS, considering that the guidelines of the Lingap Program of the City
Government of Davao has already been established, and taking cognizant of the
pressing concem vis-d-vis the government's obligation to prioritize the health and wellbeing of the people and in order to extend medical assistance beyond Davao City
constituents, the Office of the President will allocate Fifty Million Pesos (P
50,000,000.00) monthly for the Malasakit Program, to be sourced from the Socio.Civic
Project Fund (SCPF).
WHEREAS, the OP and the City Government of Davao have agreed to expand
the coverage of the subject medical assistance to entire Mindanao;
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises and of

Fund (SCPF)for the Malasakit Program.
2. The OP - Office of the Deputy Executive Secretary for Finance and
Administration (ODESFA) shall pay direct to the hospitals, funeral parlors and

and supporting documents.

exceed P 50,000.00 net of Philhealth and other assistance for medical and P
30,000.00 for funeral.
2. The LGU-DAVAO CITY shall submit to the OP the Quarterly Summary of
Guarantee Letter issued.
