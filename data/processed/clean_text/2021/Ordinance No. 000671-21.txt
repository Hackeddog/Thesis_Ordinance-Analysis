Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in
session assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
CREATING A TRUST FUND USING PHILHEALTH FUNDS AND OTHER FUNDS
FOR THE USE OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, MARILOG DISTRICT
HOSPITAL, IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE PERTINENT BUDGETING LAWS AND
ACCOUNTING PROCEDURES OF THE CITY, PROVIDING RULES AND
REGULATIONS FOR ITS DISBURSEMENT AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES".
SECTION 2. DEFINITION OF TERMS - The Definitions and Terms used in the
revised Implementing Rules and Regulations of the National Health Insurance Act of
1995 (Republic Act No. 7875) are hereby adopted. As used in this Ordinance, the
following terms shall mean:
1. PHILHEALTH (PHIC) - refers to the Philippine Health Insurance
Corporation created under Republic Act No. 7875, otherwise known as
the National Health Insurance Act of 1995
2. PHILHEALTH CLAIMS - refers to claims filed by the seruice
provider with the PHIC to recover the costs/expenses incurred in the
treatment of patients. There are two (2) PHIC claims separately, the
PAY TO HCI claims and the PAY TO PROFESSIONAL FEE claims
3. MARILOG DISTRICT HOSPITAL TRUST FUND ACCOUNTS -
refers to the PHIC, HCI and PF accounts, account for other hospital
claims and fees, and donations
4. PROFESSIONAL FEES (PF) - refers to PHIC reimbursements for
professional seruices rendered by health providers in the hospitals
5. HEALTH CARE INSTITUTION (HCI) - refers to health care
provider institution
6. HMO - refers to the Health Maintenance Organizations
7. INSTITUTIONAL EXPENSES -refers to the expenses that shall be
incurred for trainings needed for accreditations and for any other
licensing requirements, emergency purchases or the unforeseen
expenses that are not included in the Manual Of Operation.
SECTION 3. COVERAGE AND SCOPE OF APPLICATION - This ordinance
shall cover the City Government of Davao Marilog District Hospital, a duly accredited
service provider of the PHIC.
SECTION 4. CREATION OF THE MARILOG DISTRICT HOSPITAL TRUST
FUND - There is hereby created a Marilog District Hospital Trust Fund. This hereby
create three (3) accounts, which shall be maintained to draw accounts from the
proceeds of the following:

PHIC claims paid to the Health Care Institution (MDH);
PHIC claims that are separately issued by PHIC to Professional Fees;
Claims from financial assistance, grants, subsidies, insurances,
Health Maintenance Organizations (HMO), and co-payments.
The City Accountant's Office and other relevant offices are hereby tasked to cause
the assignment of allotment class and account code pursuant to the recent government
accounting system on all sum accruing to this fund. Accounts accruing from the different
PHIC claims shall be treated separately and distinctly from each other as stipulated in
the PHIC disbursement of claims.
All other charges, fees, and other income received from the management and
operations not given to PHIC shall be remitted and deposited by the City Treasurer to
the Marilog District Hospital Trust Fund. All Donations received by the Davao City
Government intended for the development and operation of the Marilog District Hospital
shall also be deposited by the City Treasurer to the said Account.

SECTION 8. DISTRIBUTION OF PHIC PF POOLED AMOUNT The
provisions in this ordinance shall only cover the hospital personnel, individuals, and
institutions involved in giving seruices within the hospital.
The pooled amount shall be distributed as follows:
1. Fifty percent (50o/o) shall be distributed among the doctors of which the Chief
of Hospital shall be given two (2) shares and the rest shall be given one (1)
share each.
2. The other Fifty percent (50o/o) shall be distributed among the other non-doctor
personnel of the hospital, wherein three (3) shares shall be given to the
Administrative Officer, three (3) shares to the Nurse Superuisor, two (2) shares
for the nurses, head of the diagnostics and laboratory, operating room, delivery
room, pharmacy, outpatient depaftment, and other treatment units. The other
hospital personnel shall be given one (1) share each.
3. The eligibility of the personnel to avail incentives under this provision shall
take effect three (3) months after the effectivity of the employment and shall be
terminated upon the end of the contract of the employee.
The distribution of this pooled amount, with the guidance of PHIC, shall be
agreed upon by the personnel and shall be approved by the City Government of
Davao.
