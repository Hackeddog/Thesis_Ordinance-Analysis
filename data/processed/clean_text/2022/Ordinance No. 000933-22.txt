Ord. No. 0933-22
Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in
session assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
APPROVING AND ADOPTING THE DAVAO CITY DISASTER RISK REDUCTION
AND MANAGEMENT FUND INVESTMENT PLAN (DCDRRMFIP), FOR CALENDAR
YEAR 2022, IN THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF SrX HUNDRED TWENTY-SEVEN
MITLION FOUR HUNDRED NINETY.NINE THOUSAND SIX HUNDRED EIGHTY.
THREE PESOS AND 55/100 (P627,499,683.55), AS ENDORSED BY THE CITY
DEVELOPMENT COUNCIL THROUGH RESOLUTTON NO. 05, SERIES OF 2022.
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POUCY - SECTION 2, paragraph (d) of
Republic Act No. 10121, provides:
"(d) Adopt a disaster risk reduction and management approach that
is holistic, comprehensive, integrated and proactive in lessening the
socio-economic and environment impact of disasters including
climate, and promote the involvement and pafticipation of all sectors
and all stakeholders concerned, at all levels, especially the local
community"
SECTION 3. APPROVAL OF THE INVESTMENT PIAN - The Davao City
Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Fund Investment Plan (DCDRRMFIP) for CY
2022, as prepared and approved by the Davao City Disaster Risk Reduction and
Management Council, shall be approved for the purpose of developing an all hazards
planning approach that will be used for all threats and/or emergencies or disasters and
funding identified projects proposed by various concerned departments to establish
responsiveness of the City Government to lessen the impact of disasters in Davao City.
SECTION 4. BUDGETARY REqUTREMENT - THE TOTAL AMOUNT OF SIX
HUNDRED TWENTY.SEVEN MILLION FOUR HUNDRED NINETY-NINE
THOUSAND SrX HUNDRED EIGHTY-THREE PESOS AND 55/100
(P627 A991683.55) is hereby appropriated to fund the Davao City Disaster Risk
Reduction and Management Fund Investment Plan (DCDRRMFIP) for CY 2022 (hereto
attached as ANNEX "A").
SECTION 5. LEGAL BASIS - Pursuant to SECTION 458, par. (1) (iv) of the Local
Government Code of 1991, which empowers the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod to adopt
measures to protect the inhabitants of the City from harmful effects of man-made or
natural disasters and calamities and to provide relief and assistance for victims during
and in the aftermath of said disasters or calamities and their return to productive
livelihood following said events.
SECTION 6. USE OF FUNDS - The amounts herein appropriated shall be used
speciflcally for such items and expenditures approved by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod.
All disbursements and utilization of funds shall be subject to existing government
budgeting, accounting and auditing rules and regulations of the Depaftment of Budget
and Management (DBM), the Commission of Audit (COA), the Procurement Law
(Republic Act No. 9184), as well as other applicable laws, Ordinances and Presidential
directives.

Ord. No. 0933-22
SECTION 7. EFFECTMTY. This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.
ENACTED, on April 19,2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
