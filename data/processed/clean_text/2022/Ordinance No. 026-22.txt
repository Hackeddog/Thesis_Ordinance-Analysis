following Common Regulations for General Zones, to wit:
SECTION 1 of Article V is entitled REVIEW OF GENERAL ZONES which states that
general zones are subject to review by the Zoning Review Committee every five
(5) years from the enactment of this ordinance. Any amendment thereof as
recommended by the committee shall be in accordance with the provision of Article
XIV SECTION 14.
SECTION 2 of Aftic|e V is entitled RECLASSIFICATION AND CHANGE OF ZONE

general zone to another shall be considered as amendment if the zoning ordinance
and must comply with the provision of Article XIV, SECTION 14 hereof.
SECTION 3. AMENDMENTS TO THE ZONING ORDINANCE. - Article XIV,
SECTION L4 of the Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City provides that changes
in the Zoning Ordinance as a result of the review by the Local Zoning Review Committee
shall be treated as amendments, provided that any amendment to the Zoning Ordinance
or provisions thereof shall be subject to public hearing and sectoral consultation to be
conducted by the Local Zoning Review Committee and review evaluation of the Zoning
Review Committee and shall be carried out through a resolution/ordinance upon 3/4
majority votes of all the members of the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod.
