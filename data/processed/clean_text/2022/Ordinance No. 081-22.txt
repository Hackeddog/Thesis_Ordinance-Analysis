Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled, that:
SECTION l. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORIW TO THE CITY MAYOR TO UTILIZE A

SECTION 2. DECIARATION OF POLICY - It is the policy of the City
Government of Davao to adopt measures and adhere to the national principles and
standards of humanitarian assistance in response to risk reduction; and declares as its
policy to judiciously utilize its resources and put the same to proper use.
SECTION 3. BENEFICIARIES - The Municipality of Amulung, Cagayan which
was placed under a State of Calamity is hereby declared as beneficiary of the financial
assistance and shall receive an amount of Three Hundred Thousand Pesos
(P300,000.00).
SECTION 4. UEjSAI-EASIS - SECTION 324 (d) of Republic Act No. 7160 or the
Local Government Code of 1991, as amended by Republic Act No. 8185, states that five
percent (5olo) of the estimated revenue from regular sources shall be set aside as
annua! lump sum appropriations for relief, rehabilitation, reconstruction, and other
works or services in connection with calamities which may occur during the budget
year. Provided, however, that such fund shall be used only in the area, or a poftion
thereof, of the Iocal government unit or other areas affected by a disaster or calamity,
as determined and declared by the local sanggunian concerned.
SECTION 5 of the Implementing Rules and Regulations of Republic Act No. 8185
likewise states: "LGUs may also allocate/use a portion of the five percent (5olo) Calamity
Fund to other affected areas on condition that the said areas are declared as under the
State of Calamity by the Sanggunian concerned." It fufther provides "a poftion of
calamity fund may also be authorized to be used by the LGU concerned to provide
financial assistance to other LGUs whose area or poftion thereof had been declared
under a state of calamity by its Sanggunian."
SECTION 5.
