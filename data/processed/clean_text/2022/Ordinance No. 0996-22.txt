Ord No. 0996-22
Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
GIVING AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR
AND IN BEHALF OF THE CrTY OF DAVAO, THE DEEDS OF SALE INVOLVING
THE LOTS UNDER THE SLUM IMPROVEMENT AND RESETTLEMENT PROIECT
(SIR PROJECT), IN FAVOR OF THE AWARDEES, NAMELY: SUSAN A. CALAGOPALMA, DANTE M. ZABALLERO, TOMAS REMOTTCADO JR. AND ARTURO R.
LAURENTE'.
SECTION 2. AUTHORIW- The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into and
sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Deeds of Sale involving the lots under the
Slum Improvement and Resettlement Project (SIR Project), in favor of the awardees namely:
Susan A. Calago-Palma, Dante M. Zaballero, Tomas Remoticado Jr. and Arturo R. Laurente.
SECTION 3. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any SECTION or provision
of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof
not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.
ENACTED, on the 28th of )une 2022, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian present.
