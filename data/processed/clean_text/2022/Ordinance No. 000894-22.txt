INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO,

AND BETWEEN THE NATIONAL MUSEUM OF THE PHILIPPINES
AND THE CITY OF DAVAO RELATIVE TO THE IMPLEMENTATION
OF THE PHASE 3 PROJECT FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF THE
NATIONAL MUSEUM OF THE PHILIPPINES IN DAVAO CITY TO
BE KNOWN AS *MUSEO DABAWENYO NATIONAL MUSEUM IN
THE PHILIPPINES"

Ord. No. 0894-22
Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF
OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE SUPPLEMENTAL MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
(MOA) BY AND BETWEEN THE NATIONAL MUSEUM OF THE PHILIPPINES AND THE
CITY OF DAVAO RELATIVE TO THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE PHASE 3 PROJECT
FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF THE NATIONAL MUSEUM OF THE PHILIPPINES IN
DAVAO CITY TO BE KNOWN AS *MUSEO DABAWENYO NATIONAL MUSEUM IN THE
PHILIPPINES'".
