The City Government of Davao shall continuously appropriate funds for the operations
and maintenance of the College, in addition to the miscellaneous fees which may be, from time
to time, determined by the Board of Trustees, provided, however, that any or all funds accruing
to the College shall be put and kept under its name and account.
SECTION 7. SEPARABILITYCLAUSE - If any provision of this Ordinance is held invalid
or unconstitutional, any other provision not so affected shall continue to be valid and effective.
SECTION 8. REPEALING CLAUSE - Any Ordinances and its rules and regulations or
any part thereof, which are inconsistent with any of the provisions of this Ordinance, are hereby
repealed or amended accordingly.
SECTION 9. EEEEICIIIIITY - This Ordinance shall take effect upon its approval
ENACTED, on the 15n day of March, 2022 by a unanimous vote of all the Members of
the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
