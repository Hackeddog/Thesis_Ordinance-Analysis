SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "Anti-Sexual Abuse and
Exploitation Against Children Ordinance of Davao City.
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICIES - Davao City recognizes the vital
role of the youth in nation-building enshrined in the 1987 Philippine Constitution. It shall
promote and protect their physical, moral, spiritual, intellectual and social well-being.
Thus, it shall be the policy of Davao City to defend the rights of the children and
provide special protection from all forms of sexual abuse that are prejudicial to their
development. Speciflcally, the City shall provide comprehensive measures and strategies
in combating the proliferation of sexual abuse against children by engaging and
harmonizing the effofts of governmental and non-governmental agencies.
The City shall continue its tireless effort in giving priority to the rights of the
children especially in terms of protection which led the City in consistently winning awards
as one of the best child-friendly cities in the country, among others.
SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS.
1. Center-based programs - refer to the programs in an establishment
where people can seek to avail strategic programs, seruices and
interventions developed for purposes of protecting children against sexual
abuse.
2. Child (Children) - for purposes of this Ordinance, child (children) refers
to person(s) below eighteen (18) years of age or those over but unable to
fully take care of themselves and protect themselves from sexual abuse
because of physical and/or mental disability condition. (CWC 20L9, with
minor edits)
3. Child (Children) at high-risk against rape - refers to a child who is
exposed to a higher level of danger against sexual abuse compared to other
children due to risk factors and conditions affecting him or her.
4. Child Pornography the unlawful or prohibited acts defined and
punishable by Republic Act No. 9775 or the Anti-Child Pornography Act of
2009, committed through a computer system: Provided, That the penalty to
be imposed shall be one (1) degree higher than that provided for in Republic
Act No. 9775.
5. Community-based programs - refer to the programs provided by
persons, offices or organizations through approaching the community with
strategic programs, seruices and interyentions developed for purposes of
protecting children against sexual abuse.
6. Consciousness building - an approach to a child in awakening and/or
improving his or her state of mind specifically in realizing the evil sought by
sexual abuse and his or her vulnerability to it.
7. Cybersex against a child - the willful engagement, maintenance, control,
or operation directly or indirectly, of any lascivious exhibition of sexual
organs or sexual activity, with the aid of a computer system, for favor or
consideration against a child.

Ord. No. 0854-22
B. Empowerment - an approach to a child in making him or her stronger and
more confident specifically in protecting his or her right against sexual
abuse.
9. Responsible agencies -these are the agencies that are mentioned and
given specific roles in this Ordinance.
10. Sexual abuse on child - refers to an act in which a child is abused for
sexual gratification. It includes direct sexual contact, engagement in
indecent exposure (of the genitals, nipples, etc.), pornography, prostitution
and other forms of sexual gratification involving a child.

SECTION 19. RATIONALE AND CONSIDERATIONS FOR ESTABLISHING A
LOCALIZED CICC. The rationale and considerations for establishing a localized CICC
are as follows:
1. The creation of CICC of Davao City as well as its composition and functions
was inspired from the creation of Cybercrime Investigation and
Coordinating Center (CICC) under Republic Act No. 10175 or the
'tCybercrime Prevention Act of 2012". Hence, the functions and
responsibilities are already existing as well as the tools, machineries and
expeftise of personnel.
2. In this proposed Ordinance, it aims to localize the functions of CICC under
RA 10175 and took consideration of the following criteria:

Ord. No. 0854-22
a. The provisions were tailored to address online sexual abuse on
children;
b. In contrast with the National CICC, the localized version of CICC will
tend to put more focus on addressing the sexual abuse on children
within the city taking into consideration the specific attributable
factors and conditions existing within the city.
c. It will hopefully magnify the efforts in combating sexual abuse
against children since the efforts to be rendered are more isolated
and focused in the city compared to the general overview of national
CICC.
d. The specific local agencies involved are hereby determined.
e. The coordination and linkages among the local CICC to the national
and international on intelligence, investigations, training and
capacity building are hereby established;
f. The proposed localized CICC is hereby tasked to aid the Committee
on Children Protection against sexual abuse specifically in combating
online sexual abuse;

SECTION 20. MEDIA HANDLING OF NEWS AND ISSUES INVOLVING
CHILDREN.- Pursuant to Republic Act No. 9344, the mass media shall play an active
role in the promotion of child rights, and delinquency prevention by relaying consistent
messages through a balanced approach.
Media practitioners shall, therefore, have the duty to maintain the highest
critical and professional standards in repofting and covering cases of children in conflict
with the law.
In all publicity concerning children, the best interest of the child should be the
primordial and paramount concern. Any undue, inappropriate and sensationalized
publicity of any case involving a child in conflict with the law is hereby declared a
violation of the child's rights.
Fufther, pursuant to SECTION 23, Article II of Davao City Children's Welfare
Code, as Amended, children in need of special protection shall not be exposed to undue
media coverage unless consented to by the child and parent/guardian.
The component authorities shall undeftake all measures to protect this
confidentiality of proceedings, including non-disclosure of records to the media,
maintaining a separate police blotter for cases involving Children in Conflict with the
Law which shall not be used in subsequent proceedings for cases involving the same
offender as an adult, except when beneficial for the offender and upon his/her written

OTHER PROVISIONS
SECTION 24. CONFIDENTIALITY RULE. All investigations, apprehensions and
documentations conducted in relation to this Ordinance shall be done in utmost respect
for the privacy and confldentiality of the victims and the perpetrators, especially when a
minor is involved.
SECTION 25. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. If for any reason, any SECTION or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, the other sections or
provisions hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and
effect.
SECTION 26. REPEALING CLAUSE. All Ordinances, local issuances or rules
inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified
accordingly.
SECTION 27. EFFECTMTY. This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval and
after publication in a newspaper of general circulation in the City of Davao.
