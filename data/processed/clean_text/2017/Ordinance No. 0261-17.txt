Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN
BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE DEED OF USUFRUCT
WITH SOUTHERN PHILIPPINES MEDICAL CENTER (SPMC)";
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - SECTION 455, (b) (1) (vi) of the Local
Government Code provides, to wit:
"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of
which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant
to SECTION 16 of its Code, the City Mayor shall represent the city in all
its business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts
and obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the
SANGGUNIANG panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance";
SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority
to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the DEED OF
USUFRUCT WITH SOUTHERN PHILIPPINES MEDICAL CENTER (SPMC). The area subject of
the Agreement shall be used for the ONE STOP SHOP PROGRAM in order to improve public
seruice.
SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason/ any SECTION or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof which are not affected by such declaration, shall continue to be in full
force and effect;
SECTION 5. EFFECUVIT'I - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval;
ENACTED, on August22,2017, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.

This DEED is made and executed by and between:
CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a government political subdivision, created
under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with office address at City Hall

hereinafter referred to as the OWNER.
WITNESSETH:
WHEREAS, the General Welfare Clause under SECTION 16 of R.A. 7160,
otherwise known as the "Local Government Code l99L' provides that,
"Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those
necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or
incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential
to the promotion of the general welfare. Within their respective territorial
jurisdictions, local government units shall ensure and support, among other
things, the preseruation and enrichment of culture, promote health and safety,
)oc(. "
WHEREAS, in pursuance to the above General Welfare Clause, the City
Government of Davao initiates and offers to assist the medication, treatment and
hospitalization of indigent patients-constituents of the City of Davao, particularly
those admitted at the Southern Philippine Medical Center, through the institution
of "One Stop Shop Assistance Program-Pagkalinga Sa Bayan";
WHEREAS, the City Government of Davao aims to provide an efficient,
prompt, and speedy medical and other services to the public by coordinating,
integrating and unifying all government agencies providing similar medical
assistance program, such as the Philippine Charity Sweepstake Office,
Depaftment of Social Welfare and Development and Philippine Health Insurance
Corporation, and other agencies that may participate in this program;
WHEREAS, in order to effectively carry out and implement the said One
Stop Shop Assistance Program, the City shall provide an office space, including
the provision of office furniture and fixtures, for each paftner agencies by
constructing a building within the premises of Southern Philippines Medical
Center located at J.P. Laurel Street, Bajada, Davao City;
