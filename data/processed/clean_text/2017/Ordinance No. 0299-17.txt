MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
BETWEEN THE UNDERSIGNED:
Operation Smile Philippines, a nonprofit making association, governed by the laws of the
Philippines, founded on 1982, herein represented by MR WIGBERT DENNIS
FUNTAIIILLA in his capacity as President of Operation Smile Philippines, and duly
authorized by the Operation Smile Philippines Board of Trustees, whose local office is located at
Brokenshire Integrated Health Ministries, Inc. (formerly Brokenshire Hospital), Davao City,
hereinafter referred to as the "First Party"
and
The City Government of Davao, local government unit organized and existing under and by
virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines with office address in the Davao City Hall,
San Pedro Sfreet, Davao City represented herein by SARA Z. DUTERTE-CARPIO, Mayor of
Davao City, hereinafter referred as the "Second Party"
CONSIDERING THAT
l) The President of the Republic of the Philippines, encourages the private and public
parfrrership to intensify the state's call for a more advanced and sophisticated means
of providing health care services to its people;
2) Studies have shown that one (1) infant will be born with an orofacial cleft per 500
live births;
3) As of 20A7 , the population census of the National Statistical Coordination Board has
projected a growth rate of 2.39o/o per annum for Mindanao. Assuming, this rate will
be constant there will be more than 40,000 new cases of infants bom with orofacial
clefts per year;
4) It is estimated that only less than 900 of these infants receive treatment per year.
There is then a tremendous number of backlog;
5) Although numerous govemmental and non governmental agencies are addressing this
problem on a mission based approach, only Operation Smile Philippines provides
holistic treatment that encompasses medical, sugical, dental and speech therapy
during its missions only;
6) The price of reconstructive surgery would cost about Forty Four Thousand Pesos
(Php44,000) per patient.
7) The price of presulg.ical nasoalveolar moulding would cost about Two Thousand
Pesos per patient. *U

ARTICLE I: AIMS OF THE AGREEMENT
Both parties agree to cooperate to provide a safe and effective reconstructive surgery for patient
in Davao City born with facial deformities such as cleft lip and cleft palate.
ARTICLE 2.I OBLIGATIONS COMMON TO ALL PARTIES
ARTICLE 2.I.1 ADMINISTRATIVE AND PERSONNEL MANAGEMENT
2.1.1.1 The First Party will mobilize medical and non-medical volunteers to conduct free presurgical nasoalveolar moulding and reconstructive surgery to Davao City patients with cleft lip
and cleft palate.
2.1.1.2 The Second Party is responsible for supplying the human and material means in carrying
out its task of finding patients afflicted with cleft deformities including the needs of its staff,
patient and parents/guardians e.g. food, transportation and other needs as deemed necessary to
meet its aforesaid obligations.
2.1.1.3 The First Party shall be responsible for the assessment and evaluation of patients for
possible surgical treatment of those born with cleft lip and cleft palate.
2.1.1 .4 The First Party shall be responsible for the post-surgical teatment of the patients.
2.1.1.5 The Second Party shall ensure the safety and security of all personnel and volunteers
during the Operation Smile Program.
2.1.1.6 Both parties are individually in charge of the recruitrnent, fraining and administrative
management of their personnel.
ARTICLE 2. I.2 FINANCIAL OBLIGATIONS
2.1.2.1 Each party shall bear individually the responsibilities towards its donors or other
concerned parties for the implementation, follow up and evaluation of its part of the project.
ARTICLE 2.1.3 OPERATIONAL OBLIGATIONS
2.1.3.1Both parties agree to name their respective focal persons in this undertaking. The focal
persons are responsible in ensuring that the commitments of their respeglive institutions are
materialized in the program and their respective deliverables are attaine d Yl/,

ARTICLE 3.1 OPERATION SMILE OBLIGATIONS
3.1.1 Operation Smile shall provide for and supervise over the medical and non-medical
volunteers in the conduct of the free pre-surgical nasoalveolar moulding, construction of
obturators and reconstructive surgeries;
3.1.2 Operation Smile shall be responsible in the overall management of the program including
but not limited to hospital arrangement medical supplies, equipment and personnel necessary for
its operation.
ARTICLE 3.2 CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO OBLIGATIONS
3.2.1 The City Government of Davao shall designate the City Health Office as the focal agency
in the implementation of this Program;
3.2.2 The City Government of Davao shall be actively represented by the City Health Officer in
the Program as the Focal Person or any duly authorized representative;
3.2.3 The City Government of Davao shall actively initiate and lead in the information
campaigns and other social mobilization activities related to the Operation Smile Program;
3.2.4The City Government of Davao shall properly refer screened patients for operation;
3.2.5 The City Government of Davao shall only provide Two Thousand Pesos (Php2,000)
counterpart fund from the Lingap Para Sa Mahirap Program per Davao City patient with cleft lip
and cleft palate to help defray the medical costs of Operation Smile Philippines chapter in Davao
City. The said counterpart fund shall be paid through Operation Smile Philippines.
ARTICLE 4.I DURATION OF THE CONTRACT
The present contact takes effect from the date of signature by the parties, and is effective until
after 3 years. jW
