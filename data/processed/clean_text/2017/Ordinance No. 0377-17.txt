For the purposes of the implementation of the Memorandum of Agreement
(MOA) between the SOCIAL SECURIry SYSTEM (SSS) and CITY GOVERNMENT
OF DAVAO, the following guidelines and procedures are hereby issued.
l.
General Requirements
A. Agreement. The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO shall encourage the SSS
Coverage of their Contractual and Job Order (JO) personnel to register as SSS selfemployed (SE) Members.
B. Reporting. The City Government of Davao shall provide the SSS with a list
of their Job Order (JO) personnel showing the full name, SS number (if any), date of
birth and monthly income. Provided, that the Monthly Net Earnings to be declared on
the Self-employed Data Record Form (RS-1)shall not be lowerthan the amount in the
contract of service.
C. Designation of Representative. The SSS Davao llustre Branch shall
designate an SSS representative, preferably an Account Officer, who shall manage the
Gity Government of Davao account, to assist them in their compliance under the
MOA, and perform activities indicated under these guidelines. Likewise The City
Government of Davao shall designate its representative to perform activities indicated
under these guidelines.
ll.
Procedural Requirements
A. The SOGIAL SECURITY SYSTEM (SSS) Davao llustre Branch shall
1. Conduct on-site registration of the JOB Order (JO) personnel as SE member.
2. Process SS Form RS-1 in accordance with the existing guidelines, policies and
procedures
3. Provide City Government of Davao with their approved monthly SS
contributions.
4. Provide free seminars/ presentations to Job Order (JO) personnel on the
