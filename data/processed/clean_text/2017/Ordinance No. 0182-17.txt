A. Specific Duties of the Parties
The First Pafi shall
1. Allocate the amount of Two Million Eight Hundred Forty-one Thousand Four

3, Provide assistance in the preparation and signing of the Qualification Map for
GPB Program;
4. Conduct, in coordination with the Technical Vocational Institution (WI), the
pre- training process such as: Youth Profiling for Starring Careers (YP4SC),
Training Induction Program (TIP);
5. Ensure that all trainees undergo the mandatory assessment after end of the
training program;
6. Pay the training cost directly to the TVET Providers and the assessment fees

Offices, provided that all required supporting documents are complied with
subject to government accounting and auditing rules and regulations;
7. Prepare and submit repofts to all concerned agencies as reasonably required

calendar days after the start of the program while the terminal report shall be
submitted within fifteen (15) calendar days after the end of training period;

hundred percent (100o/o) completion of the training program;
