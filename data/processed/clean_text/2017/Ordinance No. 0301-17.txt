SPECIFIC IMPLEMENTATION AGREEMENTS
soctAL PENSION PROGRAM (SPP)
This is a supplementary agreement to th
and entered into on the
day of
e Unifled Memorandum of Agreement made
by and between the
Department of Social Welfare and Development Field Office XI and the City Government
of Davao. It details the implementation arrangements for the Social Pension Program
for Indigent Senior Citizens C.Y. 20L7 in the locality.
I.
ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITITES OF THE PARTIES
A. The Depaftment of Social Welfare and Development - Field
Officb XI
1. Facilitate engagement with Local Government Unit (LGU) through signing of
Memorandum of Agreement on the transfer of fund and implementation of
Social Pension for indigent senior citizens.
2. Review and approve the quarterly updated list of eligible senior citizens
submitted by the City Social Services and Development Office (CSSDO) and
Office of the Senior Citizen's Affairs (OSCA) and the Local Chief Executive
(LCE). The approved list of qualified senior citizens will be the basis for the
quarterly release of funds to the Local Government Units (LGUs).
3. Monitor the quarterly submission of liquidation repoft verified by the
Commission On Audit (COA) Auditor.
4. Provide technical assistance to the LGUs through the City Sociai Seruices and
Development Office (CSSDO) on the implementation of Social Pension.
5. Conduct regular monitoring and spot check to validate receipt of payment to
the beneficiaries in all districts of Davao City.
6. Conduct orientation, advocacy activities, meeting as necessary with LGUs,
Office of the Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA), Federation of the Senior Citizens
Association of the Philippines (FSCAP), barangay senior citizens association.
7. Demand the refund of all unutilized/disallowed amounts at the end of the
year.
B. Provide operational expenses to the LGU (direct implementers of the
program).
9. Travelling expenses in the conduct of validation, pay-out, and monitoring of
Social Pension to the beneficiaries.
10. The allowable amount for the reimbursement of travelling expenses shall be
based on allocation per municipality in accordance with existing accounting
and auditing rules and regulations per COA Circular N. 94-013.
B. City Social Seruices and Development Office (CSSDO)
1. Facilitate preparation and approval of payroll and list of beneficiaries.
2. Coordinate with OSCA Head, FSAP members, senior citizens association in the
conduct of validation and home visits to identified indigent senior citizens.
3. Submit monthly updates to the list of eligible senior citizens to the DSWD
Field Office.
4. The City Treasurer of the LGU or the authorized representative of the LGU
hall claim the check/s from the DSWD Field Office and issue an official
receipt.
5. Ensure timely delivery of payment to the beneficiaries based on the approved
list of benefi ciaries/payroll.

DTSENGAGEMENT, TERMINATION FOR CAUSE AND SANCTTONS
The Local Government Unit may request termination and this SIA and
disengagement from the Social Pension Program (SPP) upon written notice to
the other party specifying the reasons for the disengagement. The process of
termination and disengagement shall follow Social Pension Program
guidelines developed by the DSWD for the purpose.
Non-compliance or violation of the LGU to any of the provisions of this SIA, as
determined by the DSWD after investigation, shall result in the imposition of
the following sanctions, depending on the gravity of the offense: (a) warning
or imposition of probationary status; (b) termination of SPP implementation,
and (c) give rise to the filing of appropriate administrative and/ or criminal
actions against responsible offices of the Erring Party.
