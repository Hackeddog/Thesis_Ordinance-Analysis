MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
This Memorandum of Agreement (the "Agreement") executed this
tn
by and among
PLDT lNC., a corporation duly organized under the laws of the Republic
of the Philippines, with office address at Ramon Cojuangco Bldg.,
Makati Avenue, lVlakati City, represented herein by its Chief Corporate
Services Officer, ATTY. RAY C. ESPINOSA and Public Affairs Head,
MR. RAMON R. ISBERTO , hereinafter referred to as "PLDT";
The DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION- DAVAO CITY, a government
office mandated to develop functionally-literate and productive Filipinos,
with principal office in Davao City, represented by the Schools Division
Superintendent DR. MARIA INES C. ASUNCION, CESO V hereinafter
referred to as "DEPED";
-andThe DAVAO CITY LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNIT, with principal office at
Davao City, represented by its Mayor, HON. SARA Z. DUTERTECARPIO hereinafter referred to as "LGU";
Each, a "Party"; and collectively, as "Parties";
WITNESSETH THAT:
WHEREAS, PLDT believes that Filipinos shall be at par with the rest of the world amidst
this changing global lifestyle brought about by rapid modernization of the information and
communication technologies and advocates that through education, such vision of Filipinos
becoming globally competitive can be achieved;
WHEREAS, PLDT has been implementing the PLDT lnfoteach Outreach Program as
one mechanism to achieve this vision;
WHEREAS, DEPED implements programs geared towards pedagogically-based
integration of modern lnformation Communication Technology to teaching and learning in order
to improve education in their area of responsibility;
WHEREAS, the LGU likewise implements programs to make its constituents globally
competitive;
WHEREAS, pursuant to a Memorandum of Agreement signed on 29 Nov. 2016 by
PLDT, UP Open University, and the UPOU Foundation (the "MOA") for the implementation of
the lnfoteach Outreach Program, Davao City has been identified as one of the ten (10) areas to
be covered by the program;
NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing and the covenants and
stipulations herein, the Parties have agreed to collaborate for the lmplementation of PLDT's
lnfoteach Outreach Program in DAVAO CITY, (the "PLDT lnfoteach- DAVAO CITY') under
the following terms and conditions:

PLDT lnfoteach- Davao City Project Description
1. The Project shall have two components:
a. Training Component
b. PLDT HomeDSL Broadband Quiz Contest
2. The Training Component shall include two (2) teachers in the Training of Teachers and
the training of approximately 300 teachers and students from fifteen (15) schools
selected in consultation with DEPED.
3. The training will use training modules developed by UPOU for the 2016 lnfoteach
Outreach Program and approved by PLDT. The training will be a combination of Face to
Face and online instruction.
4. The second component of the Project is the PLDT HomeDSL Broadband Quiz, details of
which shall be disseminated by PLDT to DEPED and the LGU subsequent to this
Agreement.
ll. Responsibilities of DEPED
'1. Recommend additional schools who will participate in the program
2. Recommend appropriate training venue
3. Recommend participants to the Training of Teachers
4. Provide other logistics and coordinate with the Project Management Team to facilitate
implementation of the program in the area
Itl. Responsibilities of the LGU
1. Provide supervision needed to facilitate implementation of the project in the area.
2. Coordinate with DEPED and the Project Management Team to ensure successful
implementation of the project in the area.
lV. Responsibilities of PLDT and Other Partners
1. PLDT and other partners shall implement the lnfoteach Outreach Program as stipulated
in the MOA and in this Agreement.
2. All modules, learning tools, teaching aids, research/project outputs, discoveries and/or
inventions/innovations (collectively, "Works") produced by virtue of and pursuant to this
MOA shall be owned by PLDT. As such, the Works may not be used without the prior
written consent of PLDT.
Representations and Warranties
The Parties represent and warrant to each other that it has legal capacity and its
representatives have been duly authorized to execute and deliver this Agreement and that
the performance of its obligations hereunder have likewise been duly authorized and will
not violate any contract, rights of third parties, or local or national laws.
Vl. Relationship of the Parties
The Parties hereby acknowledge that nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as
creating between or among them an employer-employee relationship, partnership, or
agency.
V
Vlt. Term and Termination

This Agreement shall be effective from January 6,2017 and shall remain in force until
June 8, 2017, unless sooner terminated by any of the Parties for cause. ln case of
termination, any ongoing activity under the Project shall first be completed before the
effective date of termination.
PLDT reserves the right to terminate this Agreement with written notice to the other Parties
thirty (30) days prior to the intended date of termination.
Vlll. Limitation of Liability
ln no event shall PLDT be liable for any indirect, incidental, special or consequential
damages, even if PLDT has been advised of the possibility of such claims.
DEPED and LGU shall hold PLDT free and harmless from all claims of, and liabilities to,
the its employees, personnel, agents, representatives, participants or third parties arising
out of or in connection with this Agreement, and to the extent permitted by law, shall
indemnify PLDT against, any and all claims, losses or damages to PLDT and/or third
parties arising from or by reason of, or othenrvise in connection with, any and all acts or
omissions of DEPED and the LGU, its employees, technical personnel, agents,
representatives or any person acting under its control or direction in connection with or on
the occasion of the performance of any activity under this Agreement, as well as any and
all liability or damage that may be incurred as a result of any breach of DEPED's and
LGU's undertakings, representations, warranties and covenants herein.
lx. MiscellaneousProvisions
1. Cooperation among all Parties herein shall be based on principles of mutual benefit and
respect for each other's independence.
2. All Parties may pursue other forms of cooperation, within fields that are mutually
acceptable and subject to the requirements of each research activity and other
undertakings.
3. The Parties acknowledge that this Agreement does not, and cannot address all the
circumstances and issues that may arise during its Term. The process undertaken by
the Parties in accordance with this Agreement will be dynamic in nature, and may
change over time. This Agreement may, therefore, be amended at any time by the
mutual written agreement of the Parties.
4. ln all instances, the Parties shall strive and exert earnest efforts to settle their differences
in the most amicable manner possible, with the end-view of pursuing the objectives of
the Agreement. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be resolved by the
Parties through negotiations in good faith. Failing which, the aggrieved Party may file
appropriate action in the proper court of Makati City, to the exclusion of all other venues.
5. lf any provision of this Agreement is legally adjudged invalid or unenforceable, the Parties
agree that such invalidity or unenforceability shall not have the effect of nullifying other
provisions hereof, which shall continue in fullforce and effect.
