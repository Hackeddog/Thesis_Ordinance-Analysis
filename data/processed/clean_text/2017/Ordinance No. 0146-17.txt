RATIONALE
The CCL Project in DSWD areas shall be implemented for the indigenous communities using the
Community-Driven Development (herein after referred to as CDD) strategy of the KALAHI-CIDSS
Project;
The above-mentioned barangay of the city/municipality of Davao city in the province of Davao Del Sur
were identified by DepEd to be covered by the CCL Project;
The CCL Project also promotes DepEd's Indigenous Peoples Education (IPEd) program;
The DSWD, the CitylMunicipality and the Barangay, represented by their respective ofFrcers and local
chief executives, have expressed their interest, willingness, and commitment to participate in the
implementation of the CCL Project, and apply the PRINCIPLES of participatory, transparent and
accountable governance espoused by the KALAHI-CIDSS Project, to improve the living conditions of its
poor constituents, andhave decided to formally bind themselves for the implementation of the CCL
Project for a period of one (l) year, to commence upon the signing of this SIA.

1.1. KALAHI CIDSS-CCL, also known as the Program refers to "Kapit Bisig Laban sa Kahirapan
Comprehensive and Integrated Delivery of Social Services - Construction of Classroom for Lumad", is
the government's support for the improve access of indigenous communities in Mindanao by providing
learning facilities, using the community driven development (CDD) strategy. The Department of
Social Welfare and Development is one of the agencies implementing the program.
1.2. Sub-Project refers to the community project which is the establishment of classroom or school
building in the IP community.
1.3. NPMO means National Program Management Office of the Program based at the Central
Office of the DSWD, which assumes full responsibility for the overall management of the Program.
1.4. RPMO refers to the Regional Program Management Office based in the Regional Office of the
DSWD, which takes responsibility for Program implementation in the Region.
1.6. ACT refers to the Area Coordinating Team based in the cities/municipalities covered by KCd
CCL, which is responsible for the day-to-day operations of the Program.
1.9. Sub-Project Management Committee refers to the Committee formed under the Barangay
Development Council to provide overall management of the sub-project.
1 .10. Grant refers to the funds from the DepEd-GOP, through the DSWD, released to the Barangay for
implementation of the approved sub-project.
1.12. Specific Implementation Arrangement refers to this agreement.

ARTICLE V - OTHER TERMS AND CONDITIONS
1. The Barangay agrees to commence the sub-project within ten (10) days upon approval of this Specific
Implementation Arrangement for KALAHI-CIDSS CCL and receipt of funds and to complete the
sub-project as indicated in the Sub-Project Work Schedule, herein attached as integral part of the subproject documents;
2.In the event that the schedule stipulated in this Agreement is delayed, the Barangay shall submit a
written explanation and a catch-up plan included in the Physical Progress Report to DSWD-RPMO,
ACT and the City. The DSWD and the City shall assess the validity of the grounds for the delay.
Catch-up Plan shall be closely monitored by the DSWD and the City;
3.In the event that the Barangay could not undertake certain activity/ies due to lack of materials and/or
pertinent equipment support, and other reasons, the DSWD-RPMO and ACT shall provide technical
assistance through its designated Regional Procurement Officer;
4. Final project cost and corresponding adjustment shall be effected after cost reconciliation between the
amounts indicated in the Sub-Project Concept Form and the Sub-Project Completion Report;
5. Any complaint lodged against the Barangay for violation or non-compliance with the principles of the
Program or provisions of this Agreement shall be brought to the attention of the DSWD, which shall
review and investigate the complaint and the response or explanation of the Barangay. The DSWDRPMO, who acting through the Regional Program Director, based on substantial evidence, shall issue
an order imposing the sanction or dismissing the complaint.
7. The right of the Barangay to the use of the proceeds of the Grant shall be suspended or terminated by
the DSWD upon failure to perform its obligations under this Sub-Project Agreement;
8. In the event that the subproject is not completed on or before the closing date of the KC- CCL, the
City and BLGU shall complete the subproject and ensure its functionality using their own funds; and
9. Any dispute arising from this Agreement and not settled between the Parties shall be elevated to the
NPMO, for resolution.
