KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:
This AGREEMENT, is made and entered into by and between:
CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a government entity,
duly organized and existing under the laws of the Republic of the
Philippines, with principal office address located at city Hail Drive,
Davao City, Philippines, represented in this act by its City Mayor,
SARA A. DUTERTE-CARPIO, of legal age, Filipino, married, and a
resident of Davao city, Philippines, hereinafter referred to as the
FIRST PARTY;
-andREGIONAL TRIAL COURT OF DAVAO CITY, A
government entity, duly organized and existing under the laws of
the Republic of the Philippines, with principal office address located
at the Hall of Justice, Candelaria St., Ecoland, Davao City,
Philippines, represented in this act by its Executive Judge,
EMMANUEL C. CARPIO, of legal age, Filipino, and a resident of
Davao City, Philippines, hereinafter referred to as the SECOND
PARTY;
WITNESSETH:
That the FIRST PARTY is the owner of that parcel of land, located at
Candelaria St., fronting Davao City Overland Transport Terminal (DCOTT),
Ecoland, Davao City, including the 3-storey commercial building constructed
thereon;
That the SECOND PARTY intends to construct a building for their three
(3) newly created branches within the premises of the Hall of Justice, Ecoland,
That in the meantime that the construction of their building has not yet
been finished, the SECOND PARTY requested to use/occupy the 2nd Floor of the
building of the FIRST PARTY, where they will hold temporarily as court
chambers and for the staffs of their three (3) newly created branches, of which
the latter agreed to the same;
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises,
the parties hereto have agreed among themselves, to enter into an agreement,
subject to the following terms and conditions, to wit:
1. That the FIRST PARTY allows the SECOND PARTY to use/occupy the
2nd Floor of the building for the three (3) newly createcJ branches of the Regional
Trial Court (RTC), for FREE;
2. That the use of the property shall be on an "as is, where is" basis;
ONLY
3. That the duration of this contract shall be for a period of oNE (1) YEAR
,
4. That all expenses for the renovation of the 2nd Floor of the building of
FIRST PARTY shall be shouldered by the SECOND PARTY;
