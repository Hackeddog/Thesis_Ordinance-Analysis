MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS
This Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) executed on the _ day of November
2017, in Davao City, Philippines by and between:
HOLY CROSS OF DAVAO COLLEGE. lNC.. a non-stock, non-profit corporation
created under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, duly registered at the
Securities and Exchange Commission with Registration Number 14168 dated 4
February 1957 and with the principal address at Sta. Ana Avenue, Davao City,
8000, represented herein by its President, Rev. Msgr. Julius C. Rodulfa, herein
referred to as the FIRST PARTY;
-andThe CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a Local Government Unit, represented by
its City Mayor, SARA Z. DUTERTE, of legal age, Filipino and a resident of Davao
City, hereinafter referred to as the SECOND PARW;
WITNESSETH
WHEREAS, the First Party is engaged in offering educational courses and
programs in which the evangelizing mission of the Roman Catholic Church is
integrated in its entire curriculum, serving the educational needs of the Davao
region and forming Christian leaders to become key agents in the formation of
Christian families and communities.
WHEREAS, the First Party establishes partnerships with communities and
organizations for the provision of non-formal education and community building
through its Community Extension Services (CES) and Bachelor of Science in
Social Work (BSSW Program of the college of Humanities, Social Science and
Communication.
WHEREAS, the Second Party, is a localgovernment unit of DAVAO CITY through
the City Anti-Drugs Abuse Council (CADAC) - Community Based Rehabilitation
(CBRCP) & Aftercare Program for PSUDs (Person with Substance Use Disorder),
conducts a continuing Drug Dependents Rehabilitation Treatment pursuant to
Republic Act No. 9165, otherwise known as "Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs
Act of 2002" and DILG Memo 2015-63.

WHEREAS, the FIRST PARTY and SECOND PARTY agree to enter into a partnership
project known as "Community-based Rehabilitation, Education, and Extension Services
for Drug Reformist" (CREED) to mutually share related facilities and resources.
NOW IT IS HEREBY AGREED AS FOLLOWS:
1. Scope of Agrcement
a. For the purpose of this MOA, both parties shall agree to provide services thru
the Community-based Rehabilitation, education, and Extension Services for Drug
Reformist (CREED).
b. This shall take effect immediately upon the signing of this MOA subject to
assessment and yearly renewal vis-o-vis the projects objectives and Parties commitment
and responsibilities.
2. Project ImPlementation
pursuant to the intent of this MoA and to ensure the effective delivery of the PRolEcT,
deliberate plans according to the responsibilities of both parties are herein presented to
wit:
a. The FIRST PARW, through the office of the vice President for
Academic Affairs (VPAA) shall:
i. Designate the Dean of the College of Humanities, Social Sciences and
Communication to:
1. Assign the BSSW Program Chair Person to do the following:
(
{
a. create a concept Paper (Project Design) for the activity;
b. Based on the Concept Paper, identify and schedule the students
involved in the activitY
c, Facilitate the activiues according to the Project Design
d. Assist the Community Extension Services (CES) in the monitoring
and documentation of the project including recording of the
attendance of student participants and faculty involved;
e. Attend coordination meetings, assessments and planning
f. Perform such other function/s inherent to its responsibilities
a
ii. Designate the community Extension seruices (cEs) office to:
1, Monitor and coordinate with the academic program and the SECOND
PARTY in the whole rycle of the project management;
\

2. Respectfully notify the SECOND PARTY about the presence of HCDC
faculty, staff and students in the community;
3. Provide for the appropriate budget of the faculty and students involved
(i.e. fare, supplies etc)
4. Assist the SECOND PARTY in managing and documenting the project,
including the keeping of the records files references;
5. Facilitate the coordination meeting/s, assessment and planning;
6. Perform such other function/s inherent to its responsibilities in the
project.
iii. The FIRST PARTY waives and abandons any lawful claim it may have against the
SECOND PARTY which may arise from any untoward incident while in the performance
or execution of the provisions of this MOA.
b. The SECOND PARTY shall:
i. Provide technical assistance and capability building services to the first party
ii. Assist the first party in the implementation of the project with partner
communities
iii. Assist the first party in coordinating with other institutions which provide
