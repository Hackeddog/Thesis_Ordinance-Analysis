MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (MOU) TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND

THE ESTABLISHMENT OF AN ELECTRONIC ONLINE PAYMENT SYSTEM AS AN
ALTERNATIVE MODE OF PAYMENT OF TAXES, FEES, CHARGES AND OTHER
IMPOSITIONS DUE TO THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO";
