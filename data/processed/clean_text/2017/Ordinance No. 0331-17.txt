This Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) executed on the _ day of November
2017,_in Davao City, Philippines by and between:

WITNESSETH
WHEREAS, the First Party through the CADAC-CBRAP conducts a Community
Based Rehabilitation Program, Aftercare and Continuing Preventive Health Care
for PSUDs pursuant to RA 9165, othenryise known as the 'Comprehensive
Dangerous Drugs Act of 2002" and DILG Memo Circular 2016-116;

interventions through their community extension program;
WHEREAS, the Second Party hereby possesses human resource with the

facilities and human resource counterpart;

,2017 subject to

Carmen, Biao Escuela, Biao Guianga and Los Amigos;

charges as coordinative suppoft to the CBRAP.

a. Profiling, screening and Psychological evaluation
b. Case management, Family Education and Relapse Prevention
c. Family Life enrichment and Values formation
d. Productivity and Livelihood Training
e. Sports and Therapeutic Recreation
5. Observe CBRAP and BADAC's policies and procedures especially in relation to the
confidentiality of cases as well as observe proper decorum;
6. Attend training, orientation, briefing session that will be provided by the CADAC
and other capability building activities needed;
7. Attend regular meetings, consultations and dialogues organized by the BADAC,
CADAC and other recognized paftners;
