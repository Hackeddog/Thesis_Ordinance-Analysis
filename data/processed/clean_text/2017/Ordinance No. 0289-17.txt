PUBLIC MARKET";
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POUCY. The City recognizes that LGUs are
primarily responsible for the implementation and enforcement of the provisions of
Republic Act No. 9003, otherwise known as the Ecological Solid Waste Management Act
of 2000. Through the Waste Analysis and Characterization Study (WACS) conducted in
the city, biodegradable wastes constitute the bulk of waste generation and these come
from public markets. In order for LGUs to fulfill one of its responsibilities to diveft solid
wastes from being dumped to the Sanitary Landfill Facility, the DENR through the
Environmental Management Bureau (EMB), launched the establishment and
institutionalization of Solid waste Management System in public Markets.
SECTION 3. AUTHORITY OF THE CITY MAYOR. - Pursuant to SECTION 455,
paragraph (b),subparagraph (1) (vi) of Republic Act No 7160, othenryise known as the
Local Government Code of 1991, the City Mayor is hereby authorized to sign, for and in
behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) with
the Depaftment of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR), through the
Environmental Management Bureau (EMB), Region XI, for the Financial Assistance to be
given by the latter to the City Government of Davao for the establishment and
institutionalization of Solid Waste Management System in the City's Public Markets,
namely: Agdao Public Market, Bankerohan Public Market and Calinan Public Market.
SECTION 4. SCOPE. The MOA to be entered into by and between the City
Government of Davao and the Department of Environment and Natural Resources
through the Environmental Management Bureau (EMB), Region XI defines the scope of
the obligations and responsibilities that will bind the City Government of Davao.
SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. - Whenever any part of this Ordinance
shall be declared unconstitutional by any competent court, the remaining pafts
unaffected thereby shall continue to remain valid and in effect.

WHEREAS, SECTION 10 of Republic Act No. 9003, known as the Ecological Solid Waste
Management Act of 2000 states that pursuant to the relevant provisions of the Local Govemment
Code of 1991, (RA 7160) Local Govemment Units (LGUs) are primarily responsible for the
implementation and enforcement of the provisions of RA 9003;
WHEREAS, the Ecological Solid Waste Management Act of 2000 (RA 9003), provides
for an Ecological Solid Wastes Management System, creating the necessary institutional
mechanisms and incentives, and appropriating funds thereof to guide Local Government Units
(LGUs) in the achievement of the objectives of the Act;
WHEREAS, SECTION 20 of RA 9003 requires LGUs to divert solid waste from waste
disposal facilities through re-use, recycling and composting and other resource recovery
activities;
WIIEREAS, through the Waste Analysis and Characterization Study (WACS) conducted
in the cities/municipalities of the Region, biodegradable wastes constitute the bulk of wastes
generation and these come from public markets,
WHEREAS, SECTION 8 of the same Act, mentions the role of DENR to provide technical
and other capability building assistance and support to the LGUs in the development and
implementation of local solid waste management plans and programs;
WHEREAS, in order for LGUs to fulfill one of their responsibilities to divert solid
wastes from going into disposal facilities, the DENR through the Environmental Management
Bureau (EN,IB) launches the establishment and institutionalization of Solid Wastes Management
System in Public Markets;
WHEREAS, The EMB issued guidelines on the ESWM System for Public Markets (City
or Municipality) dated 14 February 2017;
NOW TIIEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises and the mutual
covenants herein contained, the Parties do hereby agree and bind themselves to cooperate with
each other to meet the objectives of RA 9003 through the Progam, Ecological Solid Wastes
Management (ESWM) System in Public Markets.
