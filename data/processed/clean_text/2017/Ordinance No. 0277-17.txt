This AGREEMENT made and entered into in Davao City, Philippines, this
day of
2OL6 by and between:
The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, with principal address at Davao City
Hall Drive, Davao City, represented by the Honorable Mayor of Davao City, SARA
Z. DUTERTE, hereinafter referred to as the FIRST PARTY,
-andPOLYTECHNIC COLLEGE OF DAVAO DEL SU& INC., a non-stock and
non-profit higher education institution with address at MacArthur Highway,
Barangay Kiagot, Digos City, represented by its President and CEO, DR. JOSE J.
ADIAWAN, hereinafter referred to as the SECOilD PAR.TY;
WITNESSETH: That
1. The FIRST PARTY supervises the operations of CO SU GIAN CENTER FOR
THE ELDERLY, situated in Cabantian, Buhangin District, Davao City, whose
facilities meet the standards for training agency, registered and accredited by
the Department of Social Welfare and Development.
2. The FIRST PARTY agrees and allows the affiliation and training of Nursing,
Midwifery, Health Care Services and Caregiving students of the SECOND
PARTY, subject to the Policies, Procedures and Guidelines governing
Affiliation and Training of Students of the FIRST PARTY.
3. The SECOND PARTY shall strictly comply with such policies, procedures and
guidelines of the FIRST PARW. Any violation thereof shall be a valid ground
for cancellation of the contract.
4. This contract shall be valid and effective for two (2) years, and may be
renewed for a similar period upon agreement of the parties.
5. The FIRST PARTY shall not assume responsibility for health care costs in
cases of injuries or i!lnesses sustained while a student and/or clinical
instructor is in the clinical area or as a result of the clinical experience.
6. The SECOND PARTY shall secure personal health coverage and/or health
insurance of students and clinical/s on rotation in the area.
7. The SECOND PARTY shall assign not more than 15 students per shift of
rotation with one Clinical Instructor.
8. The SECOND PARTY shall provide performance evaluation tool per student
and evaluation scales for Clinical Instructor for rating by the CO SU GIAN
STAFF on duty at the end of every rotation.
