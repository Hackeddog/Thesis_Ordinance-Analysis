ARTICLE I - DEFINITION OF TER]VIS
l.l.
KALAHI CIDSS-CCL, also known as the Program refers to "Kapit Bisig Laban sa
Kahirapan Comprehensive and Integrated Delivery of Social Services - Construction of Classroom
for Lumad", is the government's support for the improve access of indigenous communities in
Mindanao by providing learning facilities, using the community driven development (CDD) strategy.
The Department of Social Welfare and Development is one of the agencies implementing the
program.
1.2. Sub-Project refers to the community project which is the establishment of classroom or
school building in the IP community.
1.3. NPMO means National Program Management Office of the Program based at the Central
Office of the DSWD, which assumes full responsibility for the overall management of the Program.
1.4. RPMO refers to the Regional Program Management Office based in the Regional Office of
the DSWD, which takes responsibitity for Program implementation in the Region.
1.6.
ACT refers to the Area Coordinating Team based in the cities/municipalities covered by KCwhich is responsible for the day{o-day operations of the Program.
l-9. Sub-Project Management Committee refers to the Committee formed under the Barangay
Development Council to provide overall management of the sub-project.
1.10. Grant refers to the funds from the DepEd-GOP, through the DSWD, released to the Barangay for
implementation of the approved sub-project.
l.l l.
Local Counterpart Contribution refers to the contribution of the local government units and the
communities for the implementation of the approved sub-project. It can be in the form of cash or in kind.
1.12. Specific Implementation Arrangement refers to this agreement.
