WHEREAS, the CDA Davao Extension Office is mandated under Republic Act 6939 to register,
regulate and develop cooperatives under its area of jurisdiction and is fufther designated under
Executive Order 95 SERIES of 1993 as the lead agency on cooperative promotion and developmenU
WHEREAS, the LGU of Davao City under Article L7 of Republic Act 7160 is conferred upon the
responsibilities of providing technical and other form of assistance in the organization and
development of cooperatives, specifically to enhance their viability as economic enterprise and social
organization;
WHEREAS, the DCCDC was organized and created to assist the CDA Davao City Extension
Office and LGU of Davao City in the formulation of policies for cooperative development and expected
r monitor and harmonize the implementation of cooperative programs and projects within its area of
;risdiction;
WHEREAS, records show that of the total registered cooperatives in the City of Davao, only
505 operating cooperatives were able to comply re-registration required by RA 9520, othenruise
known as the New Philippine Cooperative Code of 2008, because of lack of education and training
among others on the paft of other cooperatives that failed to comply;
WHEREAS, the officers and members of most cooperatives including those newly-organized
and registered are yet to be fully oriented and mainstreamed on the true concepts and system of
cooperatives wherein many failed to understand their rights, duties and responsibilities and the
modus operandi of their respective organizations.
NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the pafties hereto
utually declared and agree on the following undertakings:

1.1. Conduct the Pre-Registration Seminar (PRS) among the prospective members of the
proposed cooperatives within the territorialjurisdiction of Davao City;

Seminar (CMES) for the existing cooperatives territorial jurisdiction of Davao City;
1.3. Assist the City Government of Davao, when necessary, in the conduct of seminars,
trainings and workshops for the training of local government officials on cooperative
promotion, organization management and development as well as researches and
development including extension support and related seruices;
t.4
Provide information to the City Government of Davao through the City Cooperative
Development Office (CCDO) on the policies and standards and other issuances that have
been formulated by the CDA concerning cooperatives;
1.5. Provide the City Government of Davao through the City Cooperative Development Office
(CCDO) statistical and other related information regarding the status of cooperatives (State
of the Cooperative Movement) in Davao City; and
2.L. Assist the CDA's Cooperative Development Specialist in the evaluation of any proposed
cooperative's economic survey and requirements for registration and recommend its
registration or non-registration;
2.2. Whenever necessary, assist the CDA in the conduct of validation of applicants for
registration as cooperative as well as in the validation of information regarding the
amendments to the Afticles of Cooperation and/or By-Laws of any existing cooperative
within its territorial jurisdiction;
2.3. Undeftake the promotion and organization of cooperatives within Davao City with the
assistance of other established cooperatives;
2.4. Conduct continuing Membership Education Seminars (CMES) for members of existing
cooperatives within Davao CiW in accordance with the standards set by CDA for this
purpose;
2.5. Require all the officers of registered cooperatives, especially those from micro and small
cooperatives, in Davao City to attend the mandatory trainings, pafticularly the
Fundamentals of Cooperatives and the Cooperative Management and Governance;
2.6. Initiate and implement programs for cooperative promotion and development in line with
the national development plan for cooperatives within Davao City and
2.7. Provide such information as required by CDA as part of the research and development as
well as related projects undertaken by CDA.
