Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in
session assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
PUNISHING THE ACT OF RECRUITING FOR AND MEMBERSHIP TO
TERRORIST GROUPS, INTERNATIONAL OR LOCAL, SUCH AS BUT NOT
LIMITED TO ISIS, AL-QAED& AND MAUTE GROUP, THE MANUFACTURE,
DISTRIBUTION AND POSSESSION OF PROPAGANDA MATERIALS,
PROVIDING PENALTTES THEREFOR AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES";
SECTION 2. SHORT TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "ANTITERRORIST GROUP ORDINANCE";
SECTION 3. DECLARATION OF POUCY - It is the policy of the State, under
the Local Government Code (Republic Act No. 7L60), that every local government unit
shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as
well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective
governance, and those which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare.
Fufthermore, local government units have the power and responsibility to ensure and
suppoft, among other things, the maintenance of peace and order within their
respective territorial jurisdictions.
Likewise, consistent with the Human Security Act (Republic Act No. 9372), it is a
declared policy of the State to protect life, libefi, and property from acts of terrorism,
to condemn terrorism as inimical and dangerous to the national security of the country
and to the welfare of the people, and to make terrorism a crime against the Filipino
people, against humanity, and against the law of nations.
sEcrIoN 4. PROHIBTTED Acrs - It shall be unlawful for any person to:
a) Recruit another person to membership to terrorist groups, international or
local, such as but not limited to ISIS, AL-QAEDA, and MAUTE GROUp;
b) Be a member of terrorist groups, international or local and actively
pafticipate in any unlawful activity;
c) Manufacture, possession, and distribution of any reading materials,
propaganda, paraphernalia, such as flag, clothing, stickers which are
related to terrorist groups, international or local;
sEcrIoN 5. DEFINTTIoN oF TERMS: For purposes of this ordinance:
a) ISIS refers to the Islamic State in Iraq and Syria, a terrorist organization
known for its human rights abuses, hate crimes, cruelty, massacre, rape,
and forced religious conversions.
b) AI-Qaeda means "The Base", "The Foundation" or "The Fundament,,. It
refers to the militant Sunni Islamist multi-national organization founded
in 19BB by Osama bin Laden, Abdullah Azzam and several other Arab
volunteers who fought against the Soviet invasion of Afghanistan in the
1980s. Al-Qaeda operates as network made up of Islamic extremists,
Salafist jihadists. It has been designated as a terrorist group by the
United Nations Security Council, the North Atlantic Treaty Organization

(NATO), the European Union, the United States, Russia, India,
various other countries. Al-Qaeda has mounted attacks on civilian
military targets in various countries.
d) TERRORIST GROUP - refers to any organization, association or group
of persons or any entity owned or controlled by any terrorist or group
that: (1) commits, or attempts to commit, terrorist acts by any means,
directly or indirectly, unlawfully or willfully; (2) pafticipates as an
accomplice in terrorist acts; (3) organizes or directs others to commit
terrorist acts; (4) contributes to the commission of terrorist acts by a
group of persons acting with common purpose of fufthering the terrorist
acts where the contribution is made intentionally and with the aim of
fufthering the terrorist acts or with the knowledge of the intention of the
group to commit terrorist acts.
SECTION 6. PENALTIES - Those convicted under this Ordinance shall be
punished with:
a) A flne of Five Thousand Pesos (P5,000.00) or
b) IMPRISONMENT of one (1) year; or both at the discretion of the court
If the violation is punishable under a national law, the person accused shall be
prosecuted under such law. If the violation is not punishable under any national law,
the penalties under this Ordinance shall be imposed.
SECTION 7. INFORMATION AND EDUCATION CAMPAIGN _ There sha|I be
a continuous and consistent intensified education and information campaign regarding
this Ordinance throughout the jurisdiction of the City Government of Davao utilizing the
tri-media including the social media, the barangays, the Department of Education
through the Division of City Schools, and other forms of communication;
SECTION 8. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason or reasons, any part
of the provision of this Ordinance shall be held unconstitutional or invalid, other parts or
provisions hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and
effect;
SECTION 9. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication in two (2) local newspapers of citywide circulation;
ENACTED, on July tB, 2017, by a majority vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
