Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled, that:

to wit:
"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of which
is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to SECTION
16 of this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all its business
transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts and obligations,
and such other documents upon authority of the SANGGUNIANG
panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance"

authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the

provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and
effect;
SECTION 5. EFFECIMTI - This ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval;

CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND BORROWERS'
MTD PROGRAM GUIDELINES
1. Purpose
The purpose of the program is to provide entrepreneurial opportunities to women
organization by strengthening their capacities and skills, making them more
productive and empowered citizens of the society.
2. Qualified Applicants
The program is available to the following:
.
Organized and registered RlC/women group;
.
Memberships are residents of Davao City;
.
Projects are Agri /Fishery related;
o As much as possible has already existing economic activity related to the
project to be funded by MTD;
The abovementioned applicants should be willing to undergo livelihood orientation
and sessions on Gender, Financial Literacy and Organization Building and
Strengthening.
3. Additional Guidelines
.
Can comply the enabling documents as required by City Government
o No outstanding balance from City Government
.
Ceftification from Barangay where the NGO is operating attesting to the
credibility of the organizations;
o A borrower cannot borrow from more than one office under the
Magnegosyo Ta Day loan program of the city;
4. Mode of Payment
The schedule of payment shall commence 12 months after full release of
the loan and every 3 months thereafter until full payment thru a post-dated
check.
5. Monitoring and Evaluation
Regular monitoring shall be conducted by the stakeholders through meeting with the
beneficiaries. Home visits shall likewise be done to ensure proper evaluation of the
program.
