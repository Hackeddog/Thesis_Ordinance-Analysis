MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:
This Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) executed on the _ day of November
2017, in Davao City, Philippines by and between:
The GITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a Local Government Unit, represented by
its City Mayor, SARA Z. DUTERTE, of legal age, Filipino and a resident of Davao
City, hereinafter referred to as the FIRST PARTY;
-andThe UNIVERSITY OF THE PHILIPPINES-MINDANAO, a higher institution with
principal office at Mintal, Tugbok District, Davao City, represented herein by its
Chancellor, DR. SYLVIA B. CONCEPCION and herein referred to as the SECOND
PARTY.
WITNESSETH
WHEREAS, the First Party through the CADAC-CBRAP conducts a Community
Based Rehabilitation Program, Aftercare and Continuing Preventive Health Care
for PSUDs pursuant to RA 9165, otherwise known as the "Comprehensive
Dangerous Drugs Act of 2A02" and DILG Memo Circular 2016-116;
WHEREAS, the First Party is in need of the services of the Second Party through
the voluntary service within its expertise, including but not limited to psychological
and social services among others;
WHEREAS, the Second Party hereby possesses human resource with the
education, experience and skills required to perform the job as described herein;
WHEREAS, the Second Party shall have the full support of the CADAC as part of
the CBRAP.
NOW THEREFORE, in consideration thereof, Parties hereby agree to the
following terms and conditions:
1. Second Party will accept referrals from the First Party to provide
extension services related to basic literacy, computer literacy, physical
education, survival skills, food making/processing and other services
within its competence and expertise at a time and date mutually agreed
upon by both parties;
2.The Second Party shall render voluntarily its extension services to the
First Party, free of charges as coordinative support to the CBRAP.
3. The First Party shall refer to the Second Party, the program partner;

,l
4. The First Party shall assign reformists list through their Barangay Anti Drug
Abuse Councils taking into consideration the Second Party's experts,
available time and capability;
5. The First Party and the Second Party shall closely coordinate with each other
for the collaboration of Bio-Psycho-Socio-Spiritual and Physical activities of
the CADAC-CBRAP;
6. This Memorandum of Agreement serves as support to the First Party's
CADAC-CBRAP program;
7. This Memorandum of Agreement is an agreement for services and does not
involve commitment as to fund;
8. This Memorandum of Agreement contains all the agreement and obligations
of the First Party and the Second Party. Should there be additional
agreement, the same shall be stated in writing and shall be approved by the
First Party and the Second Party;
L This Memorandum of Agreement shall take effect immediately upon signing;
10.This Memorandum of Agreement shall be terminated upon notice of either
party.
