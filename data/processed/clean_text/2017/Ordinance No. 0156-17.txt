WITNESSETH:
That the FIRST PARTY and SECOND PARTY have similar visions and are committed to the improvement of
the quality of life of the residents of Purok 6, Barangay Matina Biao, Davao City, through the extension of basic
services by providing adequate electricity to the said area;
That the FIRST PARTY and the SECOND PARTY have jointly committed to install light and power in the
aforementioned area as one of their existing projects in Davao City;
That the FIRST PARTY and the SECOND PARTY hereby agree to undertake the ELECTRIFICATION OF
PUROK 6 BARANGAY MATINA BIAO, DAVAO CITY (the Project), subject to the following terms and
conditions, to wit:
l. Enclosures. The following enclosures are integral parts of this Agreement:

NINETY EIGHT THOUSAND SEVEN HUNDRED FIFTY SEVEN & 341100 (p398,757.34)
PESOS from ADF, CY20l4. Copy of the Certification of Funds is hereto attached as Annex "A".

hereto attached as Annex "B" and "B-1". Energization of the Project shall be made by the SECOND
PARTY upon full payment of the Projecr Cost by the FIRST pARTy.

implementation of the Project.

those described in Annex "B" and "B-1", shall at all times remain the property of the SECOND pARTy
notwithstanding that the electrical equipment, facilities or any of its parts may now be, or hereafter become,
in any manner attached to the property of the SECOND PARTY, temporarily or permanently.
4. Refund of Advances. The SECOND PARTY shall refund/remit to the FIRST PARTY without interest the
appropriated amount at the rate of SEVENTY FIVE PERCENT (75%) of its annual gross dishibution
revenue derived from the electric billing of the consumers served from the electrical facilities until such time
that the total amount appropriated shall have been liquidated or refunded to the FIRST pARTy.
5. Effectivity. This Agreement shall take effect on the day when this Agreement and its Annexes shall have
been executed.

6.1 This Agreement shall be kept confidential and shall not be disclosed to any person without the prior
written approval from the Parties, as the case may be, except for the following instances:
a. Disclosure as may be required by law or by a judicial decree or order, or as required by the rules
