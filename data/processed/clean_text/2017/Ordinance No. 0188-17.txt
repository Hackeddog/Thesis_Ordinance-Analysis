Pafi's institution;

any payment of tuition fees by the First Pafi in excess should be refunded directly by
the Second PartY to the First PadY;
5. Require the grantee to visit the Office of the Dean of the College of Law twice per
semester for monitoring and guidance;
6. Advise the EBSU of any increase in tuition/miscellaneous fees at least thirty (30) days
prior to the start of the school year or effectivity of the increase;
7. Make recommendations to improve the implementation of the Program; and
8. Such other acts that will assist the First Pafi in the effective implementation of the
Program.
