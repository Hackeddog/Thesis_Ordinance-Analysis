MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
This Memorandum of Agreement entered into and executed this _of
by:
The Depaftment of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) herein
referred to as the DILG-Region XI with principal address at 58 tvlcArthur Highway,
Brgy. l/atina Crossing, Talomo District, Davao City, represented herein by Regional
Director ALEX C. ROLDAN, CESO V.
and -
The Local Government Unit of Davao City herein referred to as LGU PCF
recipient with principal office at San Pedro St., City Hall, Davao City and represented
in this agreement by its City Mayor, HONORABLE SARA Z. DUTERTE by virtue of
the Sanggunian Resolution No.enP:yo.aoS SERIES of ?oE
WITNESSETH
WHEREAS, the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) is
mandated through the Bureau of Local Government Development (BLGD) to
strengthen local government capability aimed towards development-oriented
communities and through the Regional Office in capacitating LGUs to effectively
discharge their powers and functions towards improvlng the delivery of basic services
and facilities to their constituents;
WHEREAS, the Development Budget Coordinating Committee (DBCC) of the
Department of Budget and Management (DBM) approved the Performance-Based
Incentive Policy (PBIC) which provides for an incentive framework to rationalize
national government and intergovernmental transfers to LGUs towards improving
LGU performance in governance and delivery of basic services.
WHEREAS, the Performance Challenge Fund (PCF) for Local Government
Units is a flagship program of the DILG that serves as a facility to help stimulate
LGUs that put a premium on transparency and accountability to enable them to avail
themselves of financial support to jumpstart and sustain local socio-economic
development initiatives supportive of national government goals and priorities. It is
an incentive to eligible LGUs to support the implementation of high-impact
investment projects in the Annual Investment Program (AIP).
WHEREAS, the Performance Challenge Fund (PCF) is being administered by
the DILG in collaboration with the Department of Budget and Management (DBM) to
recognize governance performance particularly in the adoption of "good local
governance" along the governance areas of (i) Good Financial Housekeeping, (2)
Disaster Preparedness (3) Social Protection (4) Business Friendliness and
Competitiveness (5) Peace and Order and (6) Environmental Management;
WHEREAS, the Performance Challenge Fund progressive realization to
achieve the Philippine Development Plan (PDP) 2011-2016 thru programs and
projects supporting the Millennium Development Goals (MDGs), stimulating local
economic development, encouraging disaster preparedness and climate change
adaptation, promoting ecological solid waste management and promotion of
transparency and accountability are anchored on the contribution of local
government units (LGUs) as well as non-government and private sector
organizations;
or*a

s
WHEREAS, the Local Government Units (LGUs), as provided under the Local
Government Code of 1991 or Republic Act 7760, assume the primary responsibility of
providing basic services and facilities and the improvement of quality of life of their
constituents through the implementation of these programs and projects;
WHEREAS, Davao City has been awarded the Seal of Good Local Governance
(SGLG) thereby entitles the LGU to receive the PC Fund Subsidy amounting to a
maximum of Three Million Four hundred Thousand (P3, 4OO,000.00) Pesos.
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the above premises and of
the mutual covenants stipulated hereinafter set forth the PARTIES hereto agree to
enter into this Memorandum of Agreement to adhere to the following terms and
conditions:
SECTION I. Project Details
a. Project Title: School Computer Laboratory
b. Brief Description of Project:
This project is in line with the City Government of Davao's effort to
assist in raising the standard of learning paraphernalia for students in
all subject areas through the provision of E-Classroom Building and
computer packages. The Emilio Ramos National High School was
being chosen as beneficiary of the 2016 PCF financial award.
The project includes the construction of a 7 m x 9 m one-storey
building that will function as a computer laboratory room. It will be
equipped with eighteen (18) units computer sets with computer tables
and two (2) units of printers.
Through this project, the school will be more technologically updated
and for the learner's advancement and abilities for global
competitiveness.
c. Project Cost: P 3,400,000.00
d. Project Location: E. Ramos Nationa! High School (NHS), Brgy.
Angliongto, Buhangin District
SECTION 2. Eligible Projects
Eligible projects for the PC Fund Subsidy are those that support the national
priorities of the present administration aligned to the Philippine Development Plan
2011-2016 such as those achieving the Millennium Development Goals (MDGs);
preparedness in disasters and adaptation to climate change; sustainable
environmental management; local economic development and transparent and
accountable local governance, which are implementable within one (1) year from the
date of receipt of the Notice to Implement (NTI) signed by the DILG Regional Office.

SECTION 3. Delineation of Responsibilities
PCF Recipient LGU shall:
1. Maintain a book of accounts for the PD Fund;
2. Issue an official receipt corresponding to the amount of PC fund subsidy
received;
3. Crate a PCF project team responsible for the implementation of the project;
4. Submit to the DILG Regional Office thru channels the complete documentary
and reportorial requirements of the proposed project/s and reports as
stipulated in memorandum Circular No. 2015
dated _;
5. The maximum completion of the proposed project is 12 months and must
staft on the date of the receipt of the Notice to Implement from the DILG
Regional Office;
6. Put up a signage of the reports using formats such as billboards, tarpaulins,
banners, or posters in the place where the project is being implemented
following the template/design stipulated in COA Circular No. 2013-004; and
7. For disclosure of projects, post in conspicuous places within public buildings
(in front of provincial capitol, city/municipal halls, public library, market, etc.)
monthly/quarterly progress of project implementation and completed
projects, including the disbursed PCF subsidy. In the absence of a website
(in the case of cities/municipalities), said documents shall be posted on the
website of the provincial government or on the DILG regional websites.
DILG Regional Office sha//:
1. Prepare and issue checks as financial subsidy to the LGU beneficiary;
2. Facilitate the release of PCF check amounting to P 3,400,000.00 to the
recipient LGU upon signing of this Memorandum of Agreement (MOA);
3. Issue a Notice to Implement (NTI) to the LGU recipient upon the submission
of documentary requirements;
4. In coordination with the Provincial PCF Focal Person, Cluster Head, and
C/MLGOO, conduct periodic monitoring on the progress of project
implementation and disbursement of the PC funds by the LGU;
5. Conduct validation and ocular inspection of the LGU projects to validate
repofts submitted and substantiate comments/observations and
recommendations. In case of negative findings and observations or slippage,
the LGU beneficiary shall be required to submit an exception report that
contains the negative finding and observations or slippage, its cause/s,
recommendations, actions taken;
6. Post the status of project implementation and fund utilization on the DILG
Regional website if needed; and
7. Supervise the encoding of the LGU project details, accomplishments and
status at the PCF website in coordination with the Provincial PCF Focal
Person, Cluster Head, and C/MLGOO as per MC No. 2015
dated
