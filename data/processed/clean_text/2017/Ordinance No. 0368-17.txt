Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled that:

purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and

city mayor shall represent the city in all its business

approval;

Annex A
CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND BORROWERS'
MTD PROGRAM GUIDELINES
1. Purpose
The purpose of the program is to provide entrepreneurial oppoftunities to women
organization by strengthening their capacities and skills, making them more
productive and empowered citizens of the society.
2. Qualified Applicants
The program is available to the following:
.
Organized and registered RlC/women group;
.
Memberships are residents of Davao City;
.
Projects are Agri/Fishery related;
.
As much as possible has already existing economic activity related to the
project to be funded by MTD;
The abovementioned applicants should be willing to undergo livelihood orientation
and sessions on Gender, Financial Literacy and Values Formation.
3. Additional Guidelines
.
Can comply the enabling documents as required by City Government
.
No outstanding balance from City Government
.
Certification from Barangay where the NGO is operating attesting to the
credibility of the organizations;
o A borrower cannot borrow from more than one cooperative under the
MTD loan program of the city;
4. Mode of Payment
The schedule of payment shall commence 12 months after full release of the loan and
every 3 months thereafter until full payment thru a post-dated check.
5. Monitoring and Evaluation
Regular monitoring shall be conducted by the stakeholders through meeting with the
beneficiaries. Home visits shall likewise be done to ensure proper evaluation of the
program.
