Rationale
Malnutrition of children in targeted sites is caused primarily due to lack
of knowledge in eating and preparing nutritious food, lack of budget allocated for
food, not enough family guidance and other relevant issues in targeted families
and areas.
Children of the said ages are in the critical brain development stage.
Malnutrition in these ages can cause life-long problems connected to the brain
capacity.
Malnutrition causes poor health and hinders proper physical
development.
lll. Goals
1) To make Davao City a model for good nutrition and assure a future
generation of well-developed Filipinos.
2) To increase health and nutritional level of children who are undenueight.
