DEED OF USUFRUCT
KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:
This Deed of Donation is made and entered into by and between:
The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a government entity duly organized and
existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, represented in this act by its
Davao City Mayor, Atty. Sara Z. Duterte-Carpio, of legal age, Filipino, married and a
resident of Davao City, Philippines, hereinafter referred to as the OWNER;
and
The DAVAO CITY WATER DISTRICT, a government- owned-or-controlled
corporation created by virtue of Presidential Decree No. 198, as amended, with
principal office at J.P. Laurel Avenue, Bajada, Davao City, Philippines, duly represented
in this act by its General Manager, Engr. Edwin V. Regalado, hereinafter referred to as
the USUFRUCTUARY;
WITNESSETH, that:
WHEREAS, the OWNER, through its Office of the City Planning and
Development Coordinator, certified that it allows the USUFRUSCTUARY to make use of
the Road Right of Way and Easement in Los Amigos Relocation Phase 1, which property
is under the ownership of the OWNER. Attached as Annex "A" is a copy of Certification
dated February 20, 2077 and made integral part hereof;
WHEREAS, the OWNER owns the following materials and equipment installed in
Los Amigos Relocation Phase 1, specifically identified as follows:
708.00
Meters more or less of 150mm diameter uPVC Pipe;
Unit of 50mm diameter Blow Off Valve Assembly
WHEREAS, the OWNER installed a water distribution system in Los Amigos
Relocation Phase 1, supplying a portion of Blocks2,4,7,8, 10, 11, L2, L3, L4, L5,
L6, L7, 20, zI,t 22, 23, 24, 25, 26t 27, 28t 29, 35, 36, 4Lt 42, 43, and 44 at LOS
Amigos, Tugbok District, Davao City as per attached DCWD-Approved AS-BUILT PLAN
(Annex "B").
WHEREAS, the OWNER signified its desire to avail the services of the
USUFRUCTUARY by way of supply of water to its beneficiaries-residents, and thus
offers for the use of the USUFRUCTUARY, its water distribution system and its
appurtenances as listed above.
WHEREAS, all the abovementioned materials and equipment, fittings and
appurtenances of the OWNER were inspected and tested by the USUFRUCTUARY.
Attached is a copy of the Project Inspector's Report dated November 4, 2016 marked as
Annexes "C" and made integral part hereof.
WHEREAS, the USUFRUCTUARY found that the said pipes, fittings and
appurtenances conformed to its standards and specification and as evidenced thereto,
the USUFRUCTUARY's engineers and officers affixed their signatures in the said ASBUILT PLAN; Attached is Reference Plan as Annex "D" and made integral part hereof.
WHEREAS, the required hydrostatic pressure and leak test for the OWNER's
pipelines were conducted on November 4,20L6. Based on the Hydrotest and Leak Test
Report (Annex "E"), the OWNER's pipelines passed the required standard of the
USUFRUCTUARY.

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises:
1.
The OWNER does hereby transfers, lets and conveys by way of usufruct
unto the USUFRUCTUARY and its successors-in-interest the subject properties
consisting of a water distribution system composed of water pipes and lines, as above
enumerated, installed at Los Amigos Relocation Phase L, inside the territorial
jurisdiction of the City of Davao;
2.
The OWNER warrants that the area where the water distribution system
is located is free from any legal controversy;
3.
The OWNER, thus hereby gives its unconditional consent now and
hereafter for the USUFRUCTUARY to make repairs, system improvements,
interconnection of pipes, mainline tapping, extensions, and the like. By this, the
OWNER, allows the USUFRUCTUARY to enter the abovementioned propefty (Los
Amigos Relocation Phase 1), and introduce improvements thereon which may
necessarily or incidentally affect the existing structures such as concrete pavements,
sidewalks, curbs and gutters, landscaping works, and the like resulting from the
maintenance and operation, expansion and improvements of the water system facilities.
In such event the USUFRUCTUARY shall undertake to restore, at its expense, any
structure that will be affected by the introduction of said improvement so that the said
structures can serve the purpose that it was built for. In case there will be damage that
may be sustained as a consequence thereof, the USUFRUCTUARY shall fix the same to
its original structure and shall dispose the debris and waste materials properly;
4.
This usufruct is perpetual, If for any reason, the OWNER decides to
revoke this usufruct, it is understood that the USUFRUCTUARY will cease supplying
water services to the OWNER and all the beneficiaries of this contract.
