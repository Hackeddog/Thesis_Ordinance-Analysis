MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
This Memorandum of Agreement is made and entered into by and between
The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a Local
Government Unit, with principal office address at City Hall
Drive, San Pedro Street, Davao City, represented by its
City Mayor Sara Z. Duterte,

Whereas, the City Government of Davao operates under its jurisdiction the Davao City
Investment Promotion Center (DCIPC), a division under the Office of the City Mayor
that provides business community professional seruices in the establishment of business
presence in the City. It is spearheading the review and update of the Davao City
Investment Incentives Code.
Whereas, Isla Lipana& CO. is a Philippine member firm of the PricewaterhouseCoopers
(PwC) global network and is in the business of delivering audit and assurance, tax and
advisory services. PwC wishes to work with DCIPC, as a Knowledge Partner in u@ating
the Davao City Investment Code by providing guidance on the relevant information
required in the study and process for the evaluation and analysis.
Now, therefore, in consideration of the foregoing, and of the mutual covenants and
commitments set forth herein, the parties hereto agree as follows:
1. PURPOSE
The parties agree to undertake the review and u@ate of the Davao City Investment
Incentive Code of 1994-City Ordinance 0350-20t2. The objectives of this exercise are
as follows:
i.
To determine whether sufficient investments in an area or activity have been
attained;
ii.
To determine whether continued extension of incentives or support measures
for the specific investment area is no longer to the interest of the City;
iii.
To conclude that an area or activity cannot attract investors within reasonable
time and cost or may result in unfavorable business climate;
iv.
To conduct industry profile for each of the preferred investment areas which
will include, among others, the total value of investments per PIA starting
1994;
v.
To propose a functional staffing pattern sufficient to carry out the duties and
responsibilities of the Investment Promotion Center where positions/titles

titles under the Department of Budget and Management;
vi.
To revisit the present incentives packages and determine the need to come
up with better incentive package in accordance with what the local
government can still give without sacrificing the drive to increase revenue
collection at the local level;
vii. To identify emerging industries/sectors as possible additional investment
areas; and
viii. To identiff types of projects that are suitable for possible incentive availment.
