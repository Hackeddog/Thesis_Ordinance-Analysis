Ord. No. 0134-t7
Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled that:
SECTION 1. EIE - This Ordinance shall be known as " AN ORDINANCE GRANTING
AUTHORIW TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN THE PERFORMANCE COMMITMENT
FOR HCIs (REVISION 2) AND PROVIDER DATA RECORD HEALTH CARE
INSTITUTION OF TORIL HEATTH CENTER BIRTHING HOME FOR ITS
APPLICATION FOR PHILHEATTH ACCREDITATION FOR MATERNITY CARE."
SECTION 2. DECLARION OF POLICY - SECTION 455, (b) (1) (vi) of the Local
Government Code provideq to wit;
"For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of
which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to
SECTION 16 of this Code, the City Mayor shall represent the City in all its
business transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts and
obligations, and such other documents upon authority of the
SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of pursuant to law or ordinance';
SECTION 3. AUTHORIW - The City Mayor is hereby granted authority to sign, for
and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Peformance Commitment for HCIs
(Revision 2) and Provider Data Record Health Care Institution. The said documents are
necessary for the application of Toril Health Center Birthing Home for Philhealth
accreditation for its maternity care package;
SECTION 4. SEPARABILITV CLAUSE - If for any reason, any SECTION or provision
of this ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof
not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.
SECTION 5. EFFECTryITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.
ENACTED, on the 10n of January 2017, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of
the SANGGUNIANG present.
