RATIONALE
The CCL Project in DSWD areas shall be implemented for the indigenous communities using the
Community-Driven Development (herein after referred to as CDD) stratery of the KALAHI-CIDSS
Project;
The above-mentioned barangay of the city/municipality of Davao City in the province of Davao del
Sur were identified by DepEd to be covered by the CCL Project;
The CCL Project also promotes DepEd's lndigenous Peoples Education (lPEd) program;
The DSWD, the City/IMunicipality and the Barangay, represented by their respective officers and local
chief executives, have expressed their interest willingness, and commitment to participate in the
implementation of the CCL Project, and apply the PRINCIPLES of participatory, transparent and
accountable governance espoused by the KALAHI-CIDSS Project, to improve the living conditions of
its poor constituents, and have decided to formally bind themselves for the implementation of the CCL
Project for a period of one (l ) year, to commence upon the signing of this SIA.
