This agreement made and entered into by and between:
The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a Local Government Unit existing by
virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines with principal office at City Hall
Drive, San Pedro Street, Davao City, herein represented by the City Mayor, Hon.
SARA Z. DUTERTE-CARPIO, hereinafter referred to as the CITY GOVERNMENT;
and
The KING MULTIPURPOSE COOPERATIVE, a duly registered cooperative with
the Cooperative Development Authority, with principal office at King MPC Bldg., 4
Palma Gil St.,, Davao City herein represented by NESTOR D. ORTIGOZA, General
Manager, authorized representative, hereinafter referred to as CONDUIT
COOPERATIVE.
WITNESSETH THAT
WHEREAS, it is the policy of the CITY GOVERNMENT to uphold the rights of
women and the belief in their worth and dignity as human beings;
WHEREAS, women shall be recognized as full and equal partners of men in
development and nation building and men shall share equal with all forms of productive
and reproductive activities;
WHEREAS, in consonance to the said policy, the CITY GOVERNMENT, through
its MAGNEGOSYO TA DAY (MTD) Program extends financial and technical assistance
and other services to enable women to become social entrepreneurs through the
approval of appropriation for financing of the ADF#17026 per Ordinance No. 0122-16;
WHEREAS, by enabling women to become social entrepreneurs, they become
the CITY GOVERNMENT's partners in responding to a host of interrelated social needs
and issues, not only passive recipients of financial assistance;
WHEREAS, medium and large cooperatives engage in lending services whose
members include women who are into micro business/enterprise activities;
WHEREAS, these credit cooperatives can be partners of the CITY
GOVERNMENT in implementing the MAGNEGOSYO TA DAY Program as CONDUIT
COOPERATIVES with the objective of extending the program to its women members
particularly marginalized and vulnerable groups of women;
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the
parties hereto bind themselves to the terms and conditions of this agreement, as
follows:
1. The CITY GOVERNMENT:
a) Shall release the amount of One Million Pesos (Pl,000,000.00) as
appropriation under ADF#17026 - Magnegosyo Ta Day Program to finance
Micro Lending Project;
b) Shall ensure that the loan shall be payable within the maximum period of two
(2) years with zero interest from the date of the final release of the loan;

-3a
c) Shall ensure that the fund shall be utilized only for the livelihood/microenterprise of the target women micro-entrepreneurs especially those
marginalized and vulnerable;
d) Through the concerned line agencies, shall strengthen the capabilities and
enhance entrepreneurial skills of women through the conduct of
training/seminars, as follows:
o Pre-Loan Release Orientation Seminar
. GrassrootsEntrepreneurshipManagement
. Simplified Record Keeping
e) Shall conduct monthly monitoring of loan assistance to CONDUIT
COOPERATIVE to ensure compliance of the program;
f) Shall require the CONDUIT COOPERATIVE to submit reports on the list of
women beneficiaries that have availed of the program annually, as well as
case studies on the lives of selected beneficiaries and documentation of good
practices of the project;
g) Shall exercise relative supervision over the project to ensure that over-all
implementation is in accordance with the existing guidelines, rules and
regulations. (see attached Annex A)
2. The CONDUIT COOPERATIVE:
a) Shall utilize the loan as additional/start-up capital for women members
primarily to engage in micro-enterprise/livelihood activities;
b) Shall pay the loan granted by the CITY GOVERNMENT within the maximum
repayment period of two (2) years at zero interest;
c) Shall release the livelihood assistance to the women-members of the
cooperative who are the target beneficiaries of the program giving priority to
disadvantaged women, such as:
o lP and Moro women
o Women with disabilities
. VAWC survivors
. Solo parents
. Small farmers, rural workers and urban poor
d) Shall distribute the slots equitably to Districts 1,2 and 3, if applicable;
e) Shall make sure that the livelihood activities to be undertaken by the
beneficiaries will not violate any city ordinance or state law;
0 Shall extend a maximum loanable amount of Twenty Thousand Pesos
(P20,000.00) to each beneficiary;
g) Shall impose a maximum 1o/o per month interest on the amount borrowed by
the beneficiaries with 6 to 12 months repayment period;
h) Shall provide at least 20o/o counterpart on the total project cost or its equivalent
in the amount of Two Hundred and Fifty Thousand Pesos (P250,000.00);
i) Shall submit a report to the Local Chief Executive through the concerned
office/department of how the fund was used within ninety (90) days after the
release of the loan;

j) Must have no outstanding balance with the CITY GOVERNMENT;
k) Must have a list of the women members who have availed of the livelihood
assistance and information sheet of each beneficiary;
l) Shall issue Post Dated Check (PDC) to cover repayment of the loan amounting
to One Million Pesos (P1,000,000.00), in favor of the CITY GOVERNMENT
through the City Treasurer's Office.
This LOAN AGREEMENT shall take effect upon release hereof and shall remain
in force until expressly revoked in writing by the parties thereto.

Annex A
CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND PARTNER COOPERATIVES'
MTD PROGRAM GUIDELINES
1. Purpose
The purpose of the program is to provide entrepreneurial oppoftunities to
marginalized women by strengthening their capacities and skills, making them more
productive and empowered citizens of the society.
2. Qualified Applicants
The program is available to the following:
. Women who belong to the marginalized sectors of the society (e.9. solo
parents, IP/Moro women, VAW suruivors, small farmerc or rural
workers, fisher folks, vendorc, elderly women, women with
disabilities, among others); and
. Women who have existing small businesses or are planning to start up a
business.
The abovementioned applicants should be willing to undergo a half day livelihood
orientation and sessions on Gender, Financial Literacy and Values Formation.
3. Additional Guidelines
. A maximum amount of Twenty Thousand Pesos (P20r000.00) may be
availed by the women-beneficiaries.
. The maximum interest rate for the loan is at 1olo per month, with no seruice
fees from the cooperatives offering the same.
. The terms of repayment for the said loans are within six (6), nine (9), or
twelve (12) months, whichever is applicable.
. Loan protection insurance shall be deducted to the loanable amount.
. A borrower cannot borrow from more than one cooperative under the MTD
loan program of the city.
4. Loan Requirements
. Duly accomplished application form
. 1 pc. 2X2ID picture, nN/Valid ID, Barangay Certification or electric/water bill
(as proof of residence)
. The release of the loan shall be within the day after compliance of
requirements and approval of loan application.
5. Mode of Payment
Depending on the agreement between the cooperative and the borrower, the
schedule for the latter's payment shall be weekly, semi-monthly, monthly, or
quafterly.
6. Monitoring and Evaluation
Regular monitoring shall be conducted by the stakeholders through meeting with the
beneficiaries. Home visits shall likewise be done to ensure proper evaluation of the
program.
