MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
KNOW NU- UTM BY THESE PRESENTS:
The Memorandum of Agreement made and entered into this 5th day of October
20t7, at Quezon City, by and between:
The BUREAU OF FIRE PROTECTION is a government agency, with office
address at Agham Road, Brgy. Bagong Pag-asa, Quezon City, represented herein
by its FIRE CHIEF, F/DIRECTOR LEONARD R BANAGO, herein referred to as "BFP or
souRCE AGENCY (SA)";
-andThe CITY GOVERNMEI{T OF DAVAO, a local government unit, with office
address at Davao City, represented herein by HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE - CARPIO,
hereinafter referred to as the "IMPLEMENTING AGENCY (IA)"
WTTNESSETH, that:
WHEREAS, BFP, through DOF-DBM-DILG Joint Circular No. 2015-01, dated 12
February 2015, is hereby authorized to release the twenty percent (20a/o) share in the
Fire Code Revenues to concerned cities/municipalities upon release of Special Allotment
Release Order (SARO) / Notice of Cash Allocation (NCA) from the Department of Budget
and Management (DBM) for collections for CY 2010,2011,2012, and 2013. CY 2014 to
2015 collections will be directly released by the DBM to the city/municipality;
WHEREAS, CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO shall appropriate the twenty percent
(208/o) share exclusively for the improvement and maintenance of city/municipal BFP
fire stations to include acquisition of firefighting equipment, seruice vehicles,
investigative equipment, rescue equipment, office equipment, office furnitures &
fixtures and construction/repair of citylmunicipal BFP fire stations;
WHEREAS, BFP and CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO shall ensure the effective
collection of Fire Code Revenues and its efficient allocation for the improvement of
city/municipa! BFP fire stations;
NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the
pafties to this MOA have agreed to the following:
1.0 DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILTTIES OF BFP:
To submit Special Budget Request (SBR) for the release of the twenty
percent (20Yo) LGU share to the DBM supported by the following:
1.1.1 A specific breakdown of cities/municipalities with their
corresponding shares;
1.1.2 The Ceftificate of Collection and Deposit from the BTR; and

to authorize its Authorized Government Servicing Bank to release the twenty
Percent (20o/o) share to the concerned cities/municipalities upon release of SARO/NCA
from DBM covering the period CY 2010-2013.
1.4 To ensure compliance by the city/municipality to the repoftorial requirement
specified herein as mandated by law, prior to the release of succeeding funding.
2.0 DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILIES OF THE IMPLEMENTING AGENCY (THE LGU):
2.1 To submit to the BFP through the Fire Marshal the certificate of remittance
from the city/municipal Treasurers as one of the requirements for the release of
twenty percent (20o/o) LGU share;
2.2 To utilize the twenty percent (20o/o) share exclusive for the use of the
operation and maintenance of its city/municipal BFP fire station, including
the construction and repair of the said BFP fire station;
2.3 To ensure the improvement and maintenance of the city/municipal
BFP fire stations to include acquisition of firefighting equipment, service
vehicles, investigative equipment, rescue equipment, office equipment, office
furnitures & fixtures and construction/repair of said BFP fire stations;
2.4 To implement the approved plans for the improvement of Fire Seruices in
the City of Davao;
2.5 To submit regular repofts on the utilization of the 20olo LGU share to the
City/Municipal Fire Marshal and update the same as to the improvements of any
facilities and the inventory of any equipmenVitems purchased/constructed
from the said share;
2.6 To provide the BFP through the Fire Marshal the city/municipality's Trust
Fund Account Number where the corresponding 20olo share will be deposited through
LDDAP-ADA and to maintain subsidiary ledger of the special fund account for the
detailed monitoring of all the movements of said account;
2.7 ln no case shall the fund transferred be utilized for the payment of
ditional compensation to employees in the form of allowances, incentive pay
bonuses, honorarium, or other forms of additional compensation, except as may
be authorized by law or existing regulations, nor shall it be used to create new
positions, to augment salaries of regular personnel or in case of purchase of
motor vehicles without prior approval of the Office of the President;
2.8 To issue official receipt as acknowledgement for the amount received from
the twenty percent (20o/o) share in the Fire Code Fees Collection.

3.0 AGREEMENT ON EFFECI]VE FUND UTIUZATION:
3.1 The pafties hereby agree that the subject fund shall be used only on
essential and necessary expenses pursuant to the intention of the framers of the Fire
Code (R.A. 9514), which is "exclusively for the maintenance of its city/municipal BFP
fire station".
3.2 Pursuant to the above agreement, the parties commit to abide on the
effective fund utilization, as follows:
3.2.1The city/municipal Chief Executives shall consult the citylmunicipal
Fire Marshal regarding its intention to use, appropriate and utilize the fund subject
hereof;
3.2.2 Upon consultation, and within a period of five (5) days there from,
the city/municipal Fire Marshal shall communicate or submit his list based on the Fire
Protection PIans and Budget Matrix (FPPB Matrix) to the city/municipal Chief Executives
as regards the essential needs and requirements of each city/municipal BFP fire station
where the fund shall be used;
3,2.3 The ci{/municipal Chief Executives shall and can only choose
among the priority items included in the FPPB MatriVlist submitted by the
city/municipal Fire Marshal;
3.2.4 No amount of the fund shall be used, appropriated and devoted to
any spending not included in the FPPB Matrix/list of priorities submitted by the
city/municipal Fire Marshal.
4.0 AMENDMENTS
Amendments shall be subject to issuance of the superuening circulars
issued jointly by DOF, DBM, DILG, BFP.
