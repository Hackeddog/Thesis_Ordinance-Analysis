096-13, SERIES of 2013 hereinafter referred to as *DAVAO CITY LGU".
WITNESSETH: That
WHEREAS, the DAVAO CITY LGU requested LANDBANK to extend the
Livelihood Loan Facility (the "Facility") to its qualified elected officials and employees :
WHEREAS, LANDBANK has agreed to the DAVAO CITy LGU,s request
subject to such terms and conditions as may be mutually agreed upon:
NOW, THEREFORI, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises and of
mutual covenants and stipulations hereafter set forth, the parties hereby mutually agree as
follows:
I. The grant ofa livelihood loan under the Facility to the elected officials and
employees of the DAVAO CITY LGU are subject to the following terms and
conditions:
A.
Borrower'sEligibilityCriteria
1. Except for elected officials, the bonower must be a regular/permanent
employee of the DAVAO CITY LGU and has been in its service for at
least one (l ) year;
2. Must have no pending administrative/criminal case;
3. Must be eighteen (18) to sixty four (64) years ofage at the time ofloan
application; Elected officials and their co-terminus appointees whose
age is above the maximum requirement may be accommodated,
provided:
3.1 The co-maker requirements are satisfied; and
3.2 Special insurance coverage thru Landbank Insurance
Brokerage Inc. (LIBI) is approvedifacilitated and the special
insurance premium coverage for the whole term ofthe loan is paid.
4. Must have no pending application for retirement or not due for
retirement within the term of the loan:

5. Must be up-to-date in the payment of his/her existing loans with
LANDBANK, if any;
6. Must have a monthly net take-home pay (MNTHP) of Three Thousand
Five Hundred Pesos (P3,500.00) or such amount as may be prescribed
by the General Appropriations Act which is in effect at the time of
loan application.
7. Must have a co-maker who is employed in the same office/agency
even if assigned to a different office/branch of the agency within
the same region. With at least equal rank or salary grade and at
least MNTHP as the borrower and is not a co-maker of another
employee-bomower under this loan facility
8. Must have an existing LANDBANK ATM Payroll Account with the
Servicing Branch where proceeds of loan shall be credited and/or loan
payments shall be debited, as may be applicable.
B.
Loan Amount
1. Non-Elected
Qualified rank and file (SG 17 & below) employees may avail of a loan
equivalent to up to eight (8) months of their Gross Pay but not to exceed
Two Hundred Twenty Thousand Pesos (P220,000.00).
Qualified officers (SG 18 & up) may avail ofloan equivalent to up to eight
(8) months of their Gross Pay but not to exceed Four Hundred Fifty
Thousand Pesos (P450,000.00).
Elected officials may avail of a loan equivalent to up to eight (8) months
of their Gross Pay but not to exceed Five Hundred Thousand pesos
(Ps00,000.00).
For avoidance ofdoubt, Gross Pay shall include Basic Pay and
RATA/ACA/PERA, meal allowance and other fixed allowances of the
same nature.
C.
Manner of Release
The loan proceeds shall be net of Credit Life Insurance (CLI) premium,
penalty (if applicable) and other charges. The same shall be credited to
borrower's existing LANDBANK deposit account (ATM or Savings
Account) maintained with the servicing Branch;
D.
Repayment Terms
1. The loan shall have a minimum term of twelve (12) and maximum of
thirty six (36) months with monthly term divisor.
2. The loan shall be paid in equal monthly amortization thru:
Option 1: Debit by LANDBANK from employee-borrower,s
LANDBANK ATM Payroll Account (thru DMVAL Faciliry) on the
scheduled remittance date.
N
For avoidance of doubt, MNTHP is the resulting amount after
considering all deductions and amortizations of all existing loans,
including the livelihood loan applied for.
2. Elected

F
Option 2: Salary/Payroll deduction from employee-borrower by the
DAVAO CITY LGU and remitted to LBP over the counter (OTC)
not later than five (5) banking days after the scheduled remittance
date.
3. The loan repayment shall commence on the payroll date immediately
succeeding the month the loan was released.
E.
Loan Renewal
Loan renewal shall only be allowed (a) after payment of six (6) monthly
amortizations, and (b) the co-maker/surety shall continue as comaker/surety of the loan or the borrower/employee has a new comaker/surety who is qualified under SECTION A.7 hereto.
Interest Rates and Other Charges
1. The interest rate on the loan under the Facility shall be fixed at l2%o per
atu1um;
2. Interest for the whole term shall be payable in arrears and shall be
computed annually based on outstanding loan balance.
3. Service Fee of % of lYo or.5% on the total loan amortization shall be
deducted from the loan proceeds. In case of loan renewal, unexpired
service fee shall be applied/deducted from the computed service fee
due ofthe new loan or to be added to the loan proceeds.
4. The borrower shall be required to obtain a Credit Life Insurance
(CLI) the entire term of the loan. Accordingly, payment for premiums
thereon that may be charged within the term of the loan shall be
deducted by LANDBANK from the loan proceeds.
5. In case of loan renewal, unexpired insurance premium shall be
applied/ Deducted from the computed CLI premium due ofthe new
loan or to be added to the loan proceeds.
6. Documentary stamp taxes as may be imposed under existing tax laws
and regulations shall be for the account ofthe employee-borrower.
7. All fees and other charges that may be due under the loan may be
adjusted at any time during the term of the loan, on account of a
regulation of the Monetary Board of the Bangko Sentral ng pilipinas,
an increase in LANDBANK's cost of funding andlor maintaining the
loan or other factors including, but not limited to, any special reserve
requirements, exchange rate fluctuations and changes in the financial
market. LANDBANK shall notifu the DAVAO CITy LGU and the
Borrower of such adjustment, which shall take effect on the next
succeeding amortization period following such notice.
G.
Collaterals/Securities
The livelihood loan shall be secured by an assignment by the bonower of
his salaries, allowances, bonuses, retirement benefits, separation/gratuity
pay, monetary value of accumulated leave credits and other monetary
receivables from the DAVAO CITY LGU and ATM payroll account in
favor of LANDBANK.

fails to pay his/her amortization on due date, and such failure to pay is
not remedied within five (5) banking days from due date thereof;
2. A penalty fee equivalent to three percent (3%) per month shall be
imposed on the past due principal amount from the day after due date
ofthe loan up to the date ofactual payment.
3. The granting of Livelihood Loan to the employees of the DAVAO
CITY LGU shall be temporarily suspended whenever:
The loan release shall resume when the DAVAO CITY LGU,s
