WHEREAS, the FIRST PARTY has requested the SECOND pARTy to activate a
Special cAFGU Active Auxiliary (scAA) within its territory to supplement its efforts

NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the
parties have hereunto entered into this Memorandum of Agreement under the
following terms and conditions, a savior:

Auxiliary Companies (SCAA Coy) composed of 360 SCAA personnel

Memorandum of Agreement.

personal injury, damages or death cause by or to any of the SCAA when
such injury or death is incurred in the course of lawful performance of
duty.

Geographical Unit Active Auxiliary (SCAA Guidelines).

following purposes: guides in combat operations, porters, personal
security of any particular individual/s and all other tasks not related to
their duties and obligations as SCAA personnel.
