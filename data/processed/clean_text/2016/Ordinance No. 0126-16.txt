MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
KNOW AtL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:
ThisMemorandumofAgreementmadeandenteredintothis-dayofyear _
by and between:
The BUREAU OF SOILS AND WATER MANAGEMENT (BSWM), a staff Bureau of the
Department of Agriculture (DA) with office address at Elliptical Road corner Visayas
Avenue, Diliman, Quezon City, Philippines, represented by Officer in Charge-Director
SONIA M. SALGUERO, hereinafter referred to as the "BSWM";
ThC DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE REGIONAL FIELD OFFICE - REGION XI, an
implementing agency of DA, with office address at Davao City, represented by OIC, Regional
Executive Direcror ENGR. RICARDO M. ONATE, JR., hereinafter referred to as "DA-RFO".
-andThe LOCAL GOVERNMENT UNIT OF DAVAO CITY, a Local Government Unit (LGUJ
with principal address at Davao City, represented by HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE, City Mayor,
hereinafter referred to as the "LGU".
.WITNESSETHWHEREAS, the BSWM, in support to Republic Act Number 10068 otherwise known
as the ")rganic Agriculture Act of 20L0" ("FUI10068") will implement the establishment of
Composting Facility for Biodegradable Wastes ["CFBW") under the National Organic
Agriculture Program ("NOAP");
WHEREAS, the BSWM advocates the utilization of organic fertilizers as one of the
methods to minimize groundwater pollution, restore soil fertility, reduce the vulnerability
of marginal farmers on the high cost of chemical fertilizers, and promote the gradual
adoption of 0rganic Agriculture nationwide;
WHEREAS the BSWM adheres to SECTION 13 of ttA 10068, to continuously
implement means to alleviate the problems of market waste disposal through appropriate
methods of sorting, collecting and composting; devise and implement ways and means not
only of producing organic fertilizers and other farm inputs and needs, on and off the farm,
but also helping to alleviate the problems of industrial waste and community garbage
disposal through appropriate methods of sorting, collecting and composting;
WHEREAS the BSWM and DA-RFO supports Republic Act Number 9003 or the
Philippine Ecological Solid Waste Management Act ["RA 9003") which emphasized the use
of environmentally-sound methods that maximize the utilization of valuable resources and
encourage resource conservation and recovery;
WHEREAS, the DA-RFO as DA's implementing agency in the Region is in charge of
agricultural programs and has the technical and administrative capability to handle the
implementation, monitoring and regional coordination for the establishment of CFBW;
WHEREAS the LGU under SECTION 10 of RA 9003 shall primarily be responsible for
the implementation and enforcement of the provisions of the said Act within their
respective jurisd i ctions;
WHEREAS SECTION 1.7 of RA 9003 states that one of the components of the LGU's
Solid Waste Management Plan is the processing of waste wherein it shall define the
methods and the facilities required to process the solid waste, including the use of
intermediate treatment facilities for composting, recycling, conversion and other waste
processing systerns;

WHEREAS, the LGU has the logistical capability to handle the establishment,
operation, maintenance and management of the CFBW;
WHEREAS, the BSWM, DA-RFO and LGU agree to cooperate, collaborate and jointly
implement the establishment of the CFBW;
NOW, 'IHEREFORE, in consideration of the following premises, the parties hereto
agree to the following terms and conditions:
OBLIGATIONS OF THE PARTIES
A. The BSWM shall:
1. Coordinate with DA-RFO and LGU all activities related to the establishment of
CFBW;
Z. Facilitate the procurement and delivery of CFBW to the LGU in accordance
with the components specified in Annex A;
3. Lead in awarding the CFBW to the LGU;
4. Provide the implementing guidelines for the establishment of the CFBW;
5. Provide technical support and assistance in the establishment of the CFBW;
6. In coordination with the DA-RFO, monitor and document the status of the
project as well as the production data and overall sustainability of the CFBW;
7. In coordination with the DA-RFO, evaluate the implementation and
performance of the CFBW;
B. Submit accomplishment report to DA-NOAP Secretariat.
B. The DA-RFO shall
1. Assist BSWM in the awarding and distribution of the CFBW within the region;
2. Assist BSWM in ensuring that the CFBW is established in accordance with the
implementing guidelines of the project;
3. Assist BSWM in monitoring the status of the project as well as the production
data and overall sustainability of the CFBW;
4. Assist BSWM in the preparation of technical report and documentation of the
CFBW status;
5. Assist BSWM in evaluating the implementation and performance of the CFBW;
C. The LGU shall:
1. Receive the CFBW from BSWM and DA-RFO and establish and operate it in
accordance with the technical protocol of the project;
2. Provide a place to house the CFBW as their counterpart for the project fplease
see attached Annex A);
3. Ensure security and proper care and maintenance of all components of CFBW.
In case of loss or theft of any parts of CFBW, the LGU is responsible for the
replacement;
4. Provide the manpower necessary for the operation and management of the
CFBW;
5. Comply and abide by the implementing guidelines for the establishment of the
CFBW provided by BSWM;
6. Record monthly accomplishment report on the progress of operation,
production and utilization data of the CFBW;
7. Submit to BSWM and DA-RFO for inspection, evaluation and monitoring of the
status of the project as well as the production data and overall sustainability of
the CFBW;
B. Submit quarterly recorded data to BSWM thru the BSWM OA Focal Person and
respective BSWM Soil and Water Area Coordinator ISWAC) and to DA-RFO
thru its OA Focal Person.

SPECIAL PROVISIONS:
t. The BSWM, DA-RFO and LGU shall closely coordinate to ensure the successful
implementation and establishment of the CFBW;
2. To attain sustainability and ensure worth of government's investment, in the
event that the LGU fails to properly utilize the CFBW in accordance with its
intended purpose, the BSWM in coordination with the DA-RFO shall have the
right to retrieve and transfer the components of CFBW to a more competent
and committed beneficiary;
3. After two years of technical assistance and regular monitoring, from the date

4. After receipt of Turn-over certificate, the LGU must submit quarterly

parties.
