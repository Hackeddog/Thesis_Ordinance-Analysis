KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:
This Sisf er City Agreement is being executed by:
The City of Davao, a local government unit organized and existing
under Philippine law holding office at the Davao City Hall, A. Pichon St.,
Poblacion District, Davao City, Davao Del Sur, represented by its Mayor,
Hon. Rodrigo R. Duterte, pursuant to the authority granted to him under
Davao City SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod Resolution No. O264O-16, (the
"City of Davao");
and
The City of Bacoor, a local government unit organized and existing
under Philippine law holding office at the Bacoor City Hall, Bacoor
Government Center, Barangay Bayanan, Bacoor City, Cavite, represented
by its Mayor, Hon. Strike B. Revilla, pursuant to the authority granted
to him under Bacoor City SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod Resolution No.
CR2O15-142 (the "City of Bacoor").
WITNESSETH:
WHEREAS, the promotion of good governance and the maintenance
of peace and order are principles shared by the leaders and people of the
cities of Davao and Bacoor.
WIIEREAS, during a meeting conducted in Davao City on 2O August
2015, the local government officials of both cities agreed on the
development of a mechanism that would allow the officials of both cities to
cooperate with each other for the promotion of good governance and the
maintenance of peace and order for the common benefit of the people of
the City of Bacoor and the City of Davao.
WHEREAS, the elected government officials of both cities have
agreed that the best way to ensure the success of such cooperation is the
execution of a Sister City Agreement between the said LGUs.
NOW THEREFORE, in view of the above, the parties are executing
this Agreement subject to the following terms and conditions:
Areas of Cooperation
The parties agree to cooperate on the following areas of
Peace and Order;
Good Governance;

Oflicial Visits
4.
Pursuant to pertinent laws and government regulations, the
parties agree to authorize its respective employees to meet either at the
City of Bacoor or at the City of Davao on official business related to the
various areas of cooperation. The local goverrlment employees concerned
may only embark on such official visits with the written authority of their
respective Local Chief Executives.
5.
The security and transportation of the visiting city officials
and employees shall be the responsibility of the host city government.
Assistance on the booking of suitable accommodations shall also be the
responsibility of the host city government. Provided that: the city
goverrrment where the visiting officials are employed shall shoulder the
cost of such accommodations.

6.
The parties agree to support each other during natural or
man-made calamities by providing rescue personnel, technical assistance,
or financial assistance to each other in accordance with law subject to

Temporary Assignment of Key Personnel
8.
Pursuant to SECTION 33 of Republic Act No. 7160 (the "l,ocal
Government Code of 1991"), the parties agree to temporarily assign key
personnel under their respective plantilla to the City of Bacoor or to the
City of Davao, as the case may be, to ensure the successful implementation
of various programs or projects under the above-mentioned areas of
cooperation. Such assignment shall be subject to a separate Memorandum
of Agreement that would be approved by the parties' respective
SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod and would be consistent with pertinent civil
service regulations.
Transparency Commitment
9.
The parties commit to be transparent in the conduct of any
project or program covered by the Agreement. To this end, the parties shall
post all contracts related to the said prograrns and projects in each other's
official website (i.e., "bacoor.gov.ph" in the case of the City of Bacoor and
"davaocit5r.gov.ph" in the case of the City of Davao) and make the same
readily available to any interested party.
Binding EIfect

party, this Agreement shall remain binding and effective upon the officials
of the respective parties even beyond the terms of the Local Chief
Executives whose signatures appear hereunder.
