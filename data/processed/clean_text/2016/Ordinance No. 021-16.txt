WHEREAS, in accordance with Executive Order No. 221, s. 2003, DSWD being the lead government agency
for social welfare and development is mandated to provide assistance to local government units, nongovernment organizations, other national government agencies, people's organizations, and other members
of civil society in effectively implementing programs, projects and services that will alleviate poverty and
empower disadvantaged individuals, families and communities for an improved quality of life;
Whereas, one of the roles of the DSWD, defined in Executive Order No. 221, s.2003, is to enable Local
Government Units in implementing social welfare and development programs through technical assistance,
resource generation and augmentation;
Whereas, Republic Act 9344 as amended by Republic Act 10630 mandates that each province and highlyurbanized city (the LGUs) shall be responsible for building, funding and operating a 'Bahay Pag-asa'within
their jurisdiction following the standards that will be set by the DSWD and adopted by the JJWC;
Whereas, Rule 99.a of Revised lmplementing Rules and Regulations of the Republic Act 93444 states that
the DSWD shall develop standards for the establishment and operation of Bahay Pag-Asa.;
Whereas, the DSWD recognizes the role of the Local Government Units in responding to the needs of
children in conflict with the law (CICL) and in the management and supervision of Bahay Pag-asa;
Whereas, the DSWD, through the Social Technology Bureau, develops social technology projects and is
being represented by the DSWD Field Office in the pilot implementation of social technology projects in
partnership with Local Government Units;
Whereas, the Buklod Paglaom para sa CICL: Holistic Psychosocial lntervention for Children in Conflict with
the Law was conceptualized and developed to provide appropriate response to the psychosocial and
economic needs of children in conflict with the law in Bahay Pag-asa and their families;
/0i281
I

Whereas, the objectives of the Buklod Paglaom para sa CICL:Holistic Psychosocial lntervention for Children
in Conflict with the Law are (1) enable CICL highly at risk of re-offending to develop and maintain positive
and acceptable behavior, (2) help dysfunctional families become responsive to the needs of their children for
support and protection and (3) make community members become responsible in preventing juvenile
delinquency;
Whereas, cooperation between the DSWD and the LGU is needed to successfully implement the
components of the program that includes community-based interventions for CICL and center-based
interventions in Bahay Pag-asa;
Whereas, the LGU signifies its full support in the pilot implementation of the program;
NOW, THEREFQRE, in consideration of the foregoing premises, the parties hereto hereby agree as follows:
SECTION 1: DEFINITI0N 0F TERMS
1.1
The terms defined in this Agreement shall have the following meaning:
a. Bahay Pag.asa refers to a 24-hour child-caring institution established, funded and managed by
local government units (LGUs) and licensed and/or accredited nongovernment organizations
(NGOs) providing short{erm residential care for children in conflict with the law who are above
fifteen (1 5) but below eighteen ('lB) years of age who are awaiting court disposition of their cases
or transfer to other agencies or jurisdictton.
b. Buklod Paglaom para sa CICL is the short title of the Buktod Paglaom para sa CICL: Holistic
Psychosocial and Economic (HOPE) lntervention for Children in Conflict with the Law. lt refers to
the project / program / activities that will be implemented for the children in conflict with the law in
selected BahayPag-asa centers as may be agreed by both parties;
c. Children in Conflict with the Law(CtCL) refers to a person who is alleged as, accused of, or
adjudged as having committed an offense under Philippine laws.
d. Children at Risk refers to children who are vulnerable or at-risk of behaving in a way that can
harm themselves or others, or vulnerable and alrisk of being pushed and exploited to come into
conflict with the law because of personal, family and social circumstances.
SECTION 2: GENERAL PRINCIPLES
This 1MOA defines the roles and responsibilities of the DSWD Field Office and the LGU in
implementing the BuklodPaglaom para sa CICL: Holistic Psychosocial lntervention for Children in
Conflict with the Law.
The Parties shall at all times take all necessary action to cooperate with each other, perform their
respective duties and assume their respective responsibilities in order to achieve the over-all objective
of this Program.
