MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:
This MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT made and entered into this _day of
20l6by and between:
INFORMATION AND COMMUNICATIONS TECHNOLOGY
OFFICE, Department of Science and Technology, a govemment
entity organized and existing in accordance with the laws of the
Republic of the Philippines, with principal office located at ICT
Office Building, C.P. Garcia Avenue, IJP Diliman, Quezon City,
represented by its Cluster Director,ALIMBZAR P. ASUM, CESO V
hereinafter referred to as "ICT OFFICE";
-andCITY OFDAVAO, a local goverrunent unit duly organized and
existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with
principal office address at Davao City Hall/ represented by its
HON RODRIGO R. DUTERTE , hereinafter referred to as the
,,LGUU.
WITNESSETH:
WHEREAS, the national goverrunent through the Department of Science and
Technology (DosT)-Information and Communications Technology Office (ICT Office)
is undertaking the Free Wi-Fi Lrtemet Access in Public Places Project, across the
Philippines to accelerate economic, social and educational opportunities and in
reducing the growing Digital Divide under the overarching e-Filipino Program of the
Govemment;
WHEREAS, the Project is envisioned to provide free Wi-Fi Intemet access in
public places nationwide, but will initially cover selected cities and municipalities and
public places such as Public Schools, Libraries, Hospitals, Rural Health Units, Police
Stations, State Universities and Collegeq Public Transport Terminals, Seaports and
Airports;
WHEREAS, the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG),
together with the League of Municipalities of the Philippines (LMP) and the
Department of Science and Technology (DOST), have agreed to cooperate and jointly
commit in promoting community development through the use of information and
communications technology for effective and efficient national and local governance in
the country. Towards this end, the Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) dated ]uly
24,2A15 was entered into by the three Parties. A copy of the MOU is attached herewith
as Annex "A";

WHEREAS, pursuant to the MOU, and to accelerate the govemmenfs efforts in
enhancing Intemet accessibility for Filipinos, it is deemed imperative that this MOA be
entered into by the parties herein.
NOW THEREFORE, PREMISES CONSIDERED, the parties hereby agree as
follows:
1. POLICIES
In consonance with the promotion of the use of ICT to improve delivery of
critical function of govemment services; and provide efficient and effective ICT
infrastructure and information systems to the public, the following policies are
adopted:
a. Enjoin all barangay units and LGU offices or departments to provide
support and implement the Project without delay;
b. Work together in expanding site coverage for free Wi-Fi Intemet access at
public places within the locality;
c. Provide free Wi-Fi Intemet access in public places without interruption
except in certain circumstances such as force majeure, technical issues and
similar sifuations which are beyond the control of the service provider or
ICT Office;
e. Provide equal use and secured access to the Lrtemet and serving as many
as possible;
f. Pursue, monitor and evaluate digital literacy and Intemet adoption;
g. Maintain sustainability and viability of the free Wi-Fi Intemet access
servlce;
h. Promote responsible use of the Internet.
2. DEFINITION OF TERMS
a. Wi-Fi Internet access - for the purpose of the Project shall be defined as
connectivity to the Lrtemet.
b. ICT infrastructure capacity building - Planning component with the
responsibility of the Local Govemment Units to integrate ICT Programs in
their respective development plans on public works, utilities and
structures.
d. Abide by ICT Office issuances as well as existing laws and public policies
in the posting of contents and broadcasts at the captive portal;

c. ICT Office - a govemment agency mandated to formulate and
recommend ICT policies that will help accelerate and develop the ICT
Sector, promote the use of ICT to improve delivery of critical function of
govemment services; and provide efficient and effective ICT
infrastructure and information systems to the public under the policy,
technical and administrative supervision of DOST.
e. Public places - are social places that would be open and accessible to the
general public. Public areas such as parkq plazas, schoolyards, libraries,
public space in govemment hospitals and rural heath units, transportation
terminals, seaports and airports, etc. will typically be considered public
places. Also included would be spaces open to the public in local and
national goverrunent offices, barangay centers, etc.
f. User registration and on boarding - refers to a multi-level registration
system to access the Free Wi-Fi Intemet Service.
Level L
This shall be the lowest level of registration and will only require
the machine ID (MAC address) of a device for a user to gain access,
which is implemented at the Captive Portal page.
11.
Level 2
Registrations at this level shall require that a user present a
Philippine goverrunent issued ID, possibly through a web-based
registration system.
iii. Level3
The highest level of registration shall be where a user will be
issued a Citizen s Digital ID subject to the requirements of the
National Public Key Infrastructure that will be used to gain access
to the lrternet as well as full access to e-Govemment services.
3. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
3.1.ICT Office
a. Serve as the Project owrrer and lead agency responsible for the overall
planning, implementation and project management of the free Wi-Fi Intemet
Access service.
b. Conduct the appropriate training and orientation to the LGU
1.
c. Evaluate and conduct periodic monitoring of the Intemet corurectivity.
d. Project - refers to the Free Wi-Fi Internet Access in Public Places Proiect.

d. Prctvide the framework for govemance of the free Wi-Fi lrtemet access
service.
3.2. Local Government Units
a. Extend cooperation and access to the area.LGU will assign site
coordinator to the Project in the course of project establishment,
operations, training and implementation.
b. Monitor the participation of barangays and LGU offices and departments
in the Project and compliance with this Memorandum.
c. Provide space at designated location by the ICT Office, and permit the
installation of all public Wi-Fi equipment.
d. Shoulder the electric power cost of all public Wi-Fi equipment installed in
LGU owned facilities.
e. Secure the premises, equipment, outside plant facilities, and other
properties for the continuous delivery of the free Wi-Fi Intemet service.
This includes the provision of the temporary storage for equipment and
other properties needed for the project. The LGU will take the equipment
with utmost care.
f. Lrcorporate ICT INFRASTRUCTURE CAPACITY BUILDING in their
development plans.
g. Permit the ICT Office personnel, authorized representatives and service
providers, when necessary, reasonable access in public and other
pertinent places for carrying out the Proiect implementation and its
service.
h. When necessary appropriate security to ICT Office personnel and
authorized persons and entities involved in the Project.
i. Cooperate in the restoration of the service during a disaster and crisis
i. Designate skilled personnel and coordinate with the Committee on
Information Technology to develop local content and coordinate public
information and announcements, such as among others promotion of
tourism and commercial activities in their locality.
k. Securing visibility and information collateral materials for the project,
provided no private logo.
l. Submits the list and contact information of respective coordinator for
sites under the purview of the Local Govemment Unit.

m. Perform such other responsibilities as may be agreed upon from time to
time by the Parties.
4. LIMITATIONS
Access may be stopped or interrupted at the discretion of ICT Office when
warranted due to reasons affecting national security, domestic tranquility or when
ordered by appropriate authorities.
5. DESIGNATION OF REPRESENTATIVES
The parties shall designate their respective representatives to coordinate efforts
in the implementation of the Project in relation with this MOA. Th"y may submit their
respective reports and recommend such action and policies as may be necessary.
6. OPERATIONAL GUIDELINES
a. LGU is given content development and updating privileges on the captive
portal. It will be given limited discretion over the portal in terms of materials
and information that it may post or upload. ICT Office shall formulate
policies that will help the LGU carry out its responsibility for configuring the
location specific content of the portal.
b. The LGU shall determine and communicate to ICT Office its needs, wants
and interests of their constituents including those that will effectively
improve the delivery of services facilitated to the intemet.
c. ICT Office shall render administrative and operational services of the Project.
Moreover, LGU shall extend administrative assistance by way of record
keeping and custodianship over the equipments and facilities that will be
installed in their respective areas.
d. LGU shall be accredited, trained and equipped to serve as a Registration
Authority for the Level 3-USER REGISTRAION AND ON BOARDING
under such process as may be specffied by ICT Office.
e. LGU shall contribute to user support given by the service providers and ICT
Office as necessarily requested, such as but not limited to front desk services.
t. ICT Office shall provide specific guidelines for LGU to distribute this public
service by provisioning connectivity to those areas by providing fiscal

,'.i
support/incentives to local private sector connectivity providers to which the
LGU may subscribe to.
g. lAtrhile it will be entirely up to the LGU and/or their providers to decide what
technologies to use, the use of TV White Space and other emerging
technologies to connect far-flung areas to the hrtemet shall be advocated.
7. NET COMMUNITY SEAL
LGU shall be given the NET COMMUNITY SEAL for being connected to the
Intemet.
8. ENFORCEMENT CLAUSE
This MOA shall enforce the provision under the Special Provision(sX1), page 851
of Republic Act No. 10651 or the General Appropriations Act for Fiscal Year 2015.
9. MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
If *y clause, sentence or provision of this MOA shall be declared invalid or
unconstitutional, its remaining parts shall not be affected thereby.
Either of the parties can propose amendment in any of the provisions, sections of
the Memorandum of Agreement.
All orders, rules and regulations issued by *y of the parties herein which are
inconsistent or contrary to the provisions of this MO& and in relation with the Project,
are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.
10. EFFECTIVITY
This MOA shall take effect immediately upon signing and shall be terminated
upon mutual consent of both parties.
