ANNEX: SURVEY OUTLINE
I. BACKGROUND
In the Philippines, air pollution and water pollution caused by the rapid
economic development and growing population have become serious problems
In Davao City, where this project will be conducted, PMl0 in Davao increased
from thirty-six thousand to fifty-four thousand tons and carbon dioxide
emission there increased from two hundred ten thousand to four hundred
thousand from 2001 to 2007. Furthermore, though the figure of PM10 is less
than that of the standard in National Ambient Air Quality Standards
NAAQS) in the Philippines, that figure have exceeded twice the standard
stipulated by WHO. According to the national survey conducted in 2009, 65
percent of air pollution is considered to be attributed to the emission from
automobiles, especially from Jeepneys equipped with old diesel engines which
emit exhaust gases that includes a significant amount ofair pollutants. Land
Transportation Franchising & Regulatory Board (LTFRB) said there are
two-hundred thirty'four thousand Jeepneys running and 92o/o of them in the
country are equipped with diesel engines, which emit a lot of air pollutant.
Moreover, the heavy traffic in the urban areas heavily contributes to the
increased density of air pollutant.
Moreover, the water pollution is also becoming a serious problem in the
Philippines. According to National Water Quality Survey of the nineteen
rivers from 2006 to 2013, only one river satisfies the National Water Quality
Standards in Dissolved Oxygen (DO) and just two rivers pass the standards
of Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD). From the report released by
Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DEND in 2005, more
than 30% of water pollution is attributed to the waste water from households
which includes the illegal disposal of used cooking oil.
To address these issues, the Philippines Government issued Toxic
Substances and Hazardous and Nuclear Waste Control Act of 1990(RA6969)
and Ecological Solid Waste Management Act of 2000(RA9003) to require the
proper management of used cooking oil. However, these cannot eflectively
prohibit the illegal disposal Taking this situation into consideration, in
Page5ofl1

December 2015, the Mayor of Davao City issued an Executive Order to
prohibit improper disposal of used cooking oil into the environment and
directed the City Environment and Natural Resources OfEce(CENRO) collect
used cooking oil from establishments and households.
"MAX premium plant" manufactured by Biomass Japan Inc. is an equipment
to refine BDF loaded with air poIutaDt from used cooking oil. BDF is
categorized as a clean fuel since PM included in BDF is 47% less and SOx
emission, secondary formation of PM, is 99.1% less than that of diesel. The
unique feature of this equipment is a dry process that does not need the waste
water treatment, wbjch makes the equipment environmentally-foiendly
technology.
In this project, the used cooking oil shall be collected from restaurants,
households and ambulant vendors and then used cooking oil shall be refined to
BDF. In this project, the refined BDF shall be fueled to diesel engine Jeepneys
and garbage trucks and it will be verified if these automobiles shall run
without any defects attributed to BDF. Furthermore, it is also aimed that
propagating this technology and the collection model of used cooking oil to the
other local governments and private establishments will contribute to solving
the air pollution and the water pollution in the target country.
