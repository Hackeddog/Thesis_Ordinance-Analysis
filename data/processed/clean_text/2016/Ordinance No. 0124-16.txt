A. DEFINITION OF TERMS
1.1 The terms defined in this Agreement shall have the following meaning
a
Comprehensive Program refers to the projecV program/ activities that will be
implemented for the street children, street families and the Sama-Bajau in
critical areas as may be agreed by all parties;
Cost Sharing Scheme refers to the "Equity" of fund to be provided directly by
the LGU out of its own resources for financing portions of the project cost;
Street Children refers to a person below 18 years of age who stay most of
the time (four hours and above)on the streets and in public places and are
engaged, while in the streets, in varied types of activities other than engaging
economic activities to earn a living. They are categorized as.
c.1 Children on the Streets - those who spend most of their waking and
playing hours working on the streets but still go back to their homes/
families;
c.2
Children of the Streets - those who practically live on the streets and
who maybe alone, with friends or family members;
c.3
Completely Abandoned Children those children who are
abandoned by their parents/ families.
a
Street Adults refer to unattached individuals 18 years and above who are
also classified as vagrants and mendicants that adopted the street as place of
abode, source of livelihood, or both.
Street Families refers to a group of persons composed of a father, mother
and child/ren, or relatives who jointly adopt the streets as their primary place
of dwelling and livelihood. They are either homeless that live in makeshift
house along the streets or cart, or community-based who have residences in
a community but chooses to stay on the streets majority of the time (at least
four hours per day, every day).
c.
Sama-Bajaus - members of the indigenous ethnic group mostly from
Southern Philippines who are often seen in the streets of highly urbanized
cities.
B. GENERAL PRINCIPLES
This MOA defines the roles and responsibilities of the DSWD and the LGU in
implementing the Comprehensive Program for Street Children, Street Families and
lndigenous Peoples, especially Bajaus.
The Parties shall at all times take all necessary action to cooperate with each other,
perform their respective duties and assume their respective responsibilities in order
to achieve the over-all objective of this Program.
The Parties shall carry out properly and thoroughly all activities specified in the
Program and ensure adequate funds, facilities, services and other resources which
are required for carrying out the Program.
