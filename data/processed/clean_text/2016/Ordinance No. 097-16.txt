KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:
This Memorandum of Agreement, made and entered into by and
between:
The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, with principal address
at City Hall Bldg., City Hall Drive, San Pedro St., Davao City,
Philippines, represented by its City Mayor, SARA Z. DUTERTE,
and hereinafter referred to as the "MOTHER AGENCY"
and
The OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT (OP), with principal address
at J. P. Laurel St., San Miguel, Malacanang, Manila,
Philippines, represented by its Executive Secretary,
Atty. SALVADOR C. MEDIALDEA, and hereinafter referred to
as "OP or RECEIVING AGENCY"
WITNESSETH
WHEREAS, the Receiving Agency is in urgent need of the services of an
information technology (lT) expert with experience in management and supervision of
both lT projects and personnel, to help execute the current administration's lT
management thrusts and projects in OP-Proper;
WHEREAS, in the spirit of cooperation among govemment agencies, and
consistent with SECTION 9, Rule Vll of the Omnibus Rules lmplementing Book V of
Executive Order No. 292, otherwise known as the Administrative Code of 1987,
SECONDMENT of government employees is allowed;
WHEREAS, in the interest of the service, the RECEIVING AGENCY requested
the secondment of Mr. RODERIC R. GABIA, an employee of the MOTHER AGENCY
holding the position of lnformation Technology Officer ll, City Accountant's Office,
City Government of Davao, herein referred to as SECONDED EMPLOYEE;
WHEREAS, the CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO and the SECONDED
EMPLOYEE interpose no objection to the secondment of the latter in OP;
NOW, THEREFORE, the foregoing premises considered and subject to the
terms and conditions as herein specified, the parties hereby mutually agree as follows:
1. The SECONDMENT of Mr. RODERIC R. GABIA, lnformation Technology
Officer ll, City Accountant's Office, City Government of Davao, to the
lnformation Communication Technology Office (ICTO), this Office, as
DIRECTOR lV, shall be effective 01 October 2016 until 31 December 2018.

2. Mr. GABIA shall receive from the RECEIVING AGENCY a monthly salary
and other benefits, privileges and allowances accruing to the regular officials
of the RECEIVING AGENCY throughout the period of his secondment;
3. Nothwithstanding his official secondment to the RECEIVING AGENCY, the
SECONDED EMPLOYEE shall continue to earn leave credits which are
commutable immediately thereafter and payable by the RECEIVING
AGENCY;
4. Any modification, alteration, addition or change in the terms and conditions
hereof shall not be binding on the parties, unless the same are made in
writing and duly executed by the parties concerned;
5. All the parties shall strictly observe and comply with the requirements of the
rules and guidelines on secondment, inclusive of, but not limited to, the
provisions of Book V of Executive Order No. 292 and its Omnibus Rules, and
for records purposes, this Memorandum of Agreement shall be submitted to
the Civil Service Commission.
