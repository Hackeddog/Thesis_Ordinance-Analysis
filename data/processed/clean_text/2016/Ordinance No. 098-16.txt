MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:
This Memorandum of Agreement, made and entered into by and
between:
The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, with principal address
at City Hall Bldg., City Hall Drive, San Pedro St., Davao City,
Philippines, represented by its City Mayor, SARA Z. DUTERTE,
and hereinafter referred to as the "MOTHER AGENCY"
and
The OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT (OP), with principal address
at J. P. Laurel St., San Miguel, Malacafrang, Manila,
Philippines, represented by its Executive Secretary,
Atty. SALVADOR C. MEDIALDEA, and hereinafter referred to
as "OP or RECEIVING AGENCY"
WITNESSETH
WHEREAS, in the spirit of cooperation among government agencies, and
consistent with SECTION 9, Rule Vll of the Omnibus Rules lmplementing Book V of
Executive Order No. 292, otheruise known as the Administrative Code of 1987,
SECONDMENT of government employees is allowed;
WHEREAS, in the interest of the service, the RECEIVING AGENCY requested
the secondment of Ms. IRMINA BALURAN-ESPINO, an employee of the MOTHER
AGENCY holding the position of Supervising Administrative Officer, City Government of
Davao, herein referred to as SECONDED EMPLOYEE;
WHEREAS, the CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO and the SECONDED
EMPLOYEE interpose no objection to the secondment of the latter in OP;
NOW, THEREFORE, the foregoing premises considered and subject to the
terms and conditions as herein specified, the parties hereby mutually agree as follows:
1. The SECONDMENT of Ms. IRMINA BALURAN-ESPINO, Supervising
Administrative Officer, City Agriculture's Office, City Government of Davao, to
OSAP, as ASSISTANT SECRETARY, shall be for the period of two (2)
years, to begin on 01 September 2016, unless sooner terminated;
WHEREAS, the Receiving Agency is desirous of availing itself of the services of
an Assistant Secretary for the Office of the Special Assistant to the President (OSAP),
with experience in human resource, administrative workload and exposure to
multilateral tasking, management planning, and with team building expertise, to provide
assistance in the processing of Presidential appointments and management of
Presidential engagements;

2. Ms. ESPINO shall receive from the RECEIVING AGENCY a monthly salary
and other benefits, privileges and allowances accruing to the regular officials
of the RECEIVING AGENCY throughout the period of her secondment;
4. Any modification, alteration, addition or change in the terms and conditions
hereof shall not be binding on the parties, unless the same are made in
writing and duly executed by the parties concerned;
lN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have hereunto set their hands this
day of _,
