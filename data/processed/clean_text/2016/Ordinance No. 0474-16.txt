MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:
_- This agreement made and entered into by and between:
The City Government of Davao with principal address at Davao City Hall Drive, Davao City,
Philippines herein represented by RODRIGO R. DUTERTE, City Mayor, hereinafter referred as
FIRST PARTY;
.AND.
CHRISTIAN COLLEGES OF SOUTHEAST ASIA, non-sectarian school duly organized
and existing under and by virtue of the Law of the Philippines, with the principal address at Tulip
Drive Matina, Davao City, Philippines, represented by Leonito A. Fabian, College Dean, hereinafter
referred to as SECOND PARTY.
WITNESSETH:
WHEREAS, First Party supervises the operations of a Health Care Referral Center, Known
the CHILD MINDING for GOVERNMENT EMPLOYEES/WORIilNG PARENTS, which
ties meet the standard of the Department of Social Welfare and Development;
WHEREAS, the First Party offers its facilities to the Second Party for the affiliation of the
Caregiver students;
WHEREAS, the Second Party operates as an Educational Institution, also known as the
CHRISTIAN COLLEGES OF SOUTHEAST ASIA.
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the covenant,
terms and condition hereunder set-forth, the parties have agreed as follows to wit:
l. The First Party allows the use of its facility for affiliation and training of Caregiver to its
capacity to be determined by the same :
2. The First Party allows the students to assist the center coordinator in doing the different
procedure as follows:
2.1 PROVIDE CARE AND SUPPORT TO INFANTS AND TODDLERS:
2.1.1 Comfort infants and toddlers;
2.1.2 Bathe and dress infant and toddlers;
2.1.3 Feed infants and toddlers;
2.1.4 Put infant and toddlers into sleep;
2.1.5 Enhance social, physical, intellectual, creative and emotional activities of infants
and toddlers.
2.2 PROVIDE CARE AND SUPPORT TO CHILDREN:
2.2.1 Instill personal hygiene practice to children;
2.2.2 Bath and dress children;
2.2.3 Feed Children.
t

2.3 FOSTER THE SOCIAL, INTELLECTUAL, CREATIVE AND EMOTIONAL
DEVELOPMENT OF CHILDREN :
2.3.1 Foster children's independence and autonomy;
2.3.2 Encourage children to express their feelings, ideas and needs;
2.3.3 Stimulate children's awareness and creativity;
2.3.4 Foster children's self esteem and development of self concept.
3. That the Fist Party, provides orientation to students on affiliation/training regarding First
Party's Vision, Mission, Goals, and existing Policies, Rules and Regulations that are being
strictly implemented and followed;
4. That the First Party provides feedback and reports to the Second Party regarding specific
behavior and conduct of students on affiliation, by submitting to the Second Party the
STUDENT EVALUATION made by the supervising staff, after their completion of duty;
5. That the Second Party arranges the schedule of duty of student-trainees, until they finished
the require d 120 hours for caregiver;
6. That the Second Party agrees with the First Party that a student-trainee is required to observe
strictly the wearing of the prescribe Students Duty Uniform and School I.D. while on duty.
Submits document such as Endorsement Leffer, Memorandum of Agreement.
7. That the Second Party Agrees with the First Party that Student-trainees should always fill-up
and sign on the official log book upon arrival for duty and every after duty for attendance and
record purposes;
8. That the Second Party agrees with the First Party that the student-trainee should not be
allowed to go out from the premises while on duty, except upon request and permission and
the approval ofany authorized personnel or officer;
g. That the Second Party agrees with the First Party that student-trainee should not be allowed to
entertain visitors while on duty, except on emergency cases, upon request and permission and
the approval ofany authorized personnel or officer;
10.The Second Party agrees that the student-trainee shall replace any institution property which
has been damaged or destroyed within the duration of their duty;
I l. The Second Party shall require their students to undergo physical and laboratory examination
before training such as Complete Blood Count, Urinalysis, Fecalysis, Chest X-Ray and Hepa
B Screening (surface antigen and antibody), If Hepatitis B antibody has negative result, the
student-trainee must submit a Medical Record/Certificate of Hepa B Vaccination. All
examination results shall be submitted in their photocopy form to the First Party for Health
clearance purposes;
l2.This agreement shall take effect upon signing of this Memorandum of Agreement by the
authorized representatives for a period of three years renewable thereafter upon mutual
agreement of the parties herein, unless terminated;
l3.Violation on the part of the Second Party with any provisions of this agreement shall
authorize the First Party to rescind this agreement, even without prior demand or court order.
