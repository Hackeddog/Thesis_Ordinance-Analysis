WITNESSESTH:
WHEREAS, the first party supervises the operations Health Care Referral Center, also
known as CO SU GIAN CENTER FOR THE ELDERLY, and LINGAP CENTER FOR
MENTALLY CIIALLENGE CHILDREN, which facilities meets the standards for training
agency, registered and accredited by the Departnent of Social Welfare and Development.

WHEREAS: the second party operates as Educational Institution, also known as
TECARRO COLLEGE FOITNDATION, INC.
NOW TIIEREFORE, for and in consideration of forgoing premises, the covenants
terms and condition hereunder set-forth, the parties have agreed es follows to wit:
1. The first party allows the use of its facility for affiliation and training of BS Midwifery,
Caregiving and Healthcare Services Students NCII subjects to its capacity to determine by
the same.
2. The first party allows the student to assist the center coordinator in doing different
procedure, as follows:
2.1 taking the vital signs (BP, Temperature, pulse, respiration and heart rate);
2.2 assisting patient in doing their personal hygiene, such as shampoo in bed, oral care,
perineal care and sponge bathing;
2.3 lifting and maneuvering/ positioning the patient;
2.4 after care of hospital equipment and instruments, like washing, sterilizing, and some
simple housekeeping like dusting, or cleaning the area assigned;
and feeding elderly and mentally challenged children;
2.6 Participate in other nursing function such as listening to nursing endorsement and doing
nursing rounds with the nursing staff; and
2.7 Other delegated nursing responsibilities that are non-invasive but with the supervision
ofa registered nurse.
N

3. The fust party provides orientation to the students on affrliation regarding First Party
Vision, Mission, Goals, Policies and Rules.
4. That the FIRST PARTY, provide feedback reports to the Second Party regarding specifics
behaviors and conduct of students on affrliation, by submitting to the Second Party the
STUDENT EVALUATION made by the supervising staff, after their completion of duty.
5. That the second party will provide a Clinical Instructor to monitor, organize and supervise
the student for whole duration of training.
6. That the Second Party will be responsible for arranging the duty schedule for 8 hours
shifting per day of each student until the required 120 hours for Midwifery Caregiving and
Healthcare Services will be completed.
7 . That the second party shall coordinate schedule of affiliation of students with the first party.

ELDERLY, and LINGAP CENTER FOR MENTALLY CHALLENGE CHILDREN.

allowed to go out to the premises, except upon request and permission and approval of any
authorized personnel or officer.
10. The second party agreed with the first party that the student trainee is required to stictly
observe to wear Proper student duty uniform and school ID while on practicum. Submits
documents such as Endorsement letter, Memorandum of agreement and waiver of Claim,
to the fust party.
I l. The secondparty agreedthatthe studenttrainee shall replace any institutionpropertywhich

Screening (surface antigen and antibody), if Hepatitis B antibody has negative result the
student must submit a medical record/certification of Hepa B Vaccination. All examination
results shall be submitted in their photocopy form to the first party for health clearance
purposes.
13. That the first party shall require the student-trainees on duty to fill-up and sign the official
logbook for attendance and record purposes.
