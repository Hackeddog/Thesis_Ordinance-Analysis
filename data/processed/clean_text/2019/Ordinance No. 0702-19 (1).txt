No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides, to wit:
"For efficient, effective and economical governance
the purpose of which is the general welfare of the

o Trainees are advised to keep the confidentiality of the handled clients.
.

o Trainees shall refrain from giving negative and offensive rentarks and harsh jokes about the clients
at any time.
o Loitering is not allowed while clients are on siesta time.

with any of the rules and regulations stated above.
