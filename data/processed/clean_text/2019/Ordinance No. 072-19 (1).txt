oF LpG IN DAVAO CITY, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES"

Ord. No. 072-L9
Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINAilCE
AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 166.81, SERIES OF 1981, KNOWN AS *AN
ORDINANCE REGULATING THE SALE OF LPG Iil DAVAO CITY, AND FOR OTHER
PURPOSES".
SECTION 2. AMENDMENT- The amendment shall be as follows:
SECTION 1. All sellers and dealers of Liquified Petroleum Gas (LPG) in Davao
City contained in cylinders of all weight are required to indicate in the rylinder the
weight of the LPG contained therein and the weight of the cylinder itself.
SECTION 2. All LPG sellers and dealers in Davao City are required to have a
weighing scale in their places of business, duly inspected and scaled twice a year by
the City Treasureds Office, before they can be issued any license or business permit
to do business in Davao City.
SECTION 3. Any LPG sold which is contained in cylinders must be weighed in
the presence of the customers before any sale is made. This shall also cover those
sales made through delivery.
SECTION 4. Any person or entity violating this Ordinance shall suffer the
following penalties:
a) First offense- a fine of Php3,000.00 and/or imprisonment of not less
than three (3) months, or both, at the discretion of the Court.
b) Second offense- a fine of Php4,000.00 and/or imprisonment of not less
than six (6) months, or both, at the discretion of the Court.
c) Third offense- a fine of Php5,000.00 and/or imprisonment of not less
than one (1) year, or both, at the discretion of the Couft.
Violation under this Ordinance shall be a ground for the cancellation of
the license and business permit to sell LPG in Davao City.
In case of corporation, partnerchip, and association, the officers,
managers, or paftners responsible therefore shall be held liable.
SECTION 5. The Business Bureau and the City Treasure/s Office shall ensure
the implementation of this Ordinance and when warranted, investigate and cause
the institution of criminal and administrative legal actions against persons and
entities found violating this Ordinance.
SECTIOil 3. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If any provision of this Ordinance is
declared invalid or unconstitutional, the other provisions not affected thereby shall remain
valid and subsisting.
SECTION 4.
This Ordinance shall take effect immediately after
posting in two (2) conspicuous public places and publication for three (3) consecutive
days in a local newspaper of general circulation in Davao City.

Ord. No. 072-tg
ENACTED, October 15, 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
