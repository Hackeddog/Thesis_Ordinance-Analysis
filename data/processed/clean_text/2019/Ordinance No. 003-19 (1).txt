MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:
This Agreement is made and entered into by and between:
The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local government unit under the
Republic of the Philippines, duly represented herein by its City Mayor, SARA Z.
DUTERTE-CARPIO, hereinafter referred to as CG-DAVAO;
The CITY GOVERNMENT OF MARIKINA, a local government unit under the
Republic of the Philippines, duly represented by its City Mayor, MARCELINO R.
TEODORO, hereinafter referred to as CG-MARIKINA;
The MARIKINA SHOE TRADE FAIR MARKETING C(X)PERATM, a
cooperative duly organized and existing under the laws of the Philippines with postal
address at East Grandstand, Marikina Sports Center, McDonalds Avenue, Sto. Niffo,
Marikina City, represented herein by its Chairman, ANTOilIO E. AilDRES, SR'
hereinafter referred to as COOPERATIVE;
WITNESSETH:
WHEREAS, it is the policy of the CG-DAVAO to elevate the industry of small
medium enterprise through product development and business enterprise, and to
establish partnership with the National Government Agencies, Local Government Units
and private sectors;
WHEREAS, on June 27, 2018, the CG-DAVAO has entered into a "Sister City
Agreement" with the CG-MARIKINA to promote cultural and commercial ties between
the two cities;
WHEREAS, it is one of the initiatives of the CG-DAVAO to establish a Tourism
Oriented Project whose objective is to create more oppoftunities for local manufacturers
to showcase their guality products;
WHEREAS, the CG-MARIKINA who has been known as the Shoe Capital of the
Philippines, is committed to provide support to the shoe industry in Marikina which
creates various products out of domestic raw materials to serve as their residents'
means of livelihood;
WHEREAS, the CG-MARIKINA has made the effort to strengthen the Marikina
Shoe Industry to be able to compete in the local, national and global markets.
WHEREAS, the COOPERAIVE has expressed its intent to display for sale their
locally made products at the Pasalubong Center of the City of Davao;
WHEREAS, it is the policy of the CG-DAVAO to ensure that all its citizens are
provided with equal oppoftunities for the improvement of their well-being;

NOW, THEREFORE, for and inconsideration of the foregoing premises the
pafties have agreed that the COOPERATIVE will utilize a stall at the Pasalubong Center
of the City of Davao, under the following terms and conditions:

RESPONSIBILITIES OF PARTIES
CG-DAVAO shall:
1. Provide a space at the Pasalubong Center to be utilized by the COOPERATIVE;
2. As a form of suppoft, promote the center as hub of locally made quality
products;
3. Have the right to monitor, interuene, and institute corrective measures relative to
the operations of the Center.
4. Collaborate with the City Government of Marikina in the promotion and
marketing of the Davao and the Marikina made products.
CG.MARIKINA ShAII:
1. Determine and endorse to the CG-Davao their local producers/manufacturers
who will be doing business in the Pasalubong Center.
2. Collaborate with the City Government of Davao in the promotion and marketing
of the Marikina and the Davao made products.
3. Extend the same privileges to the Davao residences in the event that a
Pasalubong Center will be established in their locality.
COOPERAIVE shall:
1. Be responsible for the enhancement of the space that will attract locals and
tourist, subject to the approval of the CG-Davao;
2. Designate a person-in-charge to superuise the space/stall and assist in the sale
and marketing of the products;
3. Be responsible for the payment of the monthly rental of the space and
corresponding fees and charges in connection with the utilization of utilities;
4. Primarily be responsible for the promotion and marketing of its products to the
public (local and tourists);
5. Abide by the corresponding rules and regulations set by the Pasalubong Center
Management.

conditions embodied herein.
B. MODIFICATIONS OR AMENDMENTS
Any modifications or amendment to this Agreement shall be upon the prior

instrument for the purpose.
