This Memorandum of Agreement (MOAJ is executed by and between:
The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local government unit
constituted under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with
address at City Hall Building, San Pedro St., Davao City 8000, and herein
represented by the Local Chief Executive / Mayor of Davao City, SARA
Z. DUTERTE (hereinafter referred to as "Davao City").
-andRECITALS
L. SECTION 35 of the Local Government Code gives Local Government Units (LGU)
such as the Davao City, the authority to enter into agreements with the
private sector in the pursuit of developments for the general welfare.
2. Davao City passed 0rdinance No. 0339-15, SERIES of 2015, entitled "An
Ordinance Amending Ordinance No. 0353-12, Promoting Public Private
Partnership (PPP) in the Financing, Construction, Operation and Maintenance
of Local Government Infrastructure and Development Projects" fthe "PPP
Code") to provide for the procedures and guidelines that the city will follow
in entering into a Partnership Agreement with the Private Sector;
3. The PPP Center facilitates the implementation of the Public-Private
Partnership (PPP) Program and serves as the central coordinating and
monitoring agency for all PPP projects in the country. Specifically, the PPP
Center: (i) provides technical assistance to national government agencies
[NGAs), government-owned-and controlled corporations (GOCCsJ,
government financial institutions (GFIsJ, state universities and colleges
[SUCs), and local government units [LGUsJ, as well as to the private sector, to
help develop and implement critical infrastructure and other development
projects; (ii) manages the Project Development and Monitoring Facility
(PDMF), a fund available to implementing agencies in developing and
implementing PPP projects; [iii) conducts various capacity building activities
to implementing agencies; and (iv) advocates for policy reforms to enhance
the enabling environment for PPPs.
4. Davao City intends to tap the technical assistance of the PPP Center in its
various development projects, and the PPP Center signifies its willingness to
provide such assistance and to collaborate with Davao City for its
development prolectsT

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties have agreed to formalize the terms of their collaboration
as follows:
SECTION 1. PURPOSE - The purpose of this MOA is to provide a framework for
cooperation and coordination between the Parties with the goal of developing a robust
pipeline of PPP projects for Davao City.
SECTION 2. DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES - In pursuit of the above
objectives, the Parties have agreed to perform the following duties and responsibilities:
A. The PPP Center shall render assistance to Davao City, including, but not
limited to, the following:
L. review of and provide inputs to the PPP Ordinance of Davao City;
2. provide advisory service and technical assistance in project preparation
and development (including preparation of in-house Business Cases for
selected projects) of solicited projects; evaluation and negotiation of
unsolicited proposals; approval, procurement, and implementation stages
of PPP projects; and other related services;
3. conduct project facilitation and assistance in addressing impediments or
bottlenecks in the development, evaluation, negotiation, approval,
procurement, and implementation of Davao City's PPP projects;
4. provide assistance in the development of a PPP pipeline of projects based
on the available assets of Davao CiW;
5. provide capacity building support to Davao City personnel;
6. recommend plans, policies and implementation guidelines relative to
Davao City's PPP programs and projects;
7. assist Davao City in assessing whether the development of feasibility
studies, provision of transaction advisory services and assistance in the
procurement, of their identified PPP projects can be funded by the PDMF.
If Davao City agrees to consider engaging PDMF-funded consulting
services for their identified PPP projects, the PPP Center shall facilitate the
application; and
B. assign the PPP Center project team/s that will be in charge of Davao City's
concerns. For the avoidance of doubt, the PPP Center project team/s
assigned to assist Davao City shall remain under the direct control and
supervision of the PPP Center.
B. Davao City agrees to perform the following acts
1. within a reasonable period, provide or allow access to, relevant
documents and information requested by the PPP Center in connection
with the objectives of this M0A, to the extent allowed by applicable law at
the discretion of the City;
2. provide requisite authority to the Local Chief Executive / Mayor to pursue
project development and procurement of the City's PPP projects unde2;
