by and between:

RECITALS:
WHEREAS, SECTION 16 of Republic Act No. 7160 otherwise known as the Local
Government Code provides that every Iocal government unit shall exercise the powers
expressly granted, those necessarily implied there from, as well as powers necessary,
appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are
essential to the promotion of the general welfare. Within their respective territorial
jurisdictions, Iocal government units shall ensure and support, among other things, the
preseruation and enrichment of culture, promote health and safety, enhance the right
of the people to a balanced ecology, encourage and suppoft the development of
appropriate and self-reliant scientific and technological capabilities, improve public
morals, enhance economic prosperity and social justice, promote full employment
among their residents, maintain peace and order, and preserve the comfort and
convenience of their inhabitants.
WHEREAS, the same Code states that Local Government units shall likewise
exercise such other powers and discharge such other functions and responsibilities as
are necessary, appropriate, or incidental to efficient and effective provisions of the basic
seruices and facilities ;
WHEREAS, SECTION 18 of the same law allows Local Government units to
uire, develop, Iease, encumber, alienate, or otherwise dispose of real or personal
ropefi held by them in their proprietary capacity and to apply their resources and
for productive, developmental, or welfare purposes, in the exercise or
furtherance of their governmental or proprietary powers and functions and thereby
ensure their development into self-reliant communities and active pafticipants in the
attainment of national goals,
4/1/>

WHEREAS, pursuant to the above mandates of the law, the FIRST PARTY
engages in cooperative arrangements with law enforcement agencies, component
barangays, national offices and other branches and instrumentalities of the government
for a more effective and efficient delivery of basic services and the attainment of
governmental objectives in promoting the general welfare;
WHEREAS, under these arrangements, the FIRST PARTY extends assistance to
al! these law enforcement agencies, component barangays, national offices and other
branches and instrumentalities of the government by, among others, allowing them to
use tools, equipment and other government propefties owned by the former, thereby
facilitating actua! transfer of possession, custody and use of these properties by the
latter;
WHEREAS, Presidential Decree No. 1445 decrees that every officer of any
government agency whose duties permit or require the possession or custody of
government funds or property shall be accountable therefore and for the safekeeping
thereof in conformity with law. Every officer accountable for government propefi shall
be liable for its money value in case of improper or unauthorized use or misapplication
thereof, by himself or any person for whose acts he may be responsible. He shall
likewise, be liable for all losses, damages, or deterioration envisioned by negligence in
the keeping or use of the propefi whether or not it be at the time in his actual
custody.
WHEREAS, there is a need to properly identiff officers and officials primarily
and directly responsible and accountable for all these government propefties lend to
and allowed to be used by these law enforcement agencies, component barangays,
national offices and other branches and instrumentalities of the government.
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the above premises, their
mutual consent herein set forth, the parties have agreed to the following terms and
conditions:

FIRST PARTY, the possession and custody of which is transferred to and for the
temporary use of the SECOND PARTY.
