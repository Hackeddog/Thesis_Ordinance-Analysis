WHEREAS, Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991,
mandates the Local Government Units (LGUs) to provide social welfare services which include programs
and projects for rebel returnees;
WHEREAS, the Department of the lnterior and Local Government (DILG) is mandated to
strengthen localgovernment unit's capability to effectively discharge their powers and functions towards
improving the delivery of basic services and facilities to their constituents;
WHEREAS, SECTION 1 of the said A.O. states that the E-CLIP is a program for individuals who have
decided to abandon armed struggle and return to the fold of the law and mainstream the society. lt is a
peace building and social protection program that provides interventions for Former New People's Army
(NPA) Rebels (FRs) and Members of Militia ng Bayan (MB) in becoming productive citizens of society;
WHEREAS, item Vll (B)of the DILG-DND Joint Memorandum Circular No. 2018{l dated July 5,
2018, entitled: "lmplementing Guidelines for the provision of DILG-Administered Package of Assistance
for Former Rebels (FRs) and Militia ng Bayan (MB)", provides that LGU shall be allocated with Financial
Assistance from the E-CLIP for the construction of Halfway House, a facility for FR that will: 1) House the
FR while waiting his/her enrolment to the program; and 2) Serve as a venue in conducting rehabilitation
and healing sessions, and education and skills training activities;
WHEREAS, the City Government of Davao, has been assessed and qualified on the eligibility
requirement criteria in the selection and prioritization, and the procedural requirements for assessing the
f

fund thereby entitles the LGU to receive the Financial Assistance for the construction of the Halfway
House in the amount of FIVE MILLION PESOS (PHP 5,000,000.@);

stipulated thereinafter set forth, the PARTIES hereto agree to enter into this Memorandum of Agreement
to adhere to the following terms and conditions;
The City Government of Davao and DILG-Regional Office shall agree to strictly abide to the
provisions of:
1. Republic Act No. 9184, known as Government Procurement Reform Act and its implementing
Rules and Regulation specifically on the Bidding Process;
2. COA Rules and Regulations No.94{13 dated December 13, 1994 "Rules and Regulations in the
Grant, Utilization and Liquidation of Funds Transferred to lmplementing Agencies", and all other
existing COA Circulars shallbe observed;

Construction of Halfway House for Former Rebels (FRs) and Militia ng Bayan (MB).
