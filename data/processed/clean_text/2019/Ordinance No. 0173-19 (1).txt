DEED OF DONATION
KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:
This Deed executed at Davao City, Philippines, this _
day of January 2020 by and
between:
J.V. ANGELES CONSTRUCTION CORPORATION, a corporation duly established and
existing under the laws of the Republic of the Philippines with principal office located at No.
1 E. Rodriguez Jr. Avenue, Pasig City, herein represented by its Chief Executive Officer, Ms.
KATHRYN PATRICIA V. ANGELES, hereinafter called the DONOR;
and
CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a government agency duly organized and existing under
the laws of the Republic of the Philippines with office address at San Pedro Street, Davao
City herein represented by the Honorable SARA Z. DUTERTE, City Mayor, hereinafter
referred to as the DONEE;
WITNESSETH:
That the DONOR is the Engineering, Procurement and Civil Works (EPC) contractor for the
Davao City Bulk Water Supply Project (DCBWSP), a joint venture undertaking of the Davao
City Water District (DCWD) and the Apo Agua Infrastructura, Inc. (AAII);
That as EPC contractor, the DONOR has taken the initiative to include Davao City as among
the beneficiaries of its Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) activities;
That one of the identified major CSR activities is to support the City's development programs
especially for education and sustainable environmental concerns;
That to attain this program, the DONOR hereby donates twenty five (25) laptop sets for the
implementation of such programs to preferred institutions or organizations of the City
Government;
That the DONEE hereby accepts the said donation and hereby expresses its gratitude to the
DONOR and further extends an invitation to witness the turn-over of the laptops to the
selected recipients.
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties hereto have signed these presents, at the place on
the date abovementioned.
