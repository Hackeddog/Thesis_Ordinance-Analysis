WITNESSETH:
WHEREAS, the State recognizes its mandate to provide equitable and accessible quality
healthcare for all Filipinos;
WHEREAS, the Health Facility Enhancement Program (HFEP) is one of the major pillars
of "Kalusugan Pangkalahatan" ;
WHEREAS, "Kalusugan Pangkalahatan" envisions quality health care to be more
accessible to the poor and vulnerable population by ensuring the availability of functional
static and mobile health facilities, including mobile dental clinics;
WIIEREAS, studies have shown that the ratio of government dentist to population is one
(1) dentist for every 48,000 to 50,000, and that the prevalence ofdental diseases continue
to be high starting at childhood;
WHEREAS, to address the lack of dentists who will serve the dental health needs of all
the municipalities and cities in the country, the DOH is investing in the acquisition of
mobile dental clinics, including equipment, to cover the unserved communities;
WHEREAS, in CY 2015, the DOH has committed to provide mobile dental clinics,
including equipment, as an initial response to improve dental health care coverage in the
country;
WHEREAS, the City Government of Davao plays a critical role in ensuring administrative
support to augment public dental health services in the country; and
WIIEREAS, the City Government of Davao shall provide the necessary health human
resource and logistics support for the operation and maintenance of the mobile dental
a
clinic.
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises and subject to the
conditions stipulated hereunder, the Department of Health hereby donates to the City
Government of Davao one (l) unit of mobile dental clinic, including equipment.

The Donee undertakes to

days from receipt of the Notice of Revocation to transfer the possession of the mobile
dental clinic, including all its accessories and the equipment, to the Donor.
