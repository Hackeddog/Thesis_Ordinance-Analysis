The DONEE accepts the donation and undertakes to:
l. Ensure that the ambulance is properly classified and recorded in the books of assets of
the City of Davao or recipient health facility;
2. Ensure that within six (6) months upon receipt of the ambulances, said ambulances are
licensed in its name and at its own expense, in accordance with the DOH
Administrative Order No. 2018-0001 regarding the Rules and Regulations Governing
the Licensure of Land Ambulances and Ambulance Service Providers;
3. Ensure the availability of health personnel, supplies and other operational expenses
necessary for the continuous operation and maintenance of the ambulance and the
equipment therein;
4. Ensure that the ambulances are utilized in accordance with the definition and standards
provided in the abovementioned DOH Administrative Order;
5. Ensure the rational use of the ambulance within the city or locality to maximize their
benefit to the residents needing ambulance service;
6. Apply for the renewal of the ambulance vehicle registration with the Land
Transportation Office (LTO) after three years (3) using its own resources. The
DONEE may transfer the vehicle registration under its name; and
7. Designate the City Health Offrcer as the focal person who will be coordinating with
the DOH DCHD for purposes of program monitoring and evaluation.
The DONOR reserves the right to revoke this Deed of Donation in case any of the aforesaid
conditions are not faithfully complied with by the DONEE. In case this Deed of Donation is
revoked, the DONEE shall surrender the possession of the ambulances, including all of their
accessories and equipment, to the DONOR within fifteen (15) working days from receipt of
the Notice of Revocation. Ownership of the said ambulance and equipment shall then vest to
the DONOR effective upon receipt by the DONEE of the Notice of Revocation.
