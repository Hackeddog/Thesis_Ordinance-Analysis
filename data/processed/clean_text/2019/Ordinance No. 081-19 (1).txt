AR,TICLE I - NAME, LOCATtrON AND OWNERSHIP
1- The facility shall be known as the "Museo Dabawenyo - The National Museum
of the Philippines in Davao City- (hereafter to be referred as the ..MuseoDabawenyar" for brevity).
The retention of the name "Museo Dabawenyo" is in recognition of the initiatives
of the City of Davao to ensure the preservation of Davao's history, culture, and
the afts through the collection of pertinent cultural and historical aftifacts while
the name of the National Museum is included in order to signiff that it is the
National Museum of the Philippines' regional museum extension office in Davao
City;
2. The Museo Dabawenyo shall be situated in the newly-constructed multi-purpose
building within the premises of people's park, palma Gil st., Davao city.
ARTICLE II . RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIOilS OF THE PARTIES
1. Obligations of the NATIONAL MUSEUM:
a. As the regional museum of the National Museum of the Philippines in Davao City,
the expenses for the operations and activities of the Museo Dabawenyo shall be
included by the NATIONAL MUSEUM in its annual budget from the general
appropriations of the National Government;
b. The NATIONAL MUSEUM shall perform all its mandate for the establishment of
regional museums under RA. No. 11333, including but not limited to disseminating
technical and museological skills to support the development of the Museo
Dabawenyo and extending technical assistance in the preservation and restoration
of cultural properties of national significance, among others;
2. Obligations of the CITY OF DAVAO:
a. To provide the initial infrastructure facility of the Museo Dabawenyo that conform
to the standards set forth by the NATIONAT MUSEUM for its regional museums
across the country, and for this particular purpose, secure the approval of the
NATIONAL MUSEUM Director-General in all final drawings of architectural,
interior design and building systems as part of museum requirements, such
approval being the confirmation of conformity with the aforesaid standards. A
Deed of Usufruct covering the property to be provided as the initial infrastructure
facility shall be executed by and between the parties, which Deed and all its
Annexes shall form an integral part of this Memorandum of Agreement;
b. To further amend Ordinance No. 0265-06, SERIES of 2006, as amended, in order
to declare the Museo Dabawenyo as the regional museum of the National Museum
of the Philippines in Davao City, to change the name of the Museo Dabawenyo
into" Museo Dabawenyo - The National Museum of the Philippines in Davao City,to
include the NATIONAL MUSET M in the Museo Dabawenyo Advisory Board, and
to make all other necessary changes as a result thereof.2
