KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:
This agreement made and entered by and between:
The CITY GOYERNMENT OF DAVAO, located at Davao City Hall Drive, Davao City,
represented herein by Davao City Mayor SARA Z. DUTERTE, herein after referred to as
FIRST PARTY;
and
THE RIZAL N{EMORIAL COLLEGES, INC., a corporation existing under the Philippine
laws, with office address at RMC Buildings, Poblacion 7-A at F. Torres and Lopez-Jaena
Streets, Marfori Heights represented by its Vice President for Administration, ATTY.
ERNESTO RAPHAEL V. ROBILLO , hereinafter referred to as the SECOND PARTY;
WITNESSESTII
WHEREAS, the first party supervises the operations of the CO SUN GIAN HOME
FOR THE AGED located in Cabantian, Buhangin, Davao City, the institution under the City
Social Services and Development Office that provides temporary shelter for the less fortunate
elderly with special needs.
WIIEREAS, the second party desires to send and expose its students from the
Psychology Program to the CO SUN GIAN HOME FOR THE AGED for the intemship
training in compliance with the requirements of the course.
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the above premises and their mutual
covenants hereinafter set forth, the parties have agleed as follows:
TERMS OF REFERENCE (TOR)
The parties hereby bind themselves to undertake a Memorandum of Agreement for the
purpose of intemship program/practicum where students put into actual practice the
knowledge and skills learned inside the classroom. The intemship shall cover a period of
fifteen days (15) working days consisting of six (6) hours dMy, a total of ninety (90)
hours per student for the entire duration of the affiliation program.
ACCOUNTABILITY
A. Responsibilities of the First Party
a. Accept affiliates of the second party Fourth Year Students of Bachelor of Science
in Psychology and allied courses to undergo the On-the-Job Training/Practicum or
Internship Program.
b. Ensure the availability of the area/oflice or department assignment.
c. Provide competent cooperating site supervisor who will guide, direct and orient
the Second Party Psychology students on the institution's policies, rules and
regulations and who shall exercise supervision over the lntern Affiliation and
evaluate the performance of the practicum interns.
d. Provide safety and security to the Second Party within the premises of the First
Party.
e. Issue certificate of training completion at the end of the internship to the students
g**ve
satisfactorily complied with all the requirements under the program.
I

B. Responsibilities of the Second Party
a. Provide a Training Curriculum to the First Party with a list of the students who
will undergo internship, schedule of activities, rotation plan and other necessary
forms to be used by the cooperating supervisors in evaluating the practicum
intems.
b. Provide a person-in charge of the practicum intems who shall be the contact
person between the first party and the second party.
c. The Coordinator shall regularly visit the center to monitor, evaluate and supervise
the activities and training of the student interns.
2. DURATION OF AGREEMENT
The agreement will commence upon signing by both parties on November l, 2018 and
will expire on May 31, 2022 renewable upon the recommendation of both parties, unless
terminated by one of the parties for just cause of termination, within 30 days from notice to the
other parties.
