Afticle I.
TITLE OF THE ORDINANCE
SECTION l.Shoft Title. This Ordinance shall be known as the "Davao City
Comprehensive STI, HIV and AIDS Ordinance of 2013".
Article II:
DECLARATION OF POLICY
SECTION 1. Declaration of Policv. The Sexually Transmitted Infection, Human
Immunodeficiency Virus and Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (STI, HIV and AIDS) are
public health concerns that have wide ranging social, political and economic repercussions. As
such, responding to the STI, HIV and AIDS epidemic is imbued with public interest, and thus
the City Government of Davao commits itself to the fight against the epidemic. The City
Government of Davao commits to make actions to (1.) prevent the spread of HIV and other
STIs and mitigate any related social, psychological, and economic consequences to fight
against the epidemic; (2.) To prevent the spread of the virus and mitigate any related social,
psychological, and economic consequences, as follows:
1. The City Government of Davao shall establish policies and programs to prevent the
spread of STI, HIV and AIDS and deliver treatment, care and support seruices to
persons living with HIV in accordance with evidence-based strategies and approaches
that follow the principles of human rights, gender equality, and meaningful
pafticipation of communities affected by the epidemic;
2. The City Government of Davao shall adopt a multi-sectoral and culturally-sensitive
approach in responding to the STI, HIV and AIDS epidemic by ensuring that local
communities, civil society groups and persons living with HIV and their families, are
part of the response to the epidemic;
3. The City Government of Davao shall remove all barriers to STI, HIV and AlDS-related
services and shall eliminate the climate of stigma that surrounds the epidemic and the
people directly and indirectly affected by it; and
4. The City Government of Davao shall consciously adhere to generally-accepted
principles of human rights and adopt gender perspectives in dealing with STI, HIV and
AIDS as it shall even promote value formation and employ scientifically-proven
approaches. The information and education campaigns shall be carried out in all
schools, training centers, workplaces and communities.
Therefore it affirms that:
1. The protection, promotion and fulfillment of human rights as the cornerstone of an
effective response to the epidemic;
2. The meaningful inclusion and pafticipation of persons directly and indirectly affected by
the epidemic, especially persons living with HIV is crucial in eliminating the virus;
3. Unless otherwise provided in this Ordinance, the confidentiality, anonymity and noncompulsory nature of HIV testing and HlV-related testing shall always be guaranteed
and protected by the City Government of Davao; and
4. Policies and practices that discriminate on the basis of perceived or actual HIV status,
sex, gender, sexual orientation, gender identity, age, economic status, ethnicity, and
disability are inimical to national interest and contrary to the rights and freedoms
guaranteed in the Constitution, and the City Government of Davao shall eliminate these
acts of discrimination.
