Ord. No. 0701-19
Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled that:
SECTION 1. TITLE- This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND
SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE
RENEWAL OF THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO
BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND TECARRO COLLEGE
FOUNDATION, INC., RELATM TO THE EXPOSURE AND TRAINING OF ITS
CAREGIVING AND HEALTHCARE SERVICES STUDENTS AT CO SU GIAN HOME FOR
THE AGED'.
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - SECTION 455, (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act
No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides, to wit:
"For efficient, effective and economical governance
the purpose of which is the general welfare of the
city and its inhabitants pursuant to SECTION 16 of
this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in
all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all
bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other
documents upon authority of the SANGGUNIANG
panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance;
SECTION 3. AUTHORIW - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority
to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the renewal of the Memorandum
of Agreement (MOA) to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and
Tecarro Collage Foundation Inc., relative to the exposure and training of its Healthcare
Seruices students at Co Su Gian Home for the Aged;
SECTION 4. SEPARABILIW CLAUSE- If, for any reason, any SECTION or provision
of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof
not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.
SECTION 5.
approval.

ENACTED, on the 19th day of February, 2O!9, by a unanimous vote of all the
Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:
This agreement made and entered into by and between:
The City Government of Davao with principal address at Davao City Hall Drive, Davao
City, represent herein by City Mayor SARA Z. DUTERTE herein after referred as FIRST
PARTY;
.AND.
TECARRO COLLEGE FOUNDATION, INC. a corporation duly organized and
existing under and by the virtue of the Laws ofthe Philippines, with principal address at Guillermo
E. Torres St (formerly Sandawa Road), New Matina, Davao City, Philippines, represented by its
OIC School Administrator MR. BERNARDO R. CUENZA, herein after referred to as SECOI\ID
PARTY.
WITNESSESTH:
WHEREAS, the first party supervises the operations Health Care Referral Center, also
known as CO SU GIAN CENTER FOR THE ELDERLY, which facilities meets the standards
for training agency, registered and accredited by the Department of Social Welfare and
Development.
WHEREAS: the first party offers its facilities to the second party for the affrliation of
Caregiving and Healthcare Services Students.
WHEREAS: the second party operates as Educational Institution, also known as
TECARRO COLLEGE FOUNDATION, INC.
NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of forgoing premises, the covenants
terms and condition hereunder set-forth, the parties have agreed as follows to wit:
l. The first party allows the use of its facility for affrliation and training of Caregiving and
Healthcare Services Students NCII subjects to its capacity to determine by the same.
2. The first party allows the student to assist the center coordinator in doing different
procedure, as follows:
2.1 taking the vital signs (BP, Temperature, pulse, respiration and heart rate);
2.2 assisting patient in doing their personal hygiene, such as shampoo in bed, oral care,
perineal care and sponge bathing;
2.3 lifting and maneuvering/ positioning the patient;
2.4 after care of hospital equipment and instruments, like washing, sterilizing, and some
simple housekeeping like dusting, or cleaning the area assigned;
2.5 bathing and feeding elderly
2.6 Participate in other nursing function such as listening to endorsement and doing rounds
with the staff; and
2.7 Other delegated nursing responsibilities that are non-invasive but with the supervision
of a registered nurse or Clinical Instructor.
)
{

3. The first party provides orientation to the students on affrliation regarding First Party
Vision, Mission, Goals, Policies and Rules.
4. That the FIRST PARTY, provide feedback reports to the Second Party regarding specifics
behaviors and conduct of students on affiliation, by submitting to the Second Party the
STUDENT EVALUATION made by the supervising staff, after their completion of duty.
5. That the second parly will provide a Clinical Instructor to monitor, organize and supervise
the student for whole duration of training.
6. That the Second Party will be responsible for arranging the duty schedule for 8 hours
shifting per day of each student until the required 80 hours for Caregiving and Healthcare
Services will be completed.
7 . That the second party shall coordinate schedule of afliliation of students with the first party.
Students must attend the orientation conducted by the CO SU GIAN CENTER FOR THE
ELDERLY.
8. The second parfy agreed with the head of the first party; specific expectations for the
affrliation of program, specially the stipulation in sub-paragraph 2 of this agreement.
9. That the second party agreed with the first party that the student trainee should not be
allowed to go out to the premises, except upon request and permission and approval of any
authorized personnel or officer.
10. The second party agreed with the first party that the student trainee is required to strictly
observe to wear Proper student duty uniform and school ID while on practicum. Submits
documents such as Endorsement letter, Memorandum of agreement and waiver of Claim,
to the first party.
second party agreed that the student trainee shall replace any institution property which
has
damaged or destroyed within the duration of their duty.
12. The second party shall require the student to undergo physical and laboratory examination
before the training such as: CBC, Urinalysis, Fecalysis, Chest X-Ray, and Hepa B
Screening (surface antigen and antibody), if Hepatitis B antibody has negative result the
student must submit a medical record/certification of Hepa B Vaccination. All examination
results shall be submitted in their photocopy form to the first party for health clearance
purposes.
13. That the first party shall require the student-trainees on duty to fill-up and sign the offrcial
logbook for attendance and record purposes.
14. This agreement shall take effect within two (2) years and shall commence of March 2018
to March 2020.
15. Violation on the part ofthe Second Party of any provision ofthis agreement shall authorize
the first party to rescind this agreement, even without prior demand or court order.
