Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE- This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR AND IN
BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF
AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE DEPARTMENT OF
HEALTH-DAVAO CENTER FOR HEALTH DEVELOPMENT (DOH DAVAO CHD) AND
THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO WHEREIN THE FORMER SHALL PROVIDE
FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE TO THE LATTER AS PERFORMANCE.BASED GRANT
TO SUSTAIN AND MAINTAIN THE STATUS AS FILARIASIS-FREE CITY'.
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - SECTION 11, Article XIII of the 1987
Constitution, provides that the state shall adopt an integrated and comprehensive
approach to health development which shall endeavor to make essential goods, health
and other social seruices available to all the people at affordable cost.
SECTION 3. AUTHORIW - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into
and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of
Agreement to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and the
Depaftment of Health-Davao Center for Health Development (DOH DAVAO CHD) relative
to the financial assistance in the amount of One Million Pesos (P1,000,000.00) as
performance based grant as Filariasis-Free City;
SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE- If, for any reason, any SECTION or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and
effect.

WITNESSETH:
WHEREAS, Filariasis is one of the major parasitic infectious diseases endemic in 40
provinces with scattered distribution in the country;
WHEREAS, after a SERIES of Mass Drug Administration, the LGU was registered as having
an insignificant filariasis infection among the residents based on the 2018 blood examination
survey among new elementary school entrants. Thus, the World Health Organization
recommended the LGU for a declaration of a Filariasis-Free City;
WHEREAS, in line with the management's trust of declaring diseases-free zones, through
intensive and aggressive disease control program approaches, the DOH Davao CHD is providing
financial assistance to the LGU, as a performance-based grant, to be used for mobilization
activities in order to sustain and maintain the status as a Filariasis-Free City, in the amount of
One Million Pesos (P1,000,000.00) per SAA No. 2OL9-06-0205 dated June 6, 2OL9.
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises and subject to the
terms and condition herein stipulated, the parties to this agreement hereby stipulate as
follows:

1.1
This agreement covers utilization of funds for the National Filariasis Elimination Program
activities to prevent any emergence or re-emergence of filariasis case in the City of
Davao from the Public Health Management Line ltem RA tt261 GAA 2019;
