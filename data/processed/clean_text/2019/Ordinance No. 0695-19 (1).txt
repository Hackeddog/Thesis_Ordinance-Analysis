Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Palungsod of Davao City in session

SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
FAVORABLY APPROVING/ADOPTING THE DAVAO CITY DTSASTER RrSK
REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT FUND INVESTMENT P]AN (DCDRRMFIP) FOR

HUNDRED THIRTY.Sil MILLION ONE HUNDRED NINE THOUSAND TWO
HUNDRED FIFTY-EIGHT PESOS (p 436,109,258.00)".
SECTION 2. DECTARATION OF POLICY - SECTION 2, paragraph (d) of Republic
Act No. 10121, provides, to wit:
(d) Adopt a disaster risk reduction and management approach that is holistic,
comprehensive, integrated, and proactive in lessening the socio-economic and
environmental impacts of disasters including climate change and promote the involvement
and pafticipation of all sectors and all stakeholders concerned at all levels, especially the
local community.
SECTION 3. APPROVAL OF THE INVESTMENT PIAN . THE DAVAO CITY
DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT FUND INVESTMENT PI.Ail
(DCDRRMFTP) FOR CY 2019, AS PREPARED AND APPROVED By THE DAVAO
CITY DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT COUNCIL, shall be
approved for the purpose of developing an all hazards planning approach that will be
used for all threats to and/or emergencies or disasters and funding identified pQects
proposed by various concerned depaftments to establish responsiveness of the City
Government to lessen the impact of disasters in Davao City.
SECTION 4. BUDGETARY REOUIREMENT. - THE TOTAT AMOUNT OF FOUR
HUNDRED THIRTY-SIX MIIIION ONE HUNDRED NINE THOUSAND TWO
HUNDRED FIFTY-EIGHT PESOS (P436,109,258.00), is hereby appropriated to fund
the LOCAL DISASTER RISK REDUCTION AND MANAGEMENT FUND INVESTMENT PI.AN
(LDRRMFIP) FOR CY 2019 (hereto attached as ANNEX *A').
SECTION 5. IEGAL BASIS. Pursuant to SECTION 458, par. (1) (vi) of the Local
Government Code of 1991, it empowers the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod to adopt measures
to protect the inhabitants of the City from the harmful effects of man-made or natural
disasters and calamities, and to provide relief services and assistance for victims during
and in the aftermath of said disasters or calamities and their return to productive
livelihood following said events.
SECTION 6. USE OF FUNDS. The amount herein appropriated shall be used
specifically for such items and expenditures approved by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod. All
disbursements and utilization of funds shall be subject to the existing government
budgeting, accounting and auditing rules and regulations of the Depaftment of Budget
and Management (DBM), the Commission of Audit (COA), the Procurement Law (RA
9184), as well as other applicable !aws, Ordinances and Presidential directives.
SECTION 7. EEEElgffi. This Ordinance shall take effect upon approval.
