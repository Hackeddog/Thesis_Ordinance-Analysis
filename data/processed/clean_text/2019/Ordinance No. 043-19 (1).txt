The City Government of Davao, Republic of the Philippines and the City
Government of Zamboanga, Republic of the Philippines herein to referred collectively as
"the Palties";
ANTECEDENTS
1. The City Government of Davao and the City Government of Zamboanga are
always seeking for potential local partners for development and mutual
cooperation;
2. On April 9, 20t5, Zamboanga City Mayor, Ma. Isabelle G. Climaco and former
Davao City Mayor, Rodrigo R. Duterte, signed a pledge toward forging Sister
City ties between Davao City and Zamboanga City;
3. On February 19, 2018, the City Government of Davao received a letter from
Mayor Ma. Isabelle Climaco inquiring as to the status of the sister city proposal;
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises and their
mutual covenant hereinafter set forth, the pafties hereby agree on the following
provisions:
Afticle 1. Objective of the Sister City Agreement
The objective of the Sister City Agreement is to establish a Sister City Relationship to
promote mutually beneficial cooperation and deeper understanding between the parties,
through an exchange of ideas in the fields identified in Afticle 2.
Afticle 2. Cooperation
Davao City and Zamboanga City intend to create and fortify an open communicative
atmosphere to induce a heightened exchange of ideas, particularly in the following fields:
1.) Tourism and Culture:
Since the Pafties gather a vast number of tourists and highly values their respective
ritages, it is deemed favorable to exchange tourism practices and
cultural he
.t

cultural preseruation techniques. The pafties shall share their methods used to
attract and maintain tourist interest, including the promotion and preseruation of
their own respective cultures. The Parties will promote their respective Sister City,
to each Parties mutual benefit, and suppoft easy access to each city.
2.) Agriculture:
Given that both Davao City and Zamboanga City consider agriculture as one of
their major industries, both parties will endeavour to shared and exchange ideas
on agricultural enhancement, systems, and programs. The parties may provide, if
within the capacity of the Parties, an exchange in the necessary machines, crops,
and products that are lacking within their paftnered Sister City.
3.)Trade and Commerce:
Both parties will maintain open communication in terms of potential business
ventures, investment, and product trade.
The Parties may broaden the communication fields and make concefted effofts to
promote best practices and exchanges in other fields that are beneficial to both
Pafties.
Afticle 3. Fufther Agreements
It is understood that this MOA shall serve as a preliminary agreement of the Parties, and
that the activities being contemplated herein, as well as the performance by the Pafties
of this undertaking, or in fuftherance of this MOA, shall be subject to more definitive
agreements.
Afticle 4. Amendments and Approvals
No amendment or supplement to thls MOA, and no approval or consent constituting an
amendment, shall be effective for any purpose unless made in writing and signed by a
duly authorized officer of both Parties.
Afticle 5. Effectivity, Validity and Renewal
This MOA shall be effective and valid for five (5) years from the date of signing hereof
and shall be considered extended and/or renewed for another five (5) years if no
objection in writing from either side is serued upon the other within thirty (30) days of
f6b expiration of the initial five (5) year term.
