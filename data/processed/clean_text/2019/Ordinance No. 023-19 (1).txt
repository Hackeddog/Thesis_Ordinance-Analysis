ORDINANCE NO. 023.19
SERIES of 2019
AN ORDINANCE FOR THE ADOPTION OF DAVAO CITY
FOREST LAND USE PLAN 2019-2023 TO BE INTEGRATED
TN THE CITY LAND USE PLAN, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Ord. No. 023-19
Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE- This Ordinance shall be known as "ADOPTION OF
FOREST LAND USE PLAN OF DAVAO CIW'.
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - The City Government of Davao
hereby declares the following as its policies:
1. The Local Government shall protect and advance the right of all
Dabawenyos to a balanced and healthful ecology in accord with
the rhythm and harmony of nature;
2. The Local Government shall promote conservation of our forest
lands in pursuing sustainable urban development, as a crucial
component to the aspirations of a livable city not only for the
Dabawenyos but also for the generations to come;
3. Enhancement and protection of forest lands must be established
and maintained through a pafticipative, empowered and
environmentally conscious community;
4. The Local Government recognizes the important role of the
community in the protection and conservation of natural
resources, as well as in promoting and instilling the awareness
on forest conservation and protection;
5. The Local Government recognizes the need to a balance
economic growth with the protection of forest lands for the
attainment of sustainable growth; and
6. The Local Government recognizes the need for a legislation to
ensure the protection of our forest lands by adopting the Forest
Land Use Plan as part of the Land Use plan of oavao city.
SECTION 3: DEFINITION OF TERMS - As used in this Ordinance, the
following terms shall mean:
1. Forest !and- an area of land covered by forests
2. Forest- land with an area of more than 0.5 hectare and tree
crown cover (area covered by the living branches and foliage of
trees) of more than 10 percent.
3. watershed- an area of rand that separates waters flowing to
different rivers and basins.
4. Agroforestry - a land use management system in which trees
or shrubs are grown around or among crops or pastureland,

Ord. No. 023-19
SECTION 4. SCOPE AND APPLICATION - This Ordinance shall cover all
forestland areas as well as watersheds within the territorial jurisdiction of the City of
ftavao and covers the planning period 2019-2024;
SECTION 5. FOREST LAND USE PLAN - For purposes of this Ordinance the
Forest Land Use Plan 2019-2023 crafted by the City Development Council of Davao City
and the Depaftment of Environment and Natural Resources is adopted and integrated in
the Comprehensive Land Use Plan of Davao City.
SECTION 6. REPEALING CLAUSE- All Ordinances, as well as peftinent rules
and regulations inconsistent with this Code, are hereby repealed and amended
accordingly.
SECTION 7. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE-In the event that ay word, afticle,
sentence, clause or paragraph of this Code is declared invalid for any reason, other
provisions thereof shall not be affected by such declaration and shall continue to be in
full force and effect.
SECTION 8. EFFECTIVITY- This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after completion of its publication in a local newspaper of general circulation in Davao
City.
