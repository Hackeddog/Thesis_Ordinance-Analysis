Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled
that:
SECTION 1. TfTLE - This Ordinance shall be known as *AN ORDINANCE

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - It is hereby declared the policy of
the State to promote the rights of children for suruival, development and special
protection with full recognition of the nature of childhood as well as the need to provide
developmentally appropriate experiences to address their needs and to support parents
in their roles as primary caregivers and as their children's first teachers;
Fufther, the State hereby recognizes the age of children from zero (0) to eight
(8) years as the first crucial stage of educational development of which the age from
zero (0) to four (4) years shall be the responsibility of the Early Childhood Care and
Development (ECCD) Council;
Thus, this Ordinance seeks to promote quality early childhood education in Private
Child Development Centers/Learning Centers offiering services to young children 0 to 4
years old in order to (i) guide the ongoing development and improvement of centerbased early childhood programs; (ii) encourage self-monitoring and regular evaluation
of the centers by their staff and paftners; and (iii) serve as reference in the assessment
of the Centers for the granting of permit and recognition by local government authorities
and the ECCD Council.
SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS:
Early Childhood Care & Development Program - refers to the various
seruices of health and sanitation, nutrition, early education, child protection, social
services, and human development that may be conducted as center-based programs or
home-based programs by various ECCD seruice providers which include:
a. Center-based Programs - refer to the programs such as the day
care seruice established under Republic Act No. 6972, otherwise known
as the "Barangay Level Total Development Protection of Children Act"
and herein after referred as the child development seruice, community
or church-based early childhood development education programs
initiated by non-government organizations or people's organization,
workplace-related childcare and education programs, child minding
centers, and health centers and stations;
b. Home-based Programs - refer to programs which utilize parents as
the primary seruice providers for ECCD such as the neighborhood-based
play groups, family child care program, parent education, and home
visiting program;
Child Development Center/Learning Center - refers to an early
learning center, public or private, that offers early education and/or child
care program for children age zero to four who are taught by Child
Development Teacher/s or Worker/s;

SECTION 5. REGISTRATION - All Private CDCs/LCs shall be required to
register with the Office of the City Mayor through the City Social Seruices and
Development Office six months before it can offer early childhood programs for children
04 years old.

SECTION 6. PERMIT TO OPERATE - All Private CDCs/LG shall also secure a
permit to operate during the period of its registration or six months prior to its operation
from the City Mayor through the City Social Services and Development Officer;
The application and inspection fee in the amount of Two Hundred Fifty Pesos
(P250.00) shall thereafter be paid to the City Treasure/s Office. The application and
inspection fee may be increased subject however to a prior notice to the public of the
newly imposed fee.
SECTION 7. IISPEGIIS - All Private CDCs/LCs shall be subject to a
visit/inspection by the City Social Seruices and Development Officer to determine
whether there is faithful compliance with all the requirements laid down;
The visiVinspection shall be conducted within a period of thifi (30) days after
the application for permit to operate is filed.
SECTION 8. CERTIFICATE TO OPERATE - When the applicant has complied
with allthe requirements, a Certificate to Operate shall thereafter be given to all qualified
Private CDCs/LCs which shall be valid for three (3) years;
If the applicant is unable to comply with all the said requirements, a Letter to
Comply the Requirements shal! be serued to unqualified Private CDCs/LCs with the
Repoft of Inspection attached thereto. The Center thereafter shall have a period of five
(5) months to one (1) year to comply with all the lacking requirements. However, they
may still be given a temporary Permit to Operate valid for one (1) year only and a
recommendation for closure if failure to comply with the requirements persist.
SECTION 9. PROCESS OF RECOGNITION - A Letter of Intent for Recognition
addressed to the City Mayor through the CiW Social Seruices and Development Officer
shall be submitted by the Administrator/Principal/Director of a CDC/LC seeking for
recognition;
An Internal Assessment shall thereafter be made followed by an External
Assessment. If CDC/LC complies with the required score, the Evaluator shall recommend
to the ECCD Counci! through the DSWD Regional Office for the issuance of a Conferment
of Recognition (Level), one week after the assessment;
A Conferment of Recognition Level 1 (satisfactory rating) valid for three (3) years
shall be issued if CDC/LC has demonstrated mandatory compliance with the Standards
and Guidelines showing quality seruice implementation. A Conferment of Recognition
Level 2 (very satisfactory rating) valid for four (4) years shall be issued if CDC/LC has
demonstrated optimal compliance with the Standards and Guidelines that increases the
effectiveness of quality seruice implementation. A Conferment of Recognition Level 3
(outstanding rating) valid for five (5) years shall be issued if CDC/LC has demonstrated
the highest compliance with the Standards and Guidelines that makes CDC/LC a center
of excellence;
The CDC/LC that has been granted recognition is encouraged to maintain its
status. When the number of years for its recognition has lapsed, CDC/LC has to file
again a Letter of Intent for Recognition.
SECTION 10. GUIDELINES - The Guidelines on the Registration and Granting
of Permit and Recognition to Public and Private Child Development Centers/Learning
Centers Offering Early Childhood Programs for all 0 to 4 Years Old Children of the ECCD
Counci! shall be followed by the local authorities in granting registration, permit to
operate and recognition.

SECTION 11. PEilALW - All private CDCs/LCs shall comply with the
requirements of registration and permit to operate befure it can offer early childhood
programs to the public. Any private CDC/LC who shall operate without the required
registration and permit to operate shall be immediately closed by the Business Bureau
within five (5) days. In case a Private CDC/LC has complied with all the requirements
under this Ordinance after its closure, the Business Bureau shall lift its order of closure
and the Private CDC/LC shall thereafter be allowed to operate. In the event that an
order for closure is issued by the Business Bureau against a CDC/LC and the latter shall
continue to operate, the Private CDC/LC shall be permanently barred from registering
and securing a permit to operate.
SECTION 12. SEPARABILITY CIIUSE - If, for any reason, any SECTION or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and
effect.
SECTION 13. EFFECTMTY-This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days
after its publication.
