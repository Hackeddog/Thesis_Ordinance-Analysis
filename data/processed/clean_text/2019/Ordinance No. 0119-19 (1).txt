KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:
This Memorandum of Agreement made and entered by and between:
The OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT, with office address at Malacaffang Palace,
J.P. Laurel St., San Miguel, Manila, represented herein by the Executive Secretary
SALVADOR C. MEDIALDEA, and hereinafter referred to as the OP;
and
The CITY GOVERNMENT DAVAO, a local government unit organized and
existing under laws of the Republic of the Philippines, with office address at City Hall, San
Pedro St., Davao City, represented by its Mayor, SARA Z. DUTERTE;
yyrrlrEssETH, that:
WHEREAS, the City Government of Davao (the "City Government") constantly
promotes programs that help alleviate the economic and health conditions of its
constituents through making services accessible to less fortunate and indigent members
of the communities;
WHEREAS, among the programs implemented by the City Government in order
to address poverty focuses on the provision of medical assistance and fundamental
needs of recognized and qualified beneficiaries;
WHEREAS, the continuing increase of the public beneficiaries shows the
effectiveness of these programs thereby giving the City Government more opportunity to
advance its implementation and strategy in this aspect for the general welfare of its
people;
WHEREAS, one of these programs is the Lingap Para sa Mahirap Program (the
"Lingap Program") that has, through the years, provided medical assistance and benefits
to indigents;
WHEREAS, considering that the guidelines of the Lingap Program of the City
Govemment of Davao has already been established, and taking cognizance of the
pressing concern vis-a-vis the government's obligation to prioritize the health and wellbeing of the people and in order to extend medical assistance beyond Davao City
constituents, the Office of the President will allocate Fifty Million Pesos (P50,000,000.00)
monthly for the Malasakit Program, to be sourced from the Socio-Civic Project Fund
(scPF);
WHEREAS, the OP and the City Government of Davao have agreed to expand
the coverage of the subject medical assistance to entire Mindanao;
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises and of the
P<a
terms, conditions as they hereby agree and stipulate as follows,,av

A. OBLTGATTONS OF THE OFFTCE OF THE PRESIDENT (OP)
The OP shall allocate the amount of Fifty Million (F50,000,000.00) pesos
monthly for a period of one (1) year, chargeable against the Socio Civic Project
Fund (SCPF) for Malasakit Program.
The OP Office of the Deputy Executive Secretary for Finance and
Administration (ODESFA) shall pay direct to the hospitals, funeral parlors and
other establishments issued by Guarantee Letter upon receipt of billing
statement and supporting documents.
B. OBLIGATTONS OF THE LOCAL GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO CITY (LGUDAVAO C|TY)
1. The LGU-DAVAO CITY shall utilize the funding solely for Malasakit Program in
Mindanao thru issuance of Guarantee Letters. ln no case shall the amount exceed
P50,000.00 net of Philhealth and other assistance for medical and
P30, 000. 00 for funeral.
2. The LGU-DAVAO CITY shall submit to the OP the Quarterly Summary of
Guarantee Letter issued.
