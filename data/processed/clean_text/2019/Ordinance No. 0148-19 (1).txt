Respectfully forwarded to the Office of the City Mayor, through the Office
of the City Administrator, both this City, the subject Ordinance, with the herein
flndings:
It is well-settled rule of statutory construction that statues are to be
considered as having only prospective operations, unless the intendment of the
legislature to give them retroactive effects is expressly declared.
In the case at hand, the ordinance will retroact on the date the 2017
Revenue Code took effect as shown in SECTION 4, to quote:
)SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY-T1e provisions of this Ordinance shall
apply retroactively upon the effectivity of the 2017 Revenue Code of
Davao City.'
Further, its retroactive effect finds support as to the fact that the measure
appears to be curative in nature. It cures the unforeseen effect of market
reclassification in 2005, which was not amended in Revenue Code of 2017 vis-avis the Average Monthly Income in 2005, which was carried over in the Revenue
Code of 2077 as shown in Resolution No. 0554-19, SERIES of 2019.
Viewed therefrom, this office recommends the approval of the subject
ordinance.
