SECTION 4: Implementation of Social Protection Programs
Any social protection project, or activity to implement the purpose and obiective of this agreement
and partnership with legally binding obligations, shall be established through the execution of
SPECIFIC IMPLEMENTATION AGREEMENTS or SlA. Each SIA shall be authorized in writing by
each party's authorized representative.
Once executed, each SIA shall form part of this MOA, as annex.
Programs, proiects, and activities covered by specific SIAs under this agreement shall be subject
to periodic reviews by all parties, focusing on (i) contributions of the PPAs to the achievement of
the objective of poverty reduction; (iiJ lessons and innovations in implementation, and; [iii)
recommendations to enhance succeeding implementation.
SECTION 5: Voluntary Commitment
This Ancestral Domain Memorandum of Agreement is a voluntary commitment between the
parties, and does not obligate the parties to engage solely with the other, nor restrict any activity
of each party to engage in partnership with other groups or agencies for the delivery and/or
implementation of social protection interventions. No party shall have any right power, or
authority to create any obligation, express or implied, for and in behalf of the other party.
SECTION 6: Resources to be prcvided
Each party shall be responsible for allocating and managing resources to cover costs of PPAs to
implement the purpose of this agreement, and to cover activities and matters related to the
fulfilment of each party's responsibilities as stipulated under SECTION 3 above. The specific amount
of resources to be committed by BLGU/ICCs where applicable & system for fund transfer in
accordance with applicable laws and regulations, shall be reflected in the SIA.
SECTION 7: Grievance Redress System
Any complaint relating to the interpretation and implementation of the AD MOA shall be resolved
first in the ICC/IP community using their traditional conflict resolution process.

ARTICLE I. DEFINITION OF TER}IS

at Masaganang Pamayanan Program", is the government's peace and development
framework that aims to strengthen peacebuilding and development in conflict affected
areas (CAAs) using the community driven development (CDD) strategy. The Department
of Social Welfare and Development is one of the agencies implementing the program.
1.2. Sub-Project refers to the community project prioritized and approved by the
assembly as a response to an identified need and/or problem.
1.3. NPMO means National Program Management Office of the Program based at the
Central Office ofthe DSWD, whichassumes full responsibility forthe overall management
of the Program.

Office of the DSWD, which takes responsibility for Program implementation in the
Region.
1.5. SRPMO refers to the Sub-Regional Program Management Office based in the
provinces covered by KC-NCDDP, which is responsible for providing direct assistance to
the ACT.
1.6. ACT refers to the Area Coordinating Team based in the municipalities covered by
KC-NCDDP, which is responsible for the day-to-day operations of the Program.
1.7. DPF refers to Development Planning Forum, which is convened by the recognized
tribal chieftain and composed of representatives from the indigenous cultural communities
(ICCs) and municipal/barangay local government units covered by the ancestral domain.
The DPF develops local criteria, sanctions and ground rules for sub-project selection,
implementation and monitoring.
