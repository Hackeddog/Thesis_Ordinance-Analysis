ANNEX A
CRITERIA FOR BENEFICIARIES
Each operator and driver and/or per household are entitled to only one (1) slot of
Department of Trade and Industry's Livelihood Seeding program.
1. For public utility vehicle operators:
1.a. Must suit the following necessaryqualifications as beneficiary of the
SocialDevelopment Program of the High Priority Bus System project:
l.a.a. Owner of the unit by virtue of franchise and deed of sale.
1.a.b. Registered at Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory
Board Region XI.
1.a.c. At least one (1) year of operator service.
1.b. Must have voluntary chosen the Department of Trade and Industry's
Livelihood Seeding Program as one of the Social Development Packages of
the Social Development Program of the High Priority Bus System Project.
1.c. Must be recommended based on thorough assessment by the Social
Development Team of the High Priority Bus System Project.
2. For public utility vehicle drivers:
2.a. Must suit the following necessary qualifications as beneficiary of the
Social Development Program of the High Priority Bus System Project:
2.a.a. Registered at Land Transpoftation Franchising and Regulatory
Board Region XI and City Transpoft and Traffic Management Office.
Z.a.b. Employed and declared by operator and/or cooperative.
Z.a.c. At least one (1) year of driver seruice.
2.b. Must have voluntary chosen the Department of Trade and Industry's
Livelihood Seeding Program as one of the Social Development Packages of
the Social Development Program of the High Priority Bus System Project.
2.c. Must be recommended based on thorough assessment by the Social
Development Team of the High Priority Bus System Project.
3. For dependents of public utility vehicle drivers and operators:
3.a. Must be related up to fourth civil degree of affinity to public utility vehicle
drivers and operators.
3.b. Must have voluntary chosen the Department of Trade and Industry's
Livelihood Seeding Program as one of the Social Development Packages of
the Social Development Program of the High Priority Bus System Project.
3.c. Must be recommended based on thorough assessment by the Social
Development Team of the High Priority Bus System Project.
