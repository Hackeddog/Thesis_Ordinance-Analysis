only to situate its local projects but to accommodate projects of national
agencies which are deemed beneficial to constituents. If we are to convey the
entirety of the 31,429 square meter lot to the Boy Scout of the Philippines, r4re
would be depriving the people of Davao the benefits of other infrastructure
projects within the area.
HENCE, THIS VETO.

WHEREAS, in its expanded program of character building citizenship
training, patriotism and nationalism, the Davao City Local Council Boy Scouts
of the Philippines is in dire need of a permanent campsite, to enable them to
attain their goals;
WHEREAS, the establishment of a permanent campsite will raise the
status of the Davao City Local Council, Boy Scouts of the Philippines, for it is

chosen as the Regional Pilot Office for Eastern Mindanao Region of the Boy
Scouts of the Philippines;
NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing, the
OWNER hereby agrees, cedes, conveys, by way of Usufruct unto the
USUFRUCTRUTUARY, the aforesaid land described above, exclusively as CAMP
and TRAINING SITE, under the following terms and conditions:
PURPOSE: The Usufructuary shall develop the land solely and
exclusively as CAMP and TRAINING SITE purposes only of the Davao City Local
Council Boy Scouts of the Philippines. For this purpose, the Usufructuary is
authorized to construct new buildings, make repair, renovation, provided that
the same shall conform to the standards prescribed by law.
In return, the Usufructuary shall conduct trainings to the constituents
of the OWNER in scout craft and to inculcate in them patriotism, civic
consciousness and responsibility, courage, self-reliance, discipline and kindred
viftues and moral values
MAINTEilANCE: The Usufructuary shall maintain the cleanliness of
the land in accordance with the existing rules and regulation and ensure the
same in habitable condition during the existence of Usufruct. It shall be solely
liable for damages or criminal liability arising from its negligence while in
possession of the lot stated above subject of this usufruct.
TERM: This Contract of Usufruct shall be effective for a period of
TWENTY FIVE (25) years from the time of the signing of this contract
renewable upon the consent of both parties, However, in the event that the
USUFRUCIUARY ceases to use, operate or violated any provision of this
contract, this Usufruct is automatically terminated and all the improvements
thereon shall be forfeited in favor of the Owner;
MISCELLAN EOUS PROVISIONS
1. During the existence of Usufruct, the Usufructuary is absolutely
prohibited from leasing, disposing or alienating its righb on the
usufruct to any third person and/or any of its local council.
2. The Usufructuary is prohibited from engaging any commercial
activity inside the land except the selling of boyscout pins, uniforms
and other accessories.
3. That Usufructuary shall allow the OWNER and is given preference
and priority to use the campsite and other facilities that may be
found therein.
TERMINATION: That any violation of the terms and conditions of this
Usufruct is a valid ground for the termination of this contract without
any Court Order. And, all improvements that may be found therein shall
automatically belong to the Owner.
