Ord. No. 0625-24
Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
GRANTING EXEMPTION TO ALL EXHIBITORS IN THE DAVAO AGRITRADE
EXPO 2024 FROM THE PAYMENT OF SPECIAL PERMIT FEES COVERING
THE PERIOD SEPTEMBER 19 TO 2\ 2024, AT SMX CONVENTION CENTE&
LANANG, THIS CITY".
SECTION 2. BASIS - The SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod, as the legislative Body
of the City may grant tax exemptions, incentives, or reliefs in accordance with
SECTION 458 (aX2)(ii) of the Local Government Code of 1991. Fufther, SECTION 236,
Article 28, of Ordinance No. 0291-17, SERIES of 2017, or the 2017 Revenue Code of
Davao City provides that exemption on payment of Special Mayor's Permit may also
be granted to programs conducted by educational, charitable, religious and
governmental institutions, NGOs duly registered/accredited with the City
Government.
SECTION 3. EXEMPTION - All exhibitors who will participate in the Davao
AgriTrade Expo 2024 (DATE) are hereby exempted from the payment of Special
Permit Fees covering the period September 19 to 2L,2024.
SECTION 4. LIMITATIONS - The grant of exemption shall be limited only
to the Special Permit Fees imposed by the City. Consequently, all other fees,
impositions, and taxes shall be paid in accordance with law.
SECTION 5. EFFECTMTY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately
upon approval.
ENACTED, on the 3'd day of September 2024, by a unanimous vote of all
the Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
