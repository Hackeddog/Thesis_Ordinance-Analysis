AN ORDINANCE INSTITUTING JANUARY AS BIBLE
MONTH IN DAVAO CITY

Ord. No. 07t7-24
Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
INSTITUTING JANUARY AS BIBLE MONTH IN DAVAO CITY".
SECTION 2.
- It is the policy of Davao City to
recognize and celebrate the cultural and spiritual heritage of its people. In support
of this commitment, Davao City hereby declares the month of January as Bible
Month, an annual obseruance promoting the teachings, values, and wisdom of the
Bible as paft of Davao's cultural and moral development.
SECTION 3. @IElgre - The objectives of this Ordinance are as
follows:
a) To encourage reflection on the teachings of the Bible and its
relevance in promoting peace, unity, and moral integrity in Davao
City;
b) To promote intefaith understanding and respect for religious
diversity within the City; and
c) To inspire community engagement through organized activities that
honor the Bible and encourage values formation across all sectors.
SECTION 4. BIBLE MONTH ACTMTIES - During the Bible Month, the
following activities are encouraged across Davao City:
1. Local Bible-believing institutions/organizations and schools shall be
encouraged to hold special Bible studies, youth programs, and familycentered activities that emphasize values formation;
2. The City Government, in paftnership with the private sector, Biblebelieving institutions/organizations, and NGOs, shall coordinate citywide events such as Bible exhibits, poster-making contests, seminars,
and reflections that promote unity, moral values and other activities;
3. All Barangay Councils in Davao City are encouraged to suppoft and
participate in Bible Month activities to foster a spirit of community,
love and seruice.
SECTION 5.
- The implementation of this
Ordinance shall be under the Office of the City Mayor through the Moral Recovery
Council, in coordination with the National Bible Month Ecumenical Team. These
agencies shall encourage collaboration among educational, cultural, and Biblebelieving institutions/organizations to ensure the effective obseruance of the Bible
Month.
SECTION 6. FUNDING - Funds shall be allocated for the effective
implementation of this Ordinance and subsequent annual budget shall be necessary
subject to the accounting rules and regulations.

SECTION 8. REPEALING CLAUSE - All previous ordinances, rules and

accordingly.
