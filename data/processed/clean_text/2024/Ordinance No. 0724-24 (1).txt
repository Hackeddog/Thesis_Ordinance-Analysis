BOARD RESOLUTION NO. 018 (s.2024)
CONFIRMING THE AUTHORITY OF THE PRESIDENT AND CHIEF
EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF THE DEVELOPMENT ACADEMY OF THE
PHILIPPINES TO NEGOTIATE AND SIGN AGREEII,IENTS WITH OTHER
ENTITIES IN THE FURTHERANCE OF THE ACADEi'Y'S GOALS AND
OBJECTIVES
WHEREAS, the field of development in its various aspects, individual and national,
formal and informal, human and organizational, admit varied interventions and contributions
from individual, groups, associations, corporations, foundations and other related entities, the
dynamic and creative potential of which may not be contained nor circumscribed;
WHEREAS, the Development Academy of the Philippines (DAP) is cognizant of these
capabilities from other individuals, groups and entities which it would like to access through
appropriate tie-ups, memoranda, contracts and agreements;
WHEREAS, to achieve the mentioned objective, it is necessary to adequately empower
the management of DAP to enter into such contracts or agreements;
WHEREAS, under Referendum No. 01 SERIES of 1999, the DAP Board of Trustees
(BOT) authorized the President of the Academy to negotiate and sign agreements with other
entities to further the Academy's goals and objectives;
WHEREAS, under Board Resolution No. 2018-026, the DAP BOT, acting as the Head
of the Procurement Entity (HoPE), delegated to the DAP President and CEO (PCEO) the
authority to approve Procurement Activitiesr for purposes of economy and expediency, and
increased the amount of the Approved Budget for Contract (ABC) of the procurement activities
delegated to the DAP PCEO from Ten Million Pesos (Php 10,000,000.00) to Fifteen Million
Pesos (Php 1 5,000,000,00);
WHEREAS, the DAP BOT subsequently increased the amount of the ABC of the
Procurement Activities delegated to the DAP PCEO from Fifteen Million Pesos (Php
15,000,000) to Twenty Million Pesos (Php 20,000,000.00) by virtue of Board Resolution No.
006 (s. 2023);
WHEREAS, under Board Resolution No. 001 (s. 2024), the DAP BOT ratified the DAP
Procurement Activities from 01 July 2022 to 13 November 2023;
WHEREAS, at present, there is no existing threshold amount as to the Client-Funded
Projecls to which the DAP PCEO was authorized to negotiate and enter into;
NOW, THEREFORE, be it RESOLVED, as it is hereby RESOLVED, that the DAP
PCEO is hereby authorized to negotiate, enter into, and contract with any individual, group,
corporation, foundation, partnership, association or any other entity, domestic or foreign, for
the purpose of engaging the services or expertise of such individual or entity, in the
performance, completion or contribution to any DAP program, project or enterprise, or any
portion thereof, which, in the appreciation and discretion of the DAP PCEO, is to the best
interest of the DAP towards the achievement of its goals and objectives.
I Procurement Adivities in the nature of: 1) goods and seruices; 2) infrastructure works; 3) consulting services; and
4) elternative melhods of procurement.
Procurement Activilies delegated to the DAP PCEO pertain only to the regular course of business, affairs, and
property, including repairs and maintenance of existing facilities, but exclude the requisition of real assets.

RESOLVED FURTHER, that for contracts and agreements entered into by the DAP
such as, but not limited to, those for client-funded projects, DAP-funded learning and
development activities, partnerships and other agreements, the DAP PCEO shall be
authorized to enter into such contracts only up to the amount of Twenty Million Pesos (Php
20,000,000.00).
RESOLVED FURTHER, that the DAP Management shall submit all contracts to be
entered into by the DAP PCEO under such authority for information of the DAP BOT on the
next scheduled DAP BOT meeting immediately following the execution of such contracts.
RESOLVED FURTHER, that all contracts and agreements entered into by the DAP
exceeding the amounts of Twenty Million Pesos (Php 20,000,000.00) shall be subject to the
approval of the DAP BOT.
RESOLVED FINALLY, that this Resolution shall take effecl upon approval and
signature of majority of the members of the Board of Trustees.
APPROVED on the 21't day of August,2024 in Pasig City.

Davao City is a multi-cultural and highly urbanized city in the southernmost region of the
Philippines. lt has developed enormously over the years due to the combined efforts of its
citizens. As the capital city of the Davao region, Davao City acts as the gateway to
Mindanao. lt also serves as the entry point for the Brunei darussalam - Indonesia -
Malaysia - Philippines East ASEAN Growth Area (BIMP-EAGA), making it Mindanao's
development hub.1
To sustain this continuous growth, the City Government of Davao is committed to enhancing
and.standardizing its systems and processes to ensure the delivery of high-qualig public
seruices to its constituents. Since its last major reorganization in 1996, it has Lecome
necessary to reexamine the current organizational structure and staffing patterns to
effectively manage existing public services and meet emerging demands. tn tine with the
City's initiative to revitalize its bureaucracy, the City Government of Davao has sought
technical assistance from expert service providers,
The ,Development Academy of the Philippines (DAP), aligned with its mandate to promote
excellence in governance and public administration, welcomes the opportunity to assist the
Ci$ Government of Davao in this endeavor through technical assistance in strategic
rightsizing. The partnership aims to support Davao City in achieving its vision of becomin[ a
globally livable city and a center of excellence in governance, investment, tourism, climite
change adaptation, disaster resiliency, and sustainable groMh, driven by an empowered and
engaged citizenry.
