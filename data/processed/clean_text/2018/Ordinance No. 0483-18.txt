2. SPECTFIC GUIDELINES:
A. Fl* BNB Outlet identification
1. Must have an airconditioned/well ventilated Pharmacy or Dispensary
2. With a licensed Pharmacist or Pharmacy Assistant
3. Regularly conducts barangay or community outreach activities
4. Have sufficient storage area for medicines
5. Compliant with Good Distribution and Storage Practices (GDSP)
B. Licensing of Fl+ BNB outlets
All identified Fl+ BNB facilities shall comply with the existing licensing
guidelines of the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and shall be assisted by
the DOH Regional Offices.
C. Selection of Medicines
1. Medicines to be included in the Fl+ BNB program shall be in accordance with
Executive Order No. 49 (use of the latest edition of Philippine National
Formulary as procurement basis for government procurement agencies)
2. The medicines shall be based on the following:
a. Morbidity report
b. Hospital admission report
c. Identified priority disease
d. Commonly needed medicines
e. End-user's Requisition and Issuance Slip (RIS) - see Annex A
D. Patient Eligibility
L All patients with a valid prescription may avail of the medicines provided that
he/she will be listed to the patient registry and shall sign the patient consent
form for (Annex B) profiling.
2. Medicines shall be dispensed in complete treatment regimens for antibiotics and
one (1) month supply for maintenance medications
E. Sourcing and Procurement
1. The acquisition of medicines shall be performed through central bidding either
by PPPI or the DOH-COBAC in accordance to RA 9184.
2. ACentralized Ordering Agreement shall be used by the RHUs and HCs.
3. If drug products are not locally available or with limited
shall be undertaken to ensure continuous access.

F. LogisticsManagement
1. All Fln BNB outlets shall manage their logistics according to the standard
procedures set by the DOH on supply chain management
2. The distribution of the procured medicines shall be directly delivered to the F1+
BNB health facilities through PPPI or an outsourced shipping agency.
3. All Fl+ BNB outlets shall be compliant with Good Distribution and Storage
Practice in accordance with RA 10918.
G. Implementation and Monitoring
l. The regional health offices shall make a MOA with the LGU and oversee
implementation in the Region through the National Drug Policy Compliance
Officer (NDPCO) designates and Public Health Pharmacists (PFIPs).
2. All DOH and LGU procured medicines shall be placed inside the Fl* BNB
outlet and buffer stocks must be placed inside a designated storage room for
rational management.
3. The health facilities are required to submit monthly inventory reports and
patient registry to DOH-RO and DOH-CO as part of the program review,
accounting and auditing procedures using manual and electronic forms provided
in a stand-alone software implemented by the Pharmaceutical Division. For
outlets with no access to the Intemet, printed forms shall replace the above and
be collected by the DOH Regional Office through their public health
pharmacists.
4. All inventory data as well as the Requisitions and Issuance Slips (RIS) of all
health facilities shall be quarterly collated by PHPs and submit this to their
respective Regional Offrces to be forwarded to the DOH-Pharmaceutical
Division for replenishment of stocks.
H. Source of Funds
l. The DOH Health Regulation Cluster - Pharmaceutical Division (DOH-HRCPD) shall ensure appropriation of funds as authorized by the Secretary of Health
for the procurement of the essential medicines in the health facilities.
2. The LGUs shall still allocate funds for several medicines that are not being
supplied in the F1+BNB program as part of their responsibility to provide health
services to their constituents.
3. DOH Regional Offices shall allocate funds to augment the procurement of
medicines to ensure the sustainability of the program in case there are stock outs
or shortage of medicines from the central office.

I. Rational Use of Medicines and Quality Assurance
1. Only essential medicines shall be rationally selected and procured at the
national level and distributed to all identified health facilities. Good
dispensing/storage practice and other conditions reflected on the label of drug
products shall be adhered to at all times by the Pharmacist and Pharmacy
Assistant in charge of the Fl+BNB outlet.
2. The Pharmacist and/or his/her trained Pharmacy Assistant designate shall offer
full Pharmacy service to Fl+ BNB clients including patient counseling on
medicines use.
3. The quantity of medicines to be dispensed by the Fl+ BNB outlet shall be in
accordance with the prescribed therapeutic regimens to a single patient.
4. No Fl* BNB drug products shall find their way in the trade (e.g. retail and
pilferage). Administrative sanction shall be imposed following governments
existing policies.
5. The medicines delivered to the health facilities rnay be subjected to DOFVFDA
post marketing surveillance protocols.
6. Near expiry medicines (6 months prior expiry) and expired medicines shall be
reported to the nearest Regional Office and shall be disposed in accordance with
the pertinent procedure as prescribed by the DOH. A witness from
DOHIRO/FDA shall be present whenever disposal of expired drugs is
conducted.

C. Local Government Unit (LGU)
1. The LGU shall ensure proper implementation of the Fl*BNB Program in its
area ofjurisdiction in coordination with the Regional Office, keep and maintain
prompt inventory of drug supplies and educate the communities on rational use
of medicines.
2. They shall hire their own Pharmacists and Pharmacy Assistants to manage the
FI+BNBoutlet as the DOH shall only initially provide the necessary manpower
needed for the implementation of the program for the first three years.
3. They shall allow the DOH assigned Public Health Pharmacist to conduct
monitoring, inspection or audit of the BNB outlet as part of program evaluation.
4. The LGU shall be responsible in improving the Fl* BNB Outlet and observe
the Good Distribution and Storage Practices in accordance to FDA guidelines.
5. The LGU shall apply for a license to operate (LTO) for each FI+BNB including
continuing renewal of license.
6. They shall assist the DOH in the promotion and advocacy of the Fl+ BNB
program for better implementation and utilization of the program.
7. The Fl+BNB outlet shall allow the designated PHPs and PAs to conduct full
pharmacy services to its clients including patient profiling and counseling
during medicines dispensing.
8. The Municipal Health Officer (I\frIO) shall ensure effrcient and effective
implementation of the program in his/her locality.
9. The MHO shall determine the quantity needed by the population in the
catchment area of the health facility using the quantification tool or the

a. proper storage and warehousing of the commodities
b. coordination of slow moving and inventories to the RO
c. submission of monthly reports to the RO (utilization and inventory,
patient registry)

ARTICLE III
PROGRAM NTONITORING
A. The DOH and its Regional Offices shall be the lead units in monitoring the performance
of all F1*BNB outlets and shall have the authority to check on their inventory records
notwithstanding and without prejudice to a similar role that the LGU and hospital
proponent may undertake or to the regulatory functions of FDA.
B. The Fl+BNB outlets shall maintain the integrity and transparency of their records and
shall be made available whenever asked by the RO concerned, the DOH and other
regulatory units.
C. Reports on monitoring shall be monthly submitted by the Fl+ BNB outlets to their
respective ROs and forwarded to the DOH-HRC-PD.
D. The pro$am shall be evaluated every three years or shorter/longer as deemed
necessary.
