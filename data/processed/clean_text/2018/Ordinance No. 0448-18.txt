MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:
This Agreement made and entered into this
day of
Philippines by and between:
2018, in the City of Manila,
The NATIONAL COMMISSION FOR CULTURE AND THE ARTS, a government agency
created by and pursuant to Republic Act No. 7356 dated 3 April L992, with office address at
NCCA Building, 633 General Luna Street, lntramuros 1002 Manila represented by its Chairman,
NA VtRGtLIO S. ALMARIO, hereinafter referred to as "NCCA".
-andThe CITY OF DAVAO, a local government unit created by law and pursuant to
Commonwealth Act No. 51 dated October 16, 1936 with office address at City Hall Dr, Poblacion
District, Davao City, Davao Del Sur, represented by its Mayor, HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE,
hereinafter referred to as "City of Davao".
WITNESSETH
WHEREAS, the State recognizes that culture is a human right to be accorded respect and allowed to
flourish, that the Filipino national culture shall be evolved and developed by the people themselves in a climate
of freedom and responsibility, and that the Filipino national culture shall be equitable, effectively creating and
distributing cultural opportunities to all;
WHEREAS, the NCCA is mandated to ensure the widest dissemination of artistic and cultural products
among the greatest number across the country and overseas for their appreciation and well-being;
WHEREAS, the NCCA in this mandate to support cultural events such as cultural festivals, competitions,
lectures, seminars, fora and symposia;
WHEREAS, it is the mandate of the City of Davao to provide the delivery of basic services to its
constituent with utmost commitment, dedication and transparency for good governance;
WHEREAS, it also commits to respond to the call as well as abide by the spirit of the Local Government
Code where the Local Government Executive is mandated to address the needs of its constituency with
excellence as the sole standard in performance of executive functions;
WHEREAS, the City of Davao commits to become the Premier Socio-economic and Tourism Center in
Mindanao, East ASEAN Growth Area (EAGA) and the Asia-Pacific Region propelled by enlightened leaders and
empowered citizenry and committed to sustainable growth and development under the guidance of Divine
Providence;
NOW THEREFORE, The NCCA and the City of Davao hereto mutually agree to collaboratively undertake
the following:
1. Development and sustainability of the Magsaysay Park as a "Culture Hub for Peace and
Unity".
2. Development and sustainability of cultural villages and schools of living tradition within the
City of Davao towards cultural heritage tourism;
3. Develop and implement cultural plan and activities, to include cultural mapping and
heritage tourism program for the City of Davao'
