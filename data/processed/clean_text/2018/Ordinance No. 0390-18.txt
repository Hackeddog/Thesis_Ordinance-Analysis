The City Government of Davao, Republic of the Philippines and the County of
Kaua'i, Hawai'i, United States of America, herein to referred collectively as "the
Pafties";
ANTECEDENTS
1. The City Government of Davao and the County Government of Kaua'i are
always seeking for potential international partners for development and
mutual cooperation;
2. On February L8, 2017, as part of the 26th Trade Mission of the Filipino
Chamber of Commerce of Hawaii, a delegation from the County of Kaua'i,
Hawai'i, led by Mayor Bernard P. Carvalho Jr., visited Davao City and extended
a Friendship Ceftificate to signify their interest in establishing a Sisterhood
Relationship between the County of Kaua'i, Hawai'i and Davao City;
3. On November t, 20t7, the City Government of Davao received a proposal
from the County of Kaua'i, Hawai'i to form a Sisterhood Relationship with
4. On November 9, 20t7, Mayor Sara Z. Duterte responded to accept the
proposal from the County of Kaua'i to establish a Sisterhood Relationship with
NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises and
their mutual covenant hereinafter set forth, the parties hereby agree on the following
provisions:
Afticle 1. Objective of the Sister City Agreement
The objective of the Sister City Agreement is to establish a Sister City Relationship to
promote mutually beneficial cooperation and deeper understanding between the
pafties, through an exchange of ideas in the fields identified in Article 2.

Afticle 2. Cooperation
Davao City and the County of Kaua'i intend to create and fortify an open communicative
atmosphere to induce a heightened exchange of ideas, pafticularly in the following
fields:
1.) Tourism and Culture:
As the Parties gather a vast number of tourists, it is deemed favorable to
exchange tourism practices and cultural preservation techniques. The pafties
shall share their methods used to attract and maintain tourist interest, including
the promotion and preservation of their own respective cultures. The Parties will
promote their respective Sister City, to each Pafties mutual benefit, and support
easy access to each city.
2.) Agriculture:
Given that both Davao City and the County of Kaua'i are blessed with spectacular
natural panoramas and lush vegetation, both pafties will endeavor to share and
exchange ideas on agricultural enhancement systems and programs. The parties
may provide, if within the capacity of the Pafties, an exchange in the necessary
machines, crops, and products that are lacking within their paftnered Sister City.
3.) Trade and Commence:
Both parties will maintain open communication in terms of potential business
ventures, investment, and product trade.
The Parties may broaden the communication fields and make concefted efforts to
promote best practices and exchanges in other fields that are beneficial to both
Parties.
Afticle 3. Fufther Agrcements
It is understood that this MOA shall serue as a preliminary agreement of the Parties,
and that the activities being contemplated herein, as well as the pedormance by the
Pafties of this undertaking, or in furtherance of this MOA, shall be subject to more
definitive agreements.
Afticle 4. Amendments and Approvals
No amendment or supplement to this MOA, and no approval or consent constituting an
amendment, shall be effective for any purpose unless made in writing and signed by a
duly authorized officer of both Parties.
Afticle 5. Effectivity, Validity, and Renewal
This MOA shall be effective and valid for five (5) years from the date of signing hereof
and shall be considered extended and/or renewed for another five (5) years if no
objection in writing from either side is serued upon the other within thirty (30) days of
the expiration of the initial five (5) year term.
