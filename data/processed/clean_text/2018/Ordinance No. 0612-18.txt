'Sec. 73. Rules in the Grant of Collective Negotiation Agreement

2.0 Purpose
This Circular is issued to provide the policy and procedural guidelines on the
grant of the CNA lncentive, pursuant to the laws and executive issuances
stated in ltem 1.0 hereof.

3.0 Coverage
This Circular covers the following civilian personnel occupying regular,
contractual, or casual positions rendering services on full-time or part-time
basis in national government agencies (NGAs), including SUCs, Goccs,
LwDs, and LGUs, whether or not covered by Republic Act (RA) No. 675ga, s.
1 989:
Rank-and-file employees who are members of an employees'
organization accredited by the civil service commission (csc) as the
sole and exclusive negotiating agent (hereinafter referred to as
"negotiating agent') in accordance with the rules and regulations issued
by the Public Sector Labor Management Council (PSLMC);
3.2
Rank-and-file employees who are non-members of the csc-accredited
sole and exclusive negotiating agent but want to enjoy or accept
benefits under the CNA, subject to payment of agency fee to the
negotiating agent in accordance with PSLMC Resolution No. 1s,
s. 1993; and
3.3 Those who perform managerialfunctions.

or LGU.
(b) The grant of the CNA lncentive must be stipulated in the
CNA or in supplements thereof.

4.2 Rate of the CNA lncentive
4.2.1 The rate of CNA lncentive shall not be pre-determined in the
CNA since it is subject to compliance with the conditions in item
4.1 hereof and the availability of the allowable allotments.
4.2.2 The CNA lncentive may be given equally to all qualified
employees under item 3.0 hereof or at varying rates in
consideration of the employee's or hisiher office's contribution
to the accomplishment of performance targets, efficiency,
productivity, or profitability, as determined by the agency head
upon recommendation of the Employees' OrganizationManagement Consultative Committee.
4.2.3 ln all cases, the CNA lncentive shall nof exceed P25,000 per

4.3 Fund Sources of the GNA lncentive
4.3.1 ForNGAs
The CNA lncentive shall be sourced solely from the allowable
MOOE allotments in FY 2018, provided the same have become
available as a result of cost-cutting and systems improvement
measures undertaken collectively by the agency and its
personnel, as identified in their respective CNA and
supplements thereof. Such fund sources shall be limited to the
following MOOE items defined under the Government
Accounting and Auditing Manual:
(a) Traveling Expenses;
(b) Communication Expenses;
(c) Repairs and lvlaintenance;
(d) Transportation and Delivery Expenses;
(e) Supplies and Materials; and
(f) Utility Expenses
4.3.2 The following shall, in no case, be used as fund source of the
CNA lncentive:
a. Balances of allotment for programs/activities/projects which
were later discontinued or deferred; and
b. Released allotments intended for acquisition of goods and
seryices to be distributed/delivered to, or to be used by
agency clients.
4.3.3 The modification of any available MOOE allotments for the
payment of the CNA lncentive shall, pursuant to SECTION 73 of
the General Provisions of the FY 2018 GAA, be subject to the
approval of the DBM.
4.3.4 For GOCCs
The CNA lncentive shall be sourced solely from the allowable
MOOE allotments in FY 2018, as enumerated in Sub-item 4.3.1
hereof, under their respective approved Corporate Operating
Budgets (COBs), provided that the following conditions are
complied with:
(a) Actual operating income for the period January 1 to
September 30, 2018 shall, at least, meet the targeted
operating income in the approved COB for the same period.

For GOCCS, which by the nature of their functions
consistently incur losses, the cunent year's operating loss
should have been minimized or reduced compared to or at
most equal to that of the prior year's level;
(b) Actual operating expenses as of September 30, 2018, are
less than the DBM-approved level of operating expenses in
the COB so as to generate sufficient source of funds for the
payment of CNA lncentive; and
c) For income generating GOCCs required to remit earnings,
an amount equivalent to at least 50% of the annual eamings
of the immediately preceding year should have been
remitted to the National Treasury in accordance with
SECTION 3 of RA No. 7656, s. 1993e.
4.3.5 For LWDs
The CNA lncentive shall be sourced solely from the allowable
MOOE allotments in FY 2018, as enumerated in Sub-item 4.3.1,
under their Board of Directors-approved COBs, subject to the
provision of ltem a.1.2(c) hereof.
4.3.6 For LGUs
The CNA lncentive shall be sourced solely from the allowable
MOOE allotments in FY 2018, as enumerated in Sub-item 4.3.1,
under their respective approved LGU budgets.
