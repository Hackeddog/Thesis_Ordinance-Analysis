Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "ENACTING AN
ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND SIGN, FOR

AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT

CARE FACILTTY AND PROVIDING THE NECESSARY FUNDS THEREOF";

provides, to wit:
'SECTION 455, Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation.-

which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to
SECTION 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf
all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon
authority of the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod or pursuant to law or
ordinance;"
SECTION 3. APPROPRIATION OF FUNDS - The City Government of Davao
shall appropriate the amount of One Million Seven Hundred Fifty Thousand Pesos
(P1,750,000.00) under the Annual Development Fund (ADF), for Calendar Year 2018,
intended for the Implementation of the Integrated Clinic Information System "iCLinicSys".
SECTION 4. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby authorized to enter into
and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the Memorandum of
Agreement to be entered into with the Department of Health-Region XI pertaining to the
implementation of the Integrated Clinic Information System "iClinicsys" in a rural health
unit/health center or other primary care facility;
SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any SECTION or
provision of this ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and
effect;
SECTION 6. EFFECTMW -This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval;

V. Client Requirements
The RHU/HC/ or any primary care facility implementing the iClinicSys has the following
responsibilities and must comply with the following requirements in support to this Agrcement:
1. Report problems using the incident reporting procedures detailed in this SLA, including a
clear description of the problem through a Service Request;
2. Responsible for the provision and maintenance of necessary Information and
Communication Technology (ICT) resources required to implement the system;
3. Ensure availability of trained, appropriate and qualified personnel to use, operate, and
maintain the system;
4. Ensure the validity and/or integrity and quality of data to be entered into the system;
5. Ensure the confidentiality of the data stored in the system;
6. Ensure adherence to the Data Privacy Act of 2012 and security protocols on elecffonic
medical records;
7. Ensure regular uploading of required data;
8. Ensure daily backup of database (for offline users);
9. In case that the LGU decided to change EMR (Existing Medical Records) provider, LGU
should send a letter of disengagement made to previous EMR. All previously encoded
records can be retrieved in PHIE (Philippine Health Information Exchange) and will be
uploaded by LGU to the new EMR.
