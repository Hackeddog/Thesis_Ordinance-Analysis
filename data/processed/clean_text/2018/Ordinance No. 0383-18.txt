ORDINANCE NO. 0383.18
SERIES of 2018
AN ORDINANCE REQUIRING THE FOOD SERVICE INDUSTRY
WITHIN THE TERRITORIAL JURISDICTION OF DAVAO CITY
TO INCLUDE ONE-HALF (1/2) CUp OF COOKED RICE rN THErR
MENU, PROVIDING PENALTIES THEREFOR AND FOR OTHER
PURPOSES

Ord. No. 0383-18
Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in session assembled that:
SECTION 1. TITLE- This Ordinance shall be known as and cited by its short title as the
"DAVAO CITY RTCE CONSERVATION ORDINANCE";
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POUCY- It is hereby a policy of the City to adopt
measures which shall ensure rice conservation and shall educate the consuming public not
to waste rice to attain rice self-sufficiency;
SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS.
A. Food Seruice Industry - businesses, institutions and companies responsible for
any sale of meals, including but not limited to, restaurants, schools, offices,
hospitals, cafeterias, catering operations, fastfood chains and other food related
services;
B. Half-Cup Rice - shall refer to one-half cup of the seruing of one cup order of rice
as determined by the food establishment, provided that, the half-cup shall not be
less than B0 grams (B0g);
C. City - City Government of Davao;
SECTION 4. COVERAGE- All businesses, institutions and companies responsible for
any sale of meals, including but not limited to, restaurants, schools, offices, hospitals,
cafeterias, catering operations, fastfood chains and other food related seruices operating
within the territorial jurisdiction of the City are hereby required to include one-half cup of
cooked rice as an option in their regular menu with price set at one half of one cup.
Food service industry engaged in eat-all-you-can or buffet meals shall be exempt
from this Ordinance unless they are seruing plate-in or ala carte meals.
As a form of public information and awareness, such information shall be mandatorily
contained in the menu and be made known by the attending service crew, waiter, waitress
or cashier to the customer before the latter shall commence ordering;
SECTION 5. PENALTIES- The food service provider found violating this Ordinance
shall be penalized, as follows, for refusal to include one-half cup of cooked rice in their
menu:
1't Offense - Fine of One Thousand Five Hundred Pesos (P1, 500.00);
2nd Offense - Fine of Three Thousand Pesos (P3, 000.00) and one month
suspension of license or permit to operate;
3,.d Offense - Fine of Five Thousand Pesos (P5,000.00) and permanent
cancellation of license or permit to operate;
SECTION 6. THE IMPLEMENTING AGENCY- The City Health Office in
coordination with the City Agriculturist's Office shall be empowered and tasked to be the
lead agency in the inspection of food establishments and for purposes of implementing this
Ordinance;
SECTION 7. IMPLEMENTING RULES AND REGULATIONS- The City Mayor is
hereby authorized to create a joint Executive-Legislative Technical Working Group to
formulate the Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR) of this Ordinance within the period
of three months from the approval of this Ordinance;
