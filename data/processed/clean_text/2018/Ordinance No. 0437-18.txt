This Agreement is made and entered into by and between:
The Philippine Atmospheric, Geophysical and Astronomical Services Administration
(PAGASA), a Service Institute under the Department of Science and Technology (DOST),
created under Presidential Decree No. 78, as amended, with principal office address at PAGASA
Science Garden Complex, BIR Road, Diliman, Quezon City and represented in this Agreement
by DR. YICENTE B. MALAI{O in his capacity as Administrator, hereinafter referred to as
..PAGASA";
-andThe City of Davao, a local government unit duly organized and existing under the laws of the
Philippines, with postal address at City Hall Bldg, San Pedro St. represented herein by its City

WHEREAS, the Philippines has been subjected to numerous natural hazards such as typhoons,
storm surges, monsoon rains, floods, flashfloods, landslides, soil erosion, droughts, natural hazards which
could be fuither compounded by human activities, possible dam failures, etc.;
WHEREAS, the increasing trend of tropical cyclone occurrences in the area of Mindanao Region
has resulted to fuither increase the risk of the communities to the effects of hydro-meteorological hazards,
particularly that of flooding;
WIIEREAS, the aftermath of Typhoon Sendong in December 20ll saw the appalling effect of
the unprecedented rainfall in the area of Cagayan De Oro City, resulting to high number of fatalities and
catastrophic damages to properties and the agriculture and infrastructure sectors;
WHEREAS, the national government has continuously endeavored to reduce the negative
impacts of flooding thru the establishment of an early warning system for flood for all the major river
basins in the Philippines;
WHEREAS, the PAGASA is mandated to provide protection of life and property against natural
calamities due to typhoons, floods, drought, giant waves, high seas and the like, and to utilize scientific
knowledge and information as an effective instrument to ensure the safety, well-being, economic security
and improve the quality of life of all the people and the environment;
WHEREAS, the PAGASA will implement establishment of a network of hydrological
monitoring facilities to address the need for a functional early warning system (EWS) for flood/flashflood
for Davao River Basin to alert the community of an impending threat, thus averting the occurrence of a
similar magnitude of destruction during the occurrence of Typhoon Sendong;
WHEREAS, the said network of hydrological monitoring equipment for flood early warning will
entail the installation of automatic rainfall and water level monitoring equipment and repeater towers for
the transmission of data to the Davao River Basin Flood Forecasting and \ilarning Center
(DRBFFWC)and Main Operation Center of Hydrometeorology Division (IillD) for flood forecasting
and early warning purposes;
*
WIIERBAS, in order to ef,Fectively monitor the hydrological condition within the Davao River
Basin, data transmission will be done on a 10-minute interval, thus providing an advance information on
the hydrological condition in the upstream areas;
WHEREAS, the LGU-Davao Cityhas signified its willingness to provide the necessary
assistance and share its resources, specifically stated under the specific obligations of the Parties, for the
effective implementation, operation and maintenance and conduct of other activities that will ensure the
optimum utilization of the monitoring equipment to be installed.
