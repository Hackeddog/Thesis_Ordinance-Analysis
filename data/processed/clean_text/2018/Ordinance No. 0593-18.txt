PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT
KNOW ALL WOMEN AND MEN BY THESE PRESENTS
This Partnership Agreement made and executed by and between
The PHILIPPINE COMMISSION ON WOMEN (PCW), a government
agency under the Office of the President with official address at 1145 J.P.
Laurel Street, San Miguel, Manila represented herein by its Executive
Director, EMMELINE L. VERZOSA, Filipino, of legal age, herein referred to
as "PCW";
-andThe CITY OF DAVAO, a city local govemment unit in Region Xl with official
address at City Hall, San Pedro Street, Davao City, represented herein by its City Mayor,
SARA DUTERTE-CARPIO, a Filipino, of legal age and herein refened to as "DAVAO
CITY";
WITNESSETH THAT:
WHEREAS, the PCW as the national machinery for gender equality and women's
empowerment is mandated to capacitate all National Govemment Agencies (NGAs) and
Local Government Units (LGUs) to effectively implement Republic Act 9710 othenruise
known as Magna Carta of Women (MCW and further their efforts in promoting gender
equality and women's empowerment;
WHEREAS, SECTION 36 of the MCW mandates all govemment agencies,
instrumentalities and LGUs to adopt gender mainstreaming as a strategy to promote
women's human rights and eliminate gender discrimination in their systems, structures,
policies, programs, processes, and procedures;
WHEREAS, SECTION 3 of the Local Government Code (LGC) provides that the
realization of local autonomy shall be facilitated through improved coordination of national
government policies and programs and extension of adequate technical and material
assistance to less developed and deserving LGUs;
WHEREAS, the PCW developed the Local GAD Resource Program (LGRP) as part
of its National GAD Resource Program (NGRP) to strengthen gender mainstreaming
efforts at the regional and local levels, and build partnership, converge and replicate
notable GAD etforts;
WHEREAS, the LGRP will build on existing local GAD mechanisms, initial efforts
and existing partnership with LGUs and other institutions to tailor fit technical assistance,
policy development and advocacy, monitoring and evaluation to their specific
circumstances or needs to pursue gender mainstreaming;
WHEREAS, the LGRP has three components namely: a) Partnership and
Convergence; b) Replication Mechanisms; and c) Provision of Direct Technical Assistance
on Demand Basis and Development and Updating of GAD Tools;
*

WHEREAS, the Replication lvlechanisms component is designed to identify
showcase and replicate notable GAD efforts and practices of LGUs;
WHEREAS, the Local Learning Hub (LLH) on Gender Mainstreaming (GM) as
the sub-component of the Replication Mechanism aims to institutionalize the sharing
and replication of LGUs' experiences and lessons learned on their notable GAD efforts;
WHEREAS, under Replication lvlechanism of the LGRP, the PCW will certify and
partner with LGUs with notable GAD efforts;
WHEREAS, based on PCW's review and validation session conducted in 2014,
the following four (4) notable GAD programs of Davao City were certified as GAD LLHs
for three (3) years: Davao City lntegrated Gender and Development Division (IGDD);
Davao City Office of the Special Counsel on VAW; Davao City Child IMinding Centers;
and Ray of Hope Village of Davao City under the Bureau of Jail t\Ianagement and
Penology-Region Xl;
WHEREAS, PCW conducted its monitoring and evaluation visit on November 67, 2017 to said four (4) GAD programs and re-certified the same on March 22,2018 for
effectively sustaining its implementation and maintaining compliance to the criteria set
by PCW in certifying GAD LLHs;
WHEREAS, PCW will sign a Partnership Agreement with Davao City to facilitate
the provision of technical assistance to other LGUs and institutions in relation to the
notable GAD programs;
NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the
parties hereby declare and agree to the following:
SECTION 1
DESCRIPTION OF PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT
This covers the conditions of the Partnership between PCW and Davao City as
provided in the terms of reference (TOR).
SECTION 2
EFFECTIVITY PERIOD OF THE PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT
This Partnership Agreement shall commence upon the signing of all parties and
shall expire in three (3) years after date of signing, unless sooner terminated based on
the provisions of SECTION 4 of this Agreement.
SECTION 3
ROLES AND FUNCTIONS OF THE PARTIES
3.1. The City Government of Davao shall
3.1 .1 Assign its IGDD, an office under the City Government of Davao to coordinate
and assist PCW in the implementation of the activities under this Partnership;
3.1.2 Sustain the implementation of the four (4) notable GAD programs as local
learning hubs;

materials, peer to peer learning/coaching sessions, and site visits of LGUs
and other institutions among others;

3.1.7 Provide feedback to PCW through the submission of quarterly reports

LLHs,

3.1.9 Provide counterpart funds and/or non-cash resources such as staff time,

duration of this Partnership when necessary.
3.2. The Philippine Commission on Women shall:
3.2.1 Assign the Technical Services and Regional Coordination Division (TSRCD)

activities under this Partnership;
3.2.2 Support the capacity building of Davao City, GAD Focal Point System

capacity in providing technical assistance to other LGUs and institutions in
relation to the notable GAD programs;
3.2.3 Support Davao City in marketing and promoting the notable GAD efforts
through referrals, development of lECs, linking them to relevant organizations
and agencies and endorsing these efforts during PCW-organized
conferences, trainings and workshops;
3.2.4 Conduct a periodic monitoring and evaluation visits in coordination with
Davao City to assess the results and outputs of this Partnership; the
challenges and areas for improvement of the notable GAD efforts, lessons
learned and other emerging gender issues relative to the GAD programs
recertified;
3.2.5 Provide template format for quarterly reports to be submitted by Davao City to
PCW;
3.2.6 Participate in the assessment and learning session with Davao City relative to

TERMINATION OF AGREEMENT
The PGW and the City Government of Davao shall terminate this agreement at
any stage of the Partnership implementation should either party fail to deliver its
obligations on the deliverables mutually agreed upon.
SECTION 5
AUTHORITY TO ENTER INTO AGREEMENTS
The PCW and City Government of Davao shall have no authority and cannot
enter into any agreement or contract, nor pledge credit on behalf of the other. However,
each cooperating institution may enter into agreement with any pafty and on its own
behalf in pursuit of this Partnership.
SECTION 6
OWNERSHIP OF OUTPUTS
All outputs and reports resulting from the implementation of this
Partnership, including seminarllraining materials must be used with
utmost discretion. As a matter of courtesy and protocol, a party using the
outputs, reports or seminar/training materials of the other party shall
request permission prior to their use in subsequent seminars/trainings,
outputs or reports, with proper acknowledgement of the source, author or
creator thereof.
SECTION 7
INSURANCE GUARANTEE
Both the PCW and the City Government of Davao agree that each party is not
responsible for the personal life/ accident insurance of each one's personnel and frees
each other of any liability or obligation in case of untoward incidents that the PCW and
Davao City personnel may experience in pursuit of this Partnership.
