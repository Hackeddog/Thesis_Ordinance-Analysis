KNOWALL MEN BYTHESE PRESENTS:
This Memorandum of Agreement ('Agreement') made and entered into
this _
day of
at_,
by and between:
The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO with office address at
Pichon Street, Davao City, represented herein by SARA Z. DUTERTE,
City Mayor, hereinafter referred to as the LGU
and -
PROCTER & GAMBLE PHILIPPINES, INC., with office address at
116 Floor, Net Park Building, 5n Avenue, E Square, Crescent Park
West, Bonifacio Global City, Tagulg, L634, Metro Manila, represented
herein by its General Manager, SHANKAR VISWANATHAN hereinafter
referred to as P&G;
WITNESSETH THAT:
WHEREAS, the P&G can be a source of competent expertise to promote
health and hygiene through its P&G Linis Lusog Program, a health and
hygiene program designed to positively impact the community by teaching
children good cleaning habits and stressing the importance of keeping one's
body and surroundings clean;
WHEREAS, the P&G is willing to support particular Day Care Centers
identified by the LGU by providing health and hygiene products as well as
information on proper handwashing and health practices to the Day Care
Workers through the P&G Linis Lusog Program in accordance with the terms
and conditions of this Agreement;
WHEREAS, the P&G is willing to provide the necessary tools and
products to the identified Day Care Centers to aid in the promotion of the
program's thrust;
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises
and of the mutual covenants herein below set forth, the pafties bind
themselves and agree as follows:
ARTICLE I
THE P&G LINIS LUSOG PROGRAM
The P&G Linis Lusog Program is an initiative of the P&G to promote
cleaner and healthier communities through the health and sanitation program,
which teaches children about the impoftance of cleanliness, empowering them
to form good habits.

The P&G Linis Lusog Program aims to achieve the following objectives:
1. Promote health and hygiene among children and parents to help
prevent health issues and spread of diseases and allow them to be
empowered members of the community.
2. Enable day care centers to be more conducive learning, childcare
institutions through the prevention of diseases.
3. Educate day care workers of day care centers on how to teach
children and parents on proper hygiene
ARTICLE II:
P&G LINIS LUSOG PROGRAM IMPLEMENTATION and P&G
RESPONSIBILITIES
1. P&G, by itself or through an authorized agent, will distribute health
and hygiene products to day care centers identified by the LGU on a
bi-annual, or twice a year basis;
2. P&G by itself or through an authorized agent, will educate day care
center staff on the proper teaching of hand washing, and other
standards of keeping oneself clean;
3. Designated Early Childhood Care & Development (ECCD) Focals and
District Heads, trained by the P&G under the P&G Linis Lusog
Program will be responsible in educating Day Care Workers on how to
teach children in their day care centers on proper handwashing and
hygiene, The ECCD Focals will also be in charge of ensuring that the
day care children are correctly forming the right habits, and will
monitor health conditions of the children as well through attendance
monitoring.
4. Identified day care centers will receive P&G Hygiene Bundles wofth
P10,000 of P&G Products twice a year as support in educating their
children and parents on the proper hygiene until June 20,2019.
5. The Day Care Workers will keep track of the attendance and
performance of the children to ensure effective implementation.
Attendance Repoft will be submitted to City Social Seruices and
Development Office for submission to P&G with a Program
Assessment Report that will aid in evaluating the success of the
program. The information provided in the repoft will be used to
improve, redesign, and redevelop the framework and program
curriculum.
\
6. P&G may stage program event/s, provide other materials and
logistics needed within the allocated budget of P&G for the program.
Use of City of Davao's Logo is subject to prior written approval of the

ARTICLE III:
LGU RESPONSIBILITIES
The LGU through the City Social Services and Development Office will:
1. Recommend a list of day care centers willing to be beneficiaries of
the program of the P&G from which P&G will choose 3 (three) to be
the beneficiaries of the P&G Linis Lusog Program.
2. Ensure that the products are stored properly and usage of which will
be monitored;
3. Check and monitor attendance of each child participant, to be used
as an evaluation tool on the effectiveness of the program;
4. Monitor effectiveness of the program and provide Assessment Report
to the P&G;
5. Properly execute and oversee the overall implementation of the
program.
ARTICLE IV
OTHER PROVISIONS
The parties shall exert due diligence, and good faith to achieve the
objectives of the program. Any problem, conflict or concerns that may arise
from implementing the program shall be resolved through consultations.
ARTICLE V
EFFECTIVIW
This Memorandum of Agreement shall take effect immediately and shall
be valid until 30 June 2019, subject to renewal upon the mutual agreement of
the paties.
ARTICLE VI
OTHERS
Confidentiality Clause - The Parties shall not, during the term of this
Agreement, disclose any proprietary and confidential information relating to
programs and activities and this Agreement, including all data that may be
received or gathered in the performance of the respective Party's obligation
without prior written consent of the other Party.
Amendment - any amendment to this Agreement shall be made in
writing and signed by the both Parties.
Ownership of Work -
any material or document produced in the
course of the implementation of this Agreement shall be owned by P&G .
