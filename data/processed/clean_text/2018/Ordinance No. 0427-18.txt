Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE FOR
THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC THE STREET ALONG PIONEER,
BARANGAY 2B-C, POBLACION DISTRICT, THIS CITY, IN CELEBRATION OF THE ANNUAL
FIESTA oF SENIOR sAN JosE oN MARCH 19, 2018, FROM 9:00 A.M. To 12:00
MIDNIGHT";
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - SECTION 21 (c) of Republic Act No.
7160, othenruise known as the Local Government Code of 199!, provides that any national
or local road, alley, park or square may be temporarily closed during an actual emergency,
or fiesta celebration, public rallies and agricultural or industrial fairs...;
SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE - The Pioneer Street on Barangay 2B-C,
Poblacion District, this City, shall be temporarily closed to vehicular traffic on March 19,
2018, from 9:00 A.M. to 12:00 Midnight in celebration of the Annual Fiesta of Senior San
Jose;
SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any SECTION or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and
effect;
SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately
upon approval;
ENACTED, on March 9, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
