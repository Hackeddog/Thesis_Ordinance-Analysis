This MEMORANDUM OF COOPERATION made and entered into by and between
The Provincial Government of Bukidnon, a local govemment unit with an office address at the
Provincial Capitol, Malaybalay City, Bukidnon, represented herein by HON.JOSE MARIA R.
ZUBIRI, JR. hereinafter referred to as Provincial LGU of BUKIDNON;
The City Government of Davao, a local government unit with an office address at San Pedro St.
Davao City, represented herein by HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE, hereinafter referred to as LGUDAVAO CITY;
The Department of Environment and Natural Resources Region X with an office address at
Macabalan, Cagayan de Oro City represented herein by Regional Director ARLEIGII J.
ADORABLE herein referred to as DENR Region X;
The Department of Environment and Natural Resources Region XI with an office address at Km.
7,Lanang, Davao City represented herein by Regional Director RUTH M. TAWANTAWAN

And Mindanao Development Authority with an office address at Sasa, Old Airport, Davao City
represented herein by SECRETARY DATti HADJI ABIIL KHAYR D. ALONTO herein

WITNESSETH:
WIIEREAS, Article II, SECTION 16 of the 1987 Constitution provides for the protecfion and
advancement of the rights of Filipino people, to a healthful and balanced ecology in accord with
the rhythm and harmony of nature;
WIIEREAS, Chapter 3, Sec, 3 of the Local Govemment Code of the Philippines provides that
Local Government units may group themselves, consolidate their efforts, services, and resources
for purposes commonly beneficial to them;
WHEREAS, the LGUs shall share with the National Government the responsibility in the
management and maintenance of ecological balance within their territorial jurisdiction subject to
the provision of the Local Government Code;
WHEREAS, the LGU, pursuant to Department Administrative Order No. 92-30, SERIES of 1992,
having jointly identified watershed areas with the DENR, shall manage, protect and rehabilitate
the same through a Co-Management;
WIIEREAS, the DENR, by virtue Executive Order No. 192 SERIES of 1987, is the primary
government agency responsible for the sustainable management and development of the
country's environment and natural resources;
WIIEREAS, the govemment has adopted integrated watershed management as a national
strategy tbr sustainable developmerfi per DENR MC 2008-05, SERIES 2008;
WHEREAS, the government has created a policy for the conservation and protection of wildlife
resources and their habitats;

WHEREAS, Republic Act (RA) No. 9996 or the Mindanao Development Authority Act of
2010, created the Mindanao Development Authority (MinDA), which is tasked to establish an
effective institutional mechanism to address the need for a coordinated and integrated approach
in the formulation and integration of a Mindanao-wide, inter-regional development plans,
programs and projects;
WHEREAS, MinDA Board Resolution No. 18, SERIES of 20ll states to take the lead in
integrating the efforts of key partners and stakeholders in rehabilitating, developing and
managing Mindanao's river basins and watersheds through MindaNOW or Nurturing Our
Waters program;
WHEREAS, Executive Order (EO) No. 50 (s. 2011), abolished the Presidential Task Force on
Mindanao River Basin Rehabilitation and Development and transferred its functions to regular
government agencies, namely, the MinDA, OCD and DENR-RBCO;
WHEREAS, the other National Agencies which have specific mandates in the conservation,
management, and development of the Davao River Basin are included in supporting all the
developmental phases of the said River Basin;
WHEREAS, there are existing Watershed Management Councils within the Davao River Basin
namely: Bukidnon Watershed Protection and Development Council and Davao City
Watershed Management Council which was agreed during the first organizational meeting to
allied with each other, hence the name Davao River Basin Management Alliance @RBMA);
WHEREAS, it is imperative that the Davao River Basin Management Alliance (DRBMA) shall
be created to oversee the overall implementation of plans and programs as contained in the
Davao River Basin (DRB) Integrated Development Master Plan for the protection, conservation,
rehabilitation and management of the Davao River Basin;
WHEREAS, to facilitate with the provisions stated in the Terms of Reference (TOR), and
ensure its sustainability through resource mobilization activities;
NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the parties agreed and
hereby agree to undertake the following:

III. BUDGETARY REQUIREMENT/FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT
The Provincial Government of Bukidnon and City Government of Davao shall allocate
funds annually as its counterpart contribution to the Davao River Basin Management
Alliance.
The River Basin Control Office of the Department of Environment and Natural
Resources shall also allocate FM HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS (Php
500,000.00) annually for the strengthening of the operational capacity of the Davao River
Basin Management Alliance (DRBMA).
The Mindanao Development Authority shall allocate THREE HUI{DRED
THOUSAND (Php 300, 000.00) annually for the capacity building of the Davao River
Basin Management Alliance (DRBMA).
The Davao River Basin Management Alliance shall create a trust fund and select a trustee
or trustee organization to handle funds from among the members. All financial
transactions shall be subjected to the standard govemment accounting and auditing rules
