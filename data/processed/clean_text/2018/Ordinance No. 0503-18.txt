AN ORDINANCE FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO
VEHICULAR TRAFFIC OF A PORTION OF BOTTON STREET
FROM CORNER SAN PEDRO STREET TO PICHON STREET
FROM 4:00 P.M. ONWARDS ON AUGUST 13, 2018 IN
CONNECTION WITH THE 2018 KADAYAWAN FESTIVAL
EVENTS (HAULTNG OF MATERTALS, SET UP OF
KADAYAWAN SQUARE)

Ord. No, 0503-18
Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC OF A PORTION OF
BOLTON STREET FROM CORNER SAN PEDRO STREET TO PICHON STREET FROM
4:00 P.M. ONWARDS ON AUGUST 13, 2018 IN CONNECTION WITH THE 2018
KADAYAWAN FESTTVAL EVENTS (HAULING OF MATERTALST SET UP OF
KADAYAWAN SQUARE)";
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - SECTION 21 (c) of Republic Act No.
7160 otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that any national
or local road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during an actual
emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultural or industrial fairs....;
SECTION 3. TEMPORARY CLOSURE In connection with the 2018
Kadayawan Festival events (hauling of materials, set-up of Kadayawan Square), the City
Tourism Operations Office requested for the temporary closure to vehicular traffic of a
portion of Bolton Street from corner San Pedro Street to Pichon Street on August 13,
2018 from 4:00 P.M. onwards;
SECTION 3. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, dfly SECTION or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and
effect;
SECTION 4. EFFECTIVITY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately
upon approval;
ENACTED, on July 31, 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.
