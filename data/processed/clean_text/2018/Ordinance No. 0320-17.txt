MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
KNOWALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS
This Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) executed on the _ day of November
2017, in Davao City, Philippines by and between:
The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a Local Government Unit, represented by
its City Mayor, SARA Z. DUTERTE, of legal age, Filipino and a resident of Davao
City, hereinafter referred to as the FIRST PARTY;
-andThe UNIVERSITY OF SOUTHEASTERN PHILIPPINES, an academic institution,
created and existing under the Batasang Pambansa Bldg. 12, with office address
located at Obrero, Davao City represented by its President, DR. LOURDES C.
GENERALAO, herein referred to as the SECOND PARW.
WITNESSETH
WHEREAS, the First Party through the CADAC-CBRAP conducts a Community
Based Rehabilitation Program, Aftercare and Continuing Preventive Health Care
for PSUDs pursuant to RA 9165, otherwise known as the "Comprehensive
Dangerous Drugs Act of 2002'and DILG Memo Circular 2016-116;
WHEREAS, the First Party is in need of the services of the Second Party who shall
voluntarily render services for, but not limited to, psychological and social services
through their extension programs;
WHEREAS, the Second Party hereby possesses human resource with the
education, experience and skills required to perform the job as described herein;
WHEREAS, the Second Party shall have the full support of the CADAC as part of
the CBRAP.
NOW THEREFORE, in consideration thereof, the First Party hereby
engages the services of the Second Party effective March, 2017 subject to the
following terms and conditions:
1. The Second Party shall accept referrals from the First Part to provide
profiling/screening and case management of the clients of the CADACCBRAP;
2.The Second Party shall render voluntarily its extension services to the
First Party, free of charges as coordinative support to the CBRAP.
3. The First Party shall refer to the Second Party, the client's Case Manager;
4. The First Party shall assign a reformists' list through its Barangay AntiDrug Abuse Councils as the Second Party's caseloads;
5. The First Party and the Second Party shall closely coordinate and
collaborate with each other for the Bio-Psycho-Socio-Spiritual and
Physical activities of the CADAC-CBRAP, and;
6. The Second Party shall be allowed to use the facilities of the First Party
relevant to providing such mechanisms as safe zones and other means
of physical support.
