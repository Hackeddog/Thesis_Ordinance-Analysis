DECLARATION OF AGREEMENT:
WHEREAS, the "Wastewater Treatment System Upgrading Program" was funded by
the DOST-PCIEERD with the objective of helping local MSMEs, public facilities and
small communities through the introduction of low-cost, nature based wastewater
treatment;
WHEREAS, the "Wastewater Treatment System Upgrading Program" will be
implemented in Davao city through the partnership among DOST xI, UIC-SRC, BLMC
and City of Davao;
WHEREAS, the DOST XI is a regional arm of the DOST and is committed to serve the
Filipino nation and to relate its activities to the needs of the Filipino people and their
aspirations for social progress and transformation;
WHEREAS, Ms. Ludivina M. Porticos is project leader and committed in implementing
a Vertical Helophyte Filter System for the Ma-a Slaughterhouse which functions as a
resilient, nature-based, and low-cost wastewater treatment technology that will promote
sustainable industries through innovation;
WHEREAS, the BLMC is a Cooperative which is operating at the Ma-a Slaughterhouse
through a PPP agreement with the City of Davao;
WHEREAS, City of Davao is the Local Government Unit that has the area of
responsibility over the current address of the Ma-a Slaughterhouse;
WHEREAS, it is the mutual and collective aim of the Parties to promote sustainable
development among MSMEs and the communities by supporting sustainable and lowcost wastewater treatment technologies and to contribute to national development.
WHEREAS, DOST XI, UIC, BLMC and City of Davao jointly pledge to extend their
full cooperation to one another for the effective implementation of the project;
NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises and their mutual
covenants herein set forth, DOST XI, UIC, BLMC and City of Davao hereby stipulate
and agree to the following terms and conditions:
Project Title: This project shall be known as "Wastewater Treatment System
Upgrading Program (WATsUP) Project: Enhancing the Applicability of the
Vertical Helophyte Filter Systems for Small Communities, Local MSMEs and
Public Entities".
