MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
DAVAO CITY - UNIVERSITY OF THE PIIIIPPINES SPORTS COMPLEX
This Memorandum ofAgreement (hereinafter the "Agreemenf') entered into by and
between:
The UNIVERSITY OF THE PHILIPPINES, the National University created by virtue of Act No. 1870, as amended and strengthened by Republic Act No. 9500, otherwise known as "The University of the Philippines
Charter of 2008" with office address at Quezon Hall, UP Diliman Quezon
City, represented herein by its President, DANILO L. CONCEPCION,
herein referred as the "UNMRSITY''
and
The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a Local Government
Unit created by virtue of Republic Act No. 4354, as amended, with office
address at2nd Floor Hall, City Hall Drive, San Pedro St., Davao City, represented herein by its Mayor, Hon. SARA Z. DUTERTE, herein referred as
the "CITY OF DAVAO";
RECITALS
WHEREAS, by virtue of Republic Act No. 9500 (An Act to Strengthen the University
of the Philippines as the National University), UP is mandated to provide opportunities for
training and learning in leadership, responsible citizenship, and the development of democratic
values, institutions and practice through academic and non-academic programs, including
sports, and the enhancement of nationalism and national identity.
WHEREAS, UP is committed to lead in providing affordable quality education,
scholarly research, and responsive and relevant extension services to diverse, marginalized and
deserving sectors in Mindanao and neighboring regions through its programs in the sciences
and the arts including the development of sports, human kinetics, and physical recreation
activities to improve the peace and development as well as the sociocultural attributes of
Mindanao, and to inculcate a passion for excellence, creative thinking, and nationalism in the
context of cultural diversity in a global community;
WHEREAS, the Board of Regents (hereafter the "BOR") of UP at its 1255ft Meeting
held on June 2010 approved the UP Mindanao Land Use Plan that includes twenty (20) hectares
for the Sports Complex,
WHEREAS, UP desires to develop and construct a sports complex in said twenty (20)
hectare area, situated within the UP Mindanao Campus, at Mintal, Davao City-specifically,
within the property described as Lot 129-K-2 registered in its name under Transfer Certificate
of Title (TCT) No. 346863, of the Registry of Deeds for Davao City in order to fulfill its
mandate under its Charter;
WHEREAS, the CITY OF DAVAO envisions itself as the premier socio-economic and
tourism center in Mindanao, East Asean Growth Area (EAGA) and the Asia-Pacific Region
propelled by enlightened leaders and empowered citizenry and committed to sustainable
growth and development under the guidance of Divine Providence;
WHEREAS, the CITY OF DAVAO also desires to establish a major sports fhcility
designed to serve as unifuing venue to showcase the common and distinctive social, cultural,
indigenous heritage and identity of the people of Mindanao through the development of sports
and human kinetics, and promote the maximum exposure and positive image of the CITY OF
-DAVAO as a sports, tourist, business and investment mecca and zone for peaceful convergence2
c$Y

of divergent cultures, races, ethnicities, and nationalities, through the conduct of sports events
and contests, and human kinetic challenges, whether local, national or intemational;
WI{EREAS, the Parties have agreed to develop, construct and operate a sports complex
in the aforementioned site as a joint project whereby, on the one hand, UP will provide the
project site, and on the other, the CITY OF DAVAO will develop, construct and establish the
necessary infrastructure facilities thereof;
NOW TFIEREFORE, for and in consideration of the above premises, their mutual
consent herein set forth, the parties have agreed to the following terms and condition:
ARTICLE I _ NAME, LOCATION AND OWNERSHIP OF THE SPORTS COMPLEX
1. The facility shall be known as the l)avao City - University of the Philippines (DC-UP)
Sports Complex (hereafter to be referred to as the "Sports Complex"). This name shall signifr
the unified effort of the Davao City community and UP to provide an avenue for advancing
human, social, and economic development in Mindanao through sports.
2. The Sports Complex shall be situated in the twenty (20) hectare portion within UP campus
at Mintal, Davao City, specifically, within its property described as Lot 129-K-2 registered in
its name under Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT) No. T-346863, of the Registry of Deeds for
ARTICLE tr _ OBLIGATIONS OF THE PARTIES
II (l) Obligations of the UNIVERSITY
a. Provide and allocate a twenty (20) hectare area within its titled property in the UP Mindanao Campus, specifically, within the property described as Lot 129-K-2 registered in its
name under Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT) No. T:346863, of the Registry of Deeds for
Davao City, as project site for the Sports Complex,
b. Allow the other party, its offrcials, workers, agents, or representative free ingress to, and
egress from, the project site to examine the same, conduct field assessment and evaluation,
and undertake land and infrastructure development for the purpose of establishing, developing and constructing the Sports Complex, subject to the UP policies and procedure; and
c. Make available and impart its technical expertise on the subject ofhuman kinetics, sports
and sports management to the sports-related programs and activities of the other Party,
subject to UP policies and procedure;
II (2) Obligations of the CITY OF DAVAO:
a. Provide for and secure the funding and budgetary requirements necessary to develop,
build, construct and implement the Sports Complex, including its road network, from local,
national and international financial sources, whether public or private, and from its own
city budgetary appropriations ;
b. Undertake the site development and construction of the Sports Complex, including its
road network, in accordance with the physical and technical infrastructure plans and specifications approved by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of the CITY OF DAVAO and UP's
BOR in compliance with UP's Land Use Plan;
II (3) Joint Obligations of the LINIVERSITY and the CITY OF DAVAO
a. Jointly undertake the negotiation with the informal settlers and other affected illegal
occupants of the 20-hectare project site; Provided, that the CITY OF DAVAO provide the
site therefor, and if necessary cause the removal and relocation of, and provide2
resettlement
M

such other assistance, for qualified informal settlers and occupants in accordance with applicable laws, rules and regulations.
b. Provide allocation for the Maintenance and Other Operating Expenses (MOOE) subject
to the availability of funds.
ARTICLE III _ MANAGEMENT OF THE SPORTS COMPLEX
III (l) There is hereby created a Joint Management Committee which shall have the following
powers and functions:
A. To formulate and adopt policies, plans, programs, guidelines, rules and regulations
conceming the following.
a. The financial and budgetary requirements/matters, generation of income and other resources, for the development, construction, operations, implementation, safekeeping,
maintenance and repair of the Sports Complex, any of its facilities and equipment, subject
to the approval of the Mayor of the CITY OF DAVAO and the Chancellor of UP Mindanao.
The budgetary requirements thereof shall be sourced from local, national, international financial sources, whether public or private, including from the parties own budgetary appropriations, and from revenues derived from sports events and activities held in the Sports
Complex, if any;
b. The management, administration, and operation of the Sports Complex subject to the
approval of the Mayor of the CITY OF DAVAO and the Chancellor of UP Mindanao;
c. The Parties shall have preferential right to use the facilities of the Sports Complex. The
Sports Complex shall be used by LIP for Physical Education and other academic purposes
pursuant to its mandate under R.A. No. 9500. The CITY OF DAVAO in case of use of the
facilities of the Sports Complex shall notify UP at least thirty (30) days prior to its use
thereof, to avoid conflict of schedule with UP on the use of the same, and
d. All fees and income from the use of the Sports Complex shall be used for the maintenance and upkeep of the Sports Complex.
B. To determine the necessary operational, personal and manpower requirements and other
matters relating thereon for the Sports Complex in accordance with such policies, guidelines,
rules and regulation formulated, and make and submit a quarterly report on these and other
related matters to the parties herein.
C. To create the appropriate organizational structure to administer and operate the activities
and business of the Sports Complex.
D. Prepare and/or cause the preparation of the Comprehensive Development Plan (CDP)
indicating among others specific strategies to ensure sustainability of the Sports Complex.
Such CDP shall be presented to the UP President and the UP Board or Regent and to the
Mayor and the City Council of the CITY OF DAVAO for approval.
E. Activities not related to academic or sports use of any ofthe facilities in the Sports Complex that violate the purpose of the UP under its charter of R.A. No. 9500 or that shall expose
the UP or its officials or employees to a violation of election laws and other laws shall not
be allowed, such as, but not limited to weddings, debuts, political campaigns and similar
activities. Prior registration of the use of the Sports Complex, or its facilities, shall be established by the Joint Management Committee.
III (2) The Joint Executive Management Committee shall be composed of six (6) regular
members and four (4) ex-offrcio membersT
w
aJ

i. Regular Members:
a. Three (3) members who shall be appointed by the Mayor of the CITY OF
DAVAO; and
b. Three (3) members who shall be appointed by the Chancellor of UP Mindanao.
ii. Ex-Officio Members:
a, The Mayor of the CITY OF DAVAO'
b. The Chancellor of UP Mindanao;
c. The Head of Sports and Human Kinetics of UP Mindanao,
d. The Head of Sports and Development of the CITY OF DAVAO."
The regular members shall serve for four (4) years; Provided, that they may be eligtble for
reappointment for two (2) years only after the expiration of their term. Provided further that,
upon the inception, of the regular members appointed, two (2) appointees from the City and
two (2) appointees from UP Mindanao shall serve for a term of two (2) years only. Members
of the Committee shall not appoint any person to represent them in the Comrnittee.
The regular and ex-officio members shall serve as such only co-terminously with the
authority/oflicial appointrng them, and/or until their successors are respectively appointed
and duly qualified.
The ex-officio members shall serve as members of the Committee as long as they are
occupying their respective principal positions. However, they are disqualified to be elected
Chairperson of the Committee.
The Chairperson of the Committee shall be rotated every two years between the appointee
of the Mayor of the CITYOF DAVAO and the appointee of the Chancellor of UPMindanao,
with the first Chairperson of the Committee to be elected by the members of the Committee.
The election of the Chairperson shall require majority votes of the members of the Committee.
In case of death, incapacity, disqualification, removal, andlor resignation of any member of
the Committee, his/her successor shall serve only for the unexpired term. Immediately thereafter, he/she shall be eligible for re-appointment but only two (2) years.
Members of the Committee are accorded with voting rights. In case of a tie, a vote from
either the Chancellor of UP Mindanao or the Mayor of CITY OF DAVAO must be acquired.
The determination of whose vote to break the tie will be done through drawing of lots.
ARTICLE TV - RIGHTS OF THE PARTIES
a. The Parties shall have preferential use, free of charge, of any of the sports facilities within
the Sports Complex in accordance with their respective legal mandates, but only insofar as
they relate to their respective academic, educational, sports and human kinetics programs. In
any event, revenues or income, if any, derived from the use of the Sports Complex shall be
allocated exclusively for the upkeep and maintenance of the Sports Complex.
b. The Parties or their duly designated representatives upon prior notice shall have the right, at
reasonable times, to enter the Sports Complex, and any of its facilities, to examine the same
and to make or submit recommendations for repairs, maintenance, improvement, security, and
fire prevention, or for any other purposes that they may deem necessary to ensure compliance
witfrthe highest operational safety and standard, subject to UP's policies and procedures.zT/

ARTICLE V - ARBITRATION
In case of any dispute that may arise in connection with this Agreement, the parties shall exert
best efforts towards an applicable settlement. In the event such dispute is not resolved amicably
within a period of thirty (30) days from the date of its occurrence, the parties shall settle the
same pursuant to the rules on Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR), whenever applicable.
ARTICLE YI _ EFFECTTVITY, TERM AND PRETER}IINATION
This Agreement shall take effect upon its signing, provided, however, that as far as the UP is
concerned, such signing is contingent upon the prior approval of the BOR and as for the CITY
OF DAVAO, the prior approval of the City Council. The term of the Agreement shall be for a
period of FIFTEEN (I5) years, renewal upon mutual agreement of the Parties.
Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time prior to its expiration, for a valid cause
and without prejudice to the ascertainment of liability or obligation to the other Party, by giving
at least one hundred eighty (180) calendar days notice in writing to the other Party.
ARTICLE VII - AMENDMENTS/ PARTIAL INVALIDITY
All amendments or revisions of any provision of this Agreement shall be upon the prior written
mutual consent of both parties subject to the execution of an appropriate instrument for the
purpose.
If any of the provisions in this Agreement is declared by a competent court or quasi-judicial
body to be null and void or unenforceable, the remaining provisions not affected by the defects
shall nevertheless continue to be in full force and effect without being impaired or invalidated
in any way.
ARTICLE VtrI _ NOTICES
All notices, requests, consents, and other documents required under this Agreement shall be
given or served to the other party either by personal delivery or by registered mail, or through
reputable courier services, or fax, properly addressed or sent as the case may be, to the party
or its designated agenUrepresentative.
ARTICLE D( _ ENTIRE CONTRACT
The parties acknowledge that this Agreement constitutes the entire Agreement between them
and shall supersede all other previous communications or contracts, oral or written, between
and among the parties with respect to the subject matter thereof.
