CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local goverrrment unit

"LGU").
WHEREAS, LGU issued the City Ordinance No. 0155-23 SERIES of 2023, entitled as
"Granting the Persons with disability, with Duly Issued ldentiJication Cards by the City
Government of Davao, Free Movie Privileges to All Cinemos in the City of Davao;"
WHEREAS, NCCC offers its cinema facility in cognizance with the aforementioned
ordinance by the LGU which is to recognize and give privilege to Persons with Disability (PWD)
by means of regulated free access to cinema;
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the Parties have
agreed to bind themselves as follows:
A. City Government of Davao shall:
l. Collaborate with NCCC for the implementation of the ordinance;
2. Provide any necessary assistance and support to uphold the smooth implementation of the
ordinance.
B. LTS Malls, Inc. shall:
l. Provide free access in NCCC Cinemas to Persons with Disability (PWD) that are bonafide
residents of Davao City;
2. Require the availing PWDs to present their PWD Identification Card and movie pass
booklet issued by Person with Disabilities Affairs Office (PDAO) - Davao City upon entry.
Free access is strictly non-transferrable;

3. Implement a monitoring sheet for registration or recording of PWD who availed the free
access;
4. Designate Monday of every week for PWDs to avail free movie pass during the I't

avail20o/o PWD discount for other movie schedules;
5. Allow free movie passes to regular movie screenings only, to exclude ATMOS, 3D,

can only be availed on its second week;
6. Allocate 5% (10 seats per theater) of the total seats for availing PWDs. For availing PWDs
on wheelchair, each Cinema can accommodate a maximum of six;
7. The free movie privilege is not applicable to other existing promotions. PWDs may only
choose either to avail their free movie or the existing promotion;
