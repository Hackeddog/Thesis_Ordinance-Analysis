This Addendum, executed by and between
PHILIPPINE STATISTICS AUTHORITY, a national government
agency created by virtue of Republic Act (RA) No. 10625 or the
"Philippine Statistical Act of 2013" with office address located at
PSA Complex, East Avenue, Diliman, Quezon City, duly
represented herein by RANDOLPH ANTHONY B. GALES,
Officer-in-Charge, with office address located at Ango Bldg., J.P.

PARTY,"
and

Bldg. San Pedro St., Davao City, represented herein by
SEBASTIAN Z. DUTERTE, Local Chief Executive, and
hereinafter referred to as'SECOND PARTY,'
WITNESSETH, that:
WHEREAS, in 2024, simultaneous conduct of the Census of Population and
Community-Based Monitoring System (2024 POPCEN-CBMS), under Republic Act
Nos. 10625 and 11315, respectively, were led by the FIRST PARTY to update the middecade inventory of the population of the entire Philippines and social registries of
national government agencies and LGUs for evidence-based project development and
prog ram implementation ;
WHEREAS, despite the integration of two statisticalactivities, the POPCEN remains a
PSA-funded and facilitated undertaking, while only CBMS operations in Local
Government Units within the 1st to 6th income classifications are PSA-funded and
facilitated;
WHEREAS, Highly Urbanized Cities shall fund their own CBMS implementation, and
the FIRST PARW shall assist them either by conducting CBMS activities on its behalf
or by providing technical assistance to ensure data quality;
WHEREAS, in May 2024, the parties entered into an agreement (hereinafter referred
to as the "Original Agreement"), designating the FIRST PARTY to lead the
implementation of the Community-Based Monitoring System (CBMS) on behalf of the
SECOND PARTY within its locality; and
WHEREAS, the parties hereby agree to extend the term of the OriginalAgreement and
conduct additional activities in accordance with the terms set out herein.
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the mutual covenants contained in
this Addendum, the parties agree as follows:

in this Extended Aqreement. whichever comes finst, which will begin

Addendum and its annexes and those of the Original Agreement, the terms and
provisions of this Addendum and its annexes shall supersede those provisions
of the Original Agreement with which they conflict.
