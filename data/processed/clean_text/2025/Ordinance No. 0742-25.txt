Ord. No. 0742-25
Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:
SECTION l.WEIE
- This Ordinance shall be known as'The PaniganTamugan Sub-Waterched Ordinance ol 2025.'
SECTION 2.
- The following are hereby declared
policies of the City:
a. To promote, improve, and safeguard the Panigan-Tamugan Watershed as the
main suface water source of the City's drinking water and is the largest
source of the City's suface water supply;
b. To effectively manage and protect the physical, biological, and socio-economic
attributes of the Panigan-Tamugan Watershed based on the assessment study
based on the masterplan as specified under SECTION 4 therein;
c. To ensure the Panigan-Tamugan Watershed's cleanliness, sustainable
ecological balance, rich biodiversity, and improve the quality of life of the
community; and
d. To recognize that the watershed area is a recharged area for the City's
aquifers which are sources of the City's drinking water that must be protected,
conserved, maintained, and managed for the continued and full enjoyment of
the present and future generations.
sEcTIoN3.rc.Forthepurposeofthisordinance,the
following terms are defined as follows:
a. "Critical Waterched" pertains to areas of land which show critical
degradation as to threaten their natural feature on which rainwater drains
from a river system to a common outlet such as a dam, lake or sea; or areas
where rain from a higher level of land flows down to a river, or stream then to
the Davao Gulf; or a topographically delineated area of land from which
rainwater can drain as sufface run-off via a specific river or stream system to
a common outlet point, which may be a dam, irrigation system or a municipal
water supply take-off point, or where a river or stream discharges water into a
larger river, stream or lake.
b. "Customaty Laws" refer to a body of written and/or unwritten rules,
usages, customs and practices traditionally and continually recognized,
accepted and observed by respective ICCs/IPs;
c. "Environmentally Critical Areas (ECA)" refer to those areas declared by
law as nationa! parks, watershed reserves, wildlife preserves and sanctuaries
and all other areas identified as such by Presidential Proclamation 2L46,
specifically in Items B, 1-11.
d. "Groundwater" refers to subsurface water that occurs beneath a water table
in soils and rocks, or in geological formations.
e. "Indigenous Cultural Communities/Indigenous Peoples (ICCs llPs)'
refer to groups of people or homogenous societies identified under Republic
Act No. 837L, or The Indigenous Peoples Rights Act (IPRA) of L99T" generally
consisting of the Obu Manuvu and other tribes within the PTW.

t. "Motorized equipment" refers to any machine that uses or is activated by a
motor, engine, or other power source. For the purpose of this Ordinance,
motorized equipment shall refer to chainsaws, drilling machines, sawmills or
pumping stations.
g. "Open dump" refers to a disposal area wherein the solid wastes are
indiscriminately thrown or disposed of without due planning and consideration
for environmental and health standards (R.A. No. 9003).
h. "Protected Areas" refer to identified portions of land and water set aside by
reasons of their unique physical and biological significance, managed to
enhance biological diversity and protected against destructive human
exploitation.
i. 'PUSAI(A" is the traditiona! Obu Manuvu practice of sanctiffing entities, living
or non-living, as "precious" or "sacred" because it has become priceless, from
the perspective of a family, clan, or tribe.
j. "Settlement" refers to formal and informal communities or built-up
residential areas where people prefer to live in and land areas classified, zoned
or convefted for current or future residential or housing development
purposes, including socialized housing zones for the homeless and
underprivileged.
k. "Suface Water" refers to waters (e.9., rivers, lakes, bays) which are open
to the atmosphere and subject to surface runoff.
l. "Tourism and Recreational Activities" refer to activities people do for fun
and enjoyment which shal! refer to trekking or hiking, swimming, camping,
mountain biking, running, canoeing, kayaking, rafting, tubing, for recreational
purposes.
m. *Waterched Areas" are areas of land which rainwater drains via a river
system to a common outlet such as dam, lake or the sea; an area where rain
from the highest point flows down to a river and ultimately to the Davao Gulf
or a topographical delineated area of land from which rainwater can drain as
suface run-off via a specific stream of river system to a common outlet point
which may be a dam, irrigation system or municipal water supply take-off
point, or where the stream or river discharges water into a larger river, stream
or lake.
n. "Watershed Management" is the process of guiding and organizing uses of
Iand and other resources in a watershed to provide desired goals and services
without adversely affecting the watershed areas and water resources.
o. 'Watershed Management Council (WMC)" refers to a Body which issues
policies and guidelines and sets the direction of collaboration in gathering,
processing and evaluating information on watershed areas in Davao City and
develops plans and strategies towards ensuring its health and sustainability.
SECTION 4. PANIGAN-TAMUGAN WATERSHED ASSESSMENT STUDY .

August L7, 2022, is hereby declared as the official document and masterplan for the
long-term protection of the Panigan-Tamugan Watershed and for other similar studies
to be conducted in the future for the preseruation and protection of the PaniganTamugan Watershed.
