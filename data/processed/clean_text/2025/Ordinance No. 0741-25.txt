MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:
This Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) made and entered into this 28th day of
October 2024 in the City of Manila, Philippines, by and between:
The PHILIPPINE SPORTS COMMISSION (PSC), a national
government agency created and existing by virtue of Republic Act
No. 6847, with principal address at Administration Building, Rizal
Memorial Sports Complex, Pablo Ocampo, Sr., St., Malate, Manila,
represented by its Chairman, RICHARD E. BAGHMANN, hereinafter
referred to as the PSG;
-andCITY OF DAVAO, a Local Government Unit (LGU) duly-organized
and existing by virtue of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines,
with office address at City Hall Bldg., San Pedro St., Davao City ,
represented herein by its City Mayor, SEBASTTAN Z. DUTERTE,
hereinafter referred to as the CD;
WITNESSETH, That:
WHEREAS, the PSC by virtue of R.A. No. 6847, encourages wide participation of
all sectors, government and private, in amateur sports promotion and development, and
provides incentives, recognitions and awards to deserving associations, athletes,
referees, game officials,coaches,trainers and other persons or entities involved in or
supporting sports development as may be permissable under the rules of amateurism;
WHEREAS, Executive Order (EO) No. 163 of 1994 established the Philippine
National Games (PNG) as a comprehensive sports development program and forms part
of the national policy for sports development at the grassroots level which also serves as a
recruitment base for sports specialization.
WHEREAS, the PSG hosted and organized the Philippine National Games (PNG)
2023 held in Metro Manila from December 17-22,2023;
WHEREAS, the PSG grants rewards and incentives to the LGU's who placed
within the top 5 based on the over-all official medal tally during the PNG Championships
2023 to be used for the LGU's grassroots sports promotion and development and talent
identification programs;
WHEREAS, the GD participated in the Philippine National Games (PNG) 2023 and
won 4th place in the over-all official medal tally;
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the above premises, their mutual
consent herein set forth, the parties have agreed to the following terms and conditions:
ARTICLE I
CONDITIONS OF THE GRANT AND DUTIES OF THE PARTIES
A. PSC sha!!:
1. Grant the CD the total amount of TWO MILLION PESOS (F2,000,000.00) as
cash incentive for winning 4th place in the over-all official medal tally during the
Philippine National Games (PNG) Championships 2023;
2. lVonitor the utilization of the cash incentive by the CD and ensure that the
incentive is used exclusively for the LGU's grassroots sports promotions and
development and talent identification programs;

3. Provide the necessary technical support to the CD in the planning,
identification, establishment, coordination and implementation of its grassroots
sports development and talent identification programs, which should be aligned
with PSC's sports development policy and framework to ensure proper
coordination and implementation.
B. The Gity of Davao shall:
1. Utilize the cash incentive provided by PSC exclusively for its grassroots sports
promotion and development and talent identification programs which should be
aligned with the grassroots sports development policy and framework of PSC;
2. Submit to PSC the CD's specific sports promotion and talent identification
programs for which the cash incentive shall be utilized, as well as its proposed
plans, projects and activities, the manner of execution and program
implementation, within the timeframe provided by PSC.
ARTICLE II
EFFECTIVITY
This MOA shall effect upon its signing, provided, however, that as far as PSC is
concerned, such signing is contingent upon the prior approval of its Board of
Commissioners.
This Agreement is subject to the usual government accounting and auditing rules
and regulations'
