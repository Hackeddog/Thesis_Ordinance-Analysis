WHEREAS, DAVAO REGION, designated as Region XI, is located in the southeastern portion of
Mindanao. It consists of 5 provinces, namely, Davao de Oro, Davao del Norte, Davao Oriental, Davao del
Sur, Davao Occidental, and 1- highly-urbanized city, Davao City;
WHEREAS, it is located in the Southeastern portign of the Island of Mindanao surrounding the
Davao Gulf. It is bounded on the North by the provinces of Surigao del Sur, Agusan del Sur, and Bukidnon,
on the East by the Philippine Sea; and on the West by the Central Mindanao Provinces;
WHEREAS, in terms of annual growth in the production value in the agriculture and fisheries
sector, it was recorded in Davao Region at 3.4 percent in CY 2A22;

WHEREAS, befofe the effects of African Swine Fever [ASF), Highly Pathogenic Avian Influenza
[HPAI), and Banana diseases that devastated the livestock and banana production in the region, Region

with other regions;

' to the present, and brought forth the decrease in hog production in CY 2021 and its continuous effect on
the hog sector and its allied industries up to the present especially among the smallholder hog farmers;
WHEREAS, the threat of avian influenza which once affected the Davao Region last CY 202?, and
incidences continuously reported in several neighboring regions of Davao, moreover, there is an influx
of poultry reported entering Region XI from other regions in Mindanao due to an increase in the number
of established dressing plants in Davao Region and closure of dressing plants in other regions as part of
strategic developments of some companies involved in the poultry industry;
WHEREAS, in the agricultural crop sector, the continued incidence of banana diseases especially
the fusarium wilt provokes a continuous threat to the banana industry up to the present;
WHEREAS, export commodities such as durian, coffee, and cacao, wherein Davao Region is among
the top producers are also atriskof pests and diseases such as mealybugs, scale insects, fruitrot, and

relation to trade and transport of fish and fishery products, aquatic wildlife, by-products, or
derivatives, the BFAR-XI implements regulations and enforcement measures to ensure sustainable
and legal fishing practices;
WHEREAS, the threat to aquatic animals through the introduction and spread of diseases may
endanger the health of aquatic animals and the production of local fishermen/farmers. Moreover,
the importance of local Local Transport Permit (LTP) and Health Certificate [HC) in every transport
of aquatic animals for traceability;
WHEREAS, the strategic geographical location of the region which also seryed as a hub for

vulnerability to the entry of various livestoch poultry and agricultural diseases and pests such as the
risk for ASF, Avian influenza [AI) and other economically important and transboundary animal diseases
(TADs) of animals and plants;

and 3 BFAR quarantine stations - located in Davao International Airport, at the Davao Fish Port
Complex, Toril, Davao Cify, and in the BFARXI Regional Office, Davao City;
WHEREAS, there are no properly established in-land agricultural and fisheries border control
facilities in the regional boundaries of Region XI;
WHEREAS, there are makeshift border control checkpoints established in several areas in the
region to provide temporary sites for the evaluation of documentary requirements and inspections of
vehicles transporting livestock and agricultural products and by-products; and fish and fish byproducts.
WHEREAS, these are the identified Regional and Provincial boundaries in Davao Region that need
to be secured, and border control facilities should be established;

SECTION 1. Obiectives. The obiectives of the Memorandum of Agreement are:
1.1 To strengthen regional border control measures in Region XI against the threat of plant and
animal pests and aquatic diseases particularly transboundary diseases by providing quarantine
checkpoints to monitor the incoming transport of animals and agricultural products, byproducts, and other agricultural, and natural resources, and fish and fishery products in Region
XI for any signs of pest and disease;
L.2 To provide an area for checking of completeness of regulatory requirements for the
transport/shipment of plants, animals, and other agricultural products and natural resources,
and fish and fishery products and aquatic wildlife, by-products or derivatives to IUUF;
1.3 To provide support to DA agencies and LGU's mandate in the implementation of disease
prevention, control, and eradication of plant and animal diseases (terrestrial and aquatic),
thereby, ensuring food security to its populace;
1.4 To strengthen the implementation of the regulatory agencies under the Department of
Agriculture for the enforcement of food safety standards and regulations in the primary and
post-harvest stages of food derived from animals including eggs and honey.
SECTION 2. Scope of Agreement -- 1.
This Memorandum of Agreement covers the
implementation of the establishment of the Regional Agricultural and Fisheries Border Control
Facility with Common Service Facilities from DA-RFO XI to the City Government of Davao City
including other technical and logistical support from BAI- VQS XI , BPI- PQS XI and BFARXI for the
operationalization and maintenance of the established Regional Agricultural and Fisheries Border
Control Facilities in Region XI in Brgy. Buda, Marilog District, Davao City.
