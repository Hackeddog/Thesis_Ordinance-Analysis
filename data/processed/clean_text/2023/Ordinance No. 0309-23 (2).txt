AN ORDINANCE GRANTING THE CREATION OF THE PROJECT
IMPLEMENTATION TEAM RELATIVE TO THE HOUSING
PROJECT AGREEMENTS ENTERED INTO BY THE CITY WITH
THE NATIONAL HOUSING AUTHORIW

Ord. No. 0309-23
'
Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. EtE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
GRANTING THE CREATION OF THE PROJECT IMPLEMENTATION TEAM
RETATIVE TO THE HOUSING PROJECT AGREEMENTS ENTERED INTO BY
THE CITY WITH THE NATIONAL HOUSING AUTHORITY".
SECTION 2. PERTINENT PROVISIONS UNDER THE LOCAL
GOVERNMENT CODE OF 1991 -
"SECTION 16. General Welfare. - Every local government unit shall
exercise the power expressly granted, those necessarily implied
therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its
efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the
promotion of the general welfare. Within their respective territorial
jurisdictions, local government units shall ensure and suppoft, among
other things, the preseruation and enrichment of culture, promote health
and safety, enhance the right of the people to a balanced ecology,
encourage and support the development of appropriate and self-reliant
scientific and technological capabilities, improve public morals, enhance
economic prosperity and social justice, promote full employment among
their residents, maintain peace and order, and preserue the comfoft and
convenience of their inhabitants.
SECTION 455. Chief Executive; Powers, Duties and Compensation.-
(a) The city mayor, as chief executive of the city government, shall
exercise such powers and perform such duties and functions as provided
by this Code and other laws.
(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the purpose of
which is the general welfare of the city and its inhabitants pursuant to
SECTION 16 of this Code, the city mayor shall:
(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign in its behalf
all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other documents upon
authority of the SANGGUNIANG panlungsod or pursuant to law or
ordinance."
SECTION 3. EWBEIIIU - To ensure the implementation of all housing
projects entered into with the National Housing Authority (NHA), the Housing Project
Implementation Team (HPIT) is hereby created to be composed of the following:
1) Office of the City Planning and Development Coordinator
2) Office of the City Engineer
3) Office of the City Building Official
4) City Environment and Natural Resources Office
5) Davao City Water District
6) Davao Light and Power Company
7) National Housing Authority
B) Department of Human Settlements and Urban Development

Ord. No. 0309-23
For housing projects involving the Indigenous People, the HPIT may
include the:
9) National Commission on Indigenous People (NCIP); and
10) The concerned IP Tribe covered by the project.
The City Planning and Development Office, as the lead Office of the
project, is hereby authorized to add members to the HPIT as the need
arises.
SECTION 4. FUNCTIONS - The HPIT shall have the following functions, but
not limited to:
a. Ensure the implementation of the various housing projects;
b. Coordinate with all stakeholders to achieve the completion of the
projects;
c. Pedorm all other acts necessary to carry out the abovestated
functions.
SECTION 5. FUNDING - The HPIT may be provided with an appropriate
operating fund as may be legally allowable.
SECTION 6. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall cover the creation of the
Project Implementation Team relative to the Housing Project Agreements entered
into by the City of Davao with the National Housing Authority.
SECTION 7. SEPARABILIW CLAUSE - If for any reason, any SECTION or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof which are not affected thereby, shall continue to be in full force
and effect.
SECTION 8. EFFECTMTY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately
upon approval.
ENACTED, on the 10th day of October 2023, by a unanimous vote of all the
Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
