A TRACEABLE RESEARCH PROCESS

Method & limitations

From verified text to annual shares

  1. Freeze the corpus

    Hash cleaned text, preserve human year corrections, audit duplicates, and retain the fixed 2016–2025 eligible corpus.

  2. Build both representations afresh

    Rebuild TF-IDF/SVD and recompute frozen Legal-BERT embeddings using all content chunks. No encoder fine-tuning.

  3. Discover broad topics

    UMAP → HDBSCAN → c-TF-IDF, with six new fits across two models and three seeds.

  4. Weight each document equally

    For each document, divide units in a topic by all its modeled units. Average those fractions across documents in each resolved year, including outliers.

Read the findings responsibly

  • Coarse-grained settings are the team's chosen broad-theme analysis—not a proven universal winner.
  • Keyword titles are descriptive drafts. Broad, procedural, OCR-heavy, and mixed clusters remain visible.
  • The archive is uneven; normalized percentages do not make a small year representative.
  • Detected sections and fallback documents form the modeling units. Same-number text variants and extraction flags remain limitations.
  • Three seeds measure sensitivity, not population uncertainty or statistical significance.
  • Historical Conservative evidence is preserved separately. Its expert-review materials do not validate this new run.
