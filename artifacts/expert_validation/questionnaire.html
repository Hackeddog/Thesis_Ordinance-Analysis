Section D — Temporal Trends and Interpretation

Read the 2016–2025 annual chart/table, yearly keywords, source examples, and proposed interpretation. Each source document contributes weight one, divided among its topics. A topic's annual percentage is its share of that year's document weight, not a count of ordinances exclusively about it or a measure of city activity. Unassigned text stays in the denominator.

Archive sizes differ: 2025 has 32 modeled source documents. Years are resolved study-year assignments, not necessarily independently rechecked enactment dates.
