Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. EtE - This Ordinance shal! be known as "AN ORDINANCE
ESTABLISHING THE SHELTER FOR IP WATCHERS AND RUNNERS OF
PATIENTS FROM THE INDIGENOUS PEOPLES AND MUSLIMS BELONGING
TO THE ELEVEN TRIBES OF DAVAO CITY NAMELY: KLATA, ATA,

SAMA, MARANAO, AND MAGUINDANAO (KAMOTT-TKASAMMA)'.
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - The State shall take measures,
with the participation of the Indigenous Cultural Communities/Indigenous Peoples
(ICCs/IPs) and Muslim Cultural Tribes concerned, to protect their rights and
guarantee respect for their cultural integrity, and to ensure that members of the
ICCs/IPs benefit on an equal footing from the rights and oppoftunities which
national laws and regulations grant to other members of the population. The State
also recognizes its obligations to respond to the strong expression of the ICCs/IPs
for cultural integrity by assuring maximum ICC/IP participation in the direction of
education, health, as well as other seruices of ICCs/IPs, in order to render such
seruices more responsive to the needs and desires of these communites.
SECTION 3.SIElgre - Qualified Indigenous Peoples (IPs) and Muslim
tribe beneficiaries residing in far-flung areas of Davao City may avail of the seruices
offered by the Ordinance. In establishing an operationa! inpatient shelter facility for
Indigenous Peoples and Muslims (IPs/Muslims), the following are considerable
objectives to prioritize the well-being, cultural sensitivity, and specific needs of this
community:
1. Ensure that the facility respects and incorporates Indigenous cultural
practices, traditions, and languages to create a culturally safe and
welcoming environment;
2. Provide access to healthcare seruices that are sensitive to the unique
health challenges faced by Indigenous Cultural Communities (ICCs),
including addressing issues related to historical trauma and disparities
in healthcare access by offering a range of seruices that address not
only physical health but also mental, emotional, and spiritual wellbeing, reflecting indigenous concepts of holistic health;
3. Implement trauma-informed care principles to create a safe and
suppottive environment for Indigenous and Muslim individuals who
may have experienced historical or personal trauma by providing
counselling seruices that is culturally relevant to the individuals
seeking help;
4. Involve Indigenous and Muslim community members in the planning,
operation, and decision-making processes to ensure that the facility
meets their specific needs and preferences;
5. Collaborate with Indigenous and Muslim-led organizations and tribal
authorities to strengthen the network of support and resources
available to the community;
6. Ensure that the facility's physical environment, design, and artwork
are culturally appropriate and reflect Indigenous and Muslim
aesthetics and values;

7. Develop a sustainable operational and funding plan that ensures the
facility's long-term existence and effectiveness in meeting the needs

8. To supplement other government seruices to the eleven (11) major
tribes not mentioned in this Ordinance.
SECTION 4.
a) Inpatient Indigenous People and Muslim's shelter - This is a facility or
program designed to provide culturally sensitive, community-based
support and seruices to Indigenous and Muslim individuals who do not
require or prefer not to stay overnight in a residentiat or inpatient
shelter. These shelters are often established to address the specific
needs of Indigenous and Muslim populations, recognizing their unique
cultural, historical, and social circumstances;
b) Shelter- A facility of the City Government of Davao providing cover or
protection from weather and danger to the member of the Indigenous
Peoples and Muslim Tribes belonging to the eleven (11) major tribes
of Davao City.
