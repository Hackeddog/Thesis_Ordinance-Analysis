Ord. No. 0390-24
Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. EtE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
FOR THE TEMPORARY CLOSURE OF ROAD TO VEHICULAR TRAFFIC OF
SPECIFIED STREETS ON THE TIME AND SET.UP LOCATION MENTIONED
HEREIN IN CONNECTION WITH THE UPCOMING MASS LAUNCHING AND
PRAYER RALLY AT SAN PEDRO SQUARE".
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POUCY - SECTION 21 (c) of Republic Act
No. 7L60, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides that
"any nationa! or !oca! road, alley, park, or square may be temporarily closed during
an actual emergency, or fiesta celebrations, public rallies, agricultura! or industrial
fairs".

mass launching and prayer rally, the ZEIEN MANAGEMENT GROUP requested for a
temporary road closure of specified streets on the time and set-up location as
follows:
o January 26,2024, Friday, 10PM, until January 29,2024, Monday, 5AM:
o JanuaU 27,2024, Saturday, from 8AM to January 29,2024,5AMt
San Pedro Street.
o Janudry 27, 2024, Saturday, from 8PM to January 29, 2024, 5AM:

upon approval.
