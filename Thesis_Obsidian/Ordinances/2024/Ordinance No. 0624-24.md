ARTICLE I
GENERAL PROVISIONS
SECTION 1. TITLE
This Ordinance shall be known as the
"INSTITUTIONALIZATION OF THE DAVAO CITY TUSTICE ZONE".
SECTION 2. SAIE-EW It is the policy of the State to promote social justice
in all phases of national development. It also values the dignity of every human person
and guarantees full respect for human rights. It also upholds that no person shall be
deprived of life, liberty, or property without due process of law, nor shall any person be
denied the equal protection of laws.
SECTION 3. DEFINffiON OF TERMS - As used in this Ordinance
A. lustice Sector Coordinating Council (JSCC) - The governing body composed of
the Chief Justice of the Supreme Couft of the Philippines, the Secretary of Justice,
and the Secretary of the Interior and Local Government. JSCC determines the
policy direction and approves recommended plans, actions and projects for the
lustice Zones.
B. Justice Zone - It is an area or locality where a minimum number of inter-agenry
coordinative reforms, specified by the Justice Sector Coordinating Council (JSCC)
Technical Working Group (TWG) on Processes and Capacity Building, are present,
rendering that area or locality compliant, based on the selection and rating criteria
established by JSCC. (Glossary, Philippine Development Plan 2023-2028). It
exemplifies a locality where the Criminal Justice System is working to the people's
satisfaction because the delivery of justice is responsive and real-time. It may be
a municipality, city, province or an entire region. It refers to a local area where the
justice sector agencies identiff justice issues and consider sector-based solutions
to address them. It aims to invigorate coordinative strategies among the
institutions, ensure that complex institutional reforms are effectively linked and
sequenced, and communicate such measures to the different stakeholders of the
justice sector, and the public in general. (JSCC Joint Resolution No. 02-2022).
C. Justice Zone Convenor - A representative of a justice sector agency who leads
the Justice Zone administratively in the planning and implementation of Justice
Zone programs and projects. The Convenor communicates and coordinates on
behalf of the Justice Zone with JSCC, JSCC-TWGs, other concerned and relevant
agencies and institutions in their localities, including the Iocal government unit.

ARTICLE III
FUNCTIONS OF THE DAVAO CITY IUSTICE ZONE
SECTION 1. POLICY FORMUIATION - The Davao City Justice Zone shall
formulate measures aimed at improving the delivery of just, responsive, and accountable
justice services in the city. Such measures may be raised for consideration of the
Sanggunian, for appropriate local legislation and budgets or to the Justice Sector
Coordinating Counci! (JSCC). Al! such measures must conform and not contravene the
policy directives set forth by JSCC, the policies of the member institutions, and existing
laws and regulations.
SECTION 2. PIANNING AND PROGRAMMING - In accordance with policy
directions and resolutions of the JSCC, the Davao City Justice Zone shall prepare plans,
programs, and services with accompanying budgets aimed at operationalizing measures to
improve the delivery of just, responsive, and accountable justice services in the city.
Measures within the purview of the Davao City Justice Zone are, but not limited to, the
following:
a. Davao City Swift Justice Program to improve efficiency in the administration of
justice, addressing issues in the justice system, such as clogged dockets, and
ensuring that all pillars of the justice qystem work together to promote justice
by disposing cases expeditiously;
b. Improving access to justice of the poor and marginalized, especially women,
children, indigenous peoples, people of diverse sexual orientation and gender
identity and expression (SOGIE), among others;
c. Enabling the barangay justice system, as well as customary and traditional
justice systems to uphold rights-based approaches and build links with the
formal justice mechanisms; and
d. Rehabilitation and reintegration of Persons Deprived of Libefi (PDL) into
society, among others.
