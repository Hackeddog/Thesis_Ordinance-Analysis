Ord. No. 0552-24
Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "DABAWENYO
DCPLINADO FOR THE FUTURE GENERATION".
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POUCY - Pursuant to SECTION 16 of
Republic Act No. 7t60, otherwise known as the "Local Government Code of 1991",
the City Government of Davao shall exercise the powers expressly granted, those
necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate or
incidental for its efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential
to the promotion of general welfare; and shall ensure and suppoft preseruation and
enrichment of culture, promote safety, improve public morals, enhance social
justice, maintain peace and order and preserve the comfoft and convenience of
their inhabitants.
Republic Act No. 10533 - otherwise known as "An Act Enhancing the
Philippine Basic Education System by Strengthening its Curriculum and Increasing
the Number of Years for Basic Education and Appropriating Funds Therefor and
Other Purposes" in SECTION 5. (h) Curriculum Development states that "The DepEd
shall formulate the design and details of the enhanced basic education curriculum.
The curriculum shall be flexible enough to enable and allow schools to localize,
indigenize and enhance the same based on their respective educational and social
contexts. The production and development of locally produced teaching materials
shall be encouraged and approval of these materials shall devolve to the regional
and division education units".
Likewise, the City Government of Davao aims to ensure that the people of
Davao are empowered, protected, secure, gender and culturally sensitive to attain
the best quality of life. Davao City aims to cultivate a disciplined and law-abiding
citizenry, starting from the youngest member of society.
SECTION 3. OBJECTIVES
The objectives of this Ordinance are as follows:
a. To develop among children and the youth an authentic appreciation
of their sense of moral responsibility to their fellow citizens,
democratic institutions, and human society.
b. To empower the children and youth as future adults for them
to understand, protect and help promote the common good as
law-abiding citizens through self-discipline.
c. To enable the children and youth to understand, reflect, and fully
discern the role that social media and modern technology play in their
lives.
d. To guide the children and youth in realizing their duties as stewards
of the environment and the good of the future generation.
e. To form the children and youth into individuals who do not
discriminate against others, especially the poor, and respect women,
the elderly, Indigenous Peoples and the LGBTQ community.
