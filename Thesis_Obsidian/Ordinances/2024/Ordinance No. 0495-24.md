Ord. No. 0495-24
Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
IMPOSING ZERO PERCENT (Oo/o) TAX ON THE LOCAL BUSINESS TA)( OF
THE JAPANESE OFFICIAL DEVELOPMENT ASSISTANCE.FUNDED
PROJECTS IN DAVAO CITY SUBJECT TO ALL LAWS AND EXISTING LEGAL
RULES AND REGULATIONS".
SECTION 2. TAX RATE - A zero percent (0olo) tax on the local business tax
of the contractors and other independent contractors under Ordinance No. 0291L7, as amended by Ordinance No. OL47-L9, shall be imposed against a project
proven to be funded by the Japan-official Development Assistance.
SECTION 3. CONDITIONS - In order to be granted the privilege imposed
in SECTION 1, the applicant must submit to the City Treasurer the following:
1. confirmation from the Ambassador of Japan of the inclusion of
the project in the current and updated list of Japan-oDA
funded projects; and
2. Proof of payment of tax obligations prior to the enactment of
this Ordinance.
The applicant may, in the absence of the above-stated documents, submit
such other documents as may be prescribed by the City Treasurer in order to be
granted the privilege as prescribed in SECTION 2.
The burden of proof lies upon the applicant to prove the factual basis of its
claim for application under this Ordinance. In case of doubt, this Ordinance shall be
strictly construed against the applicant.
SECTION 4. DUTY OF THE CITY TREASURER - The City Treasurer shall
be responsible to:
1. Provide for an official list of documents necessary to prove the
authenticity of statements presented by the applicant.
2. Ensure the inclusion of the projects to be exempt in the official list of
Japan-ODA funded projects; and
3. Issue a clearance stating the fact that the applicant has no standing
tax obligations with the City.
SECTION 5. REPORTING REqUIREMENT - Applicants granted the Zero
Percent (0olo) tax privilege shall submit an annual report to thJ City Treasurer,
detailing the progress and financial status of the Japan-ODA funded project.
SECTION 6. REPEALING CLAUSE All Ordinances or poftions of
Ordinances in conflict with this Ordinance, or inconsistent with the regutations of
this Ordinance, are hereby repealed to the extent necessary to give this Ordinance
full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTMW - This Ordinance shall take effect FIFTEEN (15)
DAYS after its publication in a newspaper of general circulation.
