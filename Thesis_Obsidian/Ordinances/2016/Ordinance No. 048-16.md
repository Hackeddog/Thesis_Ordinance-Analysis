Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled that:

SECTION 1. TITLE.- This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR
AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAG, THE DEED OF
DONATION TO BE EXECUTED BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF

THE "FISHERY RESOURCE MANAGEMENT PROGRAM OF MUNICIPAL FISHERY
DEVELOPMENT PROJECT" UNDER THE BOTTOMS-UP BUDGETING PROGRAMS
FOR CY 2014";

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY. - SECTION 455, {b) (1) (wi) of the
Lecal Government Code provides:

"For efficient, effective and economical governance the
purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its
inhabitants pursuant fo SECTION 16 of this Code, the city
mayor shali represent the city in all its business
transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and
obligations, and such other documents upon authority of
the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod or pursuant to law or
ordinance";

SECTION 3. AUTHORITY. - The City Mayor is heraby granted legislative
authority to sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davac, the Deed of
Donabon to be executed by and between the City Government of Davao and Samahang
Mananagat sa Leon Garcia (SAMALGA), for the Fishery Resource Management Program
4 Municipal Fishery Development Project under the Bottoms-up Budgeting Programs

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. - if any part of this Ordinance is
deciated unconstitutional, the remaining part unaffected shall continue to remain valid

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY CLAUSE.- This Ordinance shall take effect
immediately upon approval.

ENACTED, September 13, 2016, by a majority vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian present.
