entered into by the parties herein.

follows:

critical function of govemment services; and provide efficient and effective ICT
infrastructure and information systems to the public, the following policies are
adopted:

support and implement the Project without delay;
b. Work together in expanding site coverage for free Wi-Fi Intemet access at
public places within the locality;
c. Provide free Wi-Fi Intemet access in public places without interruption
except in certain circumstances such as force majeure, technical issues and

ICT Office;
