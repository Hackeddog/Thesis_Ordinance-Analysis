DECEMBER 1, 2016, AT ABREEZA AYALA MALL, THIS CtTY"l
SECTION 2. COVERAGE. - The exemption applies only to Davao-based
exhibitors and solely refers to the payment of Mayor's Permit Fee under Article 26,
SECTION 22t paragraph (d) of the 2005 Revenue Code of the City of Davao;
SECTION 3. PERIOD AND LIMITATION. - The exemption shall be from
November 25, 20L6 to December 1, 20t6, only AND that in no case shall the organizers
and exhibitors be exempt from compliance with other provisions of the Signage
Ordinance of Davao City and other applicable laws and Ordinances;
SECTION 4. EFFECTMTY- This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval;
ENACTED, on the 22nd day of November, by a unanimous vote of all the
