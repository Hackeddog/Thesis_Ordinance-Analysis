WITNESSETH:
WHEREAS, the promotion of good governance and the maintenance
of peace and order are principles shared by the leaders and people of the
cities of Davao and Bacoor.
WIIEREAS, during a meeting conducted in Davao City on 2O August
2015, the local government officials of both cities agreed on the
development of a mechanism that would allow the officials of both cities to
cooperate with each other for the promotion of good governance and the
maintenance of peace and order for the common benefit of the people of
the City of Bacoor and the City of Davao.
WHEREAS, the elected government officials of both cities have
agreed that the best way to ensure the success of such cooperation is the
execution of a Sister City Agreement between the said LGUs.
NOW THEREFORE, in view of the above, the parties are executing
this Agreement subject to the following terms and conditions:
Areas of Cooperation
The parties agree to cooperate on the following areas of
