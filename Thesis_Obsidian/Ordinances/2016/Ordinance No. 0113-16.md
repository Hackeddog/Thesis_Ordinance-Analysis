Whereas, the Department of the Interior and Local Govemment (DILG), through the
Local Government Academy (LGA), is mandated by law to strengthen the capacities of the
grassroots Civil Society Organization (CSOs) to become effective partners of the National
government in the development of local plans and budgets under its Bottom-up Budgeting
(BUB) Program;
Whereas, the SIAD Initiatives in Mindanao Convergence for Asset Reform and
Regional Development (SIMCARRD) Inc. is a Non-Government Organization which provides
capacity building programs to the civil Society Organization (CSOs);
Whereas, the SIAD Initiatives in Mindanao Convergence for asset Reform and
Regional Development (SIMCARRD) Inc. has the knowledge, expertise and available training
ms fit for the capacity needs of the CSOs in the target LGU;

Whereas, the LGU is ready and willing to enter into an Agreement with the DILGRegional Office and the Institution concerning the implementation of the CSO Capacity
Building Project under the Bottom up budgeting (BUB) Program and to comply with all the
requirements as may be required;
Whereas, the concerned DILG Regional Office and the LGU will ensure the effective
implementation of the project;
Now thereforg for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the Parties hereto
agree to enter into this MOA under the following terms and conditions, thus;
Article I- Selection of the Institution
L. Subject to herein attached term of reference, the DILG-RO XI shall select the "
Institution" which has the capability to perform the following activities and to deliver
the corresponding outputs, to wit;
a. Develop a Detailed Work and Financial Plan for the capacity building projec[
b. Assess the capacity needs of the CSOs in the target LGUs;
c. Develop a training framework and design for the CSOs in target LGU/s including
training modules and all other learning materials needed for the training;
d. Prepare the terminal report and evaluation summary of the training;
e. Propose knowledge management strategies to sustain in the gains of the
projects;
f. Recommend next steps to ensure continued capacity development of the CSOs.
Article II a- Rights and Responsibilities of the Institution
The Institution hereby agrees to undertake the following responsibilities:
1. Organize a training team closely coordinate with the DILG-RO XI on the conduct of
capacity assessment and customization of training modules and others learning
materials;
2. In coordination with the DILG RO XI and the LGU, the Institution shall develop a
work and financial plan that will articulate the activities and fund requirements of the
project;
3. Assess the capacity needs of the CSOs in the target LGU through meetings,
consultations and discussions with them. The result of the capacity needs assessment
shall be used as basis in developing the appropriate training programs for the CSOs;
4. Develop a training framework and design for the CSOs in target LGU including
training modules and all other learning materials needed for the training;

5. Implement the training program to a minimum of 80 CSO participants in the target
LGU to be composed of LPRAT members, basic sector representatives, members of
LSBs, and registered participants of the CSOs assembly;
6. Arrange the logistical requirements of the trainings (i.e. training venue,
accommodation, meals and transportation);
7. Administer the training program for its entirety; the institution may subcontract but
limited to technical, logistical assistance and, resource person/s, and / or facilitators;
8. Prepare Terms of Reference and contracts for its subcontractors, resource person,
facilitators, and any auxiliary personnel and/ or organizations relative to the conduct
of the training program;
9. Prepare and submit a terminal report to DILG-RO XI following the prescribed
guidelines of the Local Government Academy (LGA);
L0. Prepare the evaluation report of the knowledge gains of the participants of the
training in coordination with the DILG-RO XI, M&E focal person/s
11. Submit the financial report to the DILG RO XI for the proper liquidation of the
projects funds;
1.2. Propose strategies to document and share the knowledge materials produced from
the conduct of the trainings; and,
13. Recommend an action plan and/or next steps to ensure continued capacity
development of the CSOs.
Article II b-Rights and Responsibilities of the Local Govemment Unit (LGU)
The Local Govemment Unit shall undertake the following responsibilities:
1. The LGU shall designate a "Focal Person" Who shall act as the single point of contact
with respect to any communications between the Parties. The LGU may likewise
designate any staff, as the need arises, who shall assist the DILG-RO and the
Institution in the implementation of the project;
2. Provide all documents pertinent to the project and other related BUB capacity
Building activities;
3. Effectively manage and promptly deliver the outputs and deliverables of the project;
4. Make available appropriate resources, including personnel and office space for the
use of the training team;
5. Assist the Institution and DILG RO in the selection of the CSO participants to the
training/s;
6. Support the adoption of proposed knowledge management strategies and next
steps to ensure continued capacity development of CSOs; and,
7. Review, monitor and record the projects outputs/results.
