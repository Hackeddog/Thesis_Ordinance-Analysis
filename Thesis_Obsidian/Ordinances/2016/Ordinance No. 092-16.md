Contents of Agreement
Developing a joint model project in Davao with the following themes to
become a model for intercity cooperation in the Philippines,
(i) Development of the city environment focusing on waste management
(ii) Creation of a low-carbon society which is instrumental to the
development of a green economy
lntensifying the participation of the private sector, such as enterprises and
universities in both cities, and
Encouraging support from both national governments and international
agencies to realize the model project.
(3)
2. Expense burden
The cooperation specified in the preceding article shall be implemented within
the limits of the budgetary measures of both cities. The Agreement imposes no
obligation for any new specific expense burden on the other party.
3. Term of validity
The term of validity of the Agreement shall be three (3) years from the conclusion
hereof. However, the Agreement shall be renewed for another year under the
same terms and conditions unless either party gives written notice of its intention
to terminate the Agreement one (1) month prior to the expiration of the term of
validity, and the same shall apply thereafter.
4. Others
Specific details and matters not mentioned above will be further discussed by
both cities.
