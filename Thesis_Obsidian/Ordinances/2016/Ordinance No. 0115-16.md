BY AND AMONG
The DEPARTMENT OF TOURISM, herein represented by its Regional Director,
Roberto P. Alabado, lll, with office address at Rm. 512, LANDCO Corporate Center Bldg,
J.P. LaurelAvenue, Davao City and herein referred to as "the DOT-Regional Office Xl."
The DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS AND HIGHWAYS, herein represented by its
Regional Director for Region Xl, Mariano R. Alquiza, CESO lll, with office address at

The LOGAL GOVERNMENT UNIT OF THE GITY OF DAVAO, herein represented by its

WHEREAS, the Philippine Development Plan (PDP) for 2011 to 2016 under the Aquino
Administration aims to contribute to inclusive growth by accelerating the provision of safe,
efficient, cost-effective, and sustainable infrastructure and promoting key industries such as
manufacturing, agriculture and fisheries, and tourism;
WHEREAS, the DOT is the lead Philippine government agency responsible for encouraging,
promoting, and developing tourism as a major socio-economic activity to generate foreign
currency and employment and to spread the benefits of tourism to both the private and the public
sectors in accordance with the NationalTourism Development Plan (NTDP);
WHEREAS, the DPWH is the lead Philippine govemment agency responsible for the planning,
design, construction and maintenance of infrastructure, especially national highways, flood
control and water resources development systems, and other public works in accordance with
national development objectives;
WHEREAS, the LGU, as provided under Republic Act (RA) No. 7160, otherwise known as the
Local Government Code of 1991, assumes the primary responsibility for the delivery and
provision of basic services and facilities, including local roads, and the improvement of the
quality of life of its constituents towards the achievement of the PDP goals;
WHEREAS, Chapter 3, Article One, SECTION 25.b. of Local Government Code of 1991 states that
"National agencies and offlces with project implementation functions shall coordinate with one
another and with the local government units concemed in the discharge of these functions. They
shall ensure the participation of local government units both in the planning and implementation
of said national projects;"
WHEREAS, the DOT and the DPWH are currently implementing the DOT-DPWH Convergence
Program for Tourism Road lnfrastructure Project Prioritization (TRIPP), also known as the
Tourism Road Convergence Program - a Joint undertaking of the identification, evaluation,
prioritization, and provision of tourism infrastructure - which includes local road projects - in
priority tourist destination areas in the Philippines in support of the goals and targets of the
NTDP and the PDP; and
