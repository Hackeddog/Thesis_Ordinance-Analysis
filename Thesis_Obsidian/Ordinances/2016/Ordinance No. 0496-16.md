MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:
This AGREEMENT made and executed by and between
MASICAP MSME DEVELOPMENT FOIJNDATION, INC., a foundation duly organized and
existing under and by virtue of the laws of the Philippines with principal office located at I /F DBP Bldg.,
C.M. Recto Avenue, Davao City, represented herein by MS. LEOSA NANETTE AGDEPPA, in her
capacity as President thereof, hereinafter referred to as the FOLI-NDATION,
-andCITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, with office located at A. Pichon Street, Davao City, represented
herein by HON. RODRIGO R. DUTERTE, in his capacity as City Mayor, herein referred to as LGUDAVAO CITY:
WITNESSETH:
WHEREAS, the FOUNDATION re-launched the Medium & Small Scale Industries Coordinated
Action Program II (MASICAP II), following the same concept of the previously successful MASICAP
program of the Department of Industry in 1974-1980 which harnessed the idealism, volunteerism and
service orientation of graduating Business Administration, Commerce and Economics students trained to
assist micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs) in the preparation of feasibility studies and related
documents for loan I grant applications with funding institutions;
WHEREAS, the FOUNDATION started MASICAP II pilot program in five provinces/ cities in
Southern Mindanao in August 2002 with the approval of the Commission of Higher Education (CHED),
cooperation of selected participating schools, endorsements of local government officials and private
business contributions and funding mainly by the Chairperson of the foundation with limited
contributions and volunteer works by MASICAP I alumni;
WHEREAS, results of MASICAP II pilot operations demonstrated its relevance and effectiveness
in assisting small enterprises obtain loans/ grants to start up / expand their business operations, resulting
to the program's continuance / expansion in several provinces / cities in Mindanao for succeeding Fiscal
Years;
WHEREAS, the LGU-DAVAO CITY agrees to sign a memorandum of Agreement to provide
funds for the continuance of MASICAP program operations in the city for the Calendar Year 2015;
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises, the FOUNDATION and LGUDAVAO CITY, do hereby COVENANT and AGREE:
l. That the FOUNDATION shall provide one (1) team of three (3) technical personnelfrom among
those trained by the FOLINDATION who shall assist prospective proponenVs of MSME within
the City of DAVAO as its priority in the preparation of project feasibility studies and documents
necessary for the availment of loans and/or assistance from the Cooperative Financial Assistance
of the City, banks and other funding agencies;
2. That the LGU shall pay the services of the team assigned in its area ofjurisdiction in the amount
of THREE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS (Php 300,000.00), Philippine Currency, per year
which amount shall be remitted to the FOUNDATION, without need of demand, at the latter's
office in Davao City, not later than the end of the year 2015;
3. That the FOI-INDATION shall identif the projects seeking assistance from the team assigned in
the herein LGU and shall submit from time to time a list of such project
