SECTION 1. TITLE. This Ordinance shall be known as "Anti-Sexual Abuse and
Exploitation Against Children Ordinance of Davao City.
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICIES - Davao City recognizes the vital
role of the youth in nation-building enshrined in the 1987 Philippine Constitution. It shall
promote and protect their physical, moral, spiritual, intellectual and social well-being.
Thus, it shall be the policy of Davao City to defend the rights of the children and
provide special protection from all forms of sexual abuse that are prejudicial to their
development. Specifically, the City shall provide comprehensive measures and strategies
in combating the proliferation of sexual abuse against children by engaging and
harmonizing the efforts of governmental and non-governmental agencies.
The City shall continue its tireless effort in giving priority to the rights of the
children especially in terms of protection which led the City in consistently winning awards
as one of the best child-friendly cities in the country, among others.
SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS.
1. Center-based programs - refer to the programs in an establishment
where people can seek to avail strategic programs, seruices and
interuentions developed for purposes of protecting children against sexual
abuse.
2. Child (Children) - for purposes of this Ordinance, child (children) refers
to person(s) below eighteen (18) years of age or those over but unable to
fully take care of themselves and protect themselves from sexual abuse
because of physical and/or mental disability condition. (CWC 2019, with
minor edits)
3. Child (Children) at high-risk against rape - refers to a child who is
exposed to a higher level of danger against sexual abuse compared to other
children due to risk factors and conditions affecting him or her.
4. Child Pornography - the unlawful or prohibited acts defined and
punishable by Republic Act No. 9775 or the Anti-Child Pornography Act of
2009, committed through a computer system: Provided, That the penalty to
be imposed shall be one (1) degree higher than that provided for in Republic
Act No. 9775.
5. Community-based programs - refer to the programs provided by
persons, offices or organizations through approaching the communi$l with
strategic programs, seruices and interuentions developed for purposes of
protecting children against sexual abuse.
6. Consciousness building - an approach to a child in awakening and/or
improving his or her state of mindspecifically in realizing the evil sought by
sexual abuse and his or her vulnerability to it.
7. Cybersex against a child - the willful engagement, maintenance, control,
or operation directly or indirectly, of any lascivious exhibition of sexual
organs or sexual activity, with the aid of a computer system, for favor or
consideration against a child.
