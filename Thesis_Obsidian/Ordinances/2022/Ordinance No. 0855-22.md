SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICIES AND PRINCIPLES - It has
been the policy of the Local Government of Davao City to ensure the viability and
growth of Cooperatives and People's Organizations as instrument of social justice,
equity, self-reliance, economic development and people empowerment.
Republic Act No. 9L78 or the Barangay Micro Business Enterprises (BMBES)
Act of 2002 recognizes the role of BMBEs as essential driving force for the country's
economic development. The BMBEs are seedbeds of Filipino entrepreneurial talents
and strengthening them would increase job generation, provide livelihood and better
quality of life.
Republic Act No. L0744, otherwise known as the Credit Surety Fund
Cooperative Act of 2015, aims to increase the credit worthiness of MSME which
otherwise cannot obtain loans from banks due to lack of acceptable collateral, credit
knowledge and credit track records. Said program complements the LGU's povefi
alleviation programs by creating more economic activities and job oppoftunities,
thus contributing to the revenues of the city.
The Bayanihan to Recover as One Act 2 of 2020 (R.A. LL494) directs all

Socio-economic Recovery Program such as Livelihood and Skills Training Program
for their respective targeted beneficiaries pafticularly the Micro, Small and Medium
Enterprises, especially those affected by the COVID-l9 pandemic.
The City's thrust on poveilry alleviation towards socio-economic development

TERM OF THE LOAN - As used in this Ordinance, the following terms shall mean:
a) Cooperative Financial Assistance Program (CFAP) - is an expanded loan
assistance program for cooperatives in terms of granting financial assistance in
the amount of One Hundred Thousand Pesos (PhP100,000) to Three Hundred
Thousand Pesos (PhP300,000) at zero percent (00/o) interest rate for micro and
small cooperatives payable in three (3) years; and Three Hundred One Thousand
Pesos (PhP301,000.00) to One Million Pesos (PhP1,000,000.00) at zero percent
(0o/o) interest rate for medium and large cooperatives and a repayment period of
two (2) years.
b) Cooperative Livelihood Development Program Staft-up Capital
Assistance (CLDP-SUCA) is a community-based training and
entrepreneurship platform which provides techno-vocational skills trainings
implemented by CCDO intended for the poor, vulnerable and marginalized
households and communities, but not limited to, disadvantaged women,
Indigenous Peoples (IPs), Persons with Disability (PWDs), Out/In of School Youth
(I/OSY), Farmers and Fisherfolks, in order to improve their socio-economic
conditions through provision of Staft-up Capital of PhP10,000.00 - PhP20,000.00
payable in one (1) year at zero percent (0%) interest rate; and Working Capital
of PhP30,000.00 - PhP50,000.00 with a repayment period of three (3) years at
zero percent (0%) interest.
c) Self-Employment Assistance Program (SEAP) - is an integrated package of
social welfare seruices project implemented by CSWDO, featuring a communitybased credit assistance program utilizing people's organization as credit conduits.
The target beneficiaries of this program are the low-income communities or
barangays where the potential resources for entrepreneurial activity are present,
such as, but not limited to, organization of disadvantaged women, out-of-school
youth, and persons with disabilities, which is geared towards the fundamental
concept of self-reliance, self-management and capability building with emphasis
on sustainability through a cash loan of a minimum of PhP5,000 to a maximum
of PhP10,000.00 payable from 12 to 24 months with zero interest.
d) Agricultural Development Program (AgDeP) - is a program implemented
by the City Agriculturist's Office for organized and duly registered farmers and/or
fisherfolks associations, Rural Improvement Clubs, 4-H Clubs, Cooperatives with
agriculture and/or fishery related products/seruices from Davao City as
beneficiaries and/or paftners to ensure food security and accessibility through
cash loan of a minimum of PhP50,000.00 and more than PhP300,000.00 without
interest payable in 3 years.
e) Livestock Dispersal Program (LDP) - is a livelihood program implemented
by CVO intended for individual farmers and POs as beneficiaries in order to
increase their income through livestock dispersal scheme using improved
upgraded strains of livestock and poultry production.
