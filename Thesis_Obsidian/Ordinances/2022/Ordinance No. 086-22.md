Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in
session assembled, that:
SECTION 1. EIIE - This Ordinance shall be known as "WEARIilG OF IP OR
MORO INSPIRED ATTIRE ORDINANCE".
SECTION 2.
- The City of Davao shall ensure that
its culture and tradition is appreciated, promoted and preserved. To take this into
fruition, the City shall ensure that its local culture shall showcase the beautiful culture
and rich tradition of its eleven (11) Uibes, by requiring city government employees to
wear an Indigenous People or Moro inspired attire and/or accessories, every first
Monday of the month.
SECTIOI{ 3. SIEE -
This Ordinance shall be limited to government
employees of this City, regardless whether such employee holds Office or employment
in casual, contractual, temporary, holdover, permanent or regular capacity. This shal!
also apply to newly-hired employees, Job-Order and Contract of Seruice employees.
Moreover, those whose ethnicity is outside the domain of the eleven (11) tribes of
Davao City may wear their respective ethnic attire. Finally, a genuine or authentic
Indigenous People or Moro inspired attire, may be worn by the abovementioned
government employees, provided that, a ceftification from the Office of the Cultural
Community Affairs has been secured from the said Office, agency or department, as
required under SECTION 6 of Ordinance No. A307-L7, SERIES of 20t7, otherwise known as
the "IP Attire Ordinance".
SECTION 4. rc.
- Whenever used in this Ordinance, the
following terms shall mean as herein set forth:
a) Ceftification - shall refer to any document evidencing that a prior
consultation for the use of an Indigenous Peoples Attire has been secured
by an office, agency or departnent from the Office of the Cultural Affairs.
b) City Government Employees - shall refer to those employed by the
Davao City Government.
c) Indigenous Peoples Inspired Attire - shall refer to inspired garments,
clothes or accessories which generally represent the five (5) tribes of the
Indigenous People of Davao City, to wit, hgobo, Klata, Ata, Obo Manuvu,
Matigsalug and Bagobo-Tagabawa tribes.
d) Inspired Attire - shall refer to garments, clothes or accessories which is
inspired by existing elements of the eleven (11) tribes of Davao City as
prescribed under this Ordinance;
e) Moro Inspired Attire - shall refer to inspired garments, clothes or
accessories which generally represent the six (6) Moro tribes of Davao City,
to wit: Tausug, Sama, Maranao, Maguindanaon, Iranun and Kagan tribes.
0 Office or employment in casual or contractual capacity - shall refer
to those whose employment in the government is in accordance with a
special contract to undeftake a specific work or job, requiring special or
technical skills not available in the employing agency, to be accomplished
within a specific period, which in no case shall exceed one (1) year and
performs or accomplishes the specific work or job, under his own
responsibility with a minimum of direction and supervision from the hiring
agency. This term shal! include Job-Order employees.

SECTION 5.
- All words and phrases
as used in this Ordinance shall be construed and understood according to the common
and accepted usage ofthe language.
SECTION 6. MANDATORY WEARING OF IP AND MORO INSPIRED
ATTIRE - It shall be required for all City government employees in the City of Davao to
wear an Indigenous Peoples Inspired Attire or Moro Inspired Attire and/or accessories
based on the eleven (11) tribes of Davao City, every first Monday of the month to
celebrate the local culture. City government employees who belong to any of the eleven
(11) tribes in Davao City can likewise wear their authentic attire. City government
employees whose ethnicity is outside the domain of the eleven (11) tribes of Davao
City, may likewise wear their respective ethnic attire. This shall be required for all city
government employees, whether holding casual, contractual, hold-over, permanent
and/or temporary capacity.
SECTION 7. EXCEPTIONS - The following acts or employees shall be
exempted from the implementation of this Ordinance:
7.1 Those who are regularly peforming their duties and functions in the field,
and those assigned in actual field operations;
7.2 When the nature of the work of the concerned employee demands that
he or she wears a clothing other than what is imposed in this Ordinance;
7.3 Those who are prohibited to wear such attire by reason of their religious
affiliation;
7.4 Those employees who have lost a loved one may wear their mourning
clothes during their period of mourning; and
7.5 Pregnant female employees who need to wear their maternity clothes.
