SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS - Whenever used in this Ordinance, the
following terms shall mean as herein set forth:
C.1. Indigenous Cultural Communities/Indigenous Peoples
(ICCs/IPs) - refer to a group of people or homogenous societies
identified by self-ascription and ascription by other, who have
continuously lived as organized community on a communally bounded
and defined territory, and who have, under claims of ownership since
time immemoria!, occupied, possessed customs, tradition and other
distinctive cultural traits;
C.2 Higher Education Institution - shall refer to organizations or
educational institutions providing higher, post-secondary, tertiary,
and/or th ird-level ed ucation;
C.3 Tertiary Education - shall refer to education pursued beyond
Secondary Education or High School;
C.4. Secondaly Education - shall refer to education after primary

C.5 Priority Cources - shall refer to bachelor's degree courses which

C.6 IPMR Office - The sectoral representative of the eleven (11)
ICCs/IPs of Davao City, particularly the Matigsalug, Kagan, Klata, Ata,
Tagabawa, Obo Manuvu, Maguindanao, Maranao, Tausug, Sama and
Iranun in the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod. It facilitates the screening and
recommendation of qualified IP applicants to the respective scholarship
programs of Higher Education Institutions, and other stakeholders
under the Iskolar ng Tribu Program.
SECTION 4. WHO MAY APPLY Any member of the Indigenous
Peoples/Indigenous Cultural Communities (IPs/ICCs) who has satisfactorily completed
Secondary Education may apply for Iskolar ng Tribu Program.
SECTION 5. SELECTION SHALL BE OF TWO LEVELS, AS FOLLOWS:
A.) General Conditions. The applicant must adhere to the following
conditions:
1. Bona fide member of an ethno linguistic group in his/her
community duly recognized by the City Government of Davao;
2. Favorable endorsement from at least two (2)
respected/acknowledged IP traditional leaders of his/her ethnolinguistic group recognized by the City Government of Davao;
3. Willingness to sign an undeftaking to serue his/her Indigenous
Cultural Community after completion of his/her four-year course for
a period of at least two (2) years as the case may be;
4. Taking priority courses needed in his/her community at a duly
accredited Higher Education Institution in or near his/her domicile,
provided the chosen priority course is available thereat;
5. Must be physically and mentally fit as shown in his/her
medical/psychological records; and
6. Must be a resident of Davao City.

B.) Specific Criteria. The applicant must pass the following criteria:
1. Must have finished Secondary Education with a general average of
85o/o and above with no grade lower than B0o/o in all his/her

2. Must be of good moral character; and
3. Must adhere to the standards set by the Scholarship Program of the

SECTION 6. (A) SCREEI{ING PROCESS. There shall be a screening process of
IP applicants to assess the veracity of their membership in their Indigenous Cultural
Communities (ICCs), Academic Records and Commitrnent to serue their communities after
completion of their studies. The Selection Process shall be the following:
1. Application. The IP applicant shall submit his/her application form to the
IPMR Office which includes the fullowing documents:
a) Ceftificate of Confirmation of Tribal Membership from National
Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP) or National Commission on
Muslim Filipinos (NCMF) as the case may be;
b) 2 pieces latest 2x2ID picture in Tribal Attire;
c) Academic Records issued by the School from which the IP applicant
graduated Secondary Education;
d) If currently enrolled in Teftiary Course, current Transcript of Records;
e) Certificate of Good Moral Character from the School which the IP
applicant graduated Secondary Education;
2. Nomination/Endorcement. The IP scholar applicant must have an
EndorsemenVCharacter Reference of at least two (2) respected/acknowledged
IP traditional leaders of his/her ethnolinguistic group recognized by the City
Government of Davao;
3. Inclusivity. The IP applicant must be a bona fide member of an ethnolinguistic group in his/her community duly recognized by the City Government
of Davao. Thus, any IP applicant can apply for the program, but the bona fide
members of the ethno-linguistic groups of Matigsalug, Kagan, Tagabawa, Klata,
Ata, Obo-Manuvu, Maguindanao, Maranao, Tausug, Sama, Iranun are given
priority.
4. Selection Panel. The selection panel shall evaluate the application of the
IP applicant and shall be composed of the following:
a.) A competent member of the Academe;
b.) The Indigenous Peoples' Mandatory Representative (IPMR);
c.) A competent official from the EBSU-STEP.
5. Recommendation of Priority Cources. Upon careful scrutiny and
assessment of the Selection Panel, priority courses shall be recommended to
the IP applicant. The recommendation of IP scholar applicants who are
currently enrolled in a Teftiary Course shall be subject to the discretion of the
Selection Panel. The IP applicant is qualified to be recommended upon the
unanimous approval of the Selection Panel.
