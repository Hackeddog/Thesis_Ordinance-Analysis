Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao city, in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "THE ANTI-PATIENT
DISHONESTY AND TIOTIrcOOPERATION ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY"'
SECTION 2. DEFINITION OF TERMS
a.) Health Workers or Professionals - are persons who render health
care seruices to patients seeking medical attention, such as doctors, nurses,
medics, hospital and clinic aides, laboratory technicians and the like;
b.) Hospital Workers - are persons, not falling under the aforementioned
dehnition, who likewise work or are employed in hospitals, clinics,
laboratories and diagnostic centers dealing with COVID-19 patients, whether
confirmed or not, including PUIs or PUMs.
c.) Patients - are defined as:
i. confirmed covlD-19 Infected Person - are persons tested positive
for COVID-19 by duly authorized and accredited facilities by the
Depaftment of Health.
ii. Suspect COVID-19 Case - are persons, who are classified as such
under the protocols and guidelines of the Depaftment of Health.
iii. Probable coVID-19 Case - are persons who are classifled as such
under the protocols and guidelines of the Department of Health.
d.) Close Contacts - persons who may have come into contact with
Confirmed, Suspect and Probable COVID-19 Infected Person, but who are not
declared to be Confirmed, Suspect and Probable COVID-19 Infected Persons.
SECTION 3. DISHONESTY AND UNTRUTHFULNESS IN THE DECLARATION

clinics,laboratories,diagnosticcenters,healthcenters,and
the like, be it public or private, to accomplish and fill-out a medicaypersonal data form or
any similar document, shall state the truth and disclose all pertinent and relevant
information that is asked or required to be filled-out in the said form or document and shall
affix his signature on the same. Said person shall likewise obserue honesty and truthfulness
in answering questions propounded during interview by the health worker/professional or
hospital worker.
SECTION 4. UNLAWFUL ACTS - It shall be unlawful for any person who is a
Confirmed, Suspect and probable COVID-19 infected person, to knowingly and deliberately
make false and untruthful statements and/or withhold relevant information when asked or
required to do so, such as but not limited to travel history, experience of symptoms,
medical condition and other important data during the Health Crisis brought about by the
covlD-19 outbreak as declared by the National Government.
Any omission of the required information aforementioned in SECTION 3 hereof, of
which a reasonable person is expected to have personal knowledge shall be prima facie
evidence of dishonesty, untruthfulness and constitutes a violation of this Ordinance.

discretion of the court.
