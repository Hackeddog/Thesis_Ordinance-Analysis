Respectfully forwarded to the Office of the City Mayor, through the Office
of the City Administrator, both this City, the attached Ordinance No. 0330-20
entitled "AN oRDINANCE REQUIRING ALL PUBLIC AND PRIVATE SCHOOLS IN
ALL LEVELS IN DAVAO CITY, TO INCLUDE IN THEIR CURRICULUM THE
TEACHING OF THE HISTORY OF THE CIry OF DAVAO", with the herein findings
and recommendation.
As aptly noted, the intent of the ordinance is the integration of the history
of Davao City in the school curricula both in private and public school as shown
in the title of the Ordinance and that of Resolution No. 01388-20, SERIES of 2020.
However, the ordinance failed to deliver or effectuate the intent of the
framers. It simply directed the creation of the Davao History Book Council and its
budgetary operation. While it appears in the Declaration of Policy directing all the
schools offering elementary, secondary and tertiary education to include as part
of their curriculum, the History of Davao City, the same does not serve the
purpose.
At any rate, resort to the title is applicable if the ordinance is vague. In
the case at bar, the text of the ordinance is clear and free from doubt.
VIEWED from the foregoing, it is recommended that the Ordinance be
approved.

Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as the "DAVAO
HISTORY ORDINANCE".
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - It is the policy of the City of Davao
to promote character formation and wholesome personality development by requiring
schools at the elementary, secondary and teftiary levels to inculcate patriotism and
nationalism, foster love of humanity, respect for human rights, appreciation of the role of
national heroes in the historical development of the country, teach the rights and duties
of citizenship, strengthen ethical and spiritual values, and develop moral character and
personal discipline. Thus, culture and tradition found in the history of the City will now
be an integral part of the educational system of the City.
All public and private schools in the city offering elementary, secondary and teftiary

History Book Council shall be created to be headed by the City Mayor, for the purpose of

The Technical Working Group/Research Team is allowed to organize consultation
meetings with prominent persons of Davao (old families), private
schools/universities, and other sectors of the community (public officials,
businessmen, indigenous people, media, artists, etc.), for evaluation and validation

SECTION 6. EDUCATIONAL MATERIALS - In order to carry out the purpose
of this Ordinance, the City Government of Davao shall cause the publication and
distribution, free of charge, of the "History of Davao" as its official textbook to all public
and private educational institutions in the City. Copies of the book shall also be given to
all local and national government offices in the City,
SECTION 7. ACADEMIC FREEDOM - Academic Freedom shall be allowed for
private and public colleges and universities for other supplemental learning materials
provided, however, that their learning materials are faithful to the official Davao History
Book.
SECTION 8. BUDGET - An annual appropriation shall be appropriated for
purposes of this Ordinance. The City shall appropriate funds for the first year in
constituting the Technical Working Group for the research/workshops on the
development and production of the book.
Thereafter, the cost of publication, updating of the materials and other related
expenses shall be included in the Local School Board Funds.
SECTION 9. TIMETABLE - The Teaching of the History of Davao City shall
officially commence in 2022, or earlier if feasible.
SECTION 10. REPEALING CLAUSE - All ordinances, resolutions, circulars,
memoranda, or rules and regulations inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance
are hereby repealed and modified accordingly.
SECTION 11. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If for any reason, any portion or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and
effect.
SECTION 12. EFFECTMW - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen ( 15) days
after its publication in a local newspaper.
