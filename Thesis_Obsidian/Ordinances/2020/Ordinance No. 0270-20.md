Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in
session assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE- This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
INSTITUTIONALIZING THE DAVAO CITY ONE-STOP SHOP FOR
CoNSTRUCTTON PERMTTS (OSSCP).'
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - The City Government of Davao
recognizes the need to ensure that the processing, inspection, approval and release of
construction/building permits and ceftiflcates of occupancy in the City are without delay.
sEcrIoN 3. ONE-STOP SHOP FOR CONSTRUCTTON PERMTTS (OSSCP)
covERAGE - A mechanism of coordination among the off@
(OBO), the City Assessor's Office, the City Treasurer's Office and other concerned
depaftments or offices at the local government and the Office of the City Fire Marshall
through co-location of related functions and through practical system of linkages.
OSSCP shall cover only simple applications for Building Permits and Ceftificates of
Occupancy whose floor area shall not exceed one thousand five hundred (1,500) square
meters, to wit
a, Single dwelling residential building of not more than three (3) floors/storeys;
b, commercial Buildings of not more than two (2) froors/storeys;
c. Renovation within a mall with issued building permit; and
d. Warehouse storing non-hazardous substance.
SECTION 4. OSSCP COMPOSITION - The One-Stop Shop for Construction
Permits (OSSCP) shall be composed of the Davao City Mayor or her duly authorized
representative as Chairperson; the City Administrator as Co-Chairperson and the City
Building Official, the Zoning officer/ the City Planning and Development Officer (CpDOt
the City Assessor, the City Treasurer and the Bureau of Fire protection (BFp) i;
Members.
SECTION 5. OSSCP FUNCTIONS - The One-Stop Shop for Construction
Permits (OSSCP) shall have the following functions:
a, Receive applications and documents for Building Permits and Certificates of
Occupancy, either through online or physical submission;
b. Coordinate and facilitate the conduct of technical reviews by the concerned
departments of the City of Davao and local Bureau of Fire Protection (BFp);
c. Facilitate the retrieval of documentary requirements for Building permits and
Certificates of Occupancy that will be paft of the OSSCP backroom operation.
For this purpose, backroom operation is defined as the working arrangement
among the Office of the City Building Official (OBO) and other concerned
departments and offices of the City of Davao and the Bureau of Fire protection
(BFP) where the application and documentary requirements for Building
Permits and Certificates of Occupancy are transferred and moved from one
department to another for review, evaluation and action without the physical
interference of the applicant;
d. Implement a one-time assessment of fees and charges related to applications
for Building Permits and Certificates of Occupancy in coordination with the
Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP), the Zoning Officer, the City Assessor's Office,
the City Treasurer's Office and the Office of the City Building Official (OBO);

SECTION 6. BUILDING PERMIT PROCESSING TIME _ The different offices
involved in the OSSCP, functioning as one unit, shall evaluate simple Building Permit
applications with complete documentary requirements, within a maximum period of five
(5) working days. The Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) shall process the Fire Safety
Evaluation Clearance (FSEC) within a maximum period of three (3) working days and
shall abide by the manual operations that will be developed in relation to Joint
Memorandum Circular No. 2018-01 dated January 4,20L8 issued by the Depaftment of
the Interior and Local Government (DILG), the Department of Public Works and
Highways (DPWH), the Department of Information and Communications Technology
(DICD and the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) and other supplemental
manuals that shall be separately prepared by individual OSSCP. For easy compliance by
the applicant, applications with deficiencies shall be issued a Comprehensive Correction
Sheet and/or a Notice of Disapproval by the Zoning Officer, City Building Official (OBO),
and the local Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) stating all deflciencies. For applications
that satisfy the requirements of the Zoning Ordinance, the OSSCP shall release the
Building Permit, together with the locational clearance, other ancillary permits and the
Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance (FSEC,) after payment of appropriate fees and charges.
The Office of the City Building Official (OBO) shall provide the local BFP and the
City Assessor's Office with the list of the issued Building Permits on a monthly basis
(every 5th day of the month) for their reference, in anticipation of the applicant's future
application for a Ceftiflcate of Occupancy.
SECTION 7. ASSESSMENT OF FEES - After the evaluation of the building
permit application, the Zoning Officer, the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP), and the
Office of the Building Official (OBO) shall simultaneously prepare an Order of Payment
that will be issued to the applicant, at the same time, specifying the fees that the
applicant is obligated to pay in accordance with the provisions of the Zoning Ordinance,
the National Building Code of the Philippines (NBCP), the Fire Code of the Philippines
(FCP) and the City's Revenue Code. The Zoning Officer, the Bureau of Fire Protection
(BFP) and the Office of the City Building Official (OBO) shall put in place the necessary
measures to implement a one-time assessment of building-related fees and charges
which shall include the local fees for securing the locational clearance, barangay
clearance, filing fees among others.
The fees for the issuance of the Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance (FSEC) under
the Fire Code of the Philippines shall be based on the estimated value of the building or
structure declared by the owner or applicant duly notarized. In the event that there is
substantial difference between the estimated value of the building or structure as
declared by the owner or applicant from the amount indicated in the application for the
building permit, the higher value will be adopted.

For this purpose, the estimated value of the building or structure shall be defined
as the value of the construction installed or erected at a given location and may also be
considered as cost of construction including architectural and engineering work and
labor cost.
The Office of the Building Official (OBO) and the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP)
may collect a fling fee, in addition to the NBCP and FCP prescribed fees and charges, to
cover costs incurred in assessing building permit and FSEC applications. Such fee shall
be set following the guidelines for setting fees and charges issued by the Bureau of
Local Government Finance (BLGF) under the Depaftment of Finance.
SECTION 8. PAYMENT OF FEES - The Office of the Building Official (OBO) and
the Local Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) shall jointly implement a one-time payment of
fees and charges related to the Building Permit applications. All related fees shall be paid
by the applicant in a designated cashier area in the OSSCP where the City Government
and the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) shall assign a cashier to receive payments for
applications for Building Permit and Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance (FSEC),
respectively.
