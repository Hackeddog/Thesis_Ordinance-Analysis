Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as *MANDATORY
14-DAY HOME QUARANTINE ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY."
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - The Local Government code of 1991
or Republic Act No. 7160 allows every local government to exercise specific powers for
the promotion of the general welfare. Towards this end, the City of Davao enacts an
Ordinance to promote health and safety, maintain peace and order, and preserve the
comfort and convenience of its constituents.
SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS.
a) Public Health Authority - refers to the Department of Health (DOH),
IATF-EID, the City Health Office, or any person directly authorized to act in
behalf of the aforementioned.
b) COVID-19 - a potentially severe respiratory illness caused by a coronavirus
and characterized by fever, coughing, and shortness of breath, the spread
of which is the reason the World Health Organization has declared a
worldwide pandemic.
c) Quarantine - a state or place of isolation for a person or groups of persons
who may have come in contact with contagious diseases, and is not
reserved for sick peoPle onlY.
d) Fourteen (14)-day home quarantine- a quarantine protocol aligned with the
l4-day period of incubation of the COVID-19 virus. As per the World Health
Organization (WHO), the guidelines are as follows:
1) Allocate a separate room with adequate ventilation at home if possible.
Household members should stay in another room or should be separated
from the returnee;
2) Maintain at least one meter distance from family members.
3) Other household members should use a separate bathroom. If sharing
the same bathroom, cleaning of taps, doorknobs and utensils with soap
and water are a requirement.
4) Minimize visitors in the house. The person under isolation should not
interact with any visitors.
5) Frequent handwashing with soap and water for at least twenty (20)
seconds at a time and maintain alcohol-based hygiene in instances where
handwashing facilities are inadequate.
6) Avoid touching eyes, nose and mouth with unwashed hand'
7) The home quarantined person is expected to monitor body temperature
using a thermometer twice a day. If he/she develops fever, cough,
difficulty in breathing, sore throat, body aches and pain, including flu-like
symptoms, immediately inform local health authorities.

9) Assign separate dishes, drinking glasses, cups, eating utensils, towels,

10)Used utensils, bed linen and clothes should be washed with soap and
water.
e) Rapid Antibody Test - also know as Rapid Test (RT), is an Antibody Test
that qualitatively detects the presence of IgM and IgG antibodies against
the COVID-19 virus in human serum, plasma and venous whole blood
specimen for the auxiliary diagnosis of covlD-19 infection.
Reverse Transcription Polymerase Chain Reaction (RT-PCR)- a COVID-19
test using actual swabs from patients taken from the nose or throat. This
test determines the actual presence of the coronavirus and if a person is
currently infected.
g) Asymptomatic - when a disease exists in a person without noticeable
symptoms.
h) permanent Resident Visa- a document granted to foreigners, granting them
the right to reside in the Philippines on a permanent basis.
i) Locally Stranded Individuals (LSI) - Foreign nationals or Filipino citizens in
a specific locality within the Philippines who have expressed intention to

AS:
1) Workers;
2) Students;
3) Tourists; and
4) Other individuals stranded in various localities while in transit

nation's flag.

primary reason for being abroad is not due to a contract of employment in
a foreign nation or vessel.

1) OFWs, whether documented or non-documented

SECTION 4. SCOPE AND COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall apply to all
residents of Davao City, and non-residents who are transiting, or are being held in the
City for mandatory quarantine due to COVID-19, whether undergoing the same in a
government facility, a private facility, or at one's place of residence.
SECTION 5. MANDATORY OUARANTINE - The policy on mandatory 14-day
home quarantine shall apply to the following, subject to modification and revision by the
IATF, the DOH, and/or other Public Health Authcrities:
a) All asymptomatic returning OFWs, whether sea-based or land based, subject
to the arrangements, and rules and regulations imposed by the governing
authority;
b) Asymptomatic Returning Overseas Filipinos and holder of permanent
resident visas,
c) Asymptomatic Locally Stranded Individuals
d) Asymptomatic healthcare workers with unprotected exposure to a confirmed
Covid-19 patient without appropriate PPEs, whether RT-PCR test results are
positive or negative;
e) Asymptomatic non-healthcare workers who test IgM positive in rapid tests,
are required to undergo isolation at home or at a community quarantine
facility for L4 days; and
0 Other such cases which require mandatory l4-day home quarantine as
prescribed by law.
SECTION 6, FINES AND PENALTIES - Any person caught in violation of the
Ordinance by leaving the place of residence or barangay quarantine or isolation center
before the required fourteen (14) days shall be imposed the fixed penalty of
administrative fine of Five Thousand Pesos (Php5,000.00) and/or imprisonment for a
maximum of six (6) months, or both, at the discretion of the court.
SECTION 7. NO CONTEST PROVISION - Any person apprehended for violation
of this Ordinance, who does not wish to contest the violation and is willing to voluntarily
pay the fine imposed upon him/her prior to the filing of a formal complaint with the Office
of the City Prosecutor, may be allowed to pay the said fine with the City Treasurer's Office
to avoid being criminally prosecuted.
SECTION 8. IMPLEMENTATION. -The DOH Center for Health Development
Davao, Davao City Health Office, and Davao City Police Office, shall take the lead in the
implementation of the Ordinance. At the barangay level, the Office of the Punong
Barangay and the respective Barangay Health Emergency Response Teams (BHERT) shall
ensure the enforcement of the Ordinance.
SECTION 9. APPROPRIATIONS. The City Government of Davao shall allocate
funds for the implementation of the various undeftakings relative to this Ordinance.
SECTION 10. SEPARABILIW CLAUSE.- If , for any reason, any SECTION or
provision of this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and
effect.
