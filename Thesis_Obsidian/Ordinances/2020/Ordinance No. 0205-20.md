SECTION 1. TITLE- This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - SECTION 16 of the Local Government
Code provides that every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly
granted, those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate,
or incidental for its efficient and effective governance and those which are essential to
the promotion of the general welfare.
SECTION 3. IMPOSITION OF SPECIAL REAL PROPERW TAX -There shall
be imposed Real Property Tax Rate of One and a half percent (1.50/0) on realty, power
plant, civil works, equipment, machinery and other improvements by-a DOE-registered
renewable energy developer under Republic Act No. 9513, which are actua-lly and
exclusively used for renewable energy facilities.
SECTION 4. DISTRIBUTION OF SPECIAL REAL PROPERW TAX -In
proportion to the real property tax and special education fund imposed by th-ZOtZ
Revenue Code of Davao City, the special real property tax rate of one and a half percent
(1.50/o) shall be distributed at nine-tenths percent (0.9%) to the General Fund as basic
tax and the remaining six-tenths (0.6%) to the special Education Fund.
SECTION 5. EXCEPTION -All other realty, equipment, machinery and
improvements of DOE-registered renewable energy developer not exclusively used for
the renewable energy facility or not necessary forenergy generation shall continue to be
subject to the impositions of the 20t7 Revenue Code on real property tax.
SECTION 6. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE- If, for any reason, any SECTION or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and
effect.
SECTION 7. EFFECTMW- This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.
ENACTED, on the 28th day of January 2020, by a unanimous vote of all the
Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
