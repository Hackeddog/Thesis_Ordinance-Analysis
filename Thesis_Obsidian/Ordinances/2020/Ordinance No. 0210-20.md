SECTION 5. RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT - The City

SECTION 6. COMPOSITION OF TTIE MENTAL WELLNESS CENTER - The
Facility shall have two (2) components - the halfiruay home for recovered, abandoned,
homeless and neglected persons with mental disability (the halfuiay home), and the
community-based mental wellness center (the wellness center), respectively. The proper
names of both these components shall be determined as fit at a later time.
The halfrvay home is a post-recovery home that provides shelter to recovered
persons with mental disabilities who are abandoned, neglected and homeless and found
living or staying on the streets. Its staff composition and operating procedures shall be in
consonance with the provisions of the Mental Health Code of Davao City, as well as the
accepted standards in the practice. The City Health Office shall take the lead in the
management and superuision of the Shelter, while the City Social Services and
Development Office (CSSDO) shall provide for after-care seryices at the community level
when the persons are reintegrated back to their respective families and communities.
