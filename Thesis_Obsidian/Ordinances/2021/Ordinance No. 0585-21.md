SECTION 2. PURPOSE. - It is the purpose of this Ordinance to protect the rights
of the labor force and ensure the well being of all workers within its jurisdiction and
provide them with proper information of their rights and access to protection of their
welfare.
SECTION 3. COVERAGE. - This Ordinance shall apply to all workers, whether
residents of Davao City or not, and all employers whose place of business is found within
the territorial jurisdiction of the City of Davao. This shall also include workers assigned
outside the jurisdiction of Davao City but the main/head office is located in Davao City;

Labor Code. Any words or terms not defined shall be given their plain and customary

1. Worker - means any member of the labor force, whether employed or
unemployed.
2. Handicapped Workers - are those whose earning capacity is impaired by age

labor practice if he has not obtained any other substantially equivalent and
regular employment. It refers to a person in the seruice of another under any
contract of hire, express or implied, oral or written, where the employer has the
power or right to control and direct the employee in the material details of how
the work is to be performed.

10. Barangay Workers - these include Barangay officials and members who may
be designated by law or ordinance and charged with the maintenance of public
order, protection and security of life and property, or the maintenance of a
desirable and balanced environment of the Barangay.
ll.Employer - includes any person acting in the interest of an employer, directly
or indirectly. The term shall not include any labor organization or any of its
officers or agents except when acting as an employer. It also refers to a person
or organization that hires people to perform work in exchange for compensation,
which is usually money in the form of wages or salary. Most countries have
legislation governing relationships between employers and the people they hire,
setting out the rights and responsibilities of each.
12. Construction Workers - those who are employed in the construction industry
and its subdivisions, namely: general building construction, general engineering
construction, and special trade construction, based on the classification code of
the Philippine Accreditation Board of Construction Industry Authority of the
Philippines; to companies and entities involved in demolition works; and to those
falling within the construction industry as determined by the Secretary of Labor
and Employment.
13. BPO Workers - those who are employed in a central customer seruice where
agents or customer care specialists or customer seruice representatives handle
business-related telephone calls (or e-mail) and other IT-related activities on
behalf of a client.
14. Labor Organizations - means any union or association of employees which
exists in whole or in paft for the purpose of collective bargaining or of dealing
with employers concerning terms and conditions of employment.
15. Labor or Industrial Dispute - includes any controversy or matter concerning
terms or conditions of employment or the association or representation of
persons in negotiating the fixing, maintaining, changing or arranging of terms
and conditions of employment regardless of whether or not the disputants stand
in proximate relationship of employers and employees.
16. Regular Working Hours - the normal hours of work of any employee shall
not exceed eight (B) hours a day. It shall include all time during which an
employee is required to be on duty and/or to be at a prescribed workplace; all
time during which an employee is permitted to work; and rest periods of short
duration during working hours. (Labor Code Book III, Article 83 and recent
jurisprudence)
17.Oveftime Pay - refers to the additional compensation for work peformed
beyond eight (B) hours a day, which consists of an additional compensation
equivalent to his regular wage plus at least 25olo thereof.
Work performed beyond eight hours on a holiday or rest day shall be paid an
additional compensation equivalent to the rate of the first eight hours plus at
least 30o/o thereof.
18. Holiday Pay - refers to the payment of the regular daily wage for any
unworked regular holiday.

A. For Regular Holidays- the employee is entitled to at least 1000/o of his or

he/she is present or is on leave of absence with pay on the work day

As used in this provision, "holiday" includes: New Year's Day, Maundy
Thursday, Good Friday, the ninth of April, the first of May, the twelfth of
June, the fourth of July, the thiftieth of November, the twenty-fifth and
thiftieth of December, and the day designated by law for holding a general
election.
B. For Special days - work performed on special days merits additional
compensation of at least thirty (30o/o) of the basic wage or a total of one
hundred thirly percent (130%). Where the employee works on a special day
falling on his rest day, he/she shall be entitled to an additional compensation
of at least fifty percent (50%) of his/her basic wage or a total of one hundred
fifty percent (150o/o). Note that workers who are not required or permitted
to work on special days are not entitled to any compensation.
Unless otherwise modified by law, order, or proclamation, the following are
the four (4) special days in a year under Executive Order No. 292, as

109661, that shall be obserued in the Philippines: Ninoy Aquino Day, Monday

19.Seruice Incentive Leave - Every employee who has rendered at least one

with pay.
20. Minimum Wage Earner - this refers to salaried individuals earning a
compensation of the daily minimum wage rates as determined by the Regional
