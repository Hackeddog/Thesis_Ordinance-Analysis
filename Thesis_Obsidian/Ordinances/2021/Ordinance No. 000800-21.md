Ord. No. 0800-21
Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in
session assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
AUTHORIZING THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY
OF DAVAO, THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT BY AND BETWEEN LOVE THE
CHILDREN FOUNDATION, INC. AND THE CrTY OF DAVAO RELATTVE TO THE
FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE AMOUNTING TO FOUR HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS
(P400,000.00) FoR THE LTVELIHOOD PROGRAM OF THE LATTER";
SECTION 2.
- SECTION 36 of Republic Act No.
7L60, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, states that:
"SECTION 36. Assistance to People's and NonGovernmental Organizations. A local government unit
ffidy, through its local chief executive and with the
concurrence of the Sanggunian concerned, provide assistance,
financial or otherwise, to such people's and non-governmental
organizations for economic, socially-oriented, environmental,
or cultural projects to be implemented within its territorial
jurisidiction."
SECTION 3. AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative
authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City of Davao, the Memorandum
of Agreement by and between Love the Children Foundation, Inc. and the City of Davao
relative to the financial assistance amounting to Four Hundred Thousand Pesos
(P400,000.00) for the livelihood program of the latter.
SECTION 4. SEPARABILIW CLAUSE - lf , for any reason, any sections or
provisions of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration, shall continue to be in full force and
effect.
SECTION 5. EFFECTMTY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.
ENACTED, on the 2nd day of December 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the
Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
