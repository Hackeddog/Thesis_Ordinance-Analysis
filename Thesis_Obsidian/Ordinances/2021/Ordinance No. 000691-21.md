Ord. No. 0691-21
Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known aS "HYDROGENFILLED BALLOONS IN THE CITY OF DAVAO".
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - The City Government of Davao
hereby declares the following as its policies, to wit:
a) To eliminate the practice of using hydrogen-filled balloons in all business,
leisure, enteftainment, sports, and social activities to ensure the protection of
public health and the environment;3
b) To raise awareness that hydrogen is an unstable and inflammable gas
which can easily explode and catch fire;
c) To promote responsibility and accountability in the use of hydrogen in
balloons; and
d) To ensure proper monitoring/control of hydrogen balloons within the City,
as a means for maintaining the ecological balance.
SECTION 3. DEFINITION OF TERMS -
a) Balloon - a nonporous bag of light material that is inflated especially with
gas; such as a bag that is filled with heated air or a gas lighter than air so as
to rise and float in the atmosphere;
b) Hydrogen - a nonmetallic gaseous chemical element with atomic number
1 that is the simplest and lightest of the elements and is used especially in
the processing of fossil fuels and the synthesis of ammonia.
SECTION 4. COVERAGE - This Ordinance shall cover all hydrogen-filled balloons
within the territorial jurisdiction and airspace of Davao City with the exemption of those
used for research and development purposes in the field of science and technology,
laboratory analysis and atmospheric monitoring.
SECTION 5. PROHIBITED ACTS - It shall be unlawful for any person or business
establishment to sell or offer for sale, distribute or use hydrogen-filled/inflated balloons
within the territory of Davao City.
SECTION 6. PENALTY CLAUSE - Any violation of the provisions of this
Ordinance shall be punished as follows, without prejudice to the prosecution of other civil
and criminal actions:
