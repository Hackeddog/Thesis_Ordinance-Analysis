- SECTION 16 of Republic Act No. 7L60
or the "Local Government Code of 1991" provides:
" Every local government unit shall exercise the powers expressly granted,
those necessarily implied therefrom, as well as powers necessary
appropriate, or incidental for its efficient and effective governancel and those
which are essential to the promotion of the general welfare. Within their
respective territorial jurisdictions, local government units shall ensure and
support, among other things, the preseruation and enrichment of culture,
promote health and safety, enhance the right of the people to a balanced
ecology, encourage and support the development of appropriate and selfreliant scientific and technological capabilities, improve public morals,
enhance economic prosperity and social justice, promote full employment
among their residents, maintain peace and order, and preserue the comfort
and convenience of their inhabitants."
SECTION 3. AUTHORITY-The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority
to enter into and sign the Deed of Donation between the City of Davao and Brokenshire
Integrated Health Ministries, Inc., relative to the equipment for testing to be installed at
the latter's Molecular Biology Laboratory Facility.
