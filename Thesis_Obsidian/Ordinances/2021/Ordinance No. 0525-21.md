of early childhood care and development services consistent with RA 10410,

WHEREAS, the GeneralAppropriation Act, FY 2020 Vol. 116, No.1 stated that
the target outcome indicator of SFP is 80% of malnourished children in
Community Development Centers (CDCs) and Supervised Neighborhood Play
with improved nutritional status.
WHEREAS, on 12 March 2020, the lnter-Agency Task Force on Emerging
lnfectious Diseases (IATF-EID) per Resolution No. 11, SERIES of 2020, raised
the COVID-19 threat to the highest alert level, Code Red Sublevel2, due to the
evidence of sustained community transmission of the disease. Concomitantly,
President Rodrigo Roa Duterte issued Proclamation No, 922, SERIES of 2020,
declaring a State of Public Health Emergency allthroughout the Philippines and
thereafter, placed the entire lsland of Luzon, under enhanced community
quarantine. Th e LGUs outside of Luzon have imposed localized community
quarantine in their areas of jurisdiction consistent with the parameters of the
pertinent IATF Resolution until 30 April 2020 when the President issued
Executive Order No. 112, SERIES 2020 specifically identifying the LGUs under
the enhanced and community quarantine;
WHEREAS, noting the limitation in the purchasing power of most families, SFP
implementation during the implementation of community quarantine shallassist
in addressing the issue of hunger and food security among Filipino children and
prevent regression of the current nutritional status of the beneficiaries.
WHEREAS, DSWD Memorandum Circular No. 35, SERIES of 2020 a
supplemental guideline to Memorandum Circular No. 12, SERIES of 2020,
sanctioned the amended scheme of implementation of SFP during the
community quarantine period as stringent social distancing measures prohibit
the conduct of assemblies or mass gatherings which include feeding of children
in the CDCs and SNPs;
WHEREAS, local government units (LGUs), as partners of the DSWD in the
delivering programs and projects shall also assist in the implementation of SFP;
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises,
hereinafter set forth, the parties hereto agree to undertake the program under
