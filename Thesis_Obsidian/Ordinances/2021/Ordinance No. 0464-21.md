Ord. No, 0464-21
Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
REGULATING THE USE OF VIDEOKE AND KARAOKE MACHINES DURING THE
IMPLEMENTATION OF THE BLENDED LEARNING MODALITY'.
SECTION 2. DEFINITION OF TERMS - For purposes of this Ordinance, the
following terms are defined:
a. Karaoke Machine - a device that plays instrumental accompaniments for
selection of songs to which the user sings along;
b. Videoke Machine - a device where the user sings along to pre-recorded
instrumental versions of songs, the lyrics of which are displayed for the
user on a screen in time with the music;
c. Blended Learning Modality - refers to a mix of online distance learning,
modular distance learning, and TV/Radio-based instruction;
d. KTV establishment - a commercial establishment that offers enclosed and
sound-proofed rooms for videoke machines for a fee.
SECTION 3. P
HIBITED HOURS FOR THE opERATT()N OF KARAOKF
AND VIDEOKE MACHI
ES FOR PRIVATE USE. - The operation of karaoke and
videoke machines for private use shall not be allowed between 7 otlock in the morning
until 5 o'clock in the afternoon from Mondays to Saturdays except Sundays, declared
special holidays and legal holidays during the entire period of the implementation of the
blended learning modality during the COVID-19 pandemic.
SECTION 4. LIMITATIONS AND EXEMPTIONS. - SECTION 3 of this Ordinance
shall only apply to those karaoke and videoke machines for private use, be it indoor or
outdoor. Provided, fufther, that SECTION 3 shall not apply for KTV establishments.
SECTION 5. PENALTY. - The person found violating the provisions of this
Ordinance after due notice shall be penalized based on the following:
a. First Offense - a flne of One Thousand Pesos (Php 1,000.00) and/or
community seruice;
b, Second Offense - a fine of Three Thousand Pesos (Php 3,000,00) and/or
community service;
c. Third Offense - a flne of Five Thousand Pesos (Php 5,000.00) and/or
community service or by imprisonment of not less than one (1) day but
not more than three (3) days or both, at the discretion of the court.
SECTION 6. ENFORCEMENT. The Ordinance shall be in force and effect during
the implementation by the Department of Education of the blended learning modality.
Once the blended learning modality has been lifted by the Department of Education, the
Ordinance shall no longer be enforced.
The provisions of this Ordinance shall be enforced by the Barangay Offtcials of
each barangay. Any complaint involving the provisions of this Ordinance shall be brought
before the Punong Barangay of the respective barangay and/or police personnel.
SECTION 7. ORDINANCE VIOLATION RECEIPT OR CITATION TICKET. An
Ordinance Violation Receipt, otherwise known as "Citation Ticket" shall be issued to the
violators of this Ordinance. The Citation Ticket shall state the name and address of the
violator, the specific violation committed and the provisions of the succeeding SECTION on
the "No Contest Provision."

Ord. No, 0464-21
If the violation of this Ordinance is deemed to have been committed by a
corporation, partnership or juridical entity, duly recognized in accordance with law, the
chief executive officer, president, general manager, managing partner, or such other
officer-in-charge, if known, shall be cited for the commission of the offense.
SECTION 8. NO CONTEST PROVISION. - Any person cited for violation of this
Ordinance, who does not wish to contest the violation and is willing to pay voluntarily the
fine imposed upon him or her, or to voluntarily render community service under this
Ordinance prior to the filing of a formal complaint before the Office of the City Prosecutor
shall be allowed to pay said fine with the City Treasurer's Office, or to render community
service to avoid being criminally prosecuted. The availment of the "No Contest Provision"
shall exempt an individual from criminal liability under this Ordinance.
For this purpose, the City Legal Office and the City Treasurer's Office shall provide
the procedure for the availment of the "No Contest Provision" and establish a case
inventory and recording system for all violations of this Ordinance.
Provided, that if a violator has already availed of the "No Contest Provision", he or
she can no longer avail of the same and the corresponding complaint for any violation of
this Ordinance shall be immediately filed.
SECTION 9. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE. - If for any reason, any SECTION or
provision of this Ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid, no other sections
or provisions shall be affected thereby.
SECTION 10. EFFECTMW. - This Ordinance shall take effect after fifteen (15)
days following its approval and publication in a newpaper of local circulation consistent
with the provision of the Local Government Code of 1991.
ENACTED, January 12, 2021, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the
