Agreement by and between the City of Davao and the 10th Infantry (AGILA) Division of
the Philippine Army relative to the renewal of Five (5) Citizens Armed Forces Geographical
Unit Active Auxiliary Company II (CAAC-II) performing counter-insurgency and security
