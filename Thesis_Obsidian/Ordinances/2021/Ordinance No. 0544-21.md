Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in
session assembled, that:
SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE - This Ordinance shall be referred to and cited as
the "City Water Resources Office (CWRO) Ordinance".
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF PRINCIPLES AND POLICIES.
1. This Ordinance promotes the following basic principles:
A. Equitable access to safe drinking water;
B. Safe drinking water is an essential basic human right;
C. Water resource quality must be maintained for the ecosystem;
D. Recognize diversity in different geographical locations of water resources;
E. Consistency of water resource management;
F. Integrated water resource management is a process which promotes the
coordinated development and the management of water, land and related
resources, in order to maximize the resultant economic and social welfare
in an equitable manner without compromising the sustainability of vital
ecosystems.
