Ord. No. 0658-21
Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
ADOPTING AND APPROVING THE TEN.YEAR SOLID WASTE MANAGEMENT
PLAN OF DAVAO CITY".
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POUCY - Pursuant to SECTION 16 of Republic
Act No. 9003, otherwise known as the lcological Solid Waste Management Program,
which states:
SECTION 16. Local Government Solid Waste Management Plans. The
province, city or municipality, through its local solid waste management boards,
shall prepare its respective lO-year Solid Waste Management Plans consistent with
the national solid waste management framework: Provided, that the waste
management plan shall be for the re-use, recycling and composting of wastes
generated in their respective jurisdictions: Provided, fufther, that the Solid Waste
Management Plan of the LGU shall ensure the efficient management of solid waste
generated within its jurisdiction. The plan shall place primary emphasis on
implementation of all feasible re-use, rerycling and composting programs while
identifying the amount of landfill and transformation capacity that will be needed
for solid waste which cannot be re-used, recycled, or composted. The plan shall
contain all the components provided in SECTION 17 of this Act and a timetable for
the implementation of the solid waste management program in accordance with
the National Framework and pursuant to the provisions of this Act: Provided,
finally, that is shall be reviewed and updated every year by the provincial, city or
municipal solid waste management board.
SECTION 3. APPROVAL AND ADOPTION - For purposes of this Ordinance,
the TEN-YEAR SOLID WASTE MANAGEMENT PI-AN endorsed by the City Environment and
Natural Resources Office (CENRO), as previously approved by the Davao City Ecological
Solid Waste Management Board (DCESWMB) and submitted and preliminary approved by
the National Solid Waste Management Commission (NSWMC) hereby ADOPTS and
APPROVES the TEN.YEAR SOLID WASTE MANAGEMENT PLAN OF DAVAO CITY.
SECTION 4. REPEALING CLAUSE - All Ordinances as well as peftinent rules
and regulations inconsistent with this Ordinance, are hereby repealed and amended
accordingly.
SECTION 5. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any word, afticle,
sentence, clause or paragraph of this Ordinance is declared invalid for any reason, other
provisions thereof shall not be affected by such declaration and shall continue to be in
full force and effect.
SECTION 6. EFFECTMW - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately
upon approval.

Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
