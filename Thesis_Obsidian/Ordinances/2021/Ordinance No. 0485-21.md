a Fire Station and Tourist Police Station;

the USUFRUCTUARY be liable for any loss, damage or injury that may arise by reason of
its use and enjoyment of the Property.
4. The USUFRUSTUARY shall defray all expenses for building and maintenance including the
cost of electricity, telephone, and other utilities attached, connected or related to the
operation of or improvement introduced on the Property;
5. The USUFRUCTUARY shall, at its own expense, make all necessary repairs to keep the
property and all its improvements, equipment, and facilities, from deteriorating in value
or condition during the term of the grant;
6. The USUFRUCTUARY shall not lease or alienate its right of Usufruct over any part or
portion of the premises or the whole thereof, without the express written consent of the
OWNER;
7. The OWNER hereby covenants with the USUFRUCTUARY that the latter shall peaceably
hold and enjoy the full use and possession of the Property during the period of the grant.
However, the OWNER reserves the right to recover possession of the Property prior to
the expiration of the period of the grant for purposes of redevelopment, provided that
the OWNER shall identify another suitable location within NCCC Victoria Plaza as
replacement for the Propefi and shall inform USUFRUCTUARY of such decision at least
Sixty (00) days prior to the transfer, provided, further that the cost of relocation shall be
for the exclusive account of the OWNER;
g. The USUFRUCI'UARY is exempt from making an inventory of all the property which
contains an appraisal of the movables and a description of the condition of the
immovables and giving a security binding itself to fulfill the obligations imposed upon it;
9. The OWNER may immediately recover possession of the Property, including all
permanent structures and improvements introduced thereon, without obligation to
reimburse the herein USUFRUCTUARY, upon expiration of the period of the Grant or upon
