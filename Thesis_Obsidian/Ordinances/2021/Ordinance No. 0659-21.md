Ord. No. 0659-21
Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in
session assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
GRANTING THE REQUEST OF JOE CHOA SHI FOR ADDITIONAL ALLOWABLE
USE FOR THE OPERATION OF EARTHFILL QUARRY LOCATED IN BARANGAY
WA-AN, BUHANGIN DISTRTCT, DAVAO CIW".
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - Article XII, SECTION 1 of the
Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of Davao City (2013-2022) provides for Requests for
Additional Allowable Use, which reads:
"The uses enumerated in the preceding articles on general zone
and all sub-zones are not exhaustive nor all-inclusive. The
SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod, upon application of the project proponent
and upon favorable recommendation by the Local Zoning Board of
Adjustment and Appeals (LZBAA), may allow other uses not
enumerated thereunder as it may deem flt and proper including,
but not limited to, the following projects which are socio-economic
and environmental significance and/or national interest by a 3/q
majority vote of all the Members of the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod
through resolution and ordinance."
SECTION 3. APPROVAL OF REQUEST - The application for Additional
Allowable Use of Nlr. loe Choa Shi for the operation of earthfill quarry located in
Barangay Wa-an, Buhangin District, this City, with a total area of Forty-four Thousand
Four Hundred Ninety-nine (44,499) square meters, more or less, registered under
Transfer Ceftificate of Title No. T-277970 of the Registry of Deeds of Davao City in the
name of Ms. Janel C. Gaisano is hereby APPROVED. Provided, no permit for earthfill
quarry shall be issued without the Affidavit of No Objection of all concerned adjacent lot
owners.
SECTION 4. OBLIGATION OF APPLICANT - The applicant and his or her
successors-in-interest have the obligation to comply with existing rules, laws and
Ordinances. The environment shall at all times be protected. Adverse environmental
impact for any use of the propefi shall be mitigated. Non-compliance with existing
rules, laws and ordinances shall cause the repeal of this Ordinance.
SECTION 5. SEPARABILIW CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any SECTION or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and
effect.
SECTION 6. EFFECTMTY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.
ENACTED, on the 21st day of July 202t, by three-fourths (314) votes of all the
Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
