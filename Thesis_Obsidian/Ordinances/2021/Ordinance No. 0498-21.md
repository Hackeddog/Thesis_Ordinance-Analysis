Know All Men by These Presents:
This Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) made and entered into by and among
The City Government of Davao, with principal office at the Davao City Hall, Davao
City, represented in this MOA by its City Mayor, the Honorable Sara Z. Duterte,
hereinafter referred to as Davao City;
The City Government of Tagum, with principal office at the New Tagum City Hall,
Tagum City, Davao del Norte represented in this MOA by its City Mayor, the
Honorable Allan L. Rellon, hereinafter referred to as Tagum City;
The City Government of Panabo, with principal office at the Panabo City Hall,
Panabo City, Davao del Norte, represented in this MOA by its City Mayor, the
Honorable Jose E. Relampagos, hereinafter referred to as Panabo City;
The City Government of the Island Garden City of Samal (IGACOS), with
principal office at the IGACOS, Davao del Norte, represented in this MOA by its City
Mayor, the Honorable Al David T. Uy, hereinafter referred to as Island Garden City
of Samal;
The City Government of Digos, with principal office at the Digos City Hall, Davao
del Sur, represented in this MOA by its City Mayor, the Honorable Josef F. Cagas,
hereinafter referred to as Digos City;
The Municipal Government of Carmen, with principal office at the Carmen
Municipal Hall, Davao del Norte, represented in this MOA by its Municipal Mayor,
the Honorable Virginia J. Perandos, hereinafter referred to as the Carmen Municipal
Government;
The Municipal Government of Sta. Cruz, with principal office at the Sta. Cruz
Municipal Hall, Davao del Sur, represented in this MOA by its Municipal Mayor, the
Honorable Jaime G. Lao, hereinafter referred to as the Sta. Cruz Municipal
Government;
All of which entities together, shall be hereinafter referred to as the PARTIES;
WITNESSETH THAT:
WHEREAS, Davao City, along with its adjacent local government units (LGUs), shall
combine, collaborate and cooperate in order to form a metropolitan conglomerate,
extending to the cities of Tagum, Panabo and the Island Garden City of Samal, and the
municipality of Carmen, all in the province of Davao del Norte; and the city of Digos
and the municipality of Sta. Cruz, both in the province of Davao del Sur, to be known
as Metropolitan Davao, for a joint venture toward establishing an integrated and
coordinated system of urban infrastructure facilities and seruices to support the
l expanding residential, commercial, industrial and institutional activities therein;
