AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING A TRANSFORMATIVE
PROGRAM FOR CHILDREN AT RISK

Ord. No. 0729-19
Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled
that:
SECTION 1. EtE - This Ordinance shall be known as the 'TRANSFORMATM
PROGRAM FOR CHILDREil AT RISK ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CfilO.
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - The City Government of Davao
recognizes its obligations in securing the protection and welfare of every child in the City. It
is one of the primary roles of the City Government to provide necessary interuention
programs and other related seruices to Children-at-risk in relation to safeguarding their
human rights, development and well-being, and in relation to City Ordinance No. 0264-1?
SERIES of 20L2.
SECTION 3. DEFINffiON OF TERMS - As used in this Ordinance, the following
terms shall mean:
1. Children-at-risk (CAR) - refer to children who are vulnerable to and at risk of
behaving in a way that can harm themselves or others, or vulnerable and at risk of
living, pushed, and exploited to come into conflict with the law because of personal,
family, and social circumstances, such as, but not limited to, the following:
a. Being abused by any person through sexual, physical, psychological, mental,
economic or any other means and the parents or guardians refuse, unwilling or
unable to provide protection for the child;
b. Being exploited sexually or economically;
c. Being abandoned or neglected after diligent search and inquiry where the
parents or guardian cannot be found;
d. Coming from a dysfunctional or broken family or being without a parent or
guardian;
e. Being an out of school youth;
f . Being street children;
g. Being a member of a gang;
h. Living in a community with a high level of criminality or drug abuse;
i. Living in a situation where armed-conflict is prevalent;
j. Committing victimless crimes.
2. Child - referc to a person under eighteen (18) years old and those above 18 but
unable to protect themselves and/or physically or mentally incapable of doing so;
3. Interuention - refers to a SERIES of activities which are designed to address issues
that cause a child to come into conflict with the law or tend to make the vulnerability
in committing offienses. It may take the form of an individualized treatnent program
which may include counseling, skills training, education and other activities that will
enhance the psychological, emotional and psycho-social well-being of a child;
4. Parent Education - the various forms and alternative means of providing parents
with information, skills and suppoft systems to assist them in their needs as their
children's primary caregivers and educators. These include public and private parent
education programs linked to center homes and media-based children education
programs;
5. Transformative Approach are programs and interuentions that create
oppoftunities fur CAR to transform as an individual, create enabling community
conducive for children, develop community paftnership and volunteerism, enhance
responsible parenting and embrace community oriented programs;

6. Children at Risk Transformative Community-Based Center (CARTC) - is the
anchor of all community-based and psychosocial care and support program for CAR
which will operate 2al7; it will serve as their transformative and transitional home.
SECTION 4. PREVENTION AND TRANSFORMATIVE PROGRAMS -
1. The City Government of Davao through the City Social Services and Development
Office shall provide programs such as child-parent education in order for the Iatter to
enhance their capacity to raise their children in a healthy, loving and nurturing
environment and in a manner that is consistent with the child's normal growth and
development.
2. Community-based centers for children-at-risk, under the program of Children at Risk
Transformative Community-Based Center (CARTC), shall be established to provide
immediate, appropriate and holistic response to cases involving CAR. It will
temporarily shelter CAR while providing psychological care and suppoft with the
involvement of the parents, Barangay Council for the Protection of Children, NonGovernment Organizations, City Social Seruices and Development Office, and other
suppoft groups. With this end, it shall ensure a child-friendly, gender sensitive and
community-based approach that will prevent revictimization and risk of habitually
committing offenses that may cause a child to be in conflict with the law.
SECTION 5. ISIEMSfIU - When the pafties or competent authorities Ied by
the City Social Seruices and Development Office decide upon thorough assessment that
CAR must undergo the process of interuention, the following shall be taken into
consideration:
a. Upon thorough assessment, it was found out that the family or relatives are the
ones putting the child at risk;
b. Child is sexually or economically exploited;
c. When the child is physically, mentally and emotionally abused;
d. When the child is abandoned or neglected;
e. There is frequency of petty offenses;
f. Poses a threat to the safety of the community.
