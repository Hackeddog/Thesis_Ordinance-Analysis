Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE- This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
GRANTING LEGISI.ATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AND
SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, THE
RENEWAL OF THE MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT (MOA) TO BE ENTERED INTO
BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND PARKIANE WEST
ACADEMY, INC., REIATIVE TO THE EXPOSURE AND TRAINING OF THE I.ATTER'S
HEALTHCARE SERVICES STUDENTS AT THE LINGAP CENTER FOR MENTALLY
CHALLENGED CHILDREN".
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POTICY - SECTION 455, (b) (1) (vi) of Republic Act
No. 7160, othenruise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, provides, to wit:
"For efficient, effective and economical governance
the purpose of which is the general welfare of the
city and its inhabitants pursuant to SECTION 16 of
this Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in
all its business transactions and sign in its behalf all
bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such other
documents upon authority of the SANGGUNIANG
panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance;
SECTION 3. AUTHORIW - The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative authority
to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the renewal of
the Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) between the City Government of Davao and
Parklane West Academy, Inc., relative to the exposure and training of the latter's Healthcare
Seruices students at the Lingap Center for Mentally Challenged Children;
SECTION 4. SEPARABILIW CLAUSE- If, for any reason, any SECTION or provision
of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof
not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect,
SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY- This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.
ENACTED, on the 15th day of January, 2}lg, by a unanimous vote of all the
Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:
This MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT is made and executed by and between.
PARKLANE WEST ACADEMY, lNC., an educational institution duly organized and existing by virtue of
Philippine Laws with principal office at 572 L. Ma. Guerrero St. Davao City, represented by its President HUGHES
TRINIDAD A. TAN, hereinafter referred to as FIRST PARTY.
AND
THE CITY GOVERNMENT 0F DAVAO with principal address at Davao City Hall Drive, Davao City
represented in this instance by CITY MAYOR SARA Z. DUTERTE-CARPIO, hereinafter referred as SECOND
PARTY.
WITNESSETH
WHEREAS, the FIRST PARTY is an educational institution offering Health Care Services NC ll (HCS NC ll) requiring
the students to undergo an exposure and training in an affiliated health care / educational facility;
WHEREAS, the SECOND PARTY supervises the operations of a Health Care Referral Center also known as
LINGAP CENTER FOR MENTALLY - CHALLENGED CHILDREN, located at Durian St,, Tugbok, Davao City which
primarily cater neglected, abandoned and abused mentally-challenged male children and other children needing
special protection;
WHEREAS, SECOND PARry agrees to be a partner training center of the FIRST PARTY in the delivery of
concepts and practical teachings during the affiliates On the Job Training:
THAT the parties hereto mutually agree and covenant as follows:
1. FIRST PARTY shall provide SECOND PARTY the list of trainees one (1) week before training starts;
2. SECOND PARTY shall only accept trainees with endorsement letter from FIRST PARTY , approved by its
Program Director and Coordinator or duly appointed Clinical lnstructor;
3. SECOND PARTY shall provide the trainees with general orientation of its programs, services, center's
rules/guidelines and regulations.
4. FIRST PARTY trainees agree to abide the rules and regulations of SECOND PARTY which has the right
