Ord. No. 0703-19
Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled that:
SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as the'PUBLIC
SAFETY AilD SECURITY ANNOUI{CEMENT ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY'.
SECTION 2. PUBQSE - The purpose of this Ordinance is to ensure public
safety by raising awareness on disaster prevention protocols in order to take a morc
proactive stance on the matter.
SECTION 3. EOEBACE - This Ordinance shall cover all establishments with a
total floor area of the building of at least 2,790 square meterc which are:
1. Assembly establishments such as cinemas; conference rooms; exhibition
halls; places of worship; passenger stations and terminals of air, surface,
underground, and marine public transportation facilities; and recreational
facilities such as gyffis, bowling alleys, pool rooms, cockpits and the like.
2. Educational institutions such as schools; child day care facilities; museums;
libraries; and other institutions of learning or instruction.
3. Medical establishments such as hospitals; laboratories; clinics; nursing
homes; birth centers; and residential custodial care centers such as
nurseries, home for the aged and the like.
4. commercial establishments such as malls; supermarkets; departrnent
stores; shopping centers; hardware/construction supplies; showrooms;
auction rooms; and the like.
SECTION 4. MANDATORY PUBLIC SAFETY AND SECURIW
ANNOUNCEMENT - All establishments covered under this Ordinance shall announce
in an audible and persuasive manner, through public address systems, speakers, or
other audio-visual means, the emergency safety and security protocol of the
establishment.
The safety and security protocol shall be announced three (3) times
throughout the operational hours of the establishment.
SECTION 5. PENALTY CLAUSE - Owners of establishments who do not
comply with the above Ordinance shall be penalized as such:
. lst Offense - A fine of P3,000.00
. znd Offense - A fine of P5,000.00 and/or imprisonment of three months at
the discretion of the court
. 3d Offense - A fine of P5,000.00 and/or imprisonment of six months at
the discretion of the couft and revocation of business permit.
SECTION 6. REPEALING CTAUSE - Any Ordinance, Iocal issuances or rules
inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or modified,
accordingly.
SECTION 7. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any SECTION of this
Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof
which are not affected thereby, shall continue to be in full furce and effect.
SECTION 8. EFFECTMW CLAUSE - This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen
(15) days upon approval, posting and publication in a newspaper of local circulation.
ENACTED, on the 19th day of February, 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the
Members of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
