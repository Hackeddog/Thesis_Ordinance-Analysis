Ord. No. 0678-19
Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled that:
SECTION 1. EtE- This Ordinance shall be known as "IP ALTERNATM
DISPUTE RESOLUTION IN DAVAO CIW".
SECTION 2. PURPOSE - The purpose of this Ordinance is to preserue the rights
of Indigenous Peoples who voluntarily invoke the traditional way of resolving their
disputes through the Council of Elders in an Indigenous Cultural Community (ICC) duly
recognized in Davao City.
SECTION 3. AUTHORIW - This Ordinance is enacted pursuant to the provisions of
the following:
R.A. 8371 OR THE *INDIGENOUS PEOPLES'
RIGHTS ACT OF 1997'
SECTION 15. Justice System, Conflict Resolution
Institutions and Peace Building Processes. -The ICCs/IPs
shall have the right to use their own commonly accepted
justice systems, conflict resolution institutions, peace
building processes or mechanisms and other customary
laws and practices within their respective communities and
as may be compatible with the national legal system and
with internationally recognized human rights.
REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7L6O OR THE *LOCAL
GOVERNMENT CODE OF 1991"
SECTION 399. Lupong Tagapamayapa. (f) In
barangays where majority of the inhabitants are members
of the indigenous cultural communities, local systems of
settling disputes through their council of datus shall be
recognized without prejudice to the applicable provisions of
this Code.
SECTION 4. SCOPE. - This Ordinance shall apply to Indigenous Peoples who
voluntarily invoke the local or customary way of settling disputes (except those expressly
prohibited by law to be the subject of compromise), as a secondary mode of dispute
resolution, between and among Indigenous Peoples legitimately belonging to a recognized
tribe in Davao City and who are residents of areas with ancestral domain as listed in
Resolution No. 02199-15, SERIES of 2015 (including those who have subsequently been
qualified to have an IPMR) such as, but not limited to, the following:

SECTION 5. DEFINITION OF TERMS
1. Indigenous Cultural Community (ICC)- Refer to a group or homogenous
societies identified by self-ascription and ascription by others, who have
continuously lived as organized community on communally bounded and
defined territory, and who have, under claims of ownership since time
immemorial, occupied, possessed and utilized such territories, sharing
common bonds of language, customs, traditions and other distinctive
cultural traits, or who have, through resistance to political, social and
cultural inroads of colonization, non-indigenous religions and cultures,
became historically differentiated from the majority of Filipinos. (Rules and
Regulations Implementing Republic Act No. 837t, otherwise known as the
Indigenous Peoples'Rights Act of 1997)
2. Council of Elders-This is a duly constituted group of indigenous elders
recognized by the state whose functions constitutes preseruation of the
customs and tradition of the tribe, and provides insights and solutions to
address conflict resolution and peace-building processes, spiritual practices,
rituals and ceremonials. They lead the community in decision-making
processes aimed at protecting and promoting the sustainable development
of their ancestral domains. The Council of Elders of each tribe or each ICC
has their own tradition for adjudication.
3. Customary Laws-Refer to a body of written and/or unwritten rules, usages,
customs and practices traditionally and continually recognized, accepted
and obserued by respective ICCs/IPs.

SECTION 6. TRADITIONAL CONCILIATION PROCEEDING- When two or more
IP pafties in dispute are residing in an ICC duly recognized in Davao City, the IP parties
may resort to the traditional conciliation proceeding by the Council of Elders.
Provided that both IP parties voluntarily invoke the jurisdiction of the Council of
Elders and where such customary law does not involve any cruel, degrading or inhumane
conduct or punishment and does not include death penalties or excessive fines.
SECTION 7. PROCEDURE- The commonly accepted local justice system,
traditional conflict mechanism, peace building process and customary law and practice of
each tribe/ICC within their respective communities shall govern the procedure or
mechanism in the conflict resolution of IP disputes under this Ordinance.
SECTION 8. ARBITRATION AGREEMENT- Agreements during the application of
the traditional conciliation proceeding shall be made in writing signed by the parties in
dispute and Council of Elders and shall be submitted to the Katarungang Pambarangay for
confirmation. It shall be binding between the IP parties and shall be accorded with due
respect and duly enforced under the customary laws. The agreements herein shall not be in
violation of any laws, public poliry, and/or public orders.
SECTION 9. IN CASE OF INACTION- If the Council of Elders fails to act upon the
complaint within fifteen (15) days upon submission of the conflict to its jurisdiction, the
issue shall automatically be raised to the jurisdiction of the Katarungang Pambarangay
through the Lupong Tagapamayapa.
SECTION 10. SANCTION FOR NON-COMPLIANCE- Where the member of the
Katarungang Pambarangay/Barangay Officials, after having been informed by the parties
that they prefer the IP local justice system, unjustifiably interuenes and refuses to refer the
matter to the Council of Elders, the concerned barangay member/s shall be administratively
charged in accordance with applicable laws. On the other hand, the Council of Elders who
refuse or fail to discharge their responsibility under this Ordinance, shall be sanctioned
under their customary law and may be enforced by the NCIP in the exercise of its
superuision, after due proceeding.
SECTION 11. SEPARABILIW CLAUSE- If any provision of this Ordinance or its
application to any person or circumstance is declared unconstitutional, or inconsistent with
national laws, the other unaffected provisions of this Ordinance shall remain in full force
and effect.
SECTION 12. REPEALING CLAUSE- Any Ordinance, local issuances or rules and
regulations inconsistent with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed or
modified accordingly.
SECTION 5. EFFECTMTY- This Ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days
following its publication in a newspaper of general circulation, and its posting for three (3)
consecutive weeks at any conspicuous places at City Hall.
