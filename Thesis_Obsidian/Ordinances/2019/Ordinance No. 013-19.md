This Deed of Donation made and entered into this 12th day of December,
2018 at Matina, Aplaya, Davao City, Davao del Sur by and between:
The I(ABANG KALIKASAN NG PILIPINAS FOUNDATION, INC. with office
address at 4th Flr, JBD Plaza Btdg, #65 Mindanao Avenue, Bagong Pag-asa, 1128
Quezon City, Philippines, herein by its President/Chief Executive Officer

-andThe Local Government Unit of Davao City herein represented by its
City Mayor Hon. Sara Duterte-Carpio, hereinafter referred to as the LGU
WITNESSETH THAT:
WHEREAS, the Local Government Unit of Davao City, is duly mandated by law
to implement Coastal Resource Management and has established the
Punta Dumalag Marine Protected Area in its city and has approved
the MPA Management Plan;
WHEREAS, the Local Government Unit of Davao City has established the City
Coastal Law Enforcement Team and provided support to its operation;
WHERAS, KKPFI through the SMARTSeas PH PQect has been assisting the
Local Government Unit of Davao City in building their capacity for coastal
resource management and establishment and management of the marine
protected area;
WHEREAS, KKPFI in collaboration with DENR and BFAR in Davao Region has
also provided technical assistance in upgrading capacities of local
governments, MPA managers and coastal law enforcers of the city;
WHERAS, the KKPFI desires to continue to assist the Local Government Unit of
Davao City in strengthening the MPA/Fish Sanctuary Management and the
City Coastal Law Enforcement Team;
NOW THEREFORE, the above parties hereby execute this Deed of
Donation with the following terms and conditions:
ARTICLE I
For and in consideration of its desire to support the Local Government
Unit of Davao City, in its endeavor for coastal resource management, MpA
Management and Coastal Law Enforcement and as an act of liberality on its part,
KKPFI hereby voluntarily and freely gives, grants, transfers and conveys, by
way of donation, unto the said Local Government Unit of Davao City the
following MARTNE PROTECTED AREA (MpA) AssETs for effective
patrolling activities at Punta Dumalag Marine protected Area:
