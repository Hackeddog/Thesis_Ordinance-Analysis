WITNESSETH:
WHEREAS, pursuant to the General Welfare Clause stated in SECTION 16 of
Republic Act No. 7L60 (Local Government Code of 1991), every lmal government unit
(LGU) is mandated to exercise its power for the promotion of the general welfare like the
comfoft and convenience of its inhabitants;
WHEREAS, SECTION 17 of the above-said law mandated that LGU's shall likewise
exercise such other powers and discharge such other functions and responsibilities as are
necessary, appropriate, or incidental to efficient and effective and provisions of the basic
services and facilities, and in the case of Cities, to provide adequate transportation
facilities;
WHEREAS, with the rising demand for an efficient and effective public
transportation within its tenitorialjurisdiction, the City of Davao addresses the challenge
by way of providing a new mode of transportation to its inhabitants, through the High
Priority Bus System (HPBS), herein referred to as the HPBS Projecfi
WHEREAS, the HPBS Project is Davao City's response to the National
Government's flagship program which envisions a restructured, modern, well-managed
and environmentally sustainable transport sector wherein the commuters get to their
desti nations qu ickly, safely and comfortably;
WHEREAS, considering the HPBS is a comprehensive system reform that will
entirely change the public land transportation industry in Davao City, present public utility
transpott operators and drivers and their families will surely be affected by the reforms
to be undeftaken by the LGU as some of them might be absorbed into the new system
while others will be displaced;
WHEREAS, it is the responsibility of the LGU to assist the displaced drivers and
operators by providing them and their affected families new oppoftunities to enable them
to have a decent, sufficient and sustainable livelihood through the establishment of new
enterprise;
