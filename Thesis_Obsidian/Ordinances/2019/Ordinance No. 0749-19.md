Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. TITLE- This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING
ORDINANCE NO. 0115.14, SERIES OF 2OL4, ENTITLED *AN ORDINANCE
ESTABLISHING POLICIES, GUIDELINES AND RULES AND REGULATIONS FOR THE

ORIENTED PROJECT OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO".
SECTION 2. AMENDMENTS - SECTION 3, No. 7; SECTION 8; SECTION 10, letters h to j;
SECTION 11 and SECTION 13.
SECTION 3. POWER AND FUNCTIONS OF THE BOARD
7. To establish as the need arises, in pursuance to this Ordinance, similar
centers within the three (3) districts of the city, or satellite extensions
within the premises of existing facilities owned or operated by the City;
SECTION 8. WHO MAY OUALIFY AS TENANTS-Any person or entity
residing in Davao city who is engaged in producing, selling or distributing
locally made delicacies and food products, goods or other souvenir items
using domestic raw materials may apply as tenant/lessee of the
Center.
Nationa! government agencies, Loca! Government Units (LGUs)
and other entities enjoying privileges and incentives under the
law may also signify their intent to avail a stalUbooth or spaoe
for the sale and promotion of their own products, seruices and
advocacies at the Center.
Fufther, in order to strengthen the Sisterhood Agreement
entered into by the City Government of Davao with other LGUs,
such LGU may be allowed to enter into a Lease or Memorandum
of Agreement with the City Government to display for sale their
locally made products at the Center.
SECTION 9. EVALUATION AND APPROVAL OF APPLICATION- The
City Economic Enterprise, acting as the Center Management, shall
evaluate the qualifications and the products of all interested lesseeapplicants based on criteria as prescribed under the Implementing Rules
and Regulations to be adopted by the Board. The list of qualified
applicants shall be submitted to the Board for approval. Consequently,
a Lease Contract or Memorandum of Agreement shall be
executed betrareen the City Government of Davao and the
awardee.
SECTION 10. LOCALLY MADE PRODUCTS SHOWCASE.
h) Clothing, textiles and other related accessories;

j) Any other items, goods, or seruices as may be approved by the Board.

Provided however, that the Chief Executive, upon recommendation by
the Board, may defer or waive the rental fee owing from those agencies

SECTION 3. REPEALING CLAUSE- All other city ordinances, Executive Orders and
rules and regulations, which are inconsistent with this Ordinance are hereby deemed amended,
repealed or modified accordingly.

this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof not
affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.
