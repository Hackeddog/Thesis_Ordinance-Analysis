SECTION 1. EtE- This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE OI{

SECTION 2. PURPOSE- This Ordinance is enacted for the following purposes:
1) Guide, control and regulate future growth and development of the City in
accordance with pertinent laws on building height regulations and on
allowable urban development mechanisms responsive to the challenges and
opportun ities of u rban ization;
2) Provide an orderly pattern of development that is both transit-oriented and
transit responsive;
3) Conform to flight safety regulations and improve disaster-resilience;
4) Preserue view of cultural and heritage sites;
5) Promote and improve urban livability index such as better air quality,
sufficient access to sunlight, open and green spaces and urban connectivity
and mobility comforts;
6) Provide incentives to the private sector in improving the built environment.
SECTION 3. DECLARATION OF POUCY - It is the policy of the City
Government of Davao to manage efficiently the urban landscape where mobility
potentials are maximized, greening programs actualized through innovative space
provision, and disaster-resilience intensified through building height restrictions. The City
shall continue to devise zoning and Iand use mechanisms responsive to the challenges
and opportunities as may be presented by urban growth and development. The scheme
for Additional floor-space credits to qualified private individuals or corporations which
have allocated open spaces for the public through the Privately Owned Public Open
Spaces (POPOS) program and the premium required from developers opting for
additional floor areas over and above the regular height limit being imposed by the city
are strategies for environment investments. The premium floor space that may be
permitted in a zone for a specified fee shall provide the City an avenue for revenue
generation that may be devoted to road improvement and greening programs.
It shall be further declared to be the policy of the City to strictly enforce measures
in accordance with existing and relevant land use provisions.
SECTION 4.
- This Ordinance shall prescribe the
rules applicable to any building construction activity within the territorial jurisdiction of
Davao City. This Ordinance shall prescribe the guidelines on the applicable permissible
height in relation to the distance from the flight path and with reference to the desired
urban densities and in setting relative order to the City's skyline and preservation effotts.
Variance or additional premium height over and above the regular height limit set by the
City shall be allowed and permitted provided that it shall not be higher than the
limitations set by the Civil Aviation Authority of the Philippines (CAAP).

SECTION 5.
- For the purpose of the Rules and
Regulations, the following terms and phrases shall mean as follows:
1) Obstacle Limitation Surface (OLS) - is an imaginary surface above ground that
sets the height limit for building structures to ensure flight navigational safety;
2) Floor Area Ratio (FAR) is the ratio of the total plot area to the total building area
and is determined by the size of the plot and the width of the road in front of the
plot;
3) "Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP)" shall refer to the document embodying
specific proposal for guiding, regulating growth and/or development;
4) HLURB - Housing and Land Use Regulatory Board;
5) Conforming Use - a use, which is in accordance with the zone classification as
provided for in the Ordinance;
6) Green Architecture - building and structure design philosophy that aims at
minimal use of non-renewable and/or polluting materials and resources/energy in
construction and use of a facility when completed;
7) Green Space- community space consisting of land (as parks) rather than
buildings;
B) Privately-Owned Public Open Spaces (POPOS) are physical spaces which are
privately owned but legally required to be open to the public under a land-use law
and arrangement with the City Govemment of Davao such as but not limited to
plazas, terraces, atriums, small parks, view decks, playground, food park, and
alleyways that connects two structures without passing through a regular street.
9) Zoning Map a duly authenticated map delineating the different zone
classifications into which the whole city is divided.
10) Mean Sea Level - the zero reference point from which the Iimits shal! be
measured from.
