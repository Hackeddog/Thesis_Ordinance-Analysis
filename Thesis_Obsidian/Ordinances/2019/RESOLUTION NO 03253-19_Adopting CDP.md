RESOLUTION NO. 03253-19
SERIES of 2019 -

ENACTING AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE 2018-2022
COMPREHENSIVE DEVELOPMENT PLAN AND DAVAO CITY
ECOLOGICAL PROFILE -

Res. No. 03253-19

WHEREAS, Atty. Zuleika T. Lopez, City Administrator, by the authority of the City
Mayor per Executive Order No. 06, dated August 1, 2016, certified the matter as urgent
to be passed under Suspended Rules;

WHEREAS, Davao City is a globally livable City and a center of excellence in
governance, investment, tourism, climate change adaptation, disaster resiliency, and
sustainable growth, driven by empowered citizenry;

WHEREAS, the 2018-2022 Comprehensive Development Plan and Davao City
Ecological Profile, which is collectively crafted with different stakeholders, is important to
easily identify priority projects and programs until 2022;

WHEREAS, the expedient passing of the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod Resolution
relative to this undertaking would meet the deadline of implementation as set by the
Department of the Interior and Local Government;

_ WHEREAS, the Office of the City Legal Officer, in its Legal Opinion No. 94, SERIES
of 2019, dated April 29, 2019, found no necessity in rendering a legal opinion on the
matter as the document forwarded merely embodies the City Government's
Development Plan;

NOW THEREFORE, on motion of Councilor J. Melchor B. Quitain Jr., duly
seconded by Councilors Victorio U. Advincula Jr., Jaffar U. Marohomsalic, Avegayle
Dalodo Ortiz and Pilar C. Braga, be it resolved, as it is hereby resolved,TO ENACT AN
ORDINANCE APPROVING THE 2018-2022 COMPREHENSIVE DEVELOPMENT
PLAN AND DAVAO CITY ECOLOGICAL PROFILE;

RESOLVED FURTHER, that copies of this Resolution be furnished the Office of
the City Mayor through the City Administrator's Office, the Vice Mayor's Office, the
Members of the 18" City Council, the City Planning and Development Office, the City
Legal Office, the City Environment and Natural Resources Office, the City Engineer's
Office, the City Tourism Operations Office and all other offices/departments concerned,
for their information, guidance and appropriate action;

ADOPTED, May 21, 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of the
Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.

ORDINANCE NO. 0781-19
SERIES of 2019.

AN ORDINANCE - APPROVING THE 2018-2022
"COMPREHENSIVE DEVELOPMENT PLAN AND DAVAO CITY
ECOLOGICAL PROFILE

Ord. No. 0781-19

Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:

SECTION 1. TITLE- This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
APPROVING THE 2018-2022 COMPREHENSIVE DEVELOPMENT PLAN AND
DAVAO CITY ECOLOGICAL PROFILE".

SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - SECTION 25 and SECTION 455(b) (1)
(vi) of the Local Government Code provides that:

SECTION 25. National Supervision over Local Government
Units. -

(b) National agencies and offices with project implementation
functions shall coordinate with one another and with the local
government units concerned in the discharge of these
functions. They shall ensure the participation of local .
government units both in the planning and implementation of
said national projects.

SECTION 455 (b) (1) (vi) -

(b) For efficient, effective and economical governance the
purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and its
inhabitants pursuant to SECTION 16 of this Code, the city
mayor shall:

(vi) Represent the city in all its business transactions and sign
in its behalf all bonds, contracts, and obligations, and such
other documents upon authority of the SANGGUNIANG
Panlungsod or pursuant to law or ordinance.

SECTION 3.AUTHORITY - The City Mayor is hereby 'granted legislative
authority to approve the 2018-2022 Comprehensive Development Plan and Davao City
Ecological Profile.

SECTION 4. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE- If, for any reason, any SECTION or provision
of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof
not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.

SECTION 5. EFFECTIVITY- This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.

ENACTED, on the 21% day of May, 2019, by a unanimous vote of all the Members of
the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
