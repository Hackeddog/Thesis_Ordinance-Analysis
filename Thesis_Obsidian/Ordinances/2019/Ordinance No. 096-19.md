SECTION 66; Exemption- The tax herein imposed does not apply in the following
Cases, provided that exemption should first be obtained for this purpose from the
SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod :
a) Where the admission fees are collected for and in behalf of the charitable,

paid admission; provided, further, that such exhibitions, show, performance,

b) Where the admission fees are collected in connection with the holding of

payment of amusement tax on the proceeds of the Concert-For-A-Cause featuring the
world-renowned Loboc Children's Choir of Bohol to be held on December 21 and 22,
2019, at the Almendras Gym, this City, is hereby approved.
