KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENT:
This MEMORAI{DUM OF AGREEMENT is made and executed by and between:
DAVAO MEDICAL SCHOOT FOUNDATION, II{C is a medica/nursing school duly organized and
existing by virtue of Philippine laws with principal office at J.P laurel Avenue Davao City, represented by
its President JOI{ATHAN A. ALEGRE, MD, FPAFP, hereinafter referred to as FIRST PARTY.
AND
THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO with principaladdress at Davao City hall Drive, Davao
City represented in this instance by CITY MAYOR SARA Z. DUTERTE, hereinafter referred as SECOND
PARTY.
WTTNESSETH
WHEREAS, the FIRST PARTY is a medica/nursing school requiring the students to undergo a Geriatric
nursing exposure in an affiliated health care/ educationalfacility;
WHEREAS, the SECOND PARTY supervises the operations of the Health Care Referral Center also known
as CO SU GIAN Home for the aged located at Cabantian Buhangin District Davao City which primarily
cater neglected, abandoned, sickly, elderly clients needing special care protection.
WHEREAS, SECOND PARTY agrees to be partner training center of the RRST PARTY in the delivery of
concepts and teachings during the affiliates Geriatric Exposure:
THAT the parties hereto mutually agree and covenant as follows
1. FIRST PARTY shall provide SECOND PARTY the list of student nurses one (1) week before
Geriatric Nursing exposure.
2. SECOND PARTY shall accept students with endorsement letter from FIRST PARTY, approved by
its DEAN College of Nursing and Clinical Coordinator.
3. SECOND PARTY shall provide the student nurses generalorientation of its programs, services,
cente/s rules/guidelines and regulations.
4. FIRST PARTY student nurses agree to abide the rules and regulations of SECOND PARTY which
has the right to decline a particular student to continue the Related Learning Experience (RLE) on
Geriatric Nursing for any violation incurred.
5. The FIRST PARTY student shall be allowed to handle elder clients, subject to the following rules:
.
Student nurses advised to keep the confidentiality of handled clients.
.
Student nurses assisting the assigned clients shall observed proper decorum.
o Student nurses shall refrain from giving negative and offensive remarks and
harsh jokes about the client at any time.
.
Loitering is not allowed while clients are on siesta time.
.
