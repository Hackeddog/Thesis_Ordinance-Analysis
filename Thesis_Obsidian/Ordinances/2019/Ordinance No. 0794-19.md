Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:

ACCEPTING THE DEED OF DONATION OF AN OPEN BASKETBALL COURT AND
MULTI-PURPOSE BUILDING OF MOLAVE HOMES SUBDIVISION LOCATED AT

OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO CIW'.
SECTION 2. ACCEPTANCE AND AUTHORIW- The open basketbalt court and multipurpose building of Molave Homes Subdivision which are the subject of the Deed of Donagon
are described as follows:
a. Open Basketball Court
Location: Phase 3 Open Space (Block 36, Lot 31)
Size: 437 square meters (28.6 meters x 15.3 meters)
Specifications: Concrete slab court with B.I. post and fiberglass board, complete
with ring and related accessories
b. Multi-Purpose Building
Location: Phase 2 Open Space (Block 28, Lot 14)
Size: 60 square meters (6 meters x 10 meters)
Specifications: Covered on pre-painted long span roof
The Deed of Donation executed by ALSoNS DEVELOPMENT & INVESTMENT CoRpoRATIoN,
represented by its Executive Vice President, Ms. Rosvida A. Dominguez, in favor of the City
Government of Davao is hereby accepted. The Honorable City Mayor of Davao is hereby
authorized to sign the Deed of Donation involving the abovementioned real propefties for and
in behalf of the City Government of Davao City.
SECTION 3. SEPARABILIW CLAUSE- If, for any reason, any SECTION or provision
of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof
not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.
SECTION 4. EFFECTMTY- This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.
