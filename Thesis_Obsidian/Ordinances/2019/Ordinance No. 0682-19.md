SECTION l. SECTION 1 of Ordinance No. 0218-09, SERIES of 2009, is hereby amended to
read as follows:
SECTION 7, EU- -This Ordinance shall be known as "Davao City Park Systems
Management Code".
SECTION 2. SECTION 2 of the said Ordinance is hereby amended to read as follows:
SECTIOM, DECLARAT1OII OF POLfiCY, - It is hereby declared the policy of the
local government of Davao City to establish and maintain free, publicly accessible, and
safe green and public open places as an impoftant component of a sustainable city.
It shall ensure the protection and sustainability of the environment taking into
consideration climate change adaptation in synergy with disaster risk reduction and
management, principles of biodiversity and preseruation of historical and cultural
heritage in all development prccesses.
SECTION 3. SECTION 3 of the said Ordinance is hereby amended to read as follows:
SECTION 3. DEFIIIITIOII OF TERtlS, -As used in this Code, the following terms
shall mean:
A. "Green Space"- An area set apart to be planted with trees and ornamental
plants and/or to be developed for water pond purposes to promote urban
biodiversity and climate change mitigation.
B. "Green Strip" - A long narrow area of land planted with trees, shrubs,
ornamental plants which include, but not limited to, sidewalks and street
islands.
C. oPark"- refers to any public area of land set aside for aesthetic, educational,
recreational or cultural use by the public in essentially urban surroundings,
such as, but not limited to, public squares, plazas, promenades, center
islands, heritage sites, historical sites, protected areas, conservation
areas, riverbanks and memorials.
D. oPermeable Paving System"- is an architectural and engineering design
that allows storm water or flood to seep through the surface material laid
down on an area.
E. *Privately-owned Public Open Spaces (POPOS)" - dte publicly
accessible spaces in the form of plazas, terraces, atriums, small parks,
and even snippets which are provided and maintained by private
developers.
The City shall adopt the concept of Privately-Owned Public Open Spaces (POPOS) and
shall also promote a participative, empowered, and environmentally conscious
community by developing linkages and interactions between and among local and
national government agencies and stakeholders, multi-sectoral pafticipation, under a
shared environmental governance of mutual accountability and collaborative
management.

SECTION 4. SCOPE AIID APPLICABILITY, - The Davao City Park Systems
