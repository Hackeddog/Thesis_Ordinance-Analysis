that
Ord. No. 0303-23
Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled,
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
INSTITUTIONALIZING THE LESS PAPER SESSION SYSTEM THROUGH THE
UPGRADING OF THE LEGISLATIVE INFORMATION SUPPORT SYSTEM PROGRAM
(ussP) oF THE SANGGUNTANG pANLUNGSOD".
SECTION 2. DECI-ARATION OF POUCY - Pursuant to Republic Act 7160,
otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, which states that:
"SECTION 16. General Welfare - Every local government unit shall
exercise the powers expressly granted, those necessarily implied
therefrom, as well as powers necessary, appropriate, or incidental for its
efficient and effective governance, and those which are essential to the
promotion of the general welfare."
SECTION 3. LESS PAPER SESSION SYSTEM - This refers to the method of
conduct of the session day wherein Members of the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod will be able
to access their agenda through their respective emails registered with the Office of the
Secretary to the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod.
The Office of the Secretary to the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod is required to produce
only three (3) copies of the Agenda and all its attachments to reduce the utilization of print
materials as a cost effective and environment friendly measure.
The Agenda and all Committee Reports, attachments and items scheduled on a
pafticular day shall be convefted to digital copies which will be flashed on screen for the
viewing benefit of everyone inside the session hall.
All Committee Reports, attachments, correspondence and all other matters for
inclusion on a pafticular session day shall be sent through electronic mail (email) which
contains the Digital signatures of the respective Members of the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod.
SECTION 4. THE LEGISIATIVE INFORMATION SUPPORT SYSTEM
PROGRAM (LTSSP)
1. A Cloud subscription shall be procured to provide for the storage of all archived
electronic files of the session's Agenda, attachments and each shall be duly
identified by date of the session day;
2. The same shall be managed by the designated staff to be determined by the
Secretary to the Sanggunian;
3. The implementation of the LISSP will be in two (2) phases wherein 1't Phase
includes the following :
a. Conversion of status of all items included in the Agenda, for items
referred to respective committees and the status of the items for committee
hearings;
b. Tracking of items, committee hearings and all other matters submitted to
the SP Secretariat for Indorsement to the respective committees of the
Sanggunian.

Ord. No. 0303-23
Phase 2 implementation shall include the following:
a. Conversion of paper documents to digital form to be used during SP
Sessions, preparation of Agenda, Committee Reports, Stenographic Notes, and
other attached documents to a central Cloud Storage;
b. Access of the items in the Agenda through the cloud storage which shall be
available to all SP Members and designated personnel of the Office of the
Secretary to the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod;
c. Use of Digital Signature shall be made by the SP Member through his/her
designated personnel for submission of Committee Repofts, communications
and attachments, to be included in the Agenda;
d. The Office of the Secretary to the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod shall provide a
system for the tracking of documents, retrieval and storage, monitoring
reports, and feedback repofts;
e. Create an archive for all approved resolutions and ordinances, and provide
sufficient identification, for easy navigation and search.
SECTION 5. APPROPRIATION - To fully implement the "Less Paper Session
System", acquisition, procurement and installation of the following items shall be included
in the budget appropriation of the Office of the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod:
1. (30) Units PORTABLE DEVICE;
2. (B) Units Laptops;
3. (1) document camera;
4. (6) Units Access Points;
5. Cloud Subscription;
6. (1) unit New Generation Firewall;
7. Enterprise Broadband Internet Access;
B. Development Cost for implementation of LISSP Phase 2.
All recurring annual expenses shall be included in the Annual Fund Budget of the Office
of the Secretary to the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod.
SECTION 6. TMPLEMENTING RULES - The Office of the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod
shall formulate the necessary guidelines for the effective operation of the Less Paper
Session implementation any time after the effectivity of the Ordinance.
SECTION 7. REPEALING CLAUSE - All resolutions, Ordinances, executive orders,
rules and regulations, or parts thereof inconsistent with this Ordinance are hereby repealed
or modified accordingly.
SECTION 8. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE - If, for any reason, any SECTION or provision of
this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof
not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect.
SECTION 9. EFFECTMTY - This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval.

Ord. No. 0303-23
ENACTED, on the 26th day of September 2023, by a unanimous vote of all the Members
of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
