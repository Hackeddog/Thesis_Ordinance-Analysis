SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORITY TO THE CITY MAYOR TO SIGN, FOR AND
IN BEHALF OF THE CITY OF DAVAO, THE AMENDMENT TO THE MEMORANDUM

12'.
