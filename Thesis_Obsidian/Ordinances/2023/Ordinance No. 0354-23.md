already full of cables, the pole owner is required to install another layer.

SECTION 14. PHASING OUT OF TELCO COPPER LINES, WIRES, AND

SECTION 15. TELCO MAINTENANCE OR EXPANSION LOOP - There
shall only be a maximum of two (2) telco maintenance or expansion loops per pole
with a maximum diameter of 1.5 feet and a maximum of ten (10) turns.
SECTION 16. UPGRADING OF TELCO NETWORK ACCESS POINT
(NAP) - To limit the number of Network Access Points (NAP) on poles, telcos may
upgrade their existing Network Access Points (NAP) to cater more subscribers. The
number of Network Access Points (NAP) boxes per pole shall be limited to four (4),
unless otherwise allowed by the pole owner provided that there shall be no obstruction
to the pole tags/pole numbers.
SECTION 17. COLOR CODING OF CABLES - All utility companies shall mark
their cables according to their respective color assignments to facilitate easy
identification particularly for removal, repairs & maintenance.
The City Engineer's Office, in consultation and coordination with the private and
public utility companies, shall immediately assign color assignments for utility
companies operating in the city.
SECTION 18. CABLE HANGER - Each Telco shall install a cable hanger along
midspan with spacing of less than one (1) meter. Telco cables extending from pole to
the next pole shall be placed in the cable hanger to avoid it from dangling.
SECTION 19. VERTICAL CLEARANCE LIMITER - The City Engineer's
Office or the Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) shall install vertical
clearance limiters at strategic roads falling within their jurisdiction to prevent the
passage of trucks with a gross height of more than the allowable gross height as
provided under the Traffic Code of the City.
