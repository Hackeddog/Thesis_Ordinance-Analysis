Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. IIIE - This Ordinance shall be known as "THE ANTI-BULLYING
ORDINANCE OF DAVAO CITY.'
SECTION 2. POUCY DECLARATION - It is hereby declared the policy of the
City of Davao to ensure that the fundamental rights of persons to life, liberty, property,
and security are promoted and protected. As a society composed of persons, the City
recognizes the impoftance of individuality and the free exercise thereof as an essential
ingredient of a well-functioning democracy. The practice of bullying, however, shall be
met with disdain as it is a form of abuse which serves to deprive others of the free
exercise of their own rights. In consonance with the national government's poliry under
Republic Act No. 10627 declaring acts of bullying as unacceptable, Republic Act No. 11313
where both men and women must be safe and secure not only in private but also in public
spaces, online, workplaces, and educational institutions, and other pertinent national
laws, all forms of bullying shall not be countenanced.
SECTION 3. ACTS OF BULLYING -"Bullying" as adopted from Republic Act No.
10627, shall refer to any severe or repeated act by one or more persons through a written,
verbal or electronic expression, or a physical act or gesture, or any combination thereof,
directed at another individual that has the effect of actually causing or placing the latter
in reasonable fear of physical or emotional harm to himself or damage to his propefi;
creating a hostile environment at the schoo!, workplace, or any other situation for the
individual; infringing on the rights of the individual; or materially and substantially
disrupting the education process or the orderly operation of a school, workplace, or any
other area, venue, or situation; such as, but not limited to, the following:
a) Any unwanted physical contact between the bully and the complainant, such as
punching, pushing, shoving, kicking, slapping, tickling, headlocks, inflicting pranks,
teasing, fighting and the use of available objects as weapons;
b) Any act that causes damage to an individual's psyche and/or emotional well-being;
c) Any slanderous statement or accusation that causes an individual undue
emotional distress, such as directing foul language or profanity at the target,
name-calling, tormenting and commenting negatively on an individual's looks,
clothes and body;
d) Cyberbullying or any bullying done through the use of technology or any
electronic means.
Bullying shall be further classified into four (4) aspects, namely:
a. Bullying in educational institutions
b. Bullying in the workplace
c. Cyberbullying
d. Bullying in other public places
Any person who commits the acts described above is considered a bully. Persons who,
through the acts described above, retaliate against another person who repofts acts of
bullying or who provides information during an investigation or is a witness under such
investigation, shall also be considered to have committed bullying.

SECTION 5. ACTS OF BULLYING IN THE WORKPI-ACE. Acts of bullying in the
workplace can be defined as the intentiona! and repeated less favorable treatment of a
person by another or others in the workplace, which may be considered unreasonable
and inappropriate workplace practice, but excludes acts which necessarily fall under
management prerogative and discipline done in good faith and without violating
employee rights. Management prerogative here refers to the employer's inherent right to
regulate, according to his own discretion and judgment, all aspects of employment,
including hiring, work assignments, working methods, the time, place and manner of
work, work supervision, transfer of employees, lay-off of workers, discipline, dismissal,
and recall of employees.

SECTION 6. CYBERLIBEL - Cyberlibel is the unlawful or prohibited acts of libel
as defined in Afticle 355 of the Revised Penal Code, as amended, committed through a
computer system or any other similar means which may be devised in the future.
Computer system refers to any device or group of interconnected or related
devices, one or more of which, pursuant to a program, performs automated processing
of data. It covers any type of device with data processing capabilities including, but not
limited to, computers and mobile phones. The device consisting of hardware and software
may include input, output and storage components which may stand alone or be
connected in a network or other similar devices. It also includes computer data storage
devices or media.
