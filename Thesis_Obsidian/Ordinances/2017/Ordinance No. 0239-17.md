This Deed of Donation, made and executed in the City of Davao, Philippines, by
TECHNO-TRADE RESOURCES, lNC., a duly organized domestic corporation under
Philippine Laws with business address at 164 R. Castillo Street, Agdao, Davao City,
represented by WILBERT C TAN, hereinafter called the DONOR;
and
THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, herein represented by SARA Z.
DUTERTE, City Mayor, with official address at the City Hall of Davao, San Pedro St.,
Davao City, hereinafter called the DONEE;
WITNESSETH
That the DONOR is a legitimate business operator within the City of Davao that
wishes to do its part in the upkeep and development of the City;
That the DONOR supports the programs of the City Government and adheres to
all its policies and regulations which enhance the City's inherent characteristics to keep
its positive image to its citizens and visitors;
t
I
That the DONOR sees the urgency of providing convenient peddstrian
overpasses in view of the City's intensified campaign against jaywalking;
That the DONOR desires to be of assistance to the City Government of Davao
by providing more convenience to its people through the provision of reliable roofings
for pedestrian overpasses;
That in view of the foregoing, the DONOR, as an act of liberality, by these
presents, transfers and conveys, by way of donation, unto the DONEE, the following
properties for the benefit of the Davaoenos and visitors of the City of Davao.
Metalplas Roofings for:
1. Overpass along Damosa, Lanang, Davao City;
2. Overpass near Almendras Gym, Davao City;
That the DONEE hereby manifests acceptance of the above described
properlies under the following guarantees laid down by the DONOR, to wit:
a. That the installation of such roofings shall be for the exclusive account
DONOR and shall be in accordance with the design approved by th
Engineer's Office;

the donated roofings and within one (1) year period from such installation,
except when caused by force majeure;
c. That the DONOR further undertakes to repair and/or replace any damaged
roofing installed by them by virtue of this donation, and will continue to take
such time that the DO
