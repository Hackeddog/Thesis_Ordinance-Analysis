and
PHILIPPINE EAGLE CONSERVATION PROGRAM FOUNDATION
INC., hereafter referred to as PECPFI, a private, non-stock, non-profit
organization, duly registered and established under and by virtue of the laws of
the Republic of the Philippines, with principal address at Malagos, Baguio
District, Davao City, represented by Mr. Dennis I. Salvador, Executive
Director.
WITNESSETH
WHEREAS, the PECPFI has embarked on a project to accelerate the captive breeding program of the
Philippine Eagle to replenish the wild eagle population now threatened with extinction and acquire resources
needed to accomplish this objective;
WFIEREAS, the Davao City Government, has committed to support PECPFI's wildlife conservation
initiatives through a grant aid in support and assistance to the conservation efforts of PECPFI;
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the Parties agree as follows.
SECTION I. SUBJECT OF AGREEMENT
A. The Davao City Government allocated in 2017 Php 1,000,000.00 to cover,food stock expense and
release said amount to the PECPFI.
B. That the PECPFI shall include the name of the Davao City Government in the acknowledgement erected
at the Philippine Eagle Center in Malagos and PECPFI's publications.
