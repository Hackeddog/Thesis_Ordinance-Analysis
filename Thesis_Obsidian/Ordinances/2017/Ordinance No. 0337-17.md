DEED OF SALE
KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:
This DEED OF SALE, made and entered into by and between:
That MAXIMINO A. SALANG JR., of legal age, Filipino, married
and a resident of Davao City, hereinafter referred to as the VENDOR;
- andThat CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a political subdivision of
the Republic of the Philippines, established pursuant to law, with
principal office at City Hall, Davao City, represented by its City Mayor,
SARA Z. DUTERTE, hereinafter referred to as the VENDEE;
WITNESSETHI
That the VENDOR is the owner of that parcel of land located at
Barangay Magtuod, Davao City, containing an area of ONE THOUSAND FOUR
HUNDREDTWEENTY EIGHTY (L,428) square meters, more or less and which
property is covered by Transfer Certificate of Title No. T-439758 of the
Registry of Deeds for the Davao City;
That the VENDOR has offered to sell the aforementioned real property
unto and in favor of the herein VENDEE, who is willing to buy to the
VENDOR;
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of P571,2OO'OO,
Philippine Currency, receipt of which is hereby acknowledged to the full
satisfaction of the VENDOR, said VENDOR hereby SELLS, TRANSFERS and
CONVEYS in an absolute and irrevocable manner unto the herein VENDEE, its
heirs, successors and assigns, the above-described real property, including
all improvements found thereon, free from all liens and encumbrances;
That FURTHER, the VENDOR hereby warrants valid title to and peaceful
possession of the property herein sold and conveyed and agrees to execute
any and all documents that may be necessary for the transfer of the title of
the property to the VENDEE.
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have hereunto affixed their
signatures this
of
at Davao City, Philippines.
A. SALA
