Ord. No. 0368-17
Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled that:
SECTION 1. TITLE- This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
GRANTING LEGISLATIVE AUTHORIW TO THE CITY MAYOR TO ENTER TNTO
AND SIGN, FOR AND IN BEHALF OF THE CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO THE
LOAN AGREEMENT TO BE ENTERED INTO BY AND BETWEEN THE CITY
GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO AND THE ASSOCIATION OF DAVAO ORGANIC
ADVOCATES RELATIVE TO THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE MAGNEGOSYO TA
DAY PROGRAM";
SECTION 2. DECLARATION OF POLICY - SECTION 455, (b) (1) (vi) of
Republic Act No. 7L60, othenruise known as the Local Government Code of 1991,
provides, to wit
"For efficient, effective and economical governance the
purpose of which is the general welfare of the city and
its inhabitants pursuant to SECTION 16 of this Code, the
city mayor shall represent the city in all its business
transactions and sign in its behalf all bonds, contracts,
and obligations, and such other documents upon
authority of the SANGGUNIANG panlungsod or pursuant to
law or ordinance";
SECTION 3. AUTHORITY- The City Mayor is hereby granted legislative
authority to enter into and sign, for and in behalf of the City Government of Davao, the
Loan Agreement to be entered into by and between the City Government of Davao and
the Association of Davao Organic Advocates relative to the implementation of the
Magnegosyo Ta Day Program;
SECTION 4. SEPARABILIW CLAUSE- If, for any reason, any SECTION or provision
of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or provisions hereof
not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and effect;
SECTION 5. EFFECTMTY- This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval;
ENACTED, on the 12th day of December,2077, by a unanimous vote of all the
Members of the Sanggunian present, there being a quorum.

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENT:
This agreement made and entered into by and between:
The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a Local Government Unit existing by virtue
of the laws of the Republic of the Philippines with principal office at City Hall Drive, San
Pedro Street, Davao City, herein represented by the City Mayor, Hon. SARA Z,
DUTERTE-CARPIO, hereinafter referred to as the CITY;
and
The ASSOCIATION OF DAVAO ORGANIC ADVOCATES (ADOA), a duly registered
worke/s association with the Department of Labor & Employment, with principal office at
City Hall 4th Floor City Agriculturist Office Annex Building, Davao City herein represented
by AMALIA C. MORDENO, President authorized representative, hereinafter referred to
as BORROWER.
WITNESSETH THAT:
WHEREAS, it is the policy of the CITY to uphold the rights of women and the belief
in their wofth and dignity as human beings;
WHEREAS, women shall be recognized as full and equal partners of men in
development and nation building and men shall share equal with all forms of productive
and reproductive activities;
WHEREAS, in consonance to the said policy, the CITY, through its MAGNEGOSYO TA
DAY (MTD) Program extends financial and technical assistance and other seruices to
enable
women to become social entrepreneurs through the approval of appropriation for
financing of the ADF#L7026 per Ordinance No. 0122-16;
WHEREAS, by enabling women to become social entrepreneurs, they become the
city government's partners in responding to a host of interrelated social needs and issues,
not only passive recipients of financial assistance;
WHEREAS, medium and large cooperatives engage in lending seruices whose
members include women who are into micro business/enterprise activities;
WHEREAS, these credit cooperatives can be paftners of the City Government in
implementing the MAGNEGOSYO TA DAY Program as conduit cooperatives with the
objective of extending the program to its women members pafticularly marginalized and
vulnerable groups of women;
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the pafties
hereto bind themselves to the terms and conditions of this agreement, as follows:

1. The CITY:
a) Shall release the amount of Two Hundred Twenty-Six Thousand Pesos (P
226,000.00) as appropriation under ADF#17026 - 2 Magnegosryo Ta Day
Program to finance Proiect 7: Demo Farm on Organic Farming; Project
2: "Plant-based Healthy Food / Snacks and Organically- gnown
Veggies / FruiB School-based filarketing" and Project 3: Additional
Capitalization for the Memberc,
b) Shall ensure that the loan shall be payable within the maximum period of
twenty-four (24) months with zero interest from the date of the final release of
the loan;
c) Shall ensure that the fund shall be utilized only for the materials / inputs in the
Demo Farm on Organic Farming, Plant-based Healthy Food / Snacks and
Organically grown Veggies/ Fruits School-based Marketing and Additional
Capitalization for the Members;
d) Through the concerned line agencies, shall strengthen the capabilities and
enhance entrepreneurial skills of women through the conduct of
training/seminars, as follows:
. Organizational Building and Strengthening
. Grassroots Entrepreneurship Management
. Simplified Record Keeping
. Other related training
e) Shall conduct monthly monitoring of loan assistance to borrower to ensure
compliance of the program;
g) Shall exercise relative superuision over the project to ensure that over-all
implementation is in accordance with the existing guidelines, rules and
regulations. (see attached Annex A)
2. The
