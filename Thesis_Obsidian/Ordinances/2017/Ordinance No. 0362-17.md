SECTION 5. POWERS AND FUNCTIONS - The Maritime Coordinating Council
shall have the following powers and functions:
1. Implements measures to address all maritime safety and security

environmental protection in the maritime waters within the territorial
jurisdiction of Davao City in support of the concerned government

2. Sustains and monitors the protection of the maritime environment and
other issues and events that may affect the local safety and security
situation of Davao City in suppoft with the concerned government
agencies and NGOs;
3. Maintains a constant and up-to-date City risk assessment on
criminality, tenorism, maritime emergencies and smuggling happening
in the maritime waters and coastal areas within Davao City and to
take law enforcement actions relative thereto;
4. Develops plan to integrate and orchestrate law enforcement
operations affecting public safety and security along Davao City
coastal water;
5. Formulates and conducts trainings, seminars, workshops and other
activities within and among concerned agencies for the proper
implementation of the duties imposed in this Ordinance;
6. Facilitates the exchange of intelligence information among involved
government agencies in order to reinforce and strengthen maritime
law enforcement operations;
7. Analyzes and identifies target personalities, and determines the
emerging threats and crimes that happened at the maritime waters of
the City for possible law enforcement operations;
8. Recommends policies, plans, programs and projects for efficient
interoperability operations among agencies involved in enforcing the
duties imposed in this Ordinance;
9. Peforms other duties and functions as may be directed.

constitute a quorum to do business;
