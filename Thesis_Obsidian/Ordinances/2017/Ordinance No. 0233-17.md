Ord. No. 0233-17
Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in
session assembled that:
SECTION 1. TITLE.- This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
GRANTING THE REQUEST OF MINDANAO TRADE EXPO FOUNDATION, INC.
FOR EXEMPTION FROM THE PAYMENT OF PERMIT FEE UNDER ARTICLE 26,
SECTION 22\ PARAGRAPHS A AND D OF THE REVENUE CODE OF 2OO5 IN
CONNECTION WITH THE *2OI7 MINDANAO TRADE EXPO"I
SECTION 2. PERIOD. -The exemption shall be from August 11 to L7,20t7 and
shall only refer to the payment of Permit Fee under Article 26, SECTION 221, Paragraphs a
and d of the Revenue Code of 2005;
SECTION 3. COVERAGE. - This ordinance only applies to the exhibitors
representing the following :
1. Local Government of Davao;
2. Other Local Government units from Davao Oriental, Davao del
Nofte and the Province of Cotabato;
3. Duly registered Davao-based micro, small and medium
enterprises;
4. Government agencies such as the Depaftment of Trade and
Industry (DTI), the Department of Agriculture (DA), the
Depaftment of Agrarian Reform (DAR) and the Bureau of
Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR);
SECTION 4. CONDITIONS:
1. One booth must be reserved for the Davao City Investment
Promotion Center (DCIPC);
2. The Local Government of Davao must be duly credited as event
paftner and its official logo displayed prominently in the exhibit
area;
3. In no case shall the organizers be exempted from complying with
the other provisions of the Revenue Code of 2005 and other
applicable laws, executive orders and ordinances;
SECTION 5. EFFECTMTY.- This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval;
ENACTED, on the lst day of August 2017, by a unanimous vote of all the
Members of the Sanggunian.
