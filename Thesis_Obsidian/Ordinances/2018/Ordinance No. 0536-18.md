KNOWALL MEN BYTHESE PR-ESENTS:
This Memorandum of Agreement made and entered into by and between:
The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local government unit existing under the laws of the
Republic of the Philippines with office address at City Hall, Davao City, represented by its City
Mayor, SARA Z. DUTERTE, hereinafter referred to as "CGDAVAO";
The DEPARTMENT OFAGRICULTURE through its REGIONAL FIELD OFFICE XI, a government
agency created and existing under the Republic of the Philippines with office address at F.
Bangoy St., Davao City, represented by its OlC, Regional Executive Director ENGR. RICARDO M.
ONATE, f R., hereinafter referred to as "DA RIO XI";
and
The DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS AND HIGHWAYS REGIONAL OFFICE XI, a government
agency of the Republic of the Philippines with office address at Gov. Chavez St. corner R.
Magsaysay Ave. Davao City, represented by its Regional Director, ENGR. ATLAN S. BORROMEO,
hereinafter referred to as "DPWH RO XI".
WITNESSETH:
WHEREAS, CG-Davao has identified infrastructure development and agriculture as among the
Top L0 Priority Programs of the government;
WHEREAS, CG-Davao aims to rehabilitate Agdao Public Market,located at Brgy. Agdao Proper,
Davao City, to provide better facilities and services to the public;
WHEREAS, CG-Davao sought the assistance of the Department of Agriculture for funding of the
above- mentioned proj ect;
WHEREAS, Department of Agriculture through DA RFO Xt ini,tialty appropriated Two Hundred
Million Pesos Only (Php 200,000,000.00) for the Construction of Agdao Farmer's Market [1st
Phase), funded under its Locally Funded Project as per approved General Appropriations Act
[GAA) FY 201.8, RA# 10964, the budget in the amount of Six Hundred Million Pesos [Php
600,000,000.00) for the Phase II of the project has already been provided in the National
Expenditures Program for submission to Congress of the Philippines to be included in the 2019
budget of the Department of Agriculture, while the Phase III in the amount of Three Hundred
Fifty Million [Php 350,000,000) for the proposed parking building will be submitted by the
Department of Agriculture for corresponding budget allocation by the Department of Budget
and Managemen!
WHEREAS, DA RFO XI has sought the assistance of DPWH RO XI in the review of plans and
quantity estimates prepared by City Engineering Office, Davao City and prepare the cost
estimates including the procurement and implementation of the Project;
WHEREAS, the DPWH RO XI will review the Project Plans and quantity estimates and prepare
the Program of Work and Detailed Cost Estimates amounting to Two Hundred Million Pesos
Only (PhP 200,000,000.00) as the Proiect Cost;
WHEREAS, DPWH RO XI is willing to provide the personnel and expertise necessary for the
completion of the project;
NOW THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the parties hereto agree
as follows:
A. RESPONSIBILITIES OF CG.DAVAO
L. Prepare the Detailed Cost Estimates and Quantity Estimates;
