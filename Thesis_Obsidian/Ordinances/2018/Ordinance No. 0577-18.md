This Agreement made and entered into by and among:
The DAVAO CITY LOCAL GOVERNMENT with office address at City Hall Drive,
Poblacion District. Davao City represented herein by HON. MAYOR SARA Z.
DUTERTE-CARPIO, and referred to hereinafter as the FUNDING AGENCY,
and
The NATIONAL DAIRY AUTHORITY, a government-owned and controlled
corporation attached to the Department of Agriculture created by RA 7884, with
office address at the NDA Bldg., BAI Compound, Visayas Avenue, Diliman, Quezon
City, represented herein by its Administrator, MS. MARILYN B. MABALE,
hereinafter referred to as the NDA;
WITNESSETH:
WHEREAS, SECTION 16 of R,A. 7884, otherwise known as the National Dairy Development Act of
1995, provides that the government nutrition program requiring milk and dairy products shall be
sourced from the small farmers and dairy cooperatives;
WHEREAS, for several years NDA has been implementing a milk feeding program nationwide for
malnourished children in rural and urban communities aimed at sustaining livelihood and
entrepreneurship opportunities for local dairy farmers and decreased prevalence of undernourished
children;
WHEREAS, the City Government of Davao will embark on a supplemental feeding among identified
children beneficiaries in various barangays in Davao City
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the foregoing premises, the parties have hereunto
agreed and covenanted to execute this Program titled"EGG AND STERILIZED FRESH IVIILK FEEDING"
under the following terms and conditions:
ARTICLE 1
The Program
1.1 The Program which shall be funded by the NDA and the FUNDING AGENCY, aims to provide
sterilized fresh milk to pre-identified Moderately Acute Malnourished (MAM) preschool
children of Davao City on a continuing and regular basis for a period of 120 feeding days.
1,.2 The FUNDING AGENCY has pre-identified 1,200 beneficiaries based on the criteria set by the
City Health Office of Davao and the NDA.
