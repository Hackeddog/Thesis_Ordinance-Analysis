, SECTION 4. DECLARATION OF BARANGAY SIBULAN AS ORGANIC
AGRICULTURAL ZONE- It is hereby declared that Barangay Sibulan, located at the third
district of Davao City, is an Organic Agricultural Zone. As such, all synthetic and chemicalbased farm inputs shall be prohibited within the said zone. Fufther, no geneticallymodifi ed/engineered agricultural products shall be introduced therein.
SECTION 5. EUEEER ZNE- To preserue the integrity of the area as an organic
agricultural zone and to prevent contamination from non-organic practices surrounding the
area, a buffer zone shall be established.
5.1 A minimum of 40-meter buffer zone shall be established
between the organic agricultural zone and non-organic agricultural
areas near and/or adjacent to the former which shall be measured
from the outermost boundary of the organic agricultural zone. Buffer
Zones shall be planted with diversified plant species.
SECTION 6. FARM CONVERSION- Non-organic farms within the organic
agricultural zone shall have a maximum period of five (5) years from the date of approval
of this Ordinance within which to conveft their conventional farm to organic farm. No new
conventional farm using synthetic chemicals shall hereafter be established within the
organic zone.
SECTION 7. INCENTIVES TO INDIVIDUALS, FARMERS AND SCHOOLS. ThC
City shall give incentives, awards and financial suppoft to individuals, farmers and schools
which pursue, promote and practice organic farming. The City Agriculturist's Office shall
formulate guidelines for developing incentive programs, award systems and/or financial
support.
SECTION 8. IMPLEMENTING AGENCIES-The City Agriculturist's Office (CAO)
shall serue as the lead agency in implementing this order in collaboration with the City
Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO). The Depaftment of Agriculture Region
XI (DA XI), the Depaftment of Environment and Natural Resources Region XI (DENR XI),
and Feftilizers and Pesticides Authority Region XI (FPA XI ) are hereby enjoined to give
their full suppoft, assistance, and cooperation in the course of the implementation of the
provisions of this order.

Agriculture of Davao City 2009 or Ordinance No. 0384-10, SERIES of 2010, state that
provision of suppoft seruices to organic farmers and other assistance as may be deemed
necessary by the implementing agencies;
SECTION 10. SIGNS- The Barangay Sibulan LGU is hereby directed to provide
appropriate signages in collaboration with the cooperative advocating organic agriculture
and the City Agriculturist's Office indicating that the area is an organic agricultural zone, as
well as the prohibited acts within the area.
SECTION 11. SEPARABILITY CLAUSE- lf , for any reason, any SECTION or
provision of this Ordinance is declared unconstitutional or invalid, other sections or
provisions hereof not affected by such declaration shall continue to be in full force and
effect.

after completion of its publication in a local newspaper of general circulation in Davao City.
