Ord. No.0610-1 B
Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session
assembled, that:
SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE- This Ordinance shall be known as "THE PHOTOLUMINESCENT SAFEW AND DIRECTIONAL MARKINGS ORDINANCE OF DAVAO
CITY'.
SECTION 2. DEFINITION OF TERMS - As used in this Ordinance the following terms
shall mean:
1. Assembly-Assembly occupancies include, but are not
limited to, all buildings or portions of building used for
gathering together nfty (50) or more persons for such
purposes as deliberation, worship, enteftainment,
eating, drinking, amusement, awaiting transpoftation,
or similar uses;
2. Building Administrator- Any person who acts as
agent of the owner and manages the use of a building
for the latter;
3. Directional Markings-signs that give directional
information within a building/structure or
establishment. Directional markings are identified by
an arrow, multiple chevrons, or a line with chevrons
at one end to show the direction of exits;
4. Final Exit Door-any door leading directly to the
exterior or a street level lobby;
5. Means of Egress- continuous and unobstructed
route of exit from one point in a building, structure or
facility to a public way;
6. Photo-luminescent Materia!-emits light that
continues for a length of time after excitation by
visible or invisible light after the light source has been
removed;
7. Safety Signs-illuminated signs which contain
information or instruction regarding safety on a
signboard;
SECTION 3. COVERAGE- This Ordinance shall cover, but is not limited to, the
following types of establishments:
1. Place of assembly
2. Business Institutions
3. Educational Institutions
4. Storage Facilities
5. All structures with 300 or more persons
6. All buildings that are within 2,790 sq. m. floor area
7. All buildings with 3 or more storeys
B. All health care facilities with 4 or more admitted patients
9. All industrial facilities categorized as high hazard

Ord. No. 0610-18
SECTION 4. LOCATION OF MARKINGS- All establishments covered by this
Ordinance shall ensure that photo-luminescent safety markings are placed on:
1. Doors and doorframes:
a. Doodrames of final exit doors or doors
leading to means of egress;
b. Door handles, knobs, latches, panic devices
or opening devices of final exit doors or
doors leading to means of egress;
2. Stairways, handrails, ramps and steps
a. Landings, mid-landings, passageways or
corridors within the stairway;
b. Floor number and stainryay name;
c. All handrails and handrails extensions;
d. Leading edge of each step and the perimeter
of ramps;
3. Means of Egress
a. The Floors of means of egress shall be
illuminated at all points by directional
markings including angles and intersections
of corridors and passageways;
b. All safety signs leading to means of egress
and final exit doors;
i. principal routes providing
access to exit in open floor
areas and in seruice rooms;
ii. underground walkways,
basements;
iii. floor areas or parts thereof
where the public may
congregate;
4. Obstacles-all obstructions along the means of
egress, stairwells, ramps, no-exit doors, etc.
5. Safety Equipment- Fire-fighting and emergency
equipment such as fire extinguishers, fire hose,
first aid kit, etc.
SECTION 5.
- All owners
or building administrators, in case of buildings with multiple occupants, of establishments
covered by this Ordinance shall be required to install photo-luminescent safety and directional
markings prior to the issuance of the Building Occupanry Permit. Occupanry of an
establishment prior to compliance with the required installations shall constitute a violation of
this Ordinance.
The Office of the City Building Official, in cooperation with the Bureau of Fire Protection,
shall be the agency tasked with the implementation of the provisions of this Ordinance and is
empowered to conduct the inspection of the Photo-luminescent markings of each establishment
at least once every 3 years.
The Photo-luminescent markings must continue to be present and visible in the
establishment for as long as it is operational.
SECTION 6. PERIOD OF COMPLIANCE FOR EXISTING STRUCTURES.
Establishments already occupied prior to the enactment of this Ordinance shall be given six (6)
months to comply with the required installation of photo-luminescent safety and directional
markings.
