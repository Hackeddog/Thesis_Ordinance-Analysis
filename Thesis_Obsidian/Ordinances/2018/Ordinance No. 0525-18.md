This Memorandum of Agreement made and entered into by and between:
Home Development Mutual Fund, popularly known as Pag-lBlG Fund, a government

Services Operations Sector, Visayas and Mindanao Group, RIO R. TEVES and hereinafter referred

-andThe City Government of Davao, with office address at City Hall, San Pedro St., Davao City
represented by its Local Chief Executive, HON. SARA Z. DUTERTE-CARPIO and hereinafter
referred to as the SECOND PARW.
WITNESSETH:
WHEREAS, the FIRST PARTY, is the designated agency of the government in carrying the
policy of the State to establish, develop, promote and integrate a nationwide sound and viable
tax-exempt mutual provident savings system suitable to the needs of the employed and other
earning groups and to motivate to better plan and provide for their housing needs;
WHEREAT the FIRST PARW is mandated to implement the provisions of R.A. 9679
otherwise known as the Pag-lBlG Fund law of 2009 particularly the registration of all coverable
employees as provided for under the said law;
WHEREAS, the FIRST PARTY, is mandated to register allcoverable employees of the Fund
and carry out its aim to permeate every locality where there are employed and other earning
groups present through the loca! government unit concerned;
WHEREAS, the SECOND PARW aims to encourage cooperation and coordination among
government institutions through mutually beneficial programs, projects and activities
particularly to encourage its workers, laborers and other earning sectors within its area of
jurisdiction for the Fund coverage;
WHEREAS, the SECOND PARTY supports the program of the FIRST PARW in the latterrs
campaign for the registration of all covered workers within its locality through its direct
participation by allowing all its Job Orders and Contract of Service Personnel to register with the

and be covered by the Fund.

Service Personnel shall be deducted from his wage or salary by the SECOND PARTY.
