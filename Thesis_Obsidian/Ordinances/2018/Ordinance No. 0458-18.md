AN ORDINANCE PENALIZING THE SNEAKING

Ord. No. 0458-18
Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City in session assembled
that
SECTION 1. SQPE - This Ordinance shall cover alljails and detention centers
situated within the jurisdiction of the City of Davao and such other confinements as the
court may direct to place the inmates or patients for their confinement.
SECTION 2. DEC1ARATION OF POUCY- The provisions of this Ordinance, in
consonance with the City Governmenfs commitment for good governance and
maintaining public order and safety, hereby declares this policy to strengthen the
implementation of security measures imposed by the Bureau of Jail Management and
Penology (BJMP), the Philippine National Police (PNP), Rehabilitation Centers and other
Law Enforcement Agencies. For this purpose, the City of Davao adheres to the following
measures:
a) To prevent the occurrence of deaths, riots, hostage taking and escape of
inmates or patients by prohibiting the possession and entry of deadly and
bladed weapons;
b) To prevent the inmates or patients from coercing, intimidating, threatening
complainants and witnesses or other forms of retaliation, orchestrate crimes
and organize rescue plan by prohibiting the possession and entry of cellular
phones and other electronic gadgets;
c) To prevent the inmates or patients from bribing jail or detention center
personnel or custodians by prohibiting the possession of excessive amount of
money in cash or check inside jails or detention centers;
d) To safeguard public health and well-being of the inmates or patients by
penalizing the possession and entry of illegal drugs, alcoholic substances,
cigarettes and tobaccos; and
e) To promote public welfare in the community by ensuring the rehabilitation and
development of inmates or patients in preparation for their eventual
reintegration into the society upon release.
SECTION 3. rc-As
used in this Ordinance, the following
shall be construed to mean as follows:
a) Prohibited ltemF any article or thing forbidden by law and/or by jail and
detention center rules that pose as security hazards or endanger the lives of
inmates, patients, visitors or personnel. Under this Ordinance, the following
are considered prohibited items:
i.
Dangerous drugs and paraphernalia;
ii.
Deadly and bladed weapons;
iii. Firearms and/or its accessories or any part thereof,
ammunition, improvised explosive devices and any possible
components such as electric wires, batteries, fertilizers, etc.;
iv. Metal cutting saw or blades and digging tools not permitted
by the jail management;
v.
Cigarette and other tobacco products, including e-cigarettes
and the like;
vi.
Liquor, beer, coconut wine and other alcoholic substances;
vii. Cash which is more than the prescribed allowable amount,
including checks, or other commodities of exchange such as
jewelry, appliances and gadgets;
viii. Mobile phones and other electronic devices;
