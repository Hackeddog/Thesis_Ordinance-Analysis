"For efficient, effective and economical governance
the purpose of which is the general welfare of the

Code, the city mayor shall represent the city in all its
business transactions and sign in its behalf all

RECITALS
WHEREAS, by virtue of Republic Act No. 9500 (An Act to Strengthen the University
of the Philippines as the National University), UP is mandated to provide opportunities for
training and learning in leadership, responsible citizenship, and the development of democratic
values, institutions and practice through academic and non-academic programs, including
sports, and the enhancement of nationalism and national identity.
WHEREAS, UP is committed to lead in providing affordable quality education,
scholarly research, and responsive and relevant extension services to diverse, marginalized and
deserving sectors in Mindanao and neighboring regions through its programs in the sciences
and the arts including the development of sports, human kinetics, and physical recreation
activities to improve the peace and development as well as the sociocultural attributes of
Mindanao, and to inculcate a passion for excellence, creative thinking, and nationalism in the
context of cultural diversity in a global community;
WHEREAS, the Board of Regents (hereafter the "BOR") of UP at its 1255ft Meeting
held on June 2010 approved the UP Mindanao Land Use Plan that includes twenty (20) hectares
for the Sports Complex,
WHEREAS, UP desires to develop and construct a sports complex in said twenty (20)
hectare area, situated within the UP Mindanao Campus, at Mintal, Davao City-specifically,
within the property described as Lot 129-K-2 registered in its name under Transfer Certificate
of Title (TCT) No. 346863, of the Registry of Deeds for Davao City in order to fulfill its
mandate under its Charter;
WHEREAS, the CITY OF DAVAO envisions itself as the premier socio-economic and
tourism center in Mindanao, East Asean Growth Area (EAGA) and the Asia-Pacific Region
propelled by enlightened leaders and empowered citizenry and committed to sustainable
growth and development under the guidance of Divine Providence;
WHEREAS, the CITY OF DAVAO also desires to establish a major sports fhcility
designed to serve as unifuing venue to showcase the common and distinctive social, cultural,
indigenous heritage and identity of the people of Mindanao through the development of sports
and human kinetics, and promote the maximum exposure and positive image of the CITY OF
