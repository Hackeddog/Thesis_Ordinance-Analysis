donated in favor of the Second Party a Christmas Tree (including all lights, decors and materials

terms and conditions of this Agreement.

covenant and agree:

well as the labor for setting up a Christmas tree which shall be erected and displayed at Rizal
Park, Davao City. The cost of electricity however shall be for the account of the Second Party.
