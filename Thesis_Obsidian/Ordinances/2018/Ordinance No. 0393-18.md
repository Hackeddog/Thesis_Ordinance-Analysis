MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:
This Memorandum of Agreement made and entered into this day of
by and between:
The OFFIGE OF THE PRESIDENT, with office address at Malacafiang Palace,
J.P. Laurel St., San Miguel, Manila, represented herein by the Executive Secretary
SALVADOR C. MEDIALDEA., and hereinafter referred to as the OP;
and -
The CITY GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO, a local government unit in Region Xl, with
office address at San Pedro St. Davao City, Davao Sur, represented herein by MAYOR
SARA Z. DUTERTE and hereinafter referred to as the LGU-DAVAO CITY, issued
Executive Order No. 35 SERIES of 2016 An Order Strengthening the Davao City Lingap
Para sa Mahirap Program, Further Redefining the Program's Thrust, Providing for the
General Guidelines for all Processes, Allocating Funds Therefore, and For Other
Purposes;
yyrrrvEssETH, that:
WHEREAS, the City Government of Davao (the "City Government") constantly
promotes programs that help alleviate the economic and health conditions of its
constituents through making services accessible to less fortunate and indigent members
of the communities; ;
WHEREAS, among the programs implemented by the City Government in order
to address poverty focuses on the provision of medical assistance and fundamental
needs of recognized and qualified beneficiaries;
WHEREAS, the continuing increase of the public beneficiaries shows the
effectiveness of these programs thereby giving the City Government more opportunity to
advance its implementation and strategy in this aspect for the general welfare of its
people;
WHEREAS, one of these programs is the Lingap Para sa Mahirap Program (the
"Lingap Program") that has, through the years, provided medical assistance and benefits
to indigents;
WHEREAS, considering that the guidelines of the Lingap Program of the City
Government of Davao has already been established, and taking cognizant of the
pressing concern vis-d-vis the government's obligation to prioritize the health and wellbeing of the people and in order to extend medical assistance beyond Davao City
stituents, the Office of the President will allocate Thirty Million Pesos
(P30,000,000.00) monthly for the Pagkalinga sa Bayan Program, to be sourced from the
Socio-Civic Project Fund (SCPF).

Civic Project Fund (SCPF) for the Pagkalinga sa Bayan Program.
2.
The OP - Office of the Deputy Executive Secretary for Finance and
Administration (ODESFA) shall pay direct to the hospitals, funeral parlors
and other establishments issued by Guarantee Letter upon receipt of billing
statement and supporting documents.
LOCAL GOVERNMENT OF DAVAO ClrY (LGU-DAVAO ClrY)
The LGU-DAVAO CITY shall utilize the funding solely for Pagkalinga sa
Bayan Program in Mindanao particularly Region Xl, Xll and Xlll thru
issuance of Guarantee Letters. ln no case shall the amount exceed
P50,000.00 net of Philhealth and other assistance for medical and
P30, 000. 00 for funeral.
The LGU-DAVAO CITY shall submit to the OP the Quarterly Summary of
Guarantee Letters issued.
