crw

Ord. No. 0430-18
Be it ordained by the SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled that:
SECTION 1. TITLE - This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
DECLARING DATU BAGO AS HERO OF DAVAO CIW'.
SECTION 2. PARAMOUNT WARRI9R - Also called "Bawwangin" by the local
tribes), Datu Bago was of Kagan, Tausug and Maguindanawon ancestry who setUed in
Bagobo land (now known as Davao). He fought long and hard in keeping Davao Gulf
free from foreign control as the whole area around the Gulf of Davao was under his
control. In 1839, Datu Bago joined by the Bagobo and Kagan Tribes, struck his
greatest blow against foreign invaders. Davao was the last unoccupied territory in the
Philippines and Datu Bago heroically defended it against the Spaniards.
SECTION 3. COMMEMORATION A date shall be set for its annual
commemoration to be determined by the Office of the City Mayor in coordination with
the Davao Historical Society.
SECTION 4. EDUCATION- All schools in the City of Davao, public and private,
shall instill in its curriculum the life and time of Datu Bago's reign as a fitting tribute to
a local hero.
SECTION S.CONSTRUCTION OF MONUMENT - In order for the people of the
City of Davao, particularly the youth, to regard Datu Bago with respect and to
remember and appreciate his heroism, a monument of Datu Bago shall be erected at a
site most sultable to honor him. The site shall be identified by the Office of the City
Vlayor in consultation with the Davao Historical Society and Datu Bago Awardees
