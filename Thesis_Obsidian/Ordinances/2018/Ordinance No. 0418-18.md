Ord. No. 0418-18
Be it ordained by the Honorable SANGGUNIANG Panlungsod of Davao City, in session
assembled that:
SECTION 1. TITLE.- This Ordinance shall be known as "AN ORDINANCE
GRANTING THE LETTER-REQUEST OF MTNDANAO TRADE EXPO FOUNDATION
INC., FOR EXEMPTTON FROM THE PAYMENT OF MAYOR',S PERMTT FEE IMPOSED
ON DAVAO.BASED MICRO, SMALL AND MEDIUM ENTREPRENEURS DURING THE
81"t ARAW NG DABAW CELEBRATION ON MARCH 11-15, 2018 AT ABREEZA
MALL, J.P. LAUREL AVENUE, THIS CITY";
SECTION 2. COVERAGE. - The exemption applies only to Davao-based exhibitors
and solely refers to the payment of Mayor's permit fee under Article 26, SECTION 22t,
paragraphs a and d of the Revenue Code of 2005 and only covers Davao-based registered
micro, small and medium enterprises;
SECTION 3. PERIOD AND LIMITATION. - The exemption shall be from
March 11-15, 2018 and that in no case shall the organizers be exempt from compliance of
the other applicable laws and ordinances;
SECTION 5. EFFECTMTY.- This Ordinance shall take effect immediately upon
approval;
ENACTED, on the 6th day of March 2018, by a unanimous vote of all the Members
of the Sanggunian, there being a quorum.
